# AP3P2D Valuation-Algebra and Aspect-Typing Independence Audit v1 — Report
## Result: core and AP3 typing do not choose the valuation algebra or multiply lifts without aspect typing

## Question

After AP3P2C, the exact formal alternatives are clear. Does the protected identity plus present AP3 typing decide:

1. whether Q-bit structure is fibre-local or cross-sector relational; or
2. whether any residual non-gauge lift orbit is a genuine aspect to which conditional plenitude applies?

All three retain the common typed base: non-structural ground, finite proper phase contexts, intrinsic non-background successor structure, internal retentional trace, no external carrier/monitor, and nonexclusive coexistence of declared flow components.

| Extension | Valuation-content status | Gauge result | Plenitude result |

The formal counts are exact:
\[
16\ \text{sections},
\qquad 1\ \text{full-gauge orbit},
\qquad 8\ \text{uniform-gauge orbits},
\qquad 8\ \text{relative signatures}.
\]

All registered checks pass.

## Result

The protected core says:
\[
\mathsf{GenuineMode}
\equiv
\mathsf{SelfAspect}_G
\equiv
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G.
\]

It does not itself specify:

- a global Q-bit section across trace sectors;
- cross-sector observables;
- an action on valuation coordinates;
- whether lift/orbit classes are contents of a finite flow or alternative grammar laws;
- a map placing those classes into the admissible aspect domain \(\mathcal A\).

Consequently:
\[
K+\text{current AP3 typing}
\not\models
\mathcal A_{\rm loc},
\quad
\mathcal A_{\rm rel},
\quad
\text{or aspect membership of residual lift classes}.
\]

This is not an invitation to select one actual lift. Conditional plenitude has the opposite implication once genuine aspect typing is in place: under R, all eight explicitly typed aspect candidates co-realize. The audit blocks only the illicit move of treating arbitrary formal alternatives as automatically genuine aspects.

## Exact remaining debt

The programme needs a valuation-content law answering:

> Is a Q free-bit merely a fibre-local presentation coordinate, or does it carry relations across trace sectors that are intrinsic to the finite mode-flow?

It also needs an aspect-typing law answering:

> If a non-gauge quotient class remains, is it a genuine self-aspect/content of the flow, or merely a rival formalization of its continuation grammar?

Neither answer is supplied by symmetry/no-preference alone. If a relational law is independently justified and its eight classes are typed as genuine aspects, conditional plenitude yields co-realization rather than selection. If a fibre-local law is justified, the full redefinition orbit can be treated as one description class. If the choices remain grammar laws, their relation requires comparative formal assessment rather than ontic multiplication.

## Conclusion

AP3P2D closes the available inference from the current core to valuation-gauge choice. The remaining issue is an explicitly new bridge, not more enumeration:
\[
\text{independent valuation-content law}
+
\text{independent aspect-typing law}.
\]

Until then, AP3P1 remains a coherent conditional intrinsic-flow grammar with unresolved gauge/content status, and AP4C1 remains a separate conditional public-record implementation.

## 4. R2 — operational witness

### 4.1 Anchor-role portion

Conditional on a completed background grammar, the frozen witness supplies the required operational profile for \(g\) and \(h\): each is closed, bindable, renamable, countable, and first-slot capable. It also supplies the two stated \(\mathsf K\)-formations and a grammar-preserving renaming \(g\leftrightarrow h\).

Thus the intended anchor-role pattern is visible:

\[
\mathsf{AnchorPattern}(g)
\land
\mathsf{AnchorPattern}(h).
\tag{R2-Anchor-Pattern}
\]

Because R0 is incomplete, this is not a fully mechanically verified derivation. It is nevertheless not the decisive defect in the frozen witness.

### 4.2 Dependent-modifier condition under test

The candidate defines dependence **relative to one fixed first-slot mark** \(s\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{DependentModifier}_{\Gamma}(q;s)
\iff{}&
\mathcal O_\Gamma(q).\mathsf{md}=1
\land
\mathcal O_\Gamma(q).\mathsf{cl}=0
\land
\mathcal O_\Gamma(q).\mathsf{bd}=0\\
&\land
\mathcal O_\Gamma(q).\mathsf{ct}=0
\land
\mathcal O_\Gamma(q).\mathsf{fs}=0\\
&\land
\forall\psi\,[
\Gamma\vdash\psi(q)
\Longrightarrow
\Gamma\vdash\psi(q@s)\land\mathsf{FirstSlotCapable}_{\Gamma}(s)].
\end{aligned}
\tag{R2-Fixed-Partner-Definition}
\]

The prose immediately preceding this formula is equally explicit: every well-formed occurrence of a modifier must be paired with **that same** first-slot mark.

But the frozen witness supplies both of the following occurrences:

\[
\mu@g,
\qquad
\mu@h,
qquad
 g\neq h,
\tag{R2-Mu-Multiple-Partners}
\]

and likewise:

\[
\nu@g,
\qquad
\nu@h,
qquad
 g\neq h.
\tag{R2-Nu-Multiple-Partners}
\]

Therefore \(\mu\) cannot satisfy the fixed-partner condition relative to \(g\), because it also has a well-formed occurrence paired with \(h\); symmetrically it cannot satisfy it relative to \(h\). The same applies to \(\nu\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{DependentModifier}_{\Gamma}(\mu;g)=\mathsf{False},
\qquad
\mathsf{DependentModifier}_{\Gamma}(\mu;h)=\mathsf{False},\\
&\mathsf{DependentModifier}_{\Gamma}(\nu;g)=\mathsf{False},
\qquad
\mathsf{DependentModifier}_{\Gamma}(\nu;h)=\mathsf{False},
\end{aligned}
\tag{R2-Witness-Conflict}
\]

under the candidate's own universal fixed-partner definition.

The witness's asserted result,

\[
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(\mu;g)
\land
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(\nu;g),
\tag{R2-Asserted-Role-Result}
\]

is consequently not derivable from the frozen grammar and witness.

### 4.3 R2 disposition

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{R2Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}7})
=
\mathsf{Fail}_{\substack{
\rm modifier\ witness\ pairs\ each\ mark\ with\ more\ than\ one\ first\!\text{-}slot\ mark,\\

}
\tag{R2-Result}
\]

This is a narrow formal failure in the supplied witness. It neither establishes that operational role methods are futile nor licenses a metaphysical conclusion.

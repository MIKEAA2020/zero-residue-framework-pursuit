# Sadrian Gradation and Finite Actuality Reconstruction v1
## Source-grounded, provisional A0 inquiry into *aṣālat al-wujūd* and *tashkīk al-wujūd*

## Status and result class

This dossier applies `Point-Sphere_Sadrian_Intensive_Actuality_Test_Protocol_v1.md`. It is a source-grounded reconstruction from accessible primary translations/passages and scholarly controls. It does **not** report a final A0 pass or a structural failure.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SadrianIntensiveA0Result}=	ext{Inconclusive, with substantial live articulation resources.}
}
\]

The reason is not that Ṣadrian intensity is merely metaphorical or inherently private. On the contrary, the source gives substantive resources concerning actuality, individuation, mental/extra-mental being, and the unity/multiplicity of finite existents. But the available reconstruction has not recovered a public, profile-conservative admission rule that decides the relevant finite-mode cases without relying on the contested, already interpreted order of degrees, causes, and modes of existence.

No claim follows that Ṣadrā fails in the way of Proclus, Advaita, Hegel, Leibniz, Rāmānuja, Spinoza, Aristotle, or any other source.

## 2. Reconstructed ontology

The core Ṣadrian architecture is not simply that there is one entity plus properties. It distinguishes the conceptual treatment of being from the reality of existence:

\[
\mathsf{ConceptOfExistence}\neq\mathsf{ExtraMentalRealityOfExistence}.
\]

The basic theses reconstructed from the source controls are:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_1 &: \text{existence, not quiddity/essence, is primary in extra-mental reality};\\
S_2 &: \text{quiddities are conceptual delimitations or modes of intelligibility, not self-standing}\
&\text{rivals to existence};\\
S_3 &: \text{existence is one simple reality but genuinely differentiated by gradation};\\
S_4 &: \text{gradation concerns intensity/weakness, priority/posteriority, perfection/deficiency,}\
&\text{and independence/dependence};\\
S_5 &: \text{contingent existents are relational/dependent on absolute independent being};\\
S_6 &: \text{existence, rather than quiddity, is a principle of individuation};\\
S_7 &: \text{mental and extra-mental existence are distinct, real modes of existence};\\
S_8 &: \text{substantial motion describes dynamic intensification and change in finite existence.}
\end{array}
\]

The doctrine is therefore neither a bare actual-roster criterion nor merely a statement that finite things depend on God.

## 3. The intensive proposal at its strongest

The source makes a serious answer to the problem of one and many. A finite existent is not best construed as an independently self-subsisting substance to which existence is subsequently added. It is a contingent, dependent, particular degree or act of existence:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SadrianFiniteDegree}(x)\Rightarrow {}&
\mathsf{ExtraMentalWujud}(x)\land
\mathsf{Contingent}(x)\\
&\land\mathsf{IndividuatedByWujud}(x)\land
\mathsf{Graded}(x).
\end{aligned}
\]

The target contrast is:

\[
\mathsf{Wujud}_{S}(x)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{Quiddity}_{S}(x)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{Wujud}_{S}(x)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{IndependentFiniteSubstance}(x).
\]

In a frequently reported formulation from *al-Asfār* IX:186, instances of being differ in intensity/weakness, priority/posteriority, and nobility/baseness, while conceptual quiddities differ through genus, species, and accidents. In *al-Asfār* I:42–43, the gradational marks include priority and greater intensity. The alleged distinction-maker is therefore not a conventional taxonomy alone.

This gives a genuine intensive candidate:

\[
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\in
\{\text{more/less intense, prior/posterior, perfect/deficient, independent/dependent}\}.
\]

It must not be reduced, at this stage, to a numerical scale or a simple causal-chain position.

## 4. Primary source resources relevant to A0

### 4.1 Existence is claimed to be real rather than a mere predicate

Ṣadrā rejects an ontology in which existence is an accidental label affixed to an antecedently real essence. The Kalin control reports the claim that the actualization of being occurs by itself, and that the individual tree and its quiddity are not two separable realities. The SEP control likewise records a real-predicate reading, while insisting on the distinction between existence as a mental secondary intelligible and existence as extra-mental reality.

This is not yet an A0 admission procedure, but it prevents the premature verdict:

\[
\mathsf{Wujud}_{S}(x)=\text{merely a grammatical predicate}.
\]

### 4.2 Gradation is claimed to account for genuine plurality

The IEP reports Ṣadrā’s formulation that being is one reality yet comes in grades, with finite differences by degree. The source is intended to preserve diversity rather than abolish it. The light analogy is illustrative, not the criterion itself.

Thus the live proposal is not merely:

\[
\mathsf{DependsOn}(x,G),
\]

but:

\[
\mathsf{DependsOn}(x,G)
\land
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x),
\]

where gradation is alleged to make finite plurality metaphysically intelligible.

### 4.3 The source gives an explicit individuation claim

The SEP reports *al-Asfār* III:351 as saying that existence is the principle of individuality, can intensify or weaken, and retains the individual as itself across changing contexts. Burrell’s translated *al-Asfār* selection likewise reports that existence individuates without needing an additional property and grants distinction and realization to essence.

This is a direct resource against the claim that the view cannot even address particularity:

\[
\mathsf{IndividuationAnswer}_{S}\text{ is present.}
\]

Whether it supplies a public criterion for two candidate particulars is a further question; it cannot be replaced by the assertion that Ṣadrā says nothing about individuation.

### 4.4 The source distinguishes mental from extra-mental being

Ṣadrā does not simply identify everything thinkable with an extra-mental finite entity. The SEP controls report two distinct and non-interchangeable modes, being *in re* and mental existence, and note that mental existence permits conception of entities without extra-mental reference. The IEP treats mental being as an immaterial, particular mode of existence and denies that this entails subjective idealism.

This supplies a genuine rival distinction:

\[
\mathsf{MentalMode}_{S}(x)
\neq
\mathsf{ExtraMentalMode}_{S}(x).
\]

It means that an A0 inquiry must not equate “fiction” with absolute nonbeing without fixing the intended type. A fictional unicorn may have a mental mode of being while lacking the required extra-mental reality.

### 4.5 Epistemic resources are mixed

Ṣadrā gives an important role to demonstration and judgments about whether things exist, while the full reality of being is said to require presential/illuminative knowledge. The source’s own epistemology therefore includes both discursive and non-discursive elements:

\[
\mathsf{Demonstration}_{S}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{PresenceOrWitnessing}_{S}.
\]

This is neither an automatic public pass nor an automatic failure. The remaining question is whether the theory turns either resource into an intersubjectively corrigible criterion for a proposed finite candidate.

## 5. Interface status

### What intensity contributes

The source assigns intensity several jobs at once:

\[
\mathsf{Tashkik}_{S}
\rightsquigarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{unity in multiplicity},\\
\text{gradation of actuality/perfection},\\
\text{vertical and horizontal ordering},\\
\text{a basis for individuation and dynamism.}
\end{cases}
\]

That is enough to block the allegation that this is simply the previously tested relation of dependence with a changed name.

### What remains unresolved

The source also tightly connects intensity to dependence, causality, perfection, and the Absolute. The available record does not yet show whether:

\[
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\text{ has an admission role independent of an already admitted causal/ontological hierarchy,}
\]

or whether intensity is fixed only after the hierarchy of actual beings, causes, and degrees is in place. This is a live question, not a source failure.

Hence:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm Sadra}\subseteq\mathcal K_{\rm new}
\quad\text{remains appropriate provisionally.}
\]

## 7. Why the result is not a structural failure

The protocol required structural evidence for \(S_1\text{--}S_6\). That evidence has not been earned.

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_1 &: \text{not established: the source has non-roster resources of extra-mental reality and effects};\\
S_2 &: \text{a pressure, not established: causal/hierarchical dependence may be integral rather than circular};\\
S_3 &: \text{not established: the source articulates several nontrivial ontological boundaries};\\
S_4 &: \text{not established: the source asserts an individuation principle in existence itself};\\
S_5 &: \text{profile invariance and public error conditions are missing from this reconstruction, but absence}\
&\text{from the present record does not demonstrate their impossibility};\\
S_6 &: \text{not established: presence/witnessing has not been shown to be the sole available epistemic resource.}
\end{array}
\]

The fair verdict is therefore not:

\[
\mathsf{SadrianCriterion}\to\mathsf{BridgeFail}.
\]

It is:

\[
\mathsf{SadrianCriterion}\not\Rightarrow\mathsf{A0Pass}
\quad\text{on the present source record.}
\]

## 8. Why the result is not an A0 pass

An A0 pass would require a public, noncircular membership verdict such as:

\[
\mathsf{ProposedFinite}(x)
\mapsto
\{
\mathsf{ExtraMentalFiniteMode},
\mathsf{MentalMode},
\mathsf{PropertyOnly},
\mathsf{QuiddityOnly},
\mathsf{Nonentity}
\},
\]

together with publicly stated correction conditions and a profile-conservative account of preservation under re-description.

The current reconstruction instead yields a rich conditional schema:

\[
\mathsf{ExtraMentalWujud}(x)
\land
\mathsf{IndividuatedByWujud}(x)
\land
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SadrianFiniteDegree}(x),
\]

while the conditions on the left have not yet been made independently decidable for arbitrary candidate \(x\) without relying on the theory’s wider, actual ontology of degrees, causes, and kinds.

That gap is a research burden, not a disproof.

The next stage must keep these live rather than selecting a convenient reading:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}

B_2 &: \text{intensity as an intrinsic differentiator} &\text{versus intensity fixed through causal dependence};\\
B_3 &: \text{public demonstrative access} &\text{versus a decisive role for non-public presential witnessing};\\
B_4 &: \text{finite degrees as genuine modes} &\text{versus their being only aspects/properties of the One};\\

\end{array}
\]

None is to be settled merely by selecting the most convenient paraphrase of “degree.”

## 11. Bridge limits

This dossier does not enter finite empirical reports into the CFD corpus, does not make mental being evidence that a metaphysical finite mode is genuine, and does not establish phenomenal adequacy.

Ṣadrā’s substantial motion and temporal-origination doctrines are not a QM/GR bridge, a recovery theorem, an observable, or a solution to the Problem of Time. No physical-adequacy verdict follows.

## Nonclaim

This dossier does not prove Ṣadrian ontology true, false, circular, or successful; does not treat all finite things as mere appearances; does not identify the Qur’an with a formal ontology; does not reduce Ibn ʿArabī or Ashʿarism to already completed failures; and does not identify a correct whole-reality framework.

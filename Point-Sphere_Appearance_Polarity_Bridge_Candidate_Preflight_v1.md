**Registration selected by the user:** a proposed bridge within the current strict nondual/pure-negation Appearance candidate.

**Sphere scope selected by the user:** the whole appearance as one field, not a plurality of independently real finite entities.

## 0. Scope and non-amendment rule

This preflight records a proposed Point–Sphere contrast grammar. It does not introduce a new whole-reality framework, amend the protected core, reopen a frozen P1–P4 structure, authorize a PPA successor, or convert an appearance code into a second being.

Let \(G\) be the sole absolute ontological bearer under the strict pure-negation core:

\[
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Timeless}(G).
\tag{Absolute-Core}
\]

The word “point” is a nonspatial diagram for this nonextended unity. It is not a geometrical point located in an already actual space:

\[
\mathsf{PointDiagram}(G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{LocatedInSpace}(G).
\tag{No-Literal-Point}
\]

Let \(\sigma\) be a code for the proposed whole appearance-field. The word “sphere” is likewise a diagram for comprehensive articulated appearance, not a second spatial container or independently real totality:

\[
\mathsf{SphereDiagram}(\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentEntity}(\sigma).
\tag{No-Second-Realm}
\]

## 1. The proposed appearance-level polarity

The intended contrast is represented by the ordered table:

\[
\Pi_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma)
=
\left\langle
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{absolute-side designation} & \text{appearance-side designation}\\
\hline
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G) & \mathsf{Complex}_{\rm app}(\sigma)\\
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G) & \mathsf{Spatial}_{\rm app}(\sigma)\\
\mathsf{Timeless}(G) & \mathsf{Temporal}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\end{array}
\right\rangle.
\tag{PS-Table}
\]

The subscript `app` is mandatory. It says only that the relevant structure is presented **within the appearance field**. It does not promote spatiality, temporality, or complexity to intrinsic positive constituents of \(G\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Complex}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PositiveInternalComplexity}(G),\\
&\mathsf{Spatial}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Spatiality}(G),\\
&\mathsf{Temporal}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Temporality}(G).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Core-Preservation}
\]

On the selected appearance-level reading, \(\Pi_{\rm PS}\) is a typed cross-level contrast grammar. It is not a real relation between two independent entities, an internal division in \(G\), or a process carried out by \(G\):

\[
\mathsf{Polarity}_{\rm PS}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Part}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{PositiveInternalForm}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{SecondBeing}(\sigma).
\tag{Polarity-Firewall}
\]

## 2. Correction of the temporal wording

“Parsimoniously laid out over time and space” cannot mean that the timeless Absolute first exists and subsequently performs a spatial-temporal laying-out act. That would introduce an unregistered change or external time:

\[
\mathsf{Timeless}(G)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists t_0<t_1\,
\mathsf{ProducesAt}(G,\sigma,t_1).
\tag{No-Temporal-Production}
\]

The present bridge proposal is therefore restricted to a contrast-description claim:

\[
\mathsf{PSContrast}(G,\sigma)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ContrastivelyDescribed}
\bigl(
\mathsf{Simple/Complex},
\mathsf{Spaceless/Spatial},
\mathsf{Timeless/Temporal}
\bigr).
\tag{Contrast-Only}
\]

It does **not** yet supply an atemporal grounding, constitution, manifestation, or realization operation:

\[
\mathsf{PSContrast}(G,\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Manifest}_G(\sigma)
\lor
\mathsf{ActualAppearance}(\sigma)
\lor
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(x).
\tag{No-Actualization-Bridge}
\]

Parsimony remains a methodological constraint on a proposed account. It is not an actuality-producing relation:

\[
\mathsf{Parsimonious}(\Pi_{\rm PS})
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualAppearance}(\sigma).
\tag{Parsimony-Not-Actuation}
\]

## 3. Required distinctions

The bridge must preserve the following distinctions, which its diagrams do not settle by themselves:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{diagrammatic point} &\ne& \text{a spatially located point},\\
\text{diagrammatic sphere} &\ne& \text{a physical sphere or a second ontology},\\
\text{cross-level contrast} &\ne& \text{an actuality/manifestation relation},\\
\text{appearance-field complexity} &\ne& \text{composition in }G,\\
\text{temporal appearance} &\ne& \text{change in }G,\\
\text{formal spatial-temporal representation} &\ne& \text{public physical adequacy}.
\end{array}
\tag{Required-Distinctions}
\]

In particular, a contrast pair is not a proof that its second pole obtains:

\[
\bigl[
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Timeless}(G)
\bigr]
\not\Rightarrow
\exists\sigma\,
[
\mathsf{Complex}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\land
\mathsf{Spatial}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
\land
\mathsf{Temporal}_{\rm app}(\sigma)
].
\tag{No-Polar-Generation}
\]

Nor does it determine which whole appearance-field, its internal regularities, finite loci, or profiles obtains if one does:

\[
\mathsf{PSContrast}(G,\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactAppearanceField}(\sigma).
\tag{No-Exact-Appearance}
\]

\mathsf{CoherentDiagrammaticAppearanceContrast}

\[
\langle

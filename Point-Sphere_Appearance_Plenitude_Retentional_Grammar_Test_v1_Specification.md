# AP3N Retentional Grammar Test v1
## Internal order and record coherence without background physical time

## Purpose

AP3M leaves five self-presentation grammar families. AP3N tests the natural phenomenological addition: finite appearance has internal retentional/anticipatory articulation. The test asks whether an **endogenous** order/record relation constrains the family without introducing external physical time.

## Retentional enrichment

For every finite mode context \(C\), introduce a nontrivial internal strict partial order
\[
\prec_C\ \subset C\times C.
\]
It represents an immanent order of retention/presence/anticipation, not a coordinate \(t\), a background evolution parameter, or a gravitational time variable.

Requirements:

1. **R1 nontriviality:** \(|C|\ge2\) and \(\prec_C\) contains at least one ordered pair.
2. **R2 acyclicity:** \(\prec_C\) is irreflexive and transitive.
3. **R3 overlap coherence:** for contexts \(C,D\), the induced orders agree on any common pair of contents:
\[
\prec_C|_{C\cap D}=\prec_D|_{C\cap D}.
\]
4. **R4 no external time:** the model contains only context-internal order relations, no external parameter or privileged global physical clock.

A coherent assignment is exhibited by restricting one abstract ordering of content roles. This is a mathematical witness of consistency, not a claim that the ordering is fundamental, physically temporal, or uniquely selected.

## Candidate selector deliberately withheld

A stronger condition could demand that every adjacent pair of contexts share at least two contents, so a nontrivial ordered record relation is common across their overlap. In the five-family test it would isolate the IAR-like diamond.

AP3N does **not** use it because it lacks independent AP3-level warrant. Finite self-presentation can have internal retention without every pair of finite modes sharing a two-content public record. Such a condition belongs, if anywhere, to the later AP4 public-record question.

## Registered target

All five AP3L/AP3M structural families should admit R1–R4. The test must also register that the two-content-overlap selector would isolate IAR but is withheld as IAR-biased at this stage.

## Interpretation rule

If all families survive, retentional structure is an independently motivated enrichment of finite appearance but not a topology discriminator. The programme can then move from AP3 grammar to AP4 public-record structure, where redundancy/publicity conditions receive their proper independent test.

## 0. Sole candidate and restricted aim

The only candidate bridge is **immanent entailment correctness**:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Correct}_{\rm IE}(p,c)
&\iff
T_{\rm IE}\cup\mathsf{Basis}(p)\models c,\\
\mathsf{Error}_{\rm IE}(p,c)
&\iff
\mathsf{Claims}(p,c)
\land
T_{\rm IE}\cup\mathsf{Basis}(p)\models\neg c.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{IE}
\]

\(T_{\rm IE}\) is a finite typed constraint theory intended to be immanent to the strict candidate’s ground/self-knowledge account. \(\mathsf{Basis}(p)\) is a profile’s non-assertoric structural basis, and \(\mathsf{Claims}(p,c)\) is its separately typed claim relation.

The aim is only to test whether \((\mathrm{IE})\) can provide a restricted correctness/error distinction without:

\[
\text{an external reference world},
\quad
\text{a selected witness or decoder},
\quad
\text{truth by declaration},
\quad
\text{or a finite independent subject.}
\]

No conclusion about privation, normativity, horror, evil, consciousness, agency, prophecy, physics, or a whole-reality winner is permitted.

## 1. Predeclared noncircularity requirements

The bridge fails if any of the following occurs:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&c\in\mathsf{Basis}(p)\text{ in the very case being evaluated;}\\
&T_{\rm IE}\text{ contains the desired truth/error verdict for an individual battery case;}\\
&\mathsf{Claims}(p,c)\text{ is treated as evidence that }c\text{ is true;}\\
&\text{a reference model, witness, decoder, public world, or external time is selected after case inspection;}\\
&\text{the constraint theory or syntax is changed to classify an adverse case.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{NC}
\]

The bridge must establish all of:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Typed},\quad
\mathsf{BasisClaimSeparated},\quad
\mathsf{NonVacuous},\quad
\mathsf{ProfileInvariant},\\
&\mathsf{TruthConditionIndependentOfClaim},\quad
\mathsf{ErrorCaseExists},\quad
\mathsf{NoExternalReference},\quad
\mathsf{NoFiniteWitness}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{Req}
\]

“Immanent” is not a magic label. The protocol must record whether \(T_{\rm IE}\) is merely stipulated, derived from independently declared constraint laws, or remains an open core commitment.

## 2. Q0 requirements: finite signature and frozen constraint theory

Before any case is classified, Q0 must declare:

\[
\Sigma_{\rm IE},
\qquad
T_{\rm IE},
\qquad
\mathsf{Basis},
\qquad
\mathsf{Claims},
\qquad
\simeq_{\rm IE}.
\]

The finite signature must contain distinct types for:

\[
\mathsf{Profile},\quad
\mathsf{BasisFact},\quad
\mathsf{ClaimContent},\quad
\mathsf{ConstraintToken},\quad
\mathsf{InternalIndex}.
\]

The constraint theory may include only universal typed constraints and must not contain names for the fixed battery claims. It may not include \(\mathsf{Correct}\) or \(\mathsf{Error}\) as primitive predicates.

A profile must be represented by a pair:

\[
p=\langle B_p,C_p\rangle,
\]

with:

\[
B_p\cap C_p=\varnothing,
\tag{Sep}
\]

where \(B_p\) is the basis graph and \(C_p\) is a finite set of claim literals. The separation must be syntactic and invariant under \(\simeq_{\rm IE}\).

## 3. Fixed battery

After Q0, exactly three profiles are permitted:

\[
\mathcal B_{\rm IE}=
\{p_{\rm ordinary},p_{\rm correct},p_{\rm error}\}.
\]

They must meet these fixed roles:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
p_{\rm ordinary}&:&\text{a nonempty coherent basis with no privileged truth result;}\\
p_{\rm correct}&:&T_{\rm IE}\cup B_{p_{\rm correct}}\models c_{+};\\
p_{\rm error}&:&\mathsf{Claims}(p_{\rm error},c_{-})\land
T_{\rm IE}\cup B_{p_{\rm error}}\models\neg c_{-}.
\end{array}
\tag{Battery}
\]

No claim may appear in its own basis graph. The grammar and \(T_{\rm IE}\) are frozen before these profiles are encoded.

## 4. Required adjudication

The candidate passes only if all conditions hold:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Correct}_{\rm IE}(p_{\rm correct},c_{+});\\
&\mathsf{Error}_{\rm IE}(p_{\rm error},c_{-});\\
&\neg\mathsf{Correct}_{\rm IE}(p_{\rm error},c_{-});\\
&\mathsf{Correct/Error}\text{ verdicts are invariant under }\simeq_{\rm IE};\\
&\text{the derivations use no external model, witness, decoder, or post hoc selector;}\\
&\text{the construction does not claim that formal entailment already gives public or metaphysical truth.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Pass}
\]

Permitted results are exactly:

\[
\mathsf{RestrictedTruthBridgePass},
\qquad
\mathsf{RestrictedTruthBridgeFailure},
\qquad
\mathsf{RestrictedTruthBridgeUnproved}.
\]

## 5. Stop rules and expiry

The project stops if:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Sigma_{\rm IE}\text{ or }T_{\rm IE}\text{ cannot be frozen without encoding intended battery results;}\\
&\text{basis/claim separation fails;}\\
&\text{the error case is impossible or only mislabeled;}\\
&\text{the bridge requires an external reference model or a selected interpreter;}\\
&\text{immanence is merely asserted rather than classified in the dependency ledger;}\\
&\text{any battery result requires a grammar or theory revision.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Stop}
\]

**Expiry.** This protocol permits exactly one execution of Q0 and the fixed three-case battery. It permits no changed constraint theory, alternative correctness definition, successor limitation theory, added semantic bridge, or extra candidate. Any modification requires a fresh preregistration.

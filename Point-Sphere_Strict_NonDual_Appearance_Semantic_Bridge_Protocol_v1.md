\[
\begin{aligned}

\mathsf{Typed}(B)
\land

\land

The following are prohibited circular definitions:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\mathsf{Error}(\phi,c)&\iff&\mathsf{Defeats}(\phi,c),\\
\mathsf{Truth}(c)&\iff&\text{“the candidate declares \(c\) true”},\\

## 3. Required bridge interfaces

Before execution, the candidate must declare a finite semantic signature \(\Sigma_{\rm sem}\), a base theory \(T_{\rm sem}\), a diagram translation, a finite active-scope rule, and a profile-conservative equivalence relation. No ellipses, post hoc relevance rules, or inventory assumptions are allowed.

The signature must separately type at least:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{PresentationProfile},\quad
\mathsf{Content},\quad
\mathsf{PresentationalFit},\quad
\mathsf{CorrectnessCondition},\\
&\mathsf{DueFeatureCondition},\quad
\mathsf{ReasonCondition},\quad
\mathsf{AffectiveCondition},\quad
\mathsf{NonSubstantialPerspectiveCondition}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{Types}
\]

The following bridges must be stated separately; none may be read into another.

### Q1. Truth/error

\[
\mathsf{Error}(\phi,c)
\iff
\mathsf{Presents}(\phi,c)
\land
\neg\mathsf{PresentationalFit}(\phi,c;\kappa),
\tag{Q1}
\]

where \(\kappa\) is an independently typed correctness condition. The project must establish what makes \(\kappa\) neither a hidden external world nor a candidate-controlled declaration.

### Q2. Privation

\[
\mathsf{Privation}(F,\phi)
\iff
\mathsf{DueFeatureCondition}(F,\phi;\delta)
\land
\neg\mathsf{PresentsFeature}(\phi,F),
\tag{Q2}
\]

where \(\delta\) is independently declared and may not define “due” by the very absence being classified.

### Q3. Normativity

\[
\mathsf{NormativeReason}(r,\phi)
\iff
\mathsf{ReasonCondition}(r,\phi;\nu)
\land
\mathsf{NormativeFit}(r,\phi;\nu),
\tag{Q3}
\]

where \(\nu\) must not be an unexplained selector or a disguised independent value domain.

### Q4. Horror and evil

\[
\mathsf{FeltHorror}(e,\phi)
\iff
\mathsf{AffectiveCondition}(e,\phi;\alpha)
\land
\mathsf{HarmRelevant}(e,\phi),
\tag{Q4a}
\]

\[
\mathsf{Evil}(e,\phi)
\Rightarrow
\exists r\,\mathsf{NormativeReason}(r,\phi)
\land
\mathsf{FeltHorror}(e,\phi)
\quad\text{or a separately declared non-affective evil condition}.
\tag{Q4b}
\]

The parameters \(\alpha,\delta,\kappa,\nu\) are not permitted as undeclared primitive selectors. Their status must be recorded as derived, core, bridge, open, or rejected in a dependency ledger.

### Q5. Non-substantial presentation/perspective

A bridge to phenomenal appearing may not posit a finite substantial self. It must state exactly what it means for a profile to be non-substantially perspectival:

\[
\mathsf{Perspective}^\Pi(\phi)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{IndependentFiniteSubstance}(\phi)
\land
\mathsf{ProfileFaithful}(\phi).
\tag{Q5}
\]

Calling \(\phi\) a “presentation index” is insufficient unless its proposed semantic roles in Q1–Q4 are shown noncircularly.

The mandatory order is:

\[
\boxed{
Q0\to Q1\to Q2\to Q3\to Q4\to Q5\to\mathcal B_{\rm sem}\to\text{comparison only if all bridges pass}.

Q0 is a dependency ledger that classifies every primitive and parameter in Q1–Q5 before their use.

The project stops with \(\mathsf{RestrictedBridgeFailure}\) or \(\mathsf{RestrictedBridgeUnproved}\) if:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{the semantic grammar cannot be finitely declared without post hoc relevance;}\\
&\text{a truth, due-feature, normative, affective, or perspective condition is primitive but untyped/unaccounted;}\\
&\text{a bridge imports a finite subject, external witness, external time, public-world stipulation, or second realm;}\\
&\text{a bridge fails profile-conservative invariance;}\\
&\text{a battery case requires a grammar or bridge amendment;}\\
&\text{the new semantic predicates merely rename the target phenomena.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Stop}
\]

**Expiry.** This protocol permits exactly one execution of Q0–Q5 and the seven-case battery for \(\mathcal S_{\rm NP}^{\rm sem}\). It permits no changed semantic signature, additional bridge, revised plenitude rule, real-mode realization theory, successor limitation theory, or additional candidate. Any modification requires a fresh preregistration.

# Strong CI Adjudication — CI6 Phenomenal and Physical Adequacy Audit v1
## Why a no-residue metaphysical proposal is not yet an account of reality as a whole

## Status

**CI6 complete as an adequacy-interface audit.** It does not demand that metaphysics immediately become physics. It establishes that a framework claiming to describe reality as a whole must eventually face phenomenal and physical targets through explicit bridges, rather than treating metaphysical vocabulary as sufficient by itself.

## 1. Exact distinction

Strong CI concerns explanatory closure at the ultimate level. Whole-reality truth additionally requires adequacy to the reality it purports to explain:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\not\models
\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}(F),
\qquad
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\not\models
\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}(F).
\tag{CI6-NE}
\]

A framework can be internally no-residue relative to its stipulated ontology while failing to account for phenomenal consciousness, physical regularities, or the relation between its ultimate vocabulary and those targets.

## 2. Phenomenal adequacy interface

A candidate framework need not reproduce a complete consciousness science. But to claim whole-reality scope it must predeclare:

| Requirement | Minimum content | Disallowed shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Phenomenal datum | Specify the target: prereflective self-presence, boundedness, qualitative character, intentionality, intersubjectivity, temporal experience, or another stated item | Treat the bare word “awareness” as accounting for finite consciousness |
| Constitution/dependence bridge | State whether finite experience is identical to, constituted by, grounded in, represented by, or emergent from the candidate’s finite reality | Infer consciousness from graph, integration, formal coherence, or common ground alone |
| Individuation | State what makes one bounded perspective distinct from another without importing a physical carrier, external time, or a public witness code | Equate a formal node, context, or grammar component with a subject |
| Error/appearance status | Explain how seeming, truth, error, partiality, and non-veridical appearance are typed, if invoked | Call any phenomenological datum “illusion” without an account of whose/what appearance it is |
| Defeater | State what phenomenal pattern would disfavor the proposed bridge | Declare fit because the vocabulary resembles phenomenological language |

Existing work supports only limited constraints: nonobjectifying/prereflective self-awareness, finite horizon/boundedness, and some retentional coherence are compatible with several grammars. It does not derive a successor law, persistence, subject criterion, public record, or physical clock.

## 3. Physical adequacy interface

No framework is required to solve every physical problem before it can be a metaphysical candidate. But a framework that claims to be the correct account of reality cannot count metaphysical coherence as physical recovery.

| Requirement | Minimum content | Disallowed shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | Name a physical target and relevant established theory/data | Treat “appearance,” “ground,” or a graph metaphor as physical ontology |
| Formal bridge | State equations, state space, constraint/interaction structure, or another mathematically explicit map | Relabel an existing equation or use external time while calling it intrinsic |
| Symmetry/gauge status | State transformations and physical versus descriptive quantities | Infer a physical gauge group from no-preference or nonduality |
| Recovery | Show the required limit for accepted physics | Claim compatibility as derivation |
| Discriminator | State an observable, prediction, no-go result, or experimental/empirical difference | Treat usefulness, elegance, or interpretation as evidence |

For the Problem of Time specifically, the existing Point–Sphere programme remains at T6: no Point–Sphere equation, measure, gauge-invariant time-odd observable, or probability/selection law currently changes the solution space. T7 remains held pending an explicit physical postulate.

## 6. CI6 result

A framework can reach at most a conditional SCI status without CI6. To claim an account of **reality as a whole**, it must enter the following ledger:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{WholeRealityCandidate}(F)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\land
\mathsf{PhenomenalBridge}(F)
\land
\mathsf{PhysicalInterface}(F).
}
\]

The latter two conditions need not be completed at the initial metaphysical stage, but their absence blocks a truth or unique-whole-reality conclusion.

## 7. Programme consequence

At the end of CI0–CI6, no candidate has earned L4 truth status. The proper CI7 conclusion must distinguish:

- structural/conditional metaphysical results;
- held intrinsic-nature and exact-totality bridges;
- target-relative phenomenal implementations; and
- absent physical derivations.

It must not announce a unique correct framework merely because one candidate is rhetorically satisfying under a favored interpretation of CI.

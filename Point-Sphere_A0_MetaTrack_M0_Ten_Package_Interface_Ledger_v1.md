## 1. M0 method and status vocabulary

### 1.1 Unit of record

Each row records an actual dossier or audited package, the strongest extracted admission-relevant resource, its stated actuality transition, and the narrowest documented A0 status. It does not reconstruct a source anew.

The ten packages are treated as evidence points, not as a historically exhaustive canon and not as ten independent proofs of a shared theorem.

### 1.2 Fixed M0 labels

| Label | Meaning in this ledger |
|---|---|
| `No scoped A0 pass on record` | the listed work has not established an A0 pass for the specified interface. It is not an impossibility result. |
| `Localized bridge diagnosis` | a named, bounded proposed bridge failed or remains unproved under a stated test. |
| `Protected resource` | an actual source or formal resource that later analysis must preserve and cannot dismiss by family label. |
| `Provisional interface tag` | a retrieval aid for M1/M2, explicitly not a reduction result. |
| `M0 field absent` | a field is not yet extracted; M1 may find it, identify a residual, or label the package unclassified. |

### 1.3 M0 restraint

The normal-form fields in this ledger are **prompts**, not NF1–NF7 verdicts. In particular, a field written as “not recovered” is not an M1 `No` until the later audit identifies the exact candidate scope and textual/argumentative basis.

## 3. Atomic package records

### P1 — Classical essence–existence identity

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Strong_CI_PC_Classical_Essence_Existence_Dossier_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{Simp}(G)
\land\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(G)
\land\mathsf{NoExternalUltimateSource}(G).
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** contingent entities, a candidate self-subsistent ground, and the question of whether the latter is instantiated; no finite-mode candidate field is yet recovered.
- **Admission-relevant move:** essence–existence identity is intended to block the demand that an existence be added to an independently specified essence.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** if the identity is coherently instantiated, the composition question is inapplicable to the ground.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** contrast with contingent composite beings; no public finite-mode type discriminator or profile-invariance rule recovered in this package.
- **Protected resource:** a genuine presupposition challenge to one terminal question.
- **M0 status:** the dossier does not establish the identity’s instantiation, an exact alternative-profile exclusion, or an ET map from the ground to a finite order. It is not here classified as a completed A0 admission criterion.

### P2 — Nondual self-knowledge content

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Strong_CI_ET_Nondual_Public_Domain_Bridge_Audit_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_{G}(a)
\Rightarrow R(a).
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** formal candidate aspects/contents \(a\), protected self-knowledge structure, and finite modes under protected \(P_{\rm ND}\).
- **Admission-relevant move:** genuine aspecthood is internally actualized as content of self-knowledge; no additional realization selector is required once genuine aspecthood is given.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** \(\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)\to R(a)\).
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** coherence, gauge, contextual and phenomenal constraints are present, but none is a recovered all-and-only \(\mathsf{PubDet}_{G}\) rule; rival expansion remains an explicit pressure.
- **Protected resource:** the conditional internal non-representational actualization theorem.
- **M0 status:** the public map from independent formal candidate to genuine aspect is unrecovered. This is not a conclusion about the metaphysical truth of the protected package.

### P3 — Proclean participation / proper effect

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Proclean_Participation_Articulation_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{ProperEff}_{\rm Pr}(x)
\iff
\exists c[\mathsf{Cause}(c,x)\land\mathsf{PerNaturam}(c,x)
\land\mathsf{End}(c,x)\land\mathsf{Remain/Proceed/Revert}(x,c)].
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** participants/effects, mediated participated terms, and causal sources in a stratified hierarchy.
- **Admission-relevant move:** a proper effect is distinguished from a merely parasitic/accidental outcome by natural causal procession and end-directedness.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** proper causal production and participation are intended to explain a differentiated dependent effect.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** proper/parasitic contrast and causal triad are substantive; no common profile extractor or arbitrary symmetric-pair rule is recovered.
- **Protected resource:** triadic dependence is not mere vocabulary and must not be assimilated to nondual self-content.
- **M0 status:** conditional effect/dependence is recorded; a public candidate-membership bridge is not on record.

### P4 — Advaitic *adhyāsa* / sublation status

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Advaita_Adhyasa_Articulation_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{Mithyā}_{\rm A}(x)
\iff
\mathsf{Cognized}(x)\land\mathsf{Sublatable}(x)
\land\mathsf{DependentOnSubstratum}(x).
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** presented contents, loci, correcting cognitions, and a substratum-relative appearance/status contrast.
- **Admission-relevant move:** a status rule distinguishes cognized-but-sublatable appearance from absolute reality and simple nonbeing.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** no direct finite-mode actualization transition is claimed by the reconstructed local rule; it classifies status.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** local correction/sublation is an error resource; type and re-encoding burdens remain explicit.
- **Protected resource:** a real local appearance/error semantics, not an absence of all criteria.
- **M0 status:** the package is recorded as status-first and target-mismatched for A0, not as globally failed.

### P5 — Hegelian determinate negation

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Hegelian_Self_Determination_Articulation_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{LogDet}_{\rm H}(d)
\iff
\exists p[\mathsf{Prior}(p,d)\land\mathsf{OneSided}(p)
\land\mathsf{ImmanentlyNegates}(p,p)\land\mathsf{Sublates}(d,p)
\land\mathsf{Preserves}(d,p)\land\mathsf{AddsDeterminateContent}(d,p)].
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** determinations in a logical development, plus alleged realized finite expressions.
- **Admission-relevant move:** a legitimate successor follows immanently from the predecessor instead of external selection.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** the needed transition is explicitly \(\mathsf{LogDet}_{\rm H}(d)\Rightarrow\exists m\,\mathsf{Realizes}(m,d)\); it is not recovered by the dossier.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** logical successor conditions are substantive; mode/property/type and neutral translation requirements remain unproved.
- **Protected resource:** a non-arbitrary account of conceptual determinacy.
- **M0 status:** lack of a realization theorem is a documented bridge issue, not a proof that derivation cannot concern reality.

### P6 — Leibnizian complete concept and PII

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Leibnizian_Perspectival_Individuality_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{PII}_{L}(x,y)
\iff
[\forall F(F(x)\leftrightarrow F(y))]\to x=y.
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** simple substances/monads, complete individual concepts, and perspectival expressions of a complete world.
- **Admission-relevant move:** a complete qualitative profile rules out bare numerical distinctness and gives a principled response to symmetry.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** none is extracted from PII alone; the complete profile is conditional on actual candidates and the whole-world/compossibility package.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** a strong conditional identity resource is present; a completed actual-world profile and a finite-mode/non-substance translation remain burdens.
- **Protected resource:** PII is not a mere label and directly bears on particularity conditional on full predicates.
- **M0 status:** identity/individuation is recorded separately from admission/actuality.

### P7 — Spinozist immanent productivity

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Spinozist_Immanent_Productivity_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}_{S}(x)
\iff&\ \mathsf{Finite}(x)\land\mathsf{Mode}_{S}(x)
\land\mathsf{CausedToExist}_{S}(x)\\
&\land\exists y[\mathsf{Finite}(y)\land\mathsf{SameAttribute}(y,x)
\land\mathsf{Determines}(y,x)].
\end{aligned}
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** finite determinate things, finite causal antecedents, attributes, one substance, and modes.
- **Admission-relevant move:** type distinction (`in another` / `conceived through another`), divine causation of essence/existence, and necessary finite causation are combined.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** immanent efficient causation and determination are offered as a realization claim, rather than formal derivation alone.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** a robust substance/mode contrast is present; contested finite-mode status, arbitrary-candidate public causal attribution, and profile treatment remain burdens.
- **Protected resource:** a historically substantive productive/realization account that is not a bare fixed-point model.
- **M0 status:** no public causal admission bridge is recorded; this does not generalize to all productive criteria.

### P8 — Rāmānuja body relation

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Ramanuja_Body_Relation_Articulation_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G)
\iff
\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)\land\mathsf{Dharana}_{R}(G,x)
\land\mathsf{Niyamana}_{R}(G,x)\land\mathsf{Śeṣatva}_{R}(x,G).
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** real individual selves, non-sentient entities, Brahman, and eligible body-substances.
- **Admission-relevant move:** bodyhood unifies real plurality with complete support, control, and subordination to a single independent whole.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** the relation is meant to entail real dependent non-independence; it begins from \(\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)\).
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** a partial substance/property contrast is present through \(\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}\); public conditions for that predicate, membership, and profile preservation remain open.
- **Protected resource:** real plurality plus body/dependence/control/teleology, not generic panentheism.
- **M0 status:** a relationship starting with an eligible real substance does not yet establish an independent A0 admission test for a proposed candidate.

### P9 — Aristotelian *energeia*

**Record source:** `Point-Sphere_Aristotelian_Energeia_Actuality_Reconstruction_v1.md`.

\[
\mathsf{Actual}_{A}(e,F,s)
\iff
\exists p[
\mathsf{CapacityFor}_{A}(p,F,s)
\land\mathsf{ExerciseOrCompletionOf}_{A}(e,p,F,s)
\land\mathsf{FormOrEnd}_{A}(F,s)].
\]

- **Candidate field indicated:** bearers, capacities, exercises/completions, forms, and ends.
- **Admission-relevant move:** actuality is differentiated from potentiality through capacity, exercise, form, completion, and first/second actuality.
- **Stated transition toward actuality:** a typed capacity/form/end structure yields local activity/completion verdicts.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** strong local actuality and potentiality contrasts are present; no independent public way to fix the requisite bearer/capacity/form/end inventory for arbitrary finite-mode membership is recorded.
- **Protected resource:** *energeia* is a substantive primitive-actuality proposal, not a bare roster predicate.
- **M0 status:** no public A0 finite-mode rule is on record; no general failure of primitive actuality is inferred.

**Record sources:** `Point-Sphere_Sadrian_Gradation_Actuality_Reconstruction_v1.md`, `Point-Sphere_Sadrian_Primary_Collation_and_Intensive_Ordering_Stress_Test_v1.md`, and `Point-Sphere_Sadrian_Public_Effects_Bridge_Test_v1.md`.

- **Stated transition toward actuality:** extra-mental reality is marked by effects/consequences; causal deficiency/completion expresses intensive rank.
- **Type/error/invariance resources recorded:** mental/extra-mental distinction and a negative screening resource are genuine; the PE0–PE7 test found no public-effects rule for intensive rank, exact profile twins, property/mode boundary, invariance, or extra candidates.
- **Protected resource:** the broader intensive interface is unreduced and source-grounded; direct witnessing remains a localized pressure, not a blanket privacy result.
- **M0 status:** `public-effects externalization: targeted failure`; `intensive interface: protected residual resource`. No global Sadrian failure is entered.

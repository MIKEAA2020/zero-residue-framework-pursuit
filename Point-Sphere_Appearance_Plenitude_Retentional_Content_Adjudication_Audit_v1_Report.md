# AP3P2H Retentional-Content Adjudication Audit v1 — Report
## Retention supplies neither overlap exhaustion nor a canonical P/Q comparator

## Question

AP3P2G found a conditional full-gauge theorem if finite retentional self-presentation is exhausted by the declared two-content overlap:
\[
\mathsf{RC}:
\quad
\text{cross-phase content is exhausted by declared ordered overlap.}
\]

It also found the rival intra-component relation:
\[
J_s=c\oplus d,
\]
which blocks full gauge if P and Q free-value fibres have intrinsic equality/inequality comparability:
\[
\mathsf{CPC}.
\]

This audit tests both against the restricted phenomenological self-presentation resources already admitted by AP3J.

## What retention actually supports

The admissible result is modest:

- a just-past phase can be immanently available as past;
- that availability need not be an external memory, second observer, or public record;
- finite self-presence is bounded/horizonal, not exhaustively transparent;
- no formal map equating values of two distinct nonoverlapping content positions has been supplied.

This immediately distinguishes three possibilities.

| Retentional payload | Literal overlap exhaustive? | Contains \(J_s=c\oplus d\)? | Gauge consequence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| \(R_0=\{r_0,r_1\}\) | Yes | No | RC branch can yield full gauge with the AP3P2G auxiliary premises |
| \(R_1=\{r_0,r_1,\operatorname{Past}(p_v,c)\}\) | No | No | Full gauge does not follow from RC; comparator still absent |
| \(R_2=R_1\cup\{J_s\}\) | No | Yes | Full gauge is blocked |

All three are compatible with immanence, non-objectification, finite boundedness, and no external-time or public-memory import.

All executable checks pass.

## Result

The key middle possibility is \(R_1\). A finite mode can retentionally include more than literal shared graph nodes—e.g., the just-past free P-content—without thereby having a canonical Boolean equality/XOR relation to a free Q-content.

Therefore neither of these inferences is valid:
\[
\text{shared trace}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{RC},
\]
\[
\text{retention of just-past content}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{CPC}.
\]

The first confuses a declared common trace with exhaustive retentional content. The second confuses temporal availability of distinct contents with a specified comparator between their valuation fibres.

## Consequence for the full-gauge derivation

The retentional route has now been tested at the exact point on which it turns. Current self-presentation and phenomenological resources do not derive the premise needed by the AP3P2G full-gauge theorem:
\[
\mathsf{RC}.
\]
They also do not derive its rival:
\[
\mathsf{CPC}.
\]

So the formal result remains conditional:
\[
\mathsf{RC}
+
\text{component closure}
+
\text{structural identity}
\Longrightarrow
H_{\rm full}\text{ gauge}.
\]

But \(R_1\) shows why the conclusion cannot be forced from finite temporal self-presentation alone.

## Honest stopping point for this derivation family

The ground-unity, bare mode-unity, exact-overlap, and retentional-content routes have now all been explicitly separated and tested. To go further, the programme must add—not conceal—one of the following:

1. **Retentional-closure bridge:** only ordered overlap is intrinsic cross-phase content; or
2. **Comparator bridge:** a relation such as \(J_s\) is intrinsic; or
3. **Minimal structural quotient:** all undeclared fibre-coordinate sewing is descriptive, while later grammar extensions may add relations that defeat that quotient.

The third is a legitimate labelled grammar policy. It is not a theorem of the existing core, and it must remain revisable if a future independently warranted cross-phase relation is added.

## Conclusion

The effort to derive full gauge has not produced an unlabelled theorem. It has, however, reduced every remaining ambiguity to a single explicit grammar decision: the retentional content law for nonoverlapping phase determinations. No physical time, external observer, public record, or ontic selection among genuine non-gauge aspects has been introduced.

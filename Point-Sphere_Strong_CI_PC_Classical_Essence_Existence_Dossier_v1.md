# Strong CI Adjudication — PC Classical Essence–Existence Identity Dossier v1
## Can a simple self-subsistent ground discharge the terminal contrastive question?

## Status

**The classical essence–existence route partially meets the corrected presupposition challenge but has no public SI pass.** It identifies a genuine distinction that would make the existential question appropriate for contingent beings. It has not yet shown that essence–existence identity is instantiated, explanatory rather than stipulative, or sufficient for the exact finite-reality relation.

## 1. Candidate profile

Let \(G\) be a candidate simple self-subsistent ground. The core claims are:

\[
\mathsf{Simp}(G),
\qquad
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(G),
\qquad
\mathsf{NoExternalUltimateSource}(G).
\tag{EE-Core}
\]

The intended answer to the terminal question is not that \(G\) causes itself. It is that there is no real essence/existence composition in \(G\) for an external or further cause to join.

## 2. First contrastive question

\[
Q_1(G):
\quad
\text{“Why does \(G\) exist rather than fail to exist?”}
\]

### Classical presupposition challenge

The response identifies this presupposition:

\[
 d_1:
\quad
\mathsf{Ess}(G)
\text{ and }
\mathsf{Ex}(G)
\text{ are really distinct such that the former can obtain without the latter.}
\]

If \(\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(G)\) is coherently established, then the specific question “what adds existence to an already specified essence?” is malformed for \(G\). This is a genuine potential presupposition challenge; it is more than simply declaring \(G\) uncaused.

## 3. Reappearance tests

The challenge does not yet complete the argument.

### R1 — coherence of the identity

Why is it coherent for one concrete ultimate to be identical with existence/being itself rather than merely exemplify existence?

A defender must state a theory of identity, essence, existence, and property attribution that does not turn \(G\) into an abstract property or a merely verbal construction.

### R2 — existential force

Even if a concept or nature includes existence, it does not automatically follow that such a nature is instantiated:

\[
\mathsf{ConceptualContainment}(\operatorname{Ex},\mathsf{Ess}(G))
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Ex}(G).
\]

A public route needs an existence argument or a non-question-begging account of why the relevant identity is metaphysically instantiated, not only conceptually describable.

### R3 — exact identity profile

The question can reappear as:

\[
Q_2(G):
\quad
\text{“Why does reality contain this exact essence–existence identity profile rather than another self-intelligible profile?”}
\]

“No other profile is possible” is a candidate answer only if impossibility is derived from the stated ontology, not assumed by calling \(G\) absolutely simple.

### R4 — derivative relation

Even a successful terminal existence account does not entail:

\[
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(G)
\Rightarrow
\text{this exact finite order follows}.
\]

Creation, participation, emanation, aspect identity, or another relation still requires an ET-map.

## 4. Positive and negative cases

| Case | Does \(Q_1\) have clear purchase? | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Contingent entity with distinct essence/existence | yes | Its nature does not itself settle its existence |
| Bare “necessary entity” | unresolved | Necessity label does not show why nonexistence is impossible |
| Candidate essence–existence identity | challengeable | The existence-addition question is blocked only if the identity thesis is coherently established |
| Abstract necessary proposition | not enough | Conceptual/logical necessity does not by itself establish a concrete ultimate ground |

These cases give the route a nontrivial discriminator: it does not simply pronounce every terminal entity self-intelligible.

## 5. Correct result

The classical route obtains a **partial PC result**:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{EE-Core}
\Rightarrow
\text{a challenge to the essence-plus-added-existence form of }Q_1.
}
\]

It does not yet obtain:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{EE-Core}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SIpass}(G).
}
\]

The outstanding burdens are R1–R4. Thus the route is stronger than a bare necessity label but not yet a public strongest-CI closure.

## 6. Implications for rivals

- A nondual aware-ground candidate may offer a different PC argument based on nonrepresentational self-presence; it cannot be dismissed solely because it lacks essence–existence vocabulary.
- A necessary totality must supply an alternative challenge to its exact-profile question.
- A Neoplatonic source must address why productive diffusion follows from its nature and why the hierarchy does not reintroduce exactness residue.

The classical route is therefore a live candidate bridge, not a universal template imposed on all candidates.

## 7. Source note

Classical divine simplicity is a substantive theory on which the divine nature, attributes, and existence are related by strong identity claims; its coherence is disputed and requires a non-creaturely account of property possession. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/.

## 8. Expiry condition

The route advances only with:

1. a coherent metaphysical—not merely conceptual—account of essence–existence identity;
2. an argument for existential force/instantiation;
3. a non-reappearance derivation for \(Q_2\); and
4. an ET-map for finite reality.

Absent these, essence–existence identity remains a serious partial presupposition challenge, not a completed self-intelligibility proof.

## Nonclaims

This dossier does not reject classical theism, prove an ontological argument invalid, establish a nondual alternative, or settle the ground–finite relation. It gives the classical route its strongest conditional reading while preserving the unresolved terminal burdens.

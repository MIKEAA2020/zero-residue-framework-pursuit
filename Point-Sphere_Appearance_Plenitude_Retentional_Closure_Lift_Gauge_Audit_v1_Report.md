# AP3P2G Retentional-Closure Lift-Gauge Audit v1 — Report
## A conditional full-gauge theorem is available, but its decisive retentional premise has not yet been derived

## The derivation attempt

The relevant new route is the exact finite overlap of adjacent self-presenting phases, not common grounding and not bare reflexivity:
\[
C_P=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v\},
\qquad
C_Q=\{r_0,r_1,q_s,q_v\},
\]
\[
C_P\cap C_Q=\{r_0,r_1\}.
\]

The AP3P2 free values
\[
c=\nu(p_v),\qquad d=\nu(q_v)
\]
therefore belong to distinct, nonoverlapping content positions. This yields a serious candidate derivation principle:
\[
\mathsf{RC}:
\quad
\text{an intrinsic cross-phase relation in a finite mode must be carried by its declared ordered retentional overlap.}
\]

Together with component closure and structural identity, RC would derive full Q-coordinate gauge.

## Conditional theorem

For a lift bit-function \(f:\{0,1\}^2\to\mathbb Z_2\),
\[
\beta_f(a,b,c)=(a,b,c\oplus f(a,b)).
\]
For every sector-dependent Q-coordinate change \(h\),
\[
\eta_h(a,b,d)=(a,b,d\oplus h(a,b)),
\]
the exact covariance identity holds:
\[
\eta_h\circ\beta_f=\beta_{f+h}.
\]

Thus the full \((\mathbb Z_2)^4\) group acts transitively on all 16 lift laws while preserving every declared phase type, trace value, context constraint, and successor shape.

If the following three premises are adopted:

1. **RC:** cross-phase content is only declared overlap content;
2. **component closure:** a finite mode has no undeclared content relation to distinct flow components;
3. **structural identity:** isomorphic declared self-presentation grammars are one description class;

then:
\[
H_{\rm full}\cong(\mathbb Z_2)^4
\quad\text{is gauge, and the 16 lifts are one class.}
\]

This is a genuine conditional derivation, not mere lift enumeration.

## The decisive rival is intra-component, not only cross-sector

The audit also identifies a sharper possible observable than AP3P2C’s cross-sector \(I_{s,t}\). Inside one trace sector \(s=(a,b)\), a lift determines:
\[
J_s=c\oplus d=f(s).
\]

This is a would-be relation between the free P and Q valuation values in successive phases of one candidate component. It changes under a sector-dependent redefinition:
\[
J_s\mapsto J_s\oplus h(s).
\]

So full gauge fails if an intrinsic cross-phase-comparator principle is adopted:
\[
\mathsf{CPC}:
\quad
p_v\text{- and }q_v\text{-values in successive phases have an immanently meaningful equality/inequality relation.}
\]

This corrects the scope of the prior question. Cross-sector quantities were not the only possible obstacle; an intra-component comparator \(J_s\) is potentially more direct.

## Has RC been derived from current resources?

No.

AP3P1 says each adjacent pair **has** a shared ordered trace. It does not say that the trace exhausts retentional availability or every legitimate relation in a finite self-presentation. AP3J supports a bounded, non-objectifying, prereflective self-presence with temporal articulation; it explicitly does not support exhaustive transparency.

Accordingly, neither of these has current derivational standing:

- RC, which excludes \(J_s\) as unpresented sewing; or
- CPC, which makes \(J_s\) a genuine mode-internal relation.

The fact that \(p_v\) and \(q_v\) do not literally overlap is a formal fact. The conclusion that they cannot be implicitly/retentionally compared is the missing substantive bridge.

## What has been achieved

The requested derivation has been advanced one step beyond the earlier ground and mode-unity audits:

\[
\text{exact phase overlap}
+\mathsf{RC}
+\text{component closure}
+\text{structural identity}
\Longrightarrow
\text{full lift gauge}.
\]

The remaining issue is now maximally specific. It is not broadly “what counts as content?” It is:

> Does finite immanent retentional self-presentation permit a relation between nonoverlapping free contents of successive phases, or is its intrinsic cross-phase content exhausted by the declared ordered overlap?

A positive answer yields `CPC` and retains substantive lifts; a negative answer yields `RC` and derives the fibre-local quotient. Neither can be silently assumed.

## Conclusion

Full fibre-local gauge is now conditionally derivable from a compact retentional-closure package. But the package is not yet a theorem of existing self-presentation or phenomenological resources. The next focused task is to test RC versus CPC against the intended non-objectifying, finite, retentional self-presentation concept—without importing an external observer, memory register, or public record law.

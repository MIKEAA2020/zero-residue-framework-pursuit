# Aristotelian *Energeia* Actuality Test Protocol v1
## Predeclared A0 test of capacity, activity, form, and genuine finite actuality

## Status

This protocol predeclares the source test of the primitive-actuality escape route. It gives *energeia* a fair hearing: an irreducible actuality notion is not treated as circular merely because it is not reductively defined.

The criterion of success is exact:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can an Aristotelian actuality criterion decide genuine finite mode-membership}\
\text{and actuality without presupposing the relevant substance, capacity, form, and end inventory?}
}
\]

The target is not whether Aristotle has a general theory of change, form, soul, or the unmoved mover; it is whether the theory supplies the required A0 admission relation for a proposed finite candidate.

## 2. Strongest charitable criterion

Let \(p\) be a relevant potentiality/capacity, \(e\) its relevant actual exercise or completion, \(s\) the relevant bearer/substrate, and \(F\) the form/end in question.

The core contrast is:

\[
\mathsf{Potential}_{A}(p,F)
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\mathsf{Energeia}_{A}(e,F).
\]

The test reconstruction is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Actual}_{A}(x,F)\iff
&\exists p,s\,[
\mathsf{CapacityFor}_{A}(p,F,s)\\
&\land\mathsf{ExerciseOrCompletionOf}_{A}(x,p,F,s)\\
&\land\mathsf{FormOrEnd}_{A}(F,s)
].
\end{aligned}
\]

The formula is not attributed verbatim to Aristotle. It is a charitable formal aid for testing the proposal that an actuality is the exercise/completion corresponding to a potentiality, and hence can be recognized without a bare actual-roster primitive.

Two source-required qualifications are fixed:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
Q_1 &: \text{first actuality (such as possessed knowledge or soul) may itself ground further exercise};\\
Q_2 &: \text{motion/change is the actuality of the potential qua potential and may differ from}\
&\text{a completed or end-inclusive activity.}
\end{array}
\]

## 3. What counts as success

A limited A0 pass requires more than showing that actuality is intelligible by contrast with potentiality. The account must provide:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
A_1 &: \text{public truth conditions for capacity, exercise/completion, form/end, and actuality};\\
A_2 &: \text{a noncircular admission rule for a proposed finite candidate \(x\)};\\
A_3 &: \text{a mode/property/substance/nonentity boundary within the stated scope};\\
A_4 &: \text{a declared profile extractor or equivalent invariance rule};\\
A_5 &: \text{an error condition that does not merely say “the candidate was not actual after all.”}
\end{array}
\]

The account may use hylomorphic and teleological vocabulary. It may not make its success depend merely on already knowing which substances, capacities, natural kinds, and ends are actual.

## 4. Predeclared structural battery

| Test | Required Aristotelian answer |
|---|---|
| T0 | Can the account state whether a finite order lacking exercises/completions is eligible, without simply assuming eternal actual substances or natural kinds? |
| T1 | For one proposed finite \(x\), what shows that it is an actual exercise/completion rather than a merely potential capacity, representation, or possible end? |
| T2 | For a symmetric pair of proposed actualities, does \(\mathsf{Energeia}\) decide one, two, or a symmetric plurality without an imported bearer/history/name? |
| T3 | Can the account distinguish an actual activity/form from a property, disposition, or predicate of a substance? |
| T4 | Does an allegedly independent finite entity receive a verdict through the criterion rather than via a presupposed substance inventory? |
| T5 | Is the actuality verdict invariant across profile-conservative descriptions that retain the relevant capacity/exercise/form structure? |
| T6 | Can an extra candidate be admitted/rejected by the criterion without adding a new actual capacity, bearer, or end by decree? |
| \(P_1\) | What makes the relevant potentiality/capacity real rather than merely assigned? |
| \(P_2\) | What makes the alleged exercise or completion actual rather than an asserted occurrence? |
| \(P_3\) | What fixes the relevant form/end without assuming the actual substance/kind inventory? |

## 5. Fixed rival distinctions

The source must be compared only against these stated alternatives:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{MerePotential}(x) &: x\text{ is a capacity or possibility not in exercise};\\
\mathsf{MereMotion}(x) &: x\text{ is an incomplete process, not a completed/end-inclusive actuality};\\
\mathsf{PropertyOnly}(x) &: x\text{ is a predicate/disposition of a bearer, not a genuine finite actuality};\\
\mathsf{IndependentSubstance}(x) &: x\text{ is a self-standing finite substance};\\
\mathsf{RosterActual}(x) &: x\text{ counts as actual only because the framework lists it.}
\end{array}
\]

No rival-defeat conclusion is licensed unless the source supplies relevant truth conditions and the dossier distinguishes ontological type from a grammatical predicate.

## 6. Outcome rules

### Primitive-actuality A0 pass

A pass requires that \(\mathsf{Actual}_{A}(x,F)\) provide a public, non-roster-dependent and profile-invariant verdict on finite candidate membership, while passing T0–T6 and \(P_1\text{--}P_3\).

Such a result would refute the A0 no-go schema at the primitive-actuality subfamily and would show that A0 is satisfiable in at least a scoped semantic sense.

### Structural primitive-actuality failure

A failure is structural only if the criterion must:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
F_1 &: \text{presuppose the actual bearer/substance or capacity before it can adjudicate \(x\)};\\
F_2 &: \text{treat exercise/completion as actual only after an independent inventory has settled it};\\
F_3 &: \text{presuppose form/end/natural-kind facts while using them to decide membership};\\
F_4 &: \text{collapse actuality into a property or a framework-relative label};\\
F_5 &: \text{lack a profile-conservative interpretation or a public error condition.}
\end{array}
\]

Primitive status alone is not \(F_1\text{--}F_5\).

### Inconclusive result

The test is inconclusive if the source turns out to concern only a theory of local change or activity, with no claim to genuine finite reality/modehood, or if unresolved interpretive variation makes the A0 verdict reading-dependent.

## Nonclaim

This protocol does not assume that Aristotle’s forms, substances, teleology, souls, or unmoved mover are true. It does not assume that *energeia* is a mode criterion. It isolates whether an intelligible primitive actuality relation can meet the noncircular public-admission burden where productive necessity did not.

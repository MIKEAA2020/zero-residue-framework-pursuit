## 1. Audit rules

### 1.1 Restricted formal success is not ontological success

A coherent typed notation or conditional model may show that a proposal is not formally contradictory. It does not by itself show that its intended category is real, nonpartitive, or nonindependent:

\[
\mathsf{TypedCoherence}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{RealNonentityCategory}
\lor
\mathsf{NonpartitivityDefense}
\lor
\mathsf{NonindependenceDefense}.
\tag{Coherence-Not-Ontological-Defense}
\]

### 1.2 Protected burden rule

An unmet affirmative bridge is not a proof of the converse. In particular:

\[
\mathsf{Q2Status}\in\{\mathsf{PartialPass},\mathsf{Incomplete}\}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G),
\tag{No-Q2-Reversal}
\]

\[
\mathsf{Q3Status}=\mathsf{Incomplete}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(d,G)
\lor
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5}).
\tag{No-Q3-Reversal}
\]

The audit therefore evaluates only the positive-immanent candidate's own claimed warrants; it does not award a selector or comparative victory to a privative alternative.

## 3. Q1 — noncircular, non-outcome-loaded mode-reality grammar

### 3.1 Outcome-loading check

No definition of \(\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}\), \(\mathsf{ModeOccurrence}\), or formal discernibility appeals to actual finite effects, observations, phenomenal outcomes, norms, physics, truth/error targets, or a selected world. The outcome-loading prohibition is respected:

\[
\mathsf{OutcomeLoading}_{\rm WSP5}
=
\mathsf{Absent}.
\tag{Q1-No-Outcome-Loading}
\]

### 3.2 Direct circularity check

The definiens of \(\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}(d,G)\) does not literally repeat \(\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}\):

\[
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}
\notin
\mathsf{Definiens}(\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}).
\tag{Q1-No-Direct-Self-Reference}
\]

This earns a restricted syntactic credit.

### 3.3 Semantic-grounding check

The core predicates nevertheless do not receive independent application conditions. In particular, a mode is said to be real when it grounds intrinsic predication, while the explanation of that grounding invokes \(G\)'s being in the very manner \(d\) whose positive reality is at issue:

\[
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{GroundsIntrinsicPredication}(G,d),
\tag{Q1-Reality-to-Grounding}
\]

\[
\mathsf{GroundsIntrinsicPredication}(G,d)
\text{ is glossed by }
G\text{ being in manner }d.
\tag{Q1-Grounding-to-Manner}
\]

Without a fixed, independently applicable account of \(\mathsf{Determines}\), \(\mathsf{IntrinsicEssenceConstraint}\), \(\mathsf{EssentiallyGroundedIn}\), and \(\mathsf{GroundsIntrinsicPredication}\), the account has not yet shown that its positive reality condition is semantically noncircular rather than a mutually supporting cluster of primitives:

\[
\mathsf{NoDirectSelfReference}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SemanticNoncircularity}.
\tag{Q1-Syntax-Not-Semantics}
\]

\[
\mathsf{Q1Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5})
=
\mathsf{PartialPass}_{\substack{
\rm no\ outcome\ loading\ and\ no\ direct\ self\!\text{-}reference;\\
\rm positive\ mode\ predicates\ remain\ semantically\ ungrounded
}}.
\tag{Q1-Result}

## 4. Q2 — Constitutive-Node Principle

### 4.1 Material improvement over WSP-4

WSP-5 genuinely improves on WSP-4 by refusing the invalid shortcut from essential bearer-dependence to nonpartitivity:

\[
\mathsf{EssentialBearerDependence}(x,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}(x).
\tag{Q2-No-Dependence-Shortcut-Pass}
\]

It also gives \(\mathsf{ConstitutiveContributor}\) an explicit conditional definition and recognizes trope-like or nonseparable dependent items as possible constitution-countable nodes. Those changes mean that the prior WSP-4 trope/dependent-constituent objection is no longer simply assumed away.

\[
\mathsf{WSP5\text{-}CNP}
\succ_{m explicitness}
\mathsf{WSP4\text{-}ObjectualIdentityPrinciple}.
\tag{Q2-Explicitness-Only-Credit}
\]

This is a local explanatory credit, not a comparative framework verdict.

### 4.2 Independence test

CNP purports to ground parthood in constitution-countability:

\[
\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(x,y)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}(x)
\land
\mathsf{EligibleForConstitutionalCounting}(x,y).
\tag{Q2-CNP-Under-Test}
\]

But \(\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}\) itself is defined by membership in \(\mathbf E\), whose description is “entity-like constituent candidates,” whereas real immanent modes are placed in the disjoint sort \(\mathbf M\):

\[
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}(x)
\iff
x\in\mathbf E
\land
\mathsf{NumericallyIndividuable}(x),
\qquad
\mathbf E\cap\mathbf M=\varnothing.
\tag{Q2-EM-Boundary}
\]

The key question is exactly whether a real intrinsic mode belongs in \(\mathbf M\) rather than \(\mathbf E\). CNP cannot settle that question by itself, because it relies on the prior categorical exclusion:

\[
\mathsf{CNP}
\not\models
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}(d)
\quad\text{unless }d\in\mathbf M\text{ is independently warranted.}
\tag{Q2-CNP-Conditionality}
\]

Further, “eligible for constitutional counting” is explained as being countable as a constituent rather than merely characterizing a manner of being. That is a restatement of the live dispute, not yet an independent test capable of deciding it:

\[
\mathsf{EligibleForConstitutionalCounting}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentBoundaryCriterion}
(\mathbf E,\mathbf M).
\tag{Q2-Counting-Contrast-Insufficient}
\]

### 4.3 Q2 disposition

The CNP is a better and more transparent proposed defense: it admits the relevant dependent-constituent rival and does not infer nonpartitivity from dependence. But its independent warrant remains conditional on Q3's yet-unestablished category contrast.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Q2Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5})
=
\mathsf{PartialPass}_{\substack{
\rm dependence\ shortcut\ removed\ and\ rival\ admitted;\\
\rm constitution\!\text{-}countability\ still\ presupposes\ the\ disputed\ E/M\ boundary
}}.
}
\tag{Q2-Result}
\]

No inference to composition follows from the partial result:

\[
\mathsf{Q2Status}=
\mathsf{PartialPass}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G).
\tag{Q2-No-Overread}
\]

## 5. Q3 — Adverbial Grounding Principle and the three-way contrast

### 5.1 What AGP establishes conditionally

The Adverbial Grounding Principle articulates a coherent conditional option: if an intrinsic predication is essentially grounded in \(G\)'s being in a manner \(d\), no *additional entity* need be posited merely to state that grounding relation.

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{IntrinsicPredication}(\varphi,G)
\land
\mathsf{EssentiallyGroundedIn}(\varphi,G,d)\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{AdverbialGroundingOption}(G,d).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Q3-AGP-Conditional-Option}
\]

This is a real advance over a bare assertion that a mode has only one bearer. It distinguishes a nonentity option from the claim that every bearer-dependent item is nonentity-like:

\[
\mathsf{AGP}\neq
\mathsf{BareNontransferRule}.
\tag{Q3-Real-Improvement}
\]

### 5.2 Reality test

The antecedent \(\mathsf{EssentiallyGroundedIn}(\varphi,G,d)\) already assigns \(d\) the explanatory work at issue. The conclusion states only that a further entity is **not required**; it does not furnish an independent criterion showing that \(d\) is a real immanent mode rather than a device in an adverbial paraphrase:

\[
\mathsf{AdverbialGroundingOption}(G,d)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}(d,G).
\tag{Q3-Option-Not-Reality}
\]

Likewise, \(\neg\mathsf{MerelyLinguisticPredicate}(d@G)\) occurs in the proposed reality definition but has no non-question-begging test in the packet:

\[
\mathsf{ApplicationCondition}
(\neg\mathsf{MerelyLinguisticPredicate}(d@G))
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}.
\tag{Q3-No-Description-Discriminator}
\]

### 5.3 Trope/property-instance contrast test

The three-way table distinguishes a trope/property-instance from a real immanent mode by whether it is a constitution-countable node. But that is exactly the E/M boundary that Q2 found unestablished:

\[
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}(d)
\tag{Q3-Mode-Nonentity-Claim}
\]

cannot serve as an independent contrast with a trope until its own nonnode condition is defended without presupposing the mode category:

\[
\mathsf{ModeCategory}
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}
\quad\text{does not independently establish}\quad
\mathsf{ModeCategory}.
\tag{Q3-Contrast-Circularity}
\]

The candidate may be presenting a coherent third category. The supplied account has not yet shown a criterion that distinguishes it from either of the two rivals without placing the proposed category in a dedicated type and repeating the desired exclusions.

\[
\mathsf{ThreeWayTypeTable}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{RealThirdCategory}.
\tag{Q3-Type-Table-Insufficient}
\]

### 5.4 Q3 disposition

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Q3Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm AGP\ supplies\ an\ adverbial\ nonreification\ option,\\
\rm but\ not\ an\ independently\ testable\ reality\ criterion\ or\ trope\ discriminator
}}.
}
\tag{Q3-Result}
\]

The outcome is not a proof that all real intrinsic differences are tropes, parts, or independent beings:

\[
\mathsf{Q3Status}=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{TropeLike}(d,G)
\lor
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(d,G).
\tag{Q3-No-Overread}
\]

## 8. Entry disposition and stop

WSP-5 is the first positive-immanent packet in this sequence to explicitly reject the dependence-to-nonpartitivity shortcut and to distinguish a possible adverbial grounding option from a property-possession ontology. This is an improvement in **candidate formulation and burden disclosure**.

That improvement does not amount to a comparative superiority result or an admitted core. Its two decisive affirmative defenses remain mutually conditional: Q2 needs an independently warranted real nonnode category, and Q3 needs a non-question-begging discriminator for that category.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{PreflightStatus}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm Q0:\ type/application\ packet\ incomplete;\\
\rm Q1:\ positive\ mode\ grammar\ semantically\ underdefined;\\
\rm Q2\text{-}Q3:\ real\ nonnode\ mode\ category\ and\ its\ independent\ discriminator\ not\ yet\ defended
}}.
}
\tag{WSP5-Preflight-Result}
\]

The result does not refute positive-immanent differentiation, establish composition, or select a rival:

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP5}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5})
\lor
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{ComparativeDefeat}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5}).
\tag{No-Overread-of-WSP5}
\]

No field selection, actuality bridge, finite-mode bridge, public interface, or whole-framework operation is authorized:

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP5}
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Authorize}
\bigl(
\mathsf{FieldSelection}
\lor
\mathsf{ActualizationBridge}
\lor
\mathsf{FiniteModeBridge}
\lor
\mathsf{PublicInterfaceBattery}
\lor
\mathsf{WholeFrameworkPass}
\bigr).
\tag{No-Downstream-WSP5-Authorization}
\]

Any addition that supplies the missing types, application conditions, or independent reality/nonnode discriminator changes the frozen candidate:

\[
\mathsf{Add}
(\mathsf{MissingTypeClauses}
\lor
\mathsf{ModeRealityCriterion}
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentNonnodeDiscriminator})
\Longrightarrow
F_{\rm next}\neq F_{\rm WSP\text{-}5}.
\tag{Fresh-Candidate-Requirement}
\]

The WSP-5 audit stops here.

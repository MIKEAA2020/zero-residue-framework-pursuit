## What C1c adds

C1c gives an explicit, testable division of explanatory labour:

- **R layer:** which totality is realized;
- **created SCM:** why one created value follows from created parents, exogenous context, and intervention; and
- **sustenance:** why a token in the realized created history is actual rather than merely part of a mathematical/counterfactual specification.

On this architecture, sustenance does not compete with a finite action for a variable value. Both action-intervention histories are sustained, while they remain genuinely different histories. The ground is not inserted into a parent set, and the result does not erase partial perspectives or negative valence.

## Limit of the result

The formal distinction is a **candidate concurrence semantics**, not yet an adequate metaphysical explanation. Its outstanding debt is the deepest one: why B1’s actualness condition is more than a stipulated predicate, and how a good ground’s sustenance of negative-valence events is to be understood. The toy test can show non-competition and non-collapse; it cannot supply that warrant.

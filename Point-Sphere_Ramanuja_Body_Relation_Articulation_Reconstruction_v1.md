# Rāmānuja’s Body Relation Reconstruction v1
## Source-grounded test of *śarīra–śarīrī*, support, control, subordination, and finite actuality

## Status

This dossier applies the predeclared A0 test conditions in `Point-Sphere_A0_Gate_Satisfiability_Hypothesis_Register_v2.md` to a Viśiṣṭādvaita candidate that explicitly seeks to unite real plurality with nondual ultimate dependence.

It does not presume that “the world is Brahman’s body” is merely a metaphor, a result about generic panentheism, or already a public mode-membership criterion. Nor does a failure under the present A0 scope imply that Rāmānuja’s metaphysics is false, that finite reality is unreal, or that A0 is unsatisfiable.

The primary text presents Brahman as the inner controller of sentient and non-sentient entities and identifies the world and individual selves as real, distinct modes/body of Brahman. It treats the causal and manifested states as different conditions of that one qualified whole. The controls confirm that this is an organismic/metaphysical proposal, not a simple identity claim which eliminates plurality.

## 2. The candidate architecture

The reconstruction requires at least these claims:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
R_1 &: \text{Brahman is the highest, sentient, independent Self and inner controller};\\
R_2 &: \text{individual selves (\(cit\)) and non-sentient matter (\(acit\)) are real};\\
R_3 &: \text{these finite entities are distinct from Brahman and from one another in relevant respects};\\
R_4 &: \text{they nevertheless exist inseparably as Brahman’s body/modes};\\
R_5 &: \text{Brahman is their support, controller, and principal/end};\\
R_6 &: \text{the manifested world involves determinate names and forms; its causal condition is subtler};\\
R_7 &: \text{Brahman is material and efficient cause of the qualified cosmos.}
\end{array}
\]

The body relation is therefore intended to reject both rival reductions:

\[
\text{finite plurality is illusory}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\text{finite plurality is independent of Brahman}.
\]

## 3. Strongest charitable articulation criterion

The *Śrī Bhāṣya* definition makes bodyhood a substantive relation: a body is a substance that an intelligent being can completely support and control for its own purposes, while it stands in complete subordination to that being. The three familiar aspects are support/dependence, control, and *śeṣa–śeṣin* (accessory-to-principal or service-for-the-principal).

Let \(G\) be Brahman. A compressed formalization is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G)\iff
&\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)
\land\mathsf{Cetana}_{R}(G)\\
&\land\mathsf{Dharana}_{R}(G,x)
\land\mathsf{Niyamana}_{R}(G,x)\\
&\land\mathsf{Śeṣatva}_{R}(x,G).
\end{aligned}
\]

where:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\mathsf{Dharana}_{R}(G,x)&=&\text{\(G\) completely supports \(x\)’s being};\\
\mathsf{Niyamana}_{R}(G,x)&=&\text{\(G\) completely controls/governs \(x\)};\\
\mathsf{Śeṣatva}_{R}(x,G)&=&\text{\(x\) is wholly subordinate and for \(G\)’s purpose};\\
\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)&=&\text{\(x\) is a substance or real entity eligible to be a body.}
\end{array}
\]

This is much stronger than a bare assertion that finite items are “not separate.” It supplies a proposed dependence relation, a reality claim, a control relation, a teleological relation, and an account of qualified unity.

## 4. What it genuinely contributes

### 4.1 Dependence and status

Unlike the limited Advaita reconstruction, the Rāmānuja proposal does not classify the world as corrigible or ultimately merely apparent. It aims to secure:

\[
\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G)\rightarrow
\mathsf{Real}_{R}(x)\land\mathsf{Dependent}_{R}(x,G)\land\neg\mathsf{Independent}_{R}(x).
\]

This is a genuine attempt to integrate status with dependence.

### 4.2 Unity without numerical collapse

The proposal also differs from an undifferentiated identity thesis. Through body–self relation and co-ordinate predication, it claims that plurality can be real while the qualified whole remains one:

\[
\mathsf{Distinct}_{R}(x,G)
\land\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G)
\land\mathsf{Inseparable}_{R}(x,G).
\]

Thus it offers a direct response to the problem “how can distinct real entities be non-independent of the ultimate?”

### 4.3 A candidate mode/property distinction, but only conditionally

The criterion begins with \(\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)\), rather than allowing every predicate of Brahman to be a body. It can therefore state, within its own ontology, that a property as such is not simply identical to a real finite body/mode. This is a partial type distinction.

But the distinction is conditional on an independently usable account of \(\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}\). It is not yet a public procedure for deciding whether a candidate is a real substance/body rather than merely a property, representation, or fictional posit.

## 5. The actuality issue

The body criterion should not be weakened into a conditional dependence law:

\[
\mathsf{Exists}(x)\rightarrow\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G).
\]

That formula alone leaves actuality untouched. The source claims more: Brahman’s body includes real sentient and non-sentient entities in causal/subtle and effected/manifest states, and Brahman is their material and efficient cause. It also describes an ordered world in which particular embodiment is connected with beginningless karma and divine governance.

The decisive A0 question is therefore:

\[
\text{What makes}\quad\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathsf{Dharana}_{R}(G,x)\land
\mathsf{Niyamana}_{R}(G,x)\land
\mathsf{Śeṣatva}_{R}(x,G)
\quad\text{true for this }x?
\]

The sources assert that actual entities stand in this relation. They do not extract an admission rule for proposed finite candidates that is independent of the already given body of Brahman, the actuality of Brahman, the relevant causal order, and the scripturally articulated cosmic inventory.

The doctrine therefore offers an account of *why admitted reality is non-independent*, but it has not yet supplied a noncircular test of *which candidate is admitted reality*.

| **T1** one candidate | If \(x\) is already a real substance and is completely supported, controlled, and purposively subordinate, it is body/mode. | A conditional classification is available. ## 7. Profile and rival audit

### 7.1 Profile invariance

Rāmānuja’s theory of co-ordinate predication is a serious semantic resource. It explains how terms with different qualitative content can co-designate the qualified Brahman rather than forcing a numerical collapse between Brahman and a finite self.

It does not thereby give the required common extractor:

\[
\Pi(x)=\langle\bar S,\bar E,Q,H,B\rangle
\]

or an equivalent public successor. In particular, no source-grounded rule is supplied for preserving \(\mathsf{Dravya}\), total support, total control, and subordination-for-purpose across rival, profile-conservative representations. Thus T5 remains open/failed at the A0 level, not refuted as a doctrine of religious semantics.

### 7.2 Matched rivals

Three rivals can be stated but are not yet a scored metaphysical-rival corpus:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{DepOnly}(x,G) &: x\text{ depends on }G\text{ but is not a real body/mode};\\
\mathsf{PropertyOnly}(x,G) &: x\text{ is a predicate/property of }G\text{ rather than a finite real};\\
\mathsf{Independent}(x) &: x\text{ is an independently real finite substance.}
\end{array}
\]

The Rāmānuja criterion purports to exclude each through the threefold relation. Yet their exclusion requires independently assessable truth conditions for \(G\), \(x\), support, control, and purpose. In their absence, the source cannot be credited with a public rival defeat.

## 8. Articulation verdict

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{Rāmānuja supplies the strongest source-tested synthesis so far:}\
\text{a real-plurality, dependence, control, teleology, and qualified-unity schema.}\\[2mm]
\text{It does not yet pass A0 as a public criterion of genuine finite}\
\text{mode-membership and actuality.}
\end{gathered}
}
\]

The A0-relevant failure is structural under the fixed taxonomy:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_1 &: \mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}\text{ begins with an already typed real substance/entity};\\
S_2 &: \text{support, control, and purpose are claimed of the finite inventory while}\
&\text{being used to characterize that inventory};\\
S_5 &: \text{the type distinction is conditional rather than an independent finite admission test};\\
S_6 &: \text{actuality is asserted through the total Brahman–world ontology, not separately}\
&\text{decided for proposed finite candidates.}
\end{array}
\]

This is not the claim that those relations are meaningless, false, or merely verbal. It is the more limited claim:

\[
\mathsf{Śarīra}_{R}(x,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicatedActualFiniteMode}(x)
\]

without further noncircular truth conditions and an invariant profile test.

## 9. Downstream bridges

### Phenomenal bridge

Rāmānuja has a developed account of perception, devotion, and liberation. It should not be confused with a bridge from this body relation to the existing finite complete-mode CFD programme. No new coded phenomenal data, eligibility criterion, or realization theorem follows.

### Physical bridge

The doctrine has a cosmological claim concerning the organization and governance of the world. It does not supply a modern physical model, a recovery limit for quantum mechanics or general relativity, an observable, or a discriminator. It therefore does not satisfy the separate physical-adequacy obligation.

## 10. Comparative consequence

The case adds a genuinely synthetic attempted criterion to the comparison:

\[
\text{real plurality}
+\text{dependence}
+\text{status}
+\text{qualified unity}
+\text{teleological governance}.
\]

Its result strengthens the *diagnostic* hypothesis that adding the earlier fragments does not yet yield a public finite actuality/membership criterion. It does not demonstrate that no possible synthesis can do so.

Under the preregistered v2 activation rule, the outcome is a structural A0 failure. The meta-inquiry therefore becomes the primary next work package: it must develop a candidate taxonomy, scope class, exhaustiveness strategy, and escape audit. This changes research priority, not the truth status of \(H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}\).

## Nonclaim

This dossier does not show that Viśiṣṭādvaita is false, that Brahman cannot have a body, that finite selves and matter are unreal, that scripture cannot supply knowledge within the tradition, that a public criterion must be framework-neutral, or that A0 is unsatisfiable. The correct whole-reality framework remains unidentified.

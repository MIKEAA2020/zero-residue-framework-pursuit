# Point–Sphere Independent-Warrant Audit
## SR-2 Dependent-Locus Non-Exhaustion and WR-1 Immanent Self-Determination

**Audited sources:**

- `Strong-Route_SR-2_Dependent-Locus_NonExhaustion_Preregistration_v1.md`
- `Weak-Route_WR-1_Immanent_Self-Determination_Preregistration_v1.md`

**Status:** Independent-warrant audit only. This report does not reopen the expired candidates, introduce a new source principle, or derive physical structure. It asks whether either source claim has support independent of the source claim itself and the desired spacetime output.

---

## 1. Independent-warrant standard

A source principle has independent warrant only if all four conditions hold:

\[
\mathsf{IndependentWarrant}(H)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentPremises}(H)
\land
\mathsf{ValidOrDisciplinedAbductiveLink}(H)
\land
\mathsf{RivalControl}(H)
\land
\mathsf{NoOutputSmuggling}(H).
\]

The conditions mean:

1. **Independent premises:** the support does not already assume the candidate's source, field, locus, order, or desired spacetime output.
2. **Link:** the premises entail the source or support it by an explicit noncircular abductive rule.
3. **Rival control:** materially different explanations of the premises are stated and compared rather than ignored.
4. **No output smuggling:** actual plurality, causal order, metric, observer, or physical world is not inserted as the evidence the source is supposedly deriving.

A conditional clarification is not an existence warrant:

\[
(A\Longrightarrow B)
\not\Rightarrow
A
\lor
B.
\]

## 2. SR-2 proposed warrant

SR-2's Non-Exhaustive Dependence Rationale is:

\[
\mathsf{RealFiniteDependentPresentation}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ItsLimitationBelongsToDependentPresentation}.
\]

The rationale has a limited valid role. If a real finite dependent presentation is already granted, then locating its privation in that presentation rather than treating it as a negation of \(G\)'s simplicity avoids SR-1's direct contradiction:

\[
\mathsf{FiniteDependentPresentation}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{NonExhaustion}(\ell,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{StrongSimple}(G).
\]

This is a conditional localization result, not a derivation of DNE.

## 3. SR-2 premise audit

To support the source postulate:

\[
\mathsf{DNE}(G,\Sigma,L,\triangleleft),
\]

SR-2 would need independently supported premises for at least:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{S\text{-}P1} & \text{there is one objectively real finite dependent Sphere/presentation }\Sigma\\
\mathsf{S\text{-}P2} & \text{there are actual dependent loci }L\text{ rather than merely descriptions or possibilities}\\
\mathsf{S\text{-}P3} & \text{there is a directed nontemporal dependence relation }\triangleleft\\
\mathsf{S\text{-}P4} & \text{the loci and relation are neither parts nor independent beings}\\
\mathsf{S\text{-}P5} & \text{the whole dependent order is globally actual}
\end{array}
\]

None is independently supplied by the conditional rationale. Indeed, the conjunction of S-P1 through S-P5 is very close to the content of DNE itself:

\[
\bigwedge_{i=1}^{5}\mathsf{S\text{-}Pi}
\approx
\mathsf{DNE}(G,\Sigma,L,\triangleleft).
\]

Thus the proposed warrant does not reach the source principle without assuming its essential content.

### SR-2 rival control

The conditional rationale is also compatible with several rival possibilities:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{R\text{-}S1} & \text{finite differentiation is primitive or brute rather than grounded by }G\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}S2} & \text{the finite order is an epistemic presentation rather than objective structure}\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}S3} & \text{a dependent plurality exists but requires positive relational structure not supplied by privation alone}\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}S4} & \text{there is no real finite dependent order of the stipulated kind}
\end{array}
\]

SR-2 does not compare these rivals by independent criteria.

### SR-2 warrant result

\[
\mathsf{WarrantStatus}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{ConditionalLocalizationOnly}_{
\substack{
\rm avoids\ negating\ G\text{'s}\ simplicity\ if\ DNE\ is\ granted;\\
\rm does\ not\ independently\ establish\ DNE\ or\ its\ loci/order
}}.
\]

## 4. WR-1 proposed warrant

WR-1's Self-Determining Actuality Rationale is:

\[
\mathsf{ActualityThatGroundsDeterminateReality}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{NotMerelyIndeterminateBlank}.
\]

This has a limited dialectical force: a wholly blank source seems insufficient to explain determinate reality without an added selector, external source, or brute connection.

But “not merely an indeterminate blank” does not entail the candidate's specific source:

\[
\mathsf{NotIndeterminateBlank}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ISD}(G,\mathcal F,\preceq).
\]

In particular, it does not entail a positive formal field, a specific relation, global actualization, or the nonpartitive status required by weak simplicity.

---

## 5. WR-1 premise audit

To support:

\[
\mathsf{ISD}(G,\mathcal F,\preceq),
\]

WR-1 needs independent support for at least:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{W\text{-}P1} & \text{there is determinate objective reality needing ultimate grounding}\\
\mathsf{W\text{-}P2} & G\text{ is its sole or adequate ultimate ground}\\
\mathsf{W\text{-}P3} & \text{an external selector, external relation, or brute fact is unavailable}\\
\mathsf{W\text{-}P4} & \text{the adequate ground must be positive immanent formal determination}\\
\mathsf{W\text{-}P5} & \text{that determination has the field/relation/global form }\langle\mathcal F,\preceq\rangle
\end{array}
\]

W-P1 may be treated as a background datum of determinate experience and physics. But it does not by itself establish W-P2 through W-P5. W-P2 is a metaphysical core commitment; W-P3 excludes alternatives; W-P4 is the contested formal-to-ontological bridge; and W-P5 specifies the desired source structure.

\[
\mathsf{W\text{-}P1}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{W\text{-}P2}
\land
\mathsf{W\text{-}P3}
\land
\mathsf{W\text{-}P4}
\land
\mathsf{W\text{-}P5}.
\]

### WR-1 rival control

The nonblankness rationale has alternatives:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{R\text{-}W1} & \text{an external relation or additional source grounds the determinate order}\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}W2} & \text{the core-to-order connection is brute}\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}W3} & \text{the relevant differentiation is only representational or descriptive}\\
\mathsf{R\text{-}W4} & \text{a positive immanent ground exists but has a different structure from }\langle\mathcal F,\preceq\rangle
\end{array}
\]

WR-1 does not independently rule out or comparatively score these alternatives. To call ISD the only adequate option would already build its intended conclusion into the premise of the argument.

### WR-1 warrant result

\[
\mathsf{WarrantStatus}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
=
\mathsf{AntiBlanknessConsiderationOnly}_{
\substack{
\rm identifies\ pressure\ for\ an\ explanatory\ source;\\
\rm does\ not\ independently\ warrant\ ISD,\ its\ field,\ or\ its\ relation
}}.
\]

## 7. Bounded conclusion

The independent-warrant audit yields no source-principle winner:

\[
\mathsf{IndependentWarrantPass}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{IndependentWarrantPass}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
=
\mathsf{No}.
\]

This is not a refutation of dependent-locus non-exhaustion, immanent self-determination, strong simplicity, weak simplicity, or Point–Sphere monism. It establishes only that neither present source claim is independently supported strongly enough to bear the promised physical derivation.

The truth-directed choice is now explicit: either supply an independent argument for a source principle, or treat a source structure as an openly added physical/metaphysical hypothesis and evaluate it by its recovery of spacetime and empirical consequences rather than call it derived from bare simplicity.

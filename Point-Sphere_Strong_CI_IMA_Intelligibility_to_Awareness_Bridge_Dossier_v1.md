# Strong CI Adjudication — IMA Intelligibility-to-Awareness Bridge Dossier v1
## Does self-intelligibility require a self-knowing ultimate?

## Status

**IMA examined; the strong bridge is not established.** Strong CI requires an account of consciousness and of any claimed ultimate intelligibility. It does not yet prove that the ultimate is one self-knowing awareness.

## 1. The target bridge

\[
\mathsf{IMA}:
\quad
\mathsf{SelfIntelligible}(U)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Aware}(U).
\]

The bridge is often supported by the thought that intelligibility cannot be a free-floating abstraction: if ultimate reality is intelligible in itself, its intelligibility must be actual as self-present reason or awareness.

This is a serious metaphysical proposal. It is not a theorem of formal logic.

## 2. Three claims that must not be conflated

| Claim | Status | Why it falls short of IMA |
|---|---|---|
| Reality is knowable by finite minds | Epistemic datum/achievement | Does not entail that reality itself is a mind |
| Reality has non-brute explanatory structure | SCI requirement | A structure can be alleged self-intelligible without being conscious |
| Phenomenal consciousness exists | Whole-reality datum requiring account | Does not by itself entail one universal awareness rather than a neutral, plural, or constituted base |

The IMA conclusion needs an additional principle connecting explanatory intelligibility, phenomenal presence, and ultimate intrinsic nature.

## 3. Candidate arguments and limits

| Argument | Genuine contribution | Missing bridge |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligibility is always intelligibility-to-a-knower | Motivates a mind-first account of reason | Must show that every objective or intrinsic intelligibility requires an actual ultimate knower, not merely possible finite knowers |
| Consciousness cannot arise from a wholly non-conscious base without brute emergence | Pressures reductive structuralism | Does not uniquely select one aware ground; neutral/Russellian/panpsychist/constitutive candidates remain |
| Self-intimation is intrinsic to conscious experience | Supports self-presentation as a phenomenological resource | Does not show that all ultimate self-intelligibility has phenomenal character |
| Complete self-knowledge contains every intrinsic determination | Supports a nondual identity route if awareness is already accepted | Presupposes awareness/self-knowledge rather than deriving it from SCI |

## 4. Counter-architecture matrix

| Architecture | How it can claim self-intelligibility | Main SCI/adequacy burden | IMA result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-knowing aware ground | Intrinsic self-presence/self-knowledge | Explain finite differentiation and why awareness is non-composite/non-brute | Held; IMA conclusion built into core |
| Neutral intrinsic nature | Intrinsic categorical nature grounds physical/phenomenal facts | Specify neutral nature and consciousness constitution | Held; not eliminated by SCI alone |
| Structural ultimate | Necessary objective modal/explanatory structure | Explain why structure is self-intelligible and account for phenomenal consciousness | Held; no automatic awareness inference |
| Russellian/panpsychist family | Consciousness-relevant intrinsic base underlies structure | Combination, unity, and exact physical/phenomenal bridge | Held |
| Non-attributive absolute | Ultimate cannot be positively characterized | Must still explain without merely declining explanatory predicates | Held at best; risks empty closure |

## 5. Exact result

SCI plus the fact of phenomenal consciousness yields a burden, not yet an awareness theorem:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\land
\mathsf{Phenomenality}
\Rightarrow
\text{a non-brute account of phenomenal consciousness is required.}
}
\tag{IMA-R}
\]

But:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\land
\mathsf{Phenomenality}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{OneAwareGround}.
}
\tag{IMA-NE}
\]

To derive the stronger conclusion, a candidate must defend at least:

\[
\mathsf{NoNonConsciousConstitution}:
\quad
\text{no wholly non-conscious or neutral base can non-brutely constitute phenomenal consciousness};
\]

and:

\[
\mathsf{UnityOfUltimateAwareness}:
\quad
\text{the required consciousness-relevant base must be one self-knowing ultimate rather than plural or distributed}.  
\]

Both are substantive claims requiring countermodel tests.

## 6. Implication for Point–Sphere/nondual identity

The nondual identity package has a real conditional advantage once an aware ground is granted:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(a)
\Rightarrow R(a).
\]

IMA does not derive its antecedent. It locates the correct order of argument:

\[
\text{aware-ground / consciousness bridge}
\Rightarrow
\text{test nondual self-knowledge identity},
\]

not:

\[
\text{SCI alone}
\Rightarrow
\text{aware ground}
\Rightarrow
\text{all nondual consequences}.
\]

## 7. Source note

Current philosophy of consciousness has no agreed theory, and the status of consciousness—fundamental, constituted, emergent, or otherwise—remains a central explanatory question. Structural realism and Russellian monism explicitly illustrate distinct live ways of treating structure, intrinsic nature, and consciousness. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/consciousness/, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structural-realism/, and https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/.

## 8. IMA conclusion and expiry condition

The IMA route is closed unless new work supplies one of:

1. a non-circular proof that objective/self-intelligibility is impossible without actual self-aware presence;
2. a no-go result against every neutral, structural, plural-consciousness, and constitutive account of phenomenality under SCI; or
3. a phenomenological argument whose premises and scope are explicitly stated and which does not assume the desired ultimate conclusion.

Absent such a result, IMA is **held, not adopted**. Awareness remains a substantive candidate core, not a conclusion generated by the phrase “complete intelligibility.”

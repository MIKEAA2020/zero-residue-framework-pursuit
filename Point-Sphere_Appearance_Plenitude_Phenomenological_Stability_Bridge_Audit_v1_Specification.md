# AP4C3C1 Phenomenological Stability-Bridge Audit v1
## Can temporal self-presentation independently warrant AP4C1's stability/error model?

## Purpose

AP4C3A establishes that bare core identity does not derive AP4C1's perturbation, trace-preservation, error, or decoder structure. AP4C3C1 assesses the strongest phenomenological route to an independent bridge: prereflective self-manifestation together with retentional temporal consciousness.

This is a source-sensitive, rival-neutral audit. It does not treat phenomenological description as proof that an aware ground exists, as a physical theory, or as a route around AP4C3A's formal countermodels.

## Bridge requirements

A positive route to AP4C1 would need independent support for all of:

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| B1 | A non-gauge, non-arbitrary minimal perturbation/adjacency relation relevant to finite self-presentation. |
| B2 | A distinction between corrupted witness presentation and a different genuine self-presentation. |
| B3 | A trace-preservation or correction requirement across the relevant perturbation class. |
| B4 | A warranted mapping from B1–B3 to multiple witnesses, a gauge action, a code constraint, Hamming-like error class, and decoder. |

## Sources and exclusion rule

The audit considers contemporary reference accounts of phenomenological self-consciousness and temporal consciousness, including retentional and extensional alternatives. The sources may support structural self-presence, retentional continuity, limits of reflection, or a distinction between self-identification and content correctness.

They do **not** count as support for B1–B4 merely because they use terms such as “retention,” “continuity,” “trace,” “self,” or “correction.” A positive rating requires an explicit noncircular entailment or a clearly stated independently motivated bridge.

## Registered tests

1. Does retentional temporality supply a counterfactual error metric, rather than merely an experienced order/continuity structure?
2. Does prereflective self-awareness distinguish a wrong witness representation from a new genuine experience?
3. Does temporal coherence require recovery of an unchanged trace under an arbitrary local alteration, rather than constitutive modification of a retained past?
4. Do the sources select AP4C1's multi-witness code architecture over retentional, extensional, hybrid, or other accounts?
5. Would the proposed conclusion remain defensible if it favored a grammar or record law other than IAR/AP4C1?

## Success and failure conditions

- **Full bridge:** independently supports B1–B4 without importing witness code or public objectivity.
- **Partial bridge:** supports a weaker endogenous-retentional coherence condition only; it cannot upgrade AP4C1's status.
- **Failure:** no relevant support, or apparent support is just a renamed code/error premise.

The expected cautious result is partial support for intrinsic retentional coherence and failure for B1–B4 as a package.

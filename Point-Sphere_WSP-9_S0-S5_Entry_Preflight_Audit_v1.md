## 4. S2 — total profile algebra and invariant lift

The profile effect maps are total by clause, not merely tabulated at bottom. The bounded-lattice endomorphisms of the four-element chain can be exhaustively enumerated. There are ten such maps, and direct finite evaluation confirms:

\[
\mathsf{NonEliminable}(j_1,P_a)
\land
\mathsf{NonEliminable}(j_2,P_a)
\qquad(a\in\{g,h\}).
\tag{S2-Noneliminability-Pass}
\]

For \(j_1\), every bounded-lattice endomorphism that collapses \(j_1\) with \(\bot\) also collapses the BaseQuery-distinct pair \(\bot,j_1\). For \(j_2\), every such collapsing map also collapses a BaseQuery-distinct pair, including \(\bot,j_2\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
&f(j_1)=f(\bot)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{BaseQuery}(\bot)\neq\mathsf{BaseQuery}(j_1)
\land f(\bot)=f(j_1),\\
&f(j_2)=f(\bot)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{BaseQuery}(\bot)\neq\mathsf{BaseQuery}(j_2)
\land f(\bot)=f(j_2).
\end{aligned}
\tag{S2-Collapse-Witnesses}
\]

The explicitly supplied cross-presentation map from \(P_g\) to \(P_h\) preserves the heights and hence the stated BaseQuery values:

\[
\iota_{g,h}(\bot)=\bot,
\quad
\iota_{g,h}(j_1)=j_1,

\iota_{g,h}(j_2)=j_2,

\iota_{g,h}(\top)=\top.
\tag{S2-Explicit-Isomorphism-Pass}

But \(\mathsf{CrossInvariantLift}\) quantifies over \(\iota_{a,b}\) and invokes \(\mathsf{PreservesBaseQuery}\) without defining their general cases. Therefore the finite algebra calculations pass while the full cross-presentation lift is not yet internally executable:

\[

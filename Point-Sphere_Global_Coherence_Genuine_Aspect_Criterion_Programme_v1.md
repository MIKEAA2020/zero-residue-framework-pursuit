# GC3a — Genuine-Aspect Criterion Programme v1
## Reopened after the omission-theorem corrigendum

## Status

The omission theorem is closed internally by `Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Omission_Theorem_Corrigendum_v1.md`. The remaining upstream task is not realization selection but an independently typed domain:

\[
\mathcal A_{\rm genuine}.
\]

This programme does not reopen directedness, persistence, record law, physics, or general all-world plenitude. It applies the existing AP0 type discipline and Revision-C fidelity rule.

## Objective

Specify a non-circular criterion for genuine finite aspect-content that is independent of realization and avoids treating a mathematical grammar as ground structure.

## Candidate necessary conditions

For a candidate \(a\), test:

\[
\mathsf{GA}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Coherent}(a)
\land
\mathsf{GroundCompatible}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NonGauge}(a)
\land
\mathsf{ContextAdmissible}(a).
\]

The task is to determine whether these can become jointly sufficient without a hidden selector.

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Genuine_Aspect_Criterion_GA1_Audit_v1.md` makes coherence a declared semantic-satisfiability condition relative to a finite content language, interpretation class, and proper context. Under the explicit non-contradictory-determination bridge, it establishes

\[

\]

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Genuine_Aspect_Criterion_GA2_Hinge_Identity_Audit_v1.md` validly establishes only that a reduced self-knowledge theory \(K_0\), from which real finite modes have been omitted, does not entail finite articulated content. `Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Genuine_Aspect_Criterion_GA2_Core_Unpacking_Corrigendum_v1.md` corrects the debt ledger: the protected nondual appearance package already includes real finite modes and the core mode/aspect/self-knowledge-content identity. Consequently, its non-vacuity is core content, not a newly added hinge.

The determination criterion is correspondingly typed core-internally:

\[
\mathsf{Det}_{G}(a)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{Req}^{P_{\rm ND}}_M(a),
\]

where requirement is intrinsic to the fixed protected nature in a model, not the stronger claim that every model of an underdescribed theory contains one same named formula token. Under the protected identity, completeness, and finite-mode commitments, this is an internal conceptual derivation. It is not a neutral logical theorem or a public proof of the core.

## Foundational closure and downstream rule

**GA2 is complete at the protected-core level.** The framework is conditional on the protected nondual appearance package \(P_{\rm ND}\); formal grammars are target-relative descriptions rather than ground-to-grammar derivations. GA3/GA4/GA5 may proceed only as downstream grammar/fidelity work when a target phenomenon, rival grammar, and discriminator are predeclared. They are not remaining foundational gates for the reality of modes or the core mode/aspect/content identity. No further “self-knowledge requires it” reformulation is an active foundational task.

# Point–Sphere Framework: Critical Merged Synthesis and Research Programme

**Working name:** *Asymmetrical Non-Duality with Participatory Plenitude*

## Executive status

This document merges the Point–Sphere consolidation with the prior critical awareness–physics synthesis. Its result is neither a proof of God nor a new physical theory. It is a disciplined, layered research programme whose strongest current claims are conditional.

The framework is worth pursuing because it keeps together questions too often artificially separated: why anything concrete exists, why physics is structurally intelligible, why experience occurs, how finite perspectives could be real, and how a metaphysics that reaches toward science could become vulnerable to formal and empirical criticism. Its central liabilities are equally clear: it has not established a global principle of complete intelligibility, a unique simple ground, pure awareness as that ground’s intrinsic nature, literal necessary creation, plenitude, an individuation map for finite subjects, a psychophysical bridge, a Born-rule measure, or a recovery of established physics.

The best present formulation is therefore:

> **Conditional core.** If contingent concrete reality requires a complete ontological explanation; if the relevant grounding order is asymmetric, non-circular, and well-founded; and if genuine composition depends on constituents and their unification, then at least one non-derivative, non-composite ground is required. If structural description does not exhaust concrete intrinsic nature and phenomenal awareness is the best candidate for that intrinsic nature, then an awareness-ground becomes a serious abductive hypothesis. If real finite beings depend asymmetrically on that ground, then literal participatory creation is a possible ontology. Necessary creation, plenitude, finite experiential subjects, and physics-level unification each require further, separately defended principles.

This document treats every step after its antecedent as either a conditional result, a hypothesis, a proposed formal object, or a research obligation. Nothing becomes established merely by being embedded in a rich vocabulary.

## 1. Methodological standards and premise ledger

A viable version of the programme accepts six constraints.

1. **Ontological, not chronological, explanation.** The question is not what occurred first in time. Time belongs to the order to be explained. The question is what is non-derivative in the order of being.
2. **No verbal stopping point.** “It just is” can coherently mark an explanatory terminus, but does not itself show that the terminus is self-intelligible or necessary in virtue of its nature.
3. **No dismissal of phenomenality.** A theory of functions, dispositions, information processing, and report does not automatically settle why experience occurs. This does not refute physicalism; it records a live explanatory problem.
4. **No competition with established science.** The framework may interpret the ontological significance of physics. It may not replace successful measurement, relativity, quantum theory, evolutionary biology, neuroscience, or ordinary causal practice by metaphysical declaration.
5. **Public formal discipline.** At the level of physics-facing claims, definitions, axioms, mathematical structures, recovery results, parameter choices, and prospective tests are required. Symmetry, beauty, and metaphor are discovery guides, not derivations.
6. **Ethical non-defeat.** Ultimate metaphysical descriptions do not by themselves license discounting finite suffering, bypassing consent, or erasing ordinary accountability. This is a substantive practical commitment, not a theorem proved by omitting theology from an axiom set.

## 2. Controlled vocabulary

| Term | Disciplined use |
|---|---|
| **Ground / Absolute** | A proposed non-derivative reality on which derivative reality depends; not an object within an already given physical inventory. Uniqueness needs argument. |
| **Aseity / self-subsistence** | Lack of ontological dependence on anything external or prior. It is not self-causation and is stronger than merely having no earlier temporal cause. |
| **Grounding** | An asymmetric ontological dependence relation, not necessarily an event earlier in time. |
| **Simple** | Not assembled from ontologically independent constituents, conditions, or principles. It does not mean geometrically small. |
| **Complete** | Lacking no condition needed to be what it is. Completeness does not by itself entail causal productivity, a world, or all possible worlds. |
| **Derivative reality** | Reality that is genuine but non-independent. It need not be a second ultimate reality. |
| **Perspective** | A locally bounded organisation of access, record, memory, and control. A perspective is not automatically a phenomenally conscious subject. |
| **Structure** | Relations, quantities, symmetries, dispositions, dynamics, and formal organisation of the sort physical theories represent. |
| **Experience** | Phenomenal presence: what it is like, if anything, for a mode or subject. |
| **Polarity** | A proposed contrast, duality, involution, adjunction, order reversal, or symmetry-breaking relation. A list of opposites is not yet a generative law. |
| **Grade** | A precisely defined level of structural refinement or ontological dependence. “Higher” is not explanatory until an ordering is formalised. |
| **Plenitude** | The hypothesis that every admissible, mutually compossible, realisable derivative structure is actual in a specified landscape, governed by a realisation rule and measure. |

Two distinctions govern the rest of the document:

- A **bare brute terminus** is ungrounded because no further explanation is supplied. A **strongly aseitic ground** is non-derivative because its intelligible nature makes external explanation inapplicable. The second does not follow merely from the first.
- “Only the Absolute exists” may mean either that only one reality is **independent**, or that nothing derivative is numerically real. The first is compatible with literal creation; the second is strict numerical non-dualism and is a different ontology.

## 3. Grounding, brute fact, and regress

### 3.1 The strongest defensible argument

The grounding argument should be stated conditionally:

1. A derivative reality is not ultimate merely by being temporally eternal. An eternal physical order may still depend on laws, modal facts, field structure, or some more basic condition.
2. A collection of derivative explanations can explain local dependence while leaving open why the total dependence-structure obtains.
3. If the grounding order is non-circular and well-founded, following the grounds of a derivative item must terminate in a non-derivative ground.
4. If genuine composition depends on constituents and their unification, a terminal ground cannot be composite in that dependence-generating sense.
5. Physics describes the world conditional on a physical framework with laws, structures, and parameters; it does not presently prove that a particular concrete physical order exists necessarily.
6. Mathematical necessity, even if genuine, does not by itself establish concrete actuality.

**Conditional result:** if one requires a complete non-arbitrary ontological explanation, the relevant derivative order must terminate in a non-derivative ground sufficient for that derivative actuality. If one further accepts that any composite requires a unifying condition not reducible to independent constituents, that ground is non-composite in the relevant sense. If physical reality belongs to the dependent totality rather than being independently shown necessary, the ground is non-physical in the ontological sense of not being one configuration inside the physical order.

This does not establish uniqueness, pure awareness, intellect, will, literal creation, moral goodness, or a fully specified cosmology.

A useful **local foundation theorem** makes the exact formal result visible. Let \(\operatorname{Anc}(d)\) be the proper-grounding ancestry of an actually derivative item \(d\). If proper grounding is asymmetric and transitive, \(\operatorname{Anc}(d)\) is well-founded, and dependence-composite items have a proper ground, then \(\operatorname{Anc}(d)\) contains a minimal item \(g\) that is non-derivative and non-composite **in the dependence-generating sense**. The proof is immediate from minimality: a ground of \(g\), or a composition principle requiring one, contradicts minimality.

This theorem is genuinely useful, but deliberately local. Its foundation premise is substantive; it does not establish that \(g\) grounds every concrete fact, that its essence explains its existence, that it is necessary in every possible world, or that there is only one such root. A plurality of minimal roots and priority-monist countermodels remain live unless further unity, anti-haecceitist, or totality premises are defended. Likewise, categorical initiality yields uniqueness only up to isomorphism **after** a category and an initiality axiom have been supplied; it cannot independently derive metaphysical aseity, simplicity, or numerical uniqueness.

A proposed globalisation needs still more: a factual fusion or total-concrete-fact premise, a rule for full grounds of an arbitrarily large conjunction, and a position on whether grounding is full, partial, or weak/mediate. A full ground of a conjunction need not strictly ground each conjunct or every consequence individually. Thus a totality theorem, if one is adopted, establishes only what its totality and ground-introduction axioms warrant; it does not convert the local root automatically into a single ground of all concrete facts.

The five-audit package usefully exposes a specific invalid shortcut. Even if an aggregate \(C\) of contingent facts is admitted and has a full ground-set \(\Delta\), the fact that \(\Delta\) contains a necessary member does not show that that member alone fully grounds \(C\), that it is a minimal ancestor, or that it has no distinct ground. Well-foundedness supplies a minimal element only for a suitably specified non-empty set; it does not make an arbitrarily selected member of \(\Delta\) minimal. Nor does “the whole depends on parts” rule out priority monism without an independent priority principle, and an initial-object result applies only after a category and initiality axiom are given. These are useful axioms for a conditional all-contingent theorem, not deductions from grounding notation or the existence of a totality.

### 3.1.1 Global unique-foundation theorem

A genuine global result requires a stronger fact-domain semantics. Let \(F\) be a non-empty total domain of actual concrete facts, closed under the concrete grounds used by the theory. Write \(S\triangleright f\) when the non-empty set \(S\subseteq F\) is a complete immediate ground of \(f\), and write \(x\prec f\) when \(x\in S\) for some \(S\triangleright f\). Let \(M\) be the facts with no \(\prec\)-predecessor.

The following premises are needed:

1. **Totality closure:** \(F\) includes all relevant concrete facts, including grounding and unifying facts required by the theory.
2. **Well-foundedness:** \(\prec\) has no non-empty subset without a minimal member.
3. **Dependence-composition:** every composite in the dependence-generating sense has a proper complete ground.
4. **Full-ground substitution:** replacing each member of a complete ground-set by its complete grounds yields a complete transitive ground; this closure applies along the relevant well-founded ancestry trees.
5. **Anti-primitive plurality:** jointly actual, wholly independent minimal foundation facts are disallowed. This is an explicit unity premise, not a consequence of well-foundedness.

Under these premises, there is exactly one minimal foundation fact \(g\). It is non-composite in the dependence-generating sense, and \(\{g\}\) fully grounds every fact in \(F\) through the transitive closure of complete grounding. The proof is direct: well-foundedness gives a non-empty foundation set; anti-primitive plurality reduces it to one member; dependence-composition forbids that member’s composition; and full-ground substitution collapses each ancestry tree to the unique root.

This is a valid global theorem, but its premise pricing matters. Dropping well-foundedness allows infinite descent; dropping anti-primitive plurality permits multiple roots; dropping dependence-composition permits a primitive composite root; and dropping full-ground substitution leaves root reachability without one root fully grounding every fact. The theorem therefore earns one **actual non-composite foundation fact**, not necessary aseity, awareness, personhood, moral goodness, or creation. A further bearer/truthmaker principle is required even to turn the foundation fact into a ground entity; a further modal premise is required to infer that it obtains in every possible world.

### 3.2 What survives the critique of brute fact and regress

A brute-fact view and an infinite regress are not formal contradictions. They may be coherent accounts of where explanation stops. Their cost is instead explanatory: they do not satisfy a demand for an intelligible, non-brute ultimate unless they show why their total structure is necessary or self-subsistent in a strong sense.

At the level of **termination form**, the terminus taxonomy is simple: a dependence account is either non-terminating (an endless or circular family) or terminating; a terminating base is either treated as primitive/brute or as non-derivative in virtue of its nature. This taxonomy does not settle specification. A necessary physical base, mathematical/structural base, plurality of necessary bases, pure awareness, and strict non-duality remain distinct proposals within the thin non-derivative family.

An infinite series can explain each member by another member without explaining why the series, its laws, or its dependence relation exists. Conversely, an advocate may reject the demand for a further whole-level explanation. The disagreement is therefore over the scope and authority of a principle of sufficient reason, not over elementary logic.

The defensible comparative position is limited:

- a bare brute terminus has an ultimate explanatory limit;
- a ground account has a richer burden: it must show that its additional simplicity, awareness, creation, and value bridges do non-circular explanatory work;
- necessary physical, structural, plural, neutral, and nondual foundations remain live rival specifications;
- agnosticism is an epistemic attitude, not a third ontology.

The criticism of brute termination is therefore one of **rational stability**, not a formal reductio. A primitive stopping point leaves open why this base rather than another is fundamental and why consciousness, law, order, or normativity obtain within it. An epistemic policy that rejects unconstrained metaphysical inference is not self-refuting merely because the policy is not itself an ontological ground: epistemic warrant, ontological grounding, and normative authority are different questions. The remaining comparative task is to show that a richer ground account delivers genuine explanation rather than importing equal or greater unexplained commitments.

The simple awareness-ground may earn a provisional advantage over a **bare** brute terminus only if its core premises are independently credible, its bridges constrain consequences, and it is at least as coherent as the rival. Scope alone does not secure that result.

## 4. Ontological architectures: retain the fork

The merged view does not collapse three distinct frameworks.

| Question | Literal participatory creation (P) | Strict numerical non-dualism (ND) | Classical personal theism |
|---|---|---|---|
| What is independent? | One ground; creatures are real and distinct but dependent. | One reality in the strict numerical sense; plurality is conventional, perspectival, or appearance-like. | God is necessary; creatures are distinct and ordinarily contingent. |
| Main strength | Preserves real creator–creature difference and secondary causation. | Preserves numerical unity without a second substance. | Provides an explicit personal-creator model. |
| Central cost | Reception, simplicity, contingency, and modal-collapse problems. | Individuation, ignorance, conventional agency, and suffering problems. | Divine simplicity, hiddenness, suffering, and freedom/necessity problems. |
| Physics-facing task | Model dependence without making G a physical cause or extra node in physics. | Explain stable public laws and subjects under ultimate non-distinction. | Provide the same empirical discipline if physics-level claims are made. |

### 4.1 Literal creation and necessary creation

The standing working thesis is **literal real creation under a necessary ground**: finite beings are genuinely real, numerically distinct, asymmetrically dependent on the ground, and do not complete it. This is not the claim that creatures are parts, intrinsic states, or merely deceptive appearances of the ground.

The working thesis requires more than P. If creation is necessary, it also requires a C-Nec-type principle: for example, a defended claim that perfect non-arbitrariness or complete actuality entails realisation of all admissible, compossible structures. That premise has costs: possible modal collapse, a vast landscape of harms, and pressure on any claim of providential benevolence.

C-Cont is a live alternative: an infinite ground can be sufficient for finite participations without requiring them for its own completion. It avoids necessary plenitude, but leaves the contrastive question why this creaturely order obtains rather than another as a primitive or as a further fact about grounded selection. Neither C-Nec nor C-Cont is derived by calling creation an “expression.”

### 4.1.1 Creation–dependence bridge

A literal-creation thesis becomes coherent only after grounding, intraworld causation, and totality selection are typed separately. Use a two-sorted modal model
\[
\mathfrak M=(W,\mathcal R,w_*,g,\{D_w,\mathsf C_w,\mathsf G_w,\sigma_w\}_{w\in W}),
\]
where \(g\) is present in every admissible world; \(D_w^C\subseteq D_w\setminus\{g\}\) contains numerically distinct derivative creatures; \(\mathsf C_w\) is an ordinary causal model over derivative variables; \(\mathsf G_w\) is a distinct grounding relation to creaturely existential facts; and \(\sigma_w\) specifies which admissible derivative totality \(T_w\) is realised.

Literal participatory creation requires: (i) \(\Box\operatorname{Ex}(g)\); (ii) creature–ground distinctness; (iii) asymmetric existential grounding by \(g\) with no reverse grounding; (iv) no identification of \(\mathsf G_w\) with a second event-cause in \(\mathsf C_w\); and (v) genuine derivative causal roles. This permits a precise **conservative-extension theorem**: any consistent finite structural causal model \((V,U,F,\mu)\) can be extended with a new grounding sort, existential facts, and \(g\)-grounding relations while retaining exactly the same observational and intervention distributions for every query in the original causal vocabulary. The result establishes logical compatibility of non-competitive dependence and ordinary secondary causation; it does not establish the truth of the grounding layer.

The remaining issue is selection. Let \(N_g\) be the necessary nature of the ground and \(\mathsf{Act}(T)\) the realisation of totality \(T\). The following modal result is exact:
\[
\Box N_g\ \land\ \Box(N_g\rightarrow\mathsf{Act}(T))
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Box\mathsf{Act}(T).
\]
Thus a necessary nature that necessarily and sufficiently fixes a totality makes that totality necessary. “Expression,” “identity,” or “self-knowledge” language does not evade the conclusion without qualifying a premise.

The resulting accounting trilemma is conditional but exhaustive under literal creation, at least two admissible totalities, and a demand for a stated full ground of the realised totality:

1. **C-Nec:** a necessary realisation rule fully fixes the totality; the result is modally necessary and must face modal-collapse, measure, and suffering costs.
2. **C-Cont:** a non-necessitated selector—fact, act, reason, relation, law, or co-ground—helps full-ground the realised totality; it must be characterised rather than renamed “free creation,” and it carries a selection primitive or simplicity/relation cost.
3. **ND:** literal creaturely distinctness is rejected; this avoids the selection problem only by taking on the separate debts of perspectival plurality, public physics, agency, and suffering.

This formal bridge does not prove creation. It removes a recurrent equivocation: no theory may claim real distinct creation while denying both a necessary realisation rule and every selector or co-ground.

### 4.2 Concurrence and agency

A careful concurrentist proposal separates existential grounding, intra-world causation, and ownership:

\[
G\;\text{grounds the existence and causal actuality of}\;(x,P_x)\text{ and whichever exercise occurs};
\qquad x\;\text{causes}\;e\;\text{through}\;P_x.
\]

This succeeds only if G is not a second proximate cause producing the same effect in the same manner as x. An optional **non-selective concurrence** thesis says that G sustains the creature, its powers, and whichever exercise occurs through them without an additional contrast-selecting act that picks \(e\) rather than \(e^*\). This is a compatibilist proposal, not a proof of libertarian sourcehood or a solution to the reception primitive.

A useful but modest **conservative-extension construction** supports logical compatibility. Given a consistent structural causal model \(\mathcal S=(V,U,F,\mu)\), add a separate grounding sort containing \(G\) and stipulate existential dependence of each represented bearer, power, and event-token on G while leaving \(U,F,\mu\) unchanged. Internal observational and intervention probabilities then remain exactly those of \(\mathcal S\). This demonstrates that a non-competitive grounding layer can be added without changing a finite causal model; it does not show that such a layer is metaphysically true, that every event needs a ground, or that creaturely freedom has been established.

The modal constraint is equally exact. If \(N\) is a complete sufficient condition in the ground for a creaturely result \(e\), and both \(\Box N\) and \(\Box(N\!\rightarrow\!e)\) hold, then \(\Box e\). A theory of necessary ground plus contingent creation must therefore deny or qualify at least one of: necessary complete selection, necessitating connection, the ground’s complete contrastive explanation, or the contingency of the result. C-Nec, C-Cont, and a determinate-necessary-expression model are distinct options; none is selected by simplicity or completeness alone.

Finite agency can be real as reasons-responsive control, deliberation, learning, inhibition, and repair under inherited biological and social conditions. It need not be ultimate self-creation. Conditioning by genes, development, trauma, social context, or neural state does not by itself prove moral fatalism; it does undermine simple appeals to ultimate self-origination. Nor does universal grounding alone prove that desert- or responsibility-based practices fail: that verdict depends on an additional theory of responsibility.

## 5. Awareness, structural description, and finite perspective

### 5.1 The intrinsic-nature argument

The strongest structural–experiential line of reasoning is modest:

1. Physics gives exceptionally successful relational, dynamical, and structural descriptions.
2. It remains philosophically disputed whether this exhausts the intrinsic nature of concrete reality.
3. Phenomenal experience is the clearest case of an intrinsic aspect known from within.
4. One may therefore hypothesize that the concrete base of physical structure is experience-involving, dual-aspect, neutral-with-experiential-potential, or otherwise non-exhausted by third-person structure.

The next move—to pure awareness as the intrinsic nature of the ultimate ground—requires additional claims. In the Point–Sphere version they are: pure structure needs a categorical realiser; immediate acquaintance gives phenomenal awareness a methodological advantage over an opaque categorical posit; and a unified awareness base avoids hard emergence or micro-subject combination. These are useful conditional bridge premises, not results:

- structural realism may deny that a further intrinsic realiser is needed;
- a neutral or Russellian physicalist can posit a non-phenomenal or non-identified intrinsic base;
- micropsychism trades hard emergence for combination; cosmopsychism trades it for decombination;
- a simple awareness-ground trades these for a problem of differentiation and individuation.

The framework therefore **prefers** rather than proves pure awareness. A phenomenal-concept or a posteriori identity strategy may explain why physical and phenomenal concepts have different cognitive roles, but it does not automatically answer why physical processes are felt. Conversely, the felt character of pain is serious pressure on reductive explanation, not a knockdown argument against every physicalist account.

### 5.2 Four distinct claims about experience

Do not collapse these:

1. Human experience is a real first-person datum.
2. Some structural or functional organisation is associated with reports and capacities of experience.
3. All concrete physical modes have an experiential pole.
4. Pure awareness is the intrinsic nature of the ultimate ground.

Claims 3 and 4 are metaphysical hypotheses. They are not supplied by claim 1, by quantum mechanics, or by information theory.

### 5.2.1 Intrinsic-nature bridge: what structure can and cannot entail

Let \(T_{\rm str}\) be a consistent theory expressed solely in structural and causal vocabulary—relations, functions, quantities, state spaces, dynamics, symmetries, and intervention rules—and let \(\Phi\) be a phenomenal assignment. If \(T_{\rm str}\) contains no axiom constraining \(\Phi\), then it entails no non-trivial phenomenal claim: any structural model \(M\) has both an expansion assigning no phenomenal organisation and an expansion assigning some phenomenal organisation, with the same structural reduct. A functional identity, psychophysical law, neutral realiser, cosmopsychist participation principle, or illusionist deflation is therefore a further bridge claim, not a consequence of the original structural theory.

Structural twins sharpen the same point. If two candidate systems are isomorphic in the relevant causal, intervention, boundary, and record structure, a theory must choose: organisationally matching phenomenal profiles; an additional non-structural individuator; or no determinate phenomenal verdict. Differing phenomenal verdicts for structural twins cannot be obtained free of an additional posit.

The finite-perspective bridge can now be layered rather than conflated. Let \(\operatorname{MaxPC}_\Theta\) be the maximal non-vacuous D/I/S/L/P candidates. **Carrier necessity** says a phenomenal centre, if present in the target domain, has such a carrier. **Carrier sufficiency** says every such carrier is a phenomenal centre; together these form the strong \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) hypothesis. Organisational invariance, an explicit overlap/exclusion rule, and no unmodelled physical surplus are further required. This gives a precise carrier model, not a derivation of phenomenality.

The awareness-ground is consequently an abductive competitor, not a conclusion of the carrier theorem. It gains only if it preserves phenomenal data, avoids a brute psychophysical law or micro-subject combination, provides a workable decombination account, respects causal closure, and does so with no greater unexplained cost than functional-identity, neutral/Russellian, or illusionist rivals. The EΘ protocol tests the carrier bridge in restricted domains; it does not confirm the awareness-ground.

### 5.3 Decombination is the decisive subject problem

A global awareness-ground does not by itself explain finite, bounded, ignorant subjects. The required map must state:

- what individuates one perspective from another;
- what determines its accessible record and memory boundary;
- what preserves its unity through temporal change;
- why local informational restriction is accompanied by a perspective rather than merely a subsystem description; and
- how finite perspectives are real creatures under P, or conventionally valid loci of action under ND.

A restriction of information is not yet a subject. A model of domain formation is not yet a model of subject formation. A structural criterion of subject formation is not yet a derivation of phenomenality.

### 5.4 Personhood, goodness, and survival

Awareness does not entail self-knowledge, intellect, intentionality, will, moral responsiveness, personhood, or benevolence. Nor does it entail that death dissolves a finite perspective, restores omniscience, or gives an individual personal survival. Such claims require independent philosophical, theological, or empirical support.

Similarly, ontological sourcehood is distinct from **V0**, the further thesis that actuality or perfection is intrinsically metaphysically good. V0 is compatible with the recognition that severe phenomenal agony is bad-for its subject. Moral benevolence requires still more: evaluative intellect, practical orientation, non-arbitrary concern for finite welfare, and an adequate account of actual suffering.

## 6. Point, sphere, polarity, plenitude, defect

### 6.1 The point–sphere image: retain it as a constraint, not a derivation

The **point** may serve as a limiting symbol for non-composite, nonspatial, non-temporal ground; the **sphere** for articulated derivative structure, relation, boundary, and local perspective. The centre/sphere image is useful because a centre need not be one more point on a surface, and because it foregrounds unity, boundary, and localisation.

It must not be reified. A mathematical sphere presupposes a space, dimension, metric, and transformation rule. A zero-radius sphere does not generate a nonzero sphere without additional structure. The imagery therefore does not derive spacetime, field content, gauge groups, quantisation, a standard-model parameter, or consciousness.

Its proper role is a research constraint: formalise a non-temporal relation from one non-independent limit condition to a hierarchy of derivative structures without making the ground a composite physical node or making derivative reality a second self-subsistent substance.

### 6.2 Polarity and grades

Transcendental contrasts—simple/complex, unlimited/finite, timeless/temporal, unity/multiplicity, awareness/local perspective—describe a target relation between ground and derivatives. Constitutive contrasts—symmetry/symmetry-breaking, possibility/record, attraction/repulsion, order/entropy gradient, inside/outside—may organise physics-facing models.

No generic polarity law has been established. Each proposed polarity must be represented by a particular invariant, duality, involution, adjunction, order reversal, or symmetry-breaking operation, with an interpretation justified at that level. Likewise, a grade must be an object of a specified category, partially ordered class, or model hierarchy, rather than a poetic rank.

### 6.3 Plenitude

Plenitude is potentially useful because it converts the question “why this arbitrary admissible structure?” into a landscape question. It is not a free explanatory gain. It needs:

1. an **admissibility condition** distinguishing coherent, compossible, realisable structures from contradictions and arbitrary descriptions;
2. a **realisation principle** explaining why admissibility entails actuality rather than bare possibility;
3. a **measure** for typicality, observer selection, and probability.

Without these, “all possibilities exist” is too unconstrained to explain fine-tuning, probability, or constants. With them, it becomes a demanding programme that must still explain which structures are mutually compossible and how local observations receive weights. Everettian branching is not automatically the actuality of every mathematical structure.

One limited no-go result is worth retaining: no countably additive probability measure on a **countably infinite** admissible class can be invariant under every permutation while assigning every singleton the same positive weight. This rules out a naive uniform atomistic typicality measure. It does not rule out every landscape measure: uncountable/non-atomic spaces, measures tied to nontrivial geometry or dynamics, finite classes, or explicitly non-uniform measures require separate assessment. Thus it prices a measure principle rather than refuting plenitude in general. In domains where an exchangeability theorem genuinely applies, symmetry may represent a distribution as a mixture over generative laws; that relocates, rather than removes, the measure choice to law-space and cannot be assumed for an unrestricted landscape.

### 6.4 Privation and suffering

Finitude may make limitation, incompatibility, failure, loss, and privation structurally possible. It does not show that every actual injury, disease, trauma, predation, injustice, or apparent waste is necessary, deserved, educational, or morally insignificant. Privation theory classifies evil as lack of a due good rather than a rival substance; it does not explain the positive causal conditions of pain or its particular distribution.

A plenitude thesis can intensify rather than solve the ethical burden: if every admissible finite mode is realised, severe harms may occur somewhere by principle. That does not make the harm good for its sufferer. The appropriate practical conclusion is compassion, prevention, repair, and accountability—not metaphysical dismissal.

## 7. Physics and quantum theory: a constrained meta-level programme

### 7.1 Four layers

A disciplined framework separates:

1. **Ground layer:** the proposed non-derivative source, not a quantum state or a physical object in Hilbert space.
2. **Landscape layer:** admissible grades/world-structures plus any realisation and measure principles.
3. **Physical layer:** a particular grade’s spacetime, quantum fields, particles, thermodynamics, biology, and cosmology.
4. **Perspective layer:** records, local access, memory, agency, and, if the psychophysical bridge is correct, experiential interiors.

A theory proper to one layer cannot automatically answer another layer’s question. Quantum mechanics does not establish the ground; a first-person datum does not create a special collapse; a metaphysical term does not replace a physical model.

### 7.2 Quantum theory: an interpretative fit map, not a synthesis

The programme does not derive Hilbert-space quantum mechanics, select a correct quantum interpretation, or merge incompatible interpretations into one theory. The ground is not a quantum system; point-to-sphere imagery does not generate the Born rule; and plenitude is not Everettian branching.

Nor can familiar representation theorems be promoted into that derivation. GNS constructs a Hilbert-space representation **given** a \(C^*\)-algebra and state; the original projection-valued Gleason theorem has its familiar Hilbert-space dimension restriction (the qubit case requires a strengthened measurement/POVM-style assumption for analogous results), and in every case it constrains probability measures only **given** additivity and domain assumptions; normal states on general von Neumann algebras need not be represented by a trace-class density operator in the simple type-I manner; and a Lüders update requires an appropriate measurement/instrument model. Modular flow is not automatically subjective time, while entanglement-equilibrium/Jacobson-style arguments assume highly specific semiclassical, horizon, and equilibrium conditions rather than deriving a Lorentzian spacetime from candidate perspectives. These results are valuable tools, not recovery theorems for the framework.

Its legitimate role at this stage is an **interpretative fit map**. Relational facts may be congenial to a perspective-sensitive ontology; decoherence and environmental records may model stable grade-level structure; Everettian branching may be an analogy for one kind of derivative plurality; QBist updating may illuminate the perspective layer if a shared world is retained; and transactional or boundary-value formalisms may be comparison cases for global consistency. These are resonances, not derivations.

Bohmian, objective-collapse, superdeterministic, consistent-histories, and nonequilibrium proposals remain distinct physical hypotheses with their own ontology, probability, relativity, and empirical obligations. Any implementation must preserve no-signalling, Bell data, relativistic constraints or an empirically tested replacement, thermodynamic consistency, and the Born rule or a tested alternative. Consciousness has no privileged collapse-causing role merely because the programme treats experience as an intrinsic candidate.

Relational quantum mechanics can motivate careful treatment of relative facts; QBism can highlight agent-relative probabilities; Everett can focus attention on unitary branching and probability; and consistent-histories and quantum-Darwinism models can illuminate stable records. None is an ingredient to stack indiscriminately. The framework may compare their resonances but must not merge incompatible probability and ontology rules by metaphor.

### 7.2.1 Exact finite record recovery

A controlled physical module can nevertheless be recovered inside standard finite quantum theory. Let
\[
\mathcal H=\mathcal H_S\otimes\mathcal H_A\otimes\bigotimes_{k=1}^{N}\mathcal H_{E_k}
\]
and specify a pointer-basis premeasurement interaction
\[
|i\rangle_S|0\rangle_A\bigotimes_k|0\rangle_{E_k}
\longmapsto
|i\rangle_S|i\rangle_A\bigotimes_k|e_i^{(k)}\rangle_{E_k}.
\]
For \(|\psi\rangle_S=\sum_i c_i|i\rangle_S\), the reduced system–apparatus state has environmental overlap factors
\[
\Gamma_{ij}=\prod_k\langle e_j^{(k)}|e_i^{(k)}\rangle,
\qquad
\rho_{SA}=\sum_{i,j}c_ic_j^*\Gamma_{ij}|i,i\rangle\langle j,j|.
\]
Writing \(\rho_{\rm rec}=\sum_i|c_i|^2|i,i\rangle\langle i,i|\), the exact bound
\[
\|\rho_{SA}-\rho_{\rm rec}\|_1
\le\sum_{i\ne j}|c_i|\,|c_j|\,|\Gamma_{ij}|
\]
shows how environmental distinguishability quantitatively produces a stable local record; mutual orthogonality of the global record states makes the equality exact.

If an individual fragment satisfies \(\langle e_j^{(k)}|e_i^{(k)}\rangle=\delta_{ij}\), then standard Born-rule statistics give
\[
\Pr(A=i,E_k=j)=\delta_{ij}|c_i|^2.
\]
Thus apparatus and independently accessible environment fragments agree on a redundant record label. Conditional on an accessible apparatus projector, the system is assigned the relative state \(|i\rangle\langle i|\), without inserting a consciousness-triggered physical collapse. Subsequent record stability can be measured by a declared apparatus channel \(\Lambda_A\) and error \(\epsilon_{\rm stab}=\max_i[1-\operatorname{Tr}(P_i^A\Lambda_A(P_i^A))]\).

This is a genuine finite-sector recovery of decoherence-stable, publicly comparable relative records. It assumes the Hilbert-space formalism, specified interaction and pointer basis, partial trace, and the Born rule; it does not derive any of them. It gives the perspective programme a physical record variable for \(B\) or \(M\), but a stable redundant register is not thereby a D/I/S/L/P candidate or a phenomenal subject. The module should be tested by measuring overlap-controlled visibility suppression, fragment agreement, and record stability under a preregistered unitary model; no result of it confirms an awareness-ground or selects a quantum interpretation.

### 7.3 Probability and constants

Any branch or plenitude framework owes a probability rule. It must derive Born weights, supply a measure that reduces to them in our grade, or formulate an empirically distinguishable alternative. Counting branches, weighting complexity, and using observer selection are not interchangeable.

Likewise, symmetry and parsimony do not derive observed dimensionless constants. A genuine derivation needs a sharply restricted mathematical family, a selection theorem, recovery of effective low-energy physics, numerical outputs or exact relations, and a route to data that is not post hoc fitting.

## 8. Formal research programme

### 8.1 Minimal architecture

Let \(\mathcal{G}\) be a category, higher category, or partially ordered class of derivative grades; let \(g\in\mathcal{G}\). The ordering represents structural refinement or ontological dependence, not earlier/later time.

For a physically articulated grade, an algebraic quantum field theory is one possible structural resource:

\[
\mathsf{A}_g:\mathsf{Loc}_g\rightarrow\mathsf{Alg}_g.
\]

This can assign local regions or operational contexts observable algebras. It gives a formal home for locality, covariance, states, interactions, and records. It does **not** by itself derive spacetime, awareness, or the ground.

A structural candidate perspective may be represented as

\[
p=(\mathsf{A}_{\mathrm{local}},\rho,\mathcal{R},\partial p),
\]

where \(\rho\) is a state, \(\mathcal{R}\) a record structure, and \(\partial p\) an information boundary. A channel

\[
\Phi_{ab}:\mathsf{A}_a\otimes\mathsf{A}_b\rightarrow\mathsf{A}_{ab}
\]

may yield a relative fact only when it produces a robust record. The theory must specify cross-perspective compatibility rather than assume it.

The framework’s central psychophysical hypothesis can be written schematically as

\[
\mathsf E:\mathsf{Persp}\rightarrow\mathsf{Phen}.
\]

This notation names the hard problem. It does not solve it. Any credible \(\mathsf E\) must state its domain, codomain, invariance conditions, treatment of composition and decomposition, memory continuity, and relation to no-signalling. It must also explain why structurally isomorphic systems do or do not have matching phenomenal organisation.

### 8.2 Constrained graph-field model: a domain mechanism, not a subject proof

Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite connected graph, \(L\) its Laplacian, and field values \(\Psi_i\in\mathbb R^d\). On a constrained zero-mean subspace, let

\[
P=I-\frac1N\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^\top,
\qquad \sum_{i\in V}\Psi_i=0.
\]

A mathematically well-posed toy energy is

\[
F[\Psi]=\sum_{i\in V}\frac g4(\lVert\Psi_i\rVert^2-a)^2
+\frac J2\Psi^\top L\Psi+U_{\rm aniso}[\Psi],
\qquad g,a,J>0.
\]

It gives a finite-graph Gibbs density \(p_\beta(\Psi)\propto e^{-\beta F[\Psi]}\) and a mean-preserving stochastic dynamics

\[
d\Psi=-P\nabla F(\Psi)\,dt+\sqrt{2/\beta}\,P\,dW.
\]

For \(d>1\), a discrete phase vocabulary requires an explicit anisotropy term; an unmodified Mexican-hat potential has a continuous vacuum manifold. On the zero-mean subspace, if the Fiedler eigenvalue \(\lambda_2\) is simple, the zero solution has the local quadratic operator

\[
H=JL-gaI,
\qquad J_c=\frac{ga}{\lambda_2}.
\]

Near the first local instability, a one-mode Lyapunov–Schmidt approximation gives

\[
c^2=\frac{ga-J\lambda_2}{g\sum_i(v_i^{(2)})^4}+O\!\left((ga-J\lambda_2)^2\right).
\]

This is a leading-order local bifurcation formula, not generally an exact branch solution. It presumes a simple \(\lambda_2\); on many symmetric or vertex-transitive graphs that eigenvalue is degenerate, so the unstable space is the entire \(\lambda_2\)-eigenspace and nonlinear/anisotropy terms select the pattern. For \(d>1\), a discrete phase vocabulary likewise requires a specified anisotropy; the scalar \(d=1\) model is the cleanest two-well proving ground, but nonuniform minima are still only structural patterns.

This establishes at most local deterministic bifurcation and possible metastable domains. It does not establish subjects, literal division of an awareness-ground, a Markov blanket, a unified consciousness, or a neural correlate.

Two cautions are indispensable:

- the negative mass term \(ga\) generates local amplitude, whereas \(J\) controls spatial coherence, the cost of boundaries, and which mode first destabilises;
- a hard global zero-mean constraint introduces nonlocal dependence. Exact graph-separation conditional independence holds for an **unconstrained** positive nearest-neighbour Markov field, not automatically for the constrained measure. For an additive constraint and a factorising unconstrained law, conditioning additionally on a suitable interior aggregate (such as the interior sum) can restore a modified factorisation, but it changes the conditioning question and must be stated explicitly. It does not license the original local-screening claim. For continuous fields, proposed information-leakage bounds also need a discretisation or differential-entropy treatment; a bare finite “bit bound” is not automatic.

A further statistical-mechanical stress test is conditional: for finite-range models with continuous \(O(d)\)-type symmetry in spectral dimension at most two, Mermin–Wagner-style infrared arguments obstruct ordinary finite-temperature long-range orientation order. Discrete anisotropy changes that universality class, and long-range couplings or nonstandard constraint terms can change the conclusion. This supplies a model-specific phase-diagram constraint, not a theorem that every physical substrate for consciousness must have a particular dimension or phase.

### 8.3 Modest recovery in a convex sector

The most defensible recovery result is deliberately limited. For a finite connected graph with

\[
F_0(\psi)=\tfrac12\psi^\top(m^2I+JL)\psi,
\qquad m^2,J>0,
\]

the Gibbs law is Gaussian with covariance \(\beta^{-1}(m^2I+JL)^{-1}\), and the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck dynamics with the corresponding drift has that invariant distribution. In this **unconstrained massive** sector, the positive nearest-neighbour Gibbs field has the usual graph-separation Markov property. Under standard lattice scaling, its covariance approaches that of a massive Euclidean Gaussian free field in an appropriate continuum limit.

This positivity condition is not cosmetic. \(Q=JL-gaI\) has a negative uniform eigenvalue on the full graph when \(ga>0\), so \(Q^{-1}\) cannot be called an unconstrained Gaussian covariance or used to establish an unconstrained conditional-independence claim. A rank-one algebraic correction can represent an inverse restricted to a stated subspace under additional invertibility assumptions, but it does not create the missing full-space probability law or prove a universal \(\Theta(N^{-1})\) information leakage rate. Any constrained Gaussian calculation must begin with a positive restricted quadratic form, specify the conditioning convention, and prove its finite-volume and scaling estimates. Likewise, a continuum-limit assertion must fix the physical volume, lattice Laplacian normalisation, field rescaling, mass/coupling dimensions, boundary conditions, and mode of convergence; a displayed parameter scaling alone is not a recovery theorem.

This is a genuine recovery of known statistical-field structure and a useful testbed for simulations. It is not Lorentzian QFT, the Standard Model, a derivation of the Born rule, or a theory of consciousness. Claims about \(\phi^4\) universality beyond the Gaussian sector require a specified dimension, renormalisation scheme, scaling limit, and treatment of the zero-mode constraint.

### 8.4 Candidate-perspective criteria

For a candidate interior \(A\subset V\), choose a boundary \(B\) and exterior \(R=V\setminus(A\cup B)\), with B separating A from R in the ordinary interaction graph. A domain is a **candidate perspective**, not an established subject, only if all of the following independently preregistered tests are met:

1. **D — interventional dissociation/screening:** direct interventions on the exterior have a bounded residual effect on the interior once the relevant boundary, interior history, and any explicit global constraint variables are fixed. Passive conditional mutual information alone is insufficient.
2. **I — causal integration:** cutting causal influences across every non-trivial partition of A produces a preregistered divergence from the intact interventional kernel. This should be compared with degree-, constraint-, and global-mode-matched nulls.
3. **S — predictive self-model:** a physically realised internal state improves a fixed-complexity prediction of future interior/boundary dynamics over a matched null.
4. **L — causal use:** disrupting that internal state by a targeted lesion, shuffle, or intervention reduces the relevant predictive, control, or viability performance. Prediction without causal use is not self-model evidence.
5. **P — robust persistence/closure:** the interior–boundary organisation persists through a preregistered perturbation class and direct exterior influence is bounded except through the specified boundary and inputs.

The thresholds, estimator family, intervention distributions, temporal grain, nulls, and mapping to empirical observables are substantive modelling choices. The set D/I/S/L/P may be a useful **necessary operational profile only conditional on a further naturalistic perspective theory**; it is not entailed merely by saying that a psychophysical bridge respects structure. Infants, animals, atypical minds, and artificial systems are particularly important stress tests for whether self-modelling or a chosen persistence measure is actually necessary.

Even success on D/I/S/L/P establishes structured autonomy, not phenomenal consciousness. The further bridge \(E_\theta\)—that phenomenal subjects correspond to suitably maximal candidates under a stated threshold vector \(\theta\)—must be separately defended and can at most be empirically corroborated or disconfirmed, not derived from the structural criterion.

For a hard constraint such as \(\mathbf 1^\top\Psi=0\), an admissible exterior intervention must additionally name a **constraint-preserving compensation map**: which complement region is adjusted, by what rule, and whether the adjustment is boundary-channel or spatially distributed. Otherwise an intervention on R either leaves the support of the model or determines compensating variables by fiat, making a local-screening claim ill-posed. This is a design requirement, not a proof that the constrained model has exact screening. A hard constraint does not make conditional mutual information simply “undefined” in every formulation: it must be defined measure-theoretically for the specified constrained law and conditioning \(\sigma\)-algebras, and can be degenerate, infinite, or estimator-dependent. It cannot be assumed to deliver ordinary local Markov screening.

Any structural \(E_\theta\) should also state an **isomorphism/equivariance condition**. If it is invariant under all relevant automorphisms, it cannot distinguish structural twins; if it breaks that invariance, the symmetry-breaking individuator is an additional substantive posit. A self-maintenance or homeostatic measure can be a valuable candidate feature for testing passive structures, organisms, and autonomous artificial systems, but it is not yet an established necessary condition of consciousness and may not be used to exclude simulations by definition.

A more elaborate algebraic perspective functional is not thereby ready-made. Two unital algebras cannot literally have empty intersection (they share the identity); generic local algebras need not supply the tensor-product factorisation required by a product-state relative entropy or a tripartite conditional mutual information; and Type-III settings do not in general provide the ordinary trace or discrete modular-Hamiltonian eigenvalue assumed by several proposed factors and thresholds. A proposed self-maintenance rate also needs an intervention-defined counterfactual and a common decay observable. These can be repaired in a specified finite-dimensional/open-system model or an appropriate algebraic framework, but a product of named quantities and a stipulated \(\Theta_c\) is not a theorem that rocks, simulations, organisms, or neural networks have the listed phenomenal statuses.

### 8.5 Interventional perspective bridge: a finite-model strengthening

A precise restricted implementation starts with a finite controlled causal model at a declared temporal grain,
\[
X_t=(Y_t,M_t,B_t,R_t),\qquad A_t=(Y_t,M_t),
\]
where \(A\) is the candidate interior, \(M\) a physically realised proposed model state, \(B\) its declared interface, \(R\) a non-empty exterior relative to the experimental universe, and \(\Gamma\) any hard constraint. Let \(\mathfrak I_R^\Gamma\) be the preregistered constraint-respecting intervention family. Every member must state the mechanism changed, the compensation required by \(\Gamma\), the support on which its kernel is defined, and whether compensation reaches \(A\) only through \(B\) or declared inputs \(U\).

A boundary cannot be allowed to absorb every causal path by definition. For an operational boundary claim, specify a boundary-complexity/size budget, a minimum external comparison domain, and matched boundary/global-mode nulls. A global candidate with no remaining \(R\) may be a legitimate system description, but its screening score is vacuous and cannot by itself individuate a local perspective. Candidate selection, graph construction, temporal scale, overlap/tie policy, and the admissible-superset relation must all be locked before testing.

For admissible \(\iota\), write
\[
K_A^\iota(a'\mid a,b,u)
=\Pr^\iota(A_{t+1}=a'\mid A_t=a,B_t=b,U_t=u),
\]
and define the one-step screening defect
\[
\operatorname{Scr}_1(A\mid B)=
\sup_{\iota,\iota'\in\mathfrak I_R^\Gamma}
\sup_{(a,b,u)\in\mathcal C}
\left\|K_A^\iota(\cdot\mid a,b,u)-K_A^{\iota'}(\cdot\mid a,b,u)\right\|_{\rm TV},
\]
where \(\mathcal C\) is a preregistered context set containing all relevant reachable states. This supplies a finite, intervention-sensitive version of D.

**Finite-horizon screening lemma.** Suppose that, for every admissible intervention, the **induced interior process** is Markov conditional on a common controlled boundary/input history; that all contexts visited on the compared horizons remain in \(\mathcal C\); and that the two conditional trajectory laws are defined on that common history. If \(\operatorname{Scr}_1\le\varepsilon\), then for every initial \(a_t\) and horizon \(h\),
\[
\left\|\mathcal L^\iota(A_{t+1:t+h}\mid a_t,\bar b,\bar u)
-\mathcal L^{\iota'}(A_{t+1:t+h}\mid a_t,\bar b,\bar u)\right\|_{\rm TV}
\le \min\{1,h\varepsilon\}.
\]
The proof is the usual telescoping replacement of conditional kernels, using total-variation contraction. This is a conditional operational-autonomy result, not an unconditional claim that exterior changes do not alter the interior; the boundary and input trajectory are explicitly held fixed.

For each non-trivial bipartition \(A=A_1\sqcup A_2\), let \(\mathcal Q_\pi\) contain the **partition-local product kernels**
\[
Q_1(a_1'\mid a_1,b,u)Q_2(a_2'\mid a_2,b,u),
\]
and define
\[
\mathcal J_\pi(A)=\inf_{Q\in\mathcal Q_\pi}
\mathbb E_\nu\!\left[D_{\rm KL}\bigl(K_A(\cdot\mid A_t,B_t,U_t)\,\|\,Q(\cdot\mid A_t,B_t,U_t)\bigr)\right],
\qquad
\operatorname{Int}(A)=\min_\pi\mathcal J_\pi(A).
\]
In a finite model, with the tested contexts supported by \(\nu\), compactness of the product-kernel family and the equality condition for relative entropy give
\[
\mathcal J_\pi(A)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
K_A=Q_1Q_2\quad\nu\text{-almost surely for some }Q_1Q_2\in\mathcal Q_\pi.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{Int}(A)>0\) exactly excludes that specified kind of boundary-conditioned, partition-local decomposition. It does not exclude every coarser, latent-variable, longer-memory, or differently factorised explanation, and it is not a result about phenomenal unity.

S, L, and P can be made comparably exact at this scale. Require \(M\) to be a proper physical component or fixed coarse-graining of \(A\), to meet a preregistered compression budget, and to provide held-out predictive sufficiency for relevant future \((Y,B)\) beyond capacity- and autocorrelation-matched nulls. Require a viability-preserving intervention on \(M\) to alter the relevant prediction, control, or persistence target. Require a declared organisational macrostate to persist or recover across a stated perturbation class and horizon. Estimating any of these from observational neural or behavioural data additionally requires a causal-identification argument; association or decoding alone is insufficient.

A **finite-model \(\Theta\)-candidate perspective** is therefore a maximal non-vacuous candidate satisfying the budgeted D, I, S, L, and P tests at a fixed scale. It is an operational profile of bounded, integrated, model-using organisation. The separate bridge remains
\[
\mathbf E_\Theta:\qquad
\text{phenomenal centres are exactly the maximal }\Theta\text{-candidate perspectives}.
\]
This is a strong psychophysical postulate. A prospective commissurotomy study may test one consequence—two disjoint maxima during independently sustained hemispheric control, versus one in matched intact and non-commissural pathology controls—only after calibration, causal/graph construction, and thresholds are locked on an independent dataset. A failure revises the operational criterion or \(\mathbf E_\Theta\); success would support neither an awareness-ground nor a general theory of consciousness by itself.

## 9. Empirical programme and failure conditions

The programme has three levels that must not be confused.

| Level | Claim | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| I | Metaphysical interpretation: ground, dependence, structural–experiential hypothesis | Coherence, explanatory comparison, and explicit costs. |
| II | Formal architecture linking grades, records, perspectives, and possible phenomenal organisation | Definitions, proofs, recovery results, parameter constraints, and internal consistency. |
| III | Empirical theory with novel consequences | Quantitative, risky predictions that can fail and compete with alternatives. |

### 9.1 Concrete tracks

- **Relational record theory:** construct a precise model of interaction-generated stable records, cross-perspective agreement, decoherence, and probability. It must not use special consciousness-triggered collapse.
- **Structure–phenomenology research:** preregister D/I/S/L/P measures, boundary control, memory, and converging report-independent behavioural/neuronal markers; compare across anaesthesia, sleep, ketamine, split-brain cases, dissociation, and disorders of consciousness. A credible test should lock thresholds on a calibration cohort and assess held-out centres and drug classes against specified complexity/connectivity comparators. A target such as out-of-distribution AUC or improvement over a comparator is a success criterion chosen before data, not a prediction already licensed by the metaphysics.
- **Individuation tests:** the hypothesis that a complete callosotomy yields two maximal candidates, while typical unified wakefulness yields one, is a useful discriminating conjecture. It must be tested against the unsettled empirical interpretation of split-brain evidence; failure would revise the operational criterion, not directly refute grounding or awareness metaphysics.
- **Explicit experiential coupling:** if the framework posits a new physical effect, define a parameter \(\lambda\), a dimensionally coherent prospective law such as \(\Delta O=\lambda f(I(p),\text{controls})\), and an independent rationale for every exponent and calibration constant. A quoted visibility percentage or threshold is not a framework prediction if those numbers were selected only to make an experiment look decisive. A null result can falsify a preregistered positive-\(\lambda\) bridge over its tested range; it cannot falsify the wider metaphysics when \(\lambda=0\) remains allowed. If \(\lambda=0\), a dual-aspect thesis may remain metaphysically viable but loses this empirical route.
- **Landscape and constants:** define admissibility, a measure, observer selection, and prospective distributions for dimensionless constants or cosmological observables.
- **Global-constraint or nonequilibrium alternatives:** write a causal graph, probability measure, and testable deviation. “Global necessity” is not a physical model without these.

### 9.1.1 EΘ empirical bridge protocol

The candidate-perspective criterion now has a staged preregistration route rather than a generic call for “more data.” The target is a restricted split-architecture consequence of \(\mathbf E_\Theta\): after all analytic choices are locked independently of consciousness labels, verified complete commissurotomy during independently sustained hemispheric control should yield two disjoint maximal non-vacuous candidates, while matched intact and non-commissural pathology controls yield one at the same scale.

The protocol has three gates. **Tier 0** uses synthetic state-space systems with known one-module, two-module, global-constraint, and passive-memory architectures; the implementation must recover the known architecture and control false positives before biological interpretation. **Tier 1** uses a causal-calibration dataset with valid perturbations to establish D and L, which cannot be identified from passive connectivity or decoding alone. **Tier 2** is the held-out commissurotomy/control comparison. Without valid interventions, Tier 2 is only an observational projection of the full D/I/S/L/P criterion and must be labelled as such.

The finite implementation locks \(X_t=(Y_t,M_t,B_t,R_t)\), graph construction, temporal grain, candidate-size range, non-empty external comparison domain, boundary-complexity budget, constraint-preserving compensation map, nulls, and tie/overlap rule. D is estimated by a common-support, intervention-regime kernel divergence; I by held-out loss or KL against every enumerated partition-local product transition kernel; S by capacity-matched predictive sufficiency; L by randomised intervention on \(M\) relative to sham and matched controls; and P by recovery of a declared organisational macrostate under a stated perturbation class. A candidate must pass all five tests.

The primary endpoint is the preregistered candidate-count contrast between commissurotomy and both comparison groups; secondary endpoints require lateralised behavioural alignment and causal-calibration performance. Failure occurs if synthetic architecture is not recovered, D/L are not identified, target-label tuning is required, controls reproduce the two-candidate pattern, candidates lack model use or persistence, or results are preprocessing-unstable. Such failure revises the operational bridge or \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) in the tested domain; it does not directly decide global grounding or awareness metaphysics.

### 9.2 What would count against the programme

Relevant failures include inability to formulate a coherent individuation and phenomenal-unity account; failure to recover Born probabilities; violation of no-signalling, covariance, or established empirical regimes; an arbitrary landscape measure; no parameter reduction or novel prediction after sustained formal work; or a rival framework with fewer primitives and equal or greater explanatory and empirical success.

A metaphysical framework that leaves all observations equally expected may remain meaningful, but it has not thereby become empirically confirmed physics.

## 10. Ethics, theology, and interpretive guardrails

### 10.1 Finite ethics is not dissolved by ultimate metaphysics

The ethical bridge remains conditional and autonomous in the relevant practical sense. If actual welfare subjects and capable agents stand in relevant nexuses; if severe negative valence is objectively bad-for its subject in that respect; and if good-based reasons, interpersonal non-nullity, authority/non-bypass, duty-source, claim, and conflict principles are sound, then there are pro tanto reasons, some directed duties, and strong constraints against serious unjustified harm and wrongful instrumentalisation.

No claim about one ground, non-duality, plenitude, privation, or universal sustenance itself establishes those axioms. A model in which all the metaphysical claims hold while reasons remain desire-indexed is enough to show this **non-entailment**; it does not prove practical autonomy or immunise ethical premises from evidence. **CLND-N** is the further practical constraint: an ultimate-level description alone cannot defeat finite welfare, consent, or accountability. **CLND-E** is the epistemic constraint: ordinary practical warrant begins with valence, vulnerability, agency, testimony, and reflection rather than a prior proof of cosmic benevolence. Metaphysical evidence can still bear on the credibility of axiological or practical claims; the safeguard is defeasible practical modularity, not epistemic immunity.

The experience of longing for truth, beauty, wholeness, or perfection is relevant but non-decisive. A participatory view can understand it as finite orientation toward fuller participation in the good; natural selection, culture, imagination, and social aspiration can also help explain such desires. A desire is not a belief whose object must exist. It becomes an argument only with a defended natural-teleology premise linking constitutive rational orientation to a real fulfilment.

### 10.2 Sourcehood, metaphysical Good, and benevolence

Keep three levels distinct:

1. **Sourcehood:** G grounds finite actuality and the capacities in which creaturely goods can be realised.
2. **Metaphysical Good:** V0 says that actuality/perfection as such is intrinsically good in a classical sense.
3. **Moral benevolence:** G possesses evaluative intellect, practical orientation, non-arbitrary concern for finite welfare, and an adequate reason for allowing the actual scale and distribution of suffering.

The move from 1 to 2 is not automatic; the move from 2 to 3 is still less so. A privation account does not make felt agony good. A necessary-plenitude proposal raises, rather than settles, the burden of suffering. If a strong subject-centred benevolence principle is adopted, the remaining options include weakening that principle, positing trans-temporal compensation, or invoking inaccessible goods; none follows from the framework, and unlimited skeptical appeals threaten ordinary harm recognition.

### 10.3 Theology, scripture, and symbolism

The framework can resonate with divine unity, continuous dependence, graded being, creation, light, and intelligibility. It does not identify the ground with a photon, turn a null interval into a “photon perspective,” derive doctrine from geometry, or establish that nested grades are scriptural heavens. Religious texts may be compatible with parts of the framework while remaining insufficient to settle technical claims about necessary creation, plenitude, simplicity, or quantum theory.

Gödel, simulation language, Langlands, infinity, and light may guide analogy or research questions. None is a shortcut from mathematics or physics to an awareness-ground. In particular, simulations must not reintroduce an external computer and material substrate; Langlands is a specific mathematical-physics correspondence, not a general grammar of consciousness; and incompleteness theorems are warnings about formal closure under specific conditions, not proofs of metaphysical ineffability.

## 11. Priority sequence

1. **Publish a premise ledger.** Mark each claim empirical, logical/mathematical, metaphysical, theological, analogical, or derived.
2. **Formalise the grounding argument.** Test it in a grounding/modal logic against bare brute, infinite, circular, necessary-physical, plural-root, neutral, and nondual rival models.
3. **Choose or compare P and ND explicitly.** Do not call their conflict a harmless difference of language.
4. **State the modal bridge.** Defend C-Nec, C-Cont, or another stated alternative; do not infer plenitude from completeness.
5. **Specify the perspective criterion.** Establish whether D/I/S/L/P are merely operational correlates or candidates for a principled psychophysical bridge; model global constraints explicitly rather than assuming local screening.
6. **Recover a modest known physical result.** A lattice/free-field sector, AQFT example, or exact relational-record model is a better target than a premature theory of everything; state every dimension, scaling, and renormalisation assumption.
7. **Pre-register a discriminating empirical claim.** If no such claim is available, describe the work as metaphysical interpretation and formal exploration rather than new physics.
8. **Conduct a focused literature review.** Compare cosmopsychism, Russellian monism, divine simplicity, emanation and creation, grounding, AQFT, interpretations of quantum mechanics, consciousness science, and relevant theology before claiming novelty.

## Compact statement

The Point–Sphere programme is strongest when understood as follows: a non-derivative ground is conditionally motivated by a demanding account of ontological explanation; awareness is a further abductive candidate for the ground’s intrinsic nature; finite reality may be literally dependent creation or, on a rival branch, perspectival articulation within strict non-duality; point, sphere, grades, and polarity are formalisation constraints rather than physical results; plenitude is an optional and costly modal hypothesis; and finite perspectives require a distinct theory of individuation, interventional screening, integration, predictive self-modeling, causal use, robust closure, and phenomenality.

The programme is intellectually serious precisely to the extent that it preserves these distinctions, lets established science constrain every physics-facing claim, treats suffering and practical responsibility as real, and allows its bridges to fail. This update does **not** leave an unnamed “other bridge” incomplete: it adds a restricted, finite-model bridge from a candidate system to operational autonomy, integration, model use, and persistence. It does **not** establish the further bridge from that profile to phenomenal consciousness, nor the bridges from grounding to awareness, awareness to creation, or metaphysics to new physics.

The next task is therefore not a larger proclamation or an unspecified new bridge. It is to advance one of the already named bridges at its proper level: defend a stronger global grounding argument beyond the local theorem or retain the local result; test explicit C-Nec/C-Cont creation models against their modal costs; implement and independently validate the budgeted D/I/S/L/P criterion without boundary trivialisation; recover a precisely specified physical sector under correct scaling; or preregister and test a genuinely discriminating empirical consequence. Success in any one track would be important, but would not silently validate the others.

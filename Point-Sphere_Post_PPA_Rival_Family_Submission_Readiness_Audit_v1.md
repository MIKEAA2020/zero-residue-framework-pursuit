## 0. Scope discipline

This audit does not rank metaphysical theories, add a new framework, or claim that the listed clusters exhaust rival space. It tests only whether the named materials already satisfy the entry-packet threshold:

\[
\mathsf{EntryReady}(F_R)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{SubmittedRivalEntryPacket}(F_R)
\land
\mathsf{IndependentOfPPA}(F_R)
\land
\mathsf{DeclaredEntryRoles}(F_R).
\tag{Entry-Ready}
\]

The declared entry roles include scope, signature, candidate grammar, actuality rule, domain/outside rule, type rule, presentation and truth/error rules, equivalence, anti-roster and selector audits, model regime, finite battery, rival contrast, and expiry.

\[
\mathsf{NotEntryReady}(F_R)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Viable}(F_R).
\tag{No-Viability-Verdict}
\]

It means only that the cluster is not presently authorized for entry review or battery execution under the post-PPA charter.

## 2. Grounds for the non-entry labels

### 2.1 Necessary emanation / plenitude

The existing symmetric exact-totality audit requires a concrete map:

\[
\mathsf{Ess}(U)
\Longrightarrow
\langle\mathcal A,\mathcal C,R\rangle,
\tag{ET-Map}
\]

where \(\mathcal A\) is independently typed, \(\mathcal C\) handles compossibility, and \(R\) follows without a later selector. No such map is presently supplied. Calling the ground “diffusive,” “perfect,” or “necessarily expressive” cannot function as an entry-ready actuality rule without the omitted domain and output semantics.

### 2.2 Modal monism via IML

The frozen IML formulation established only modal availability:

\[
\mathsf{Admissible}_{G}(\lambda)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ModalCandidate}(m_{\lambda}),
\]

not actual finite modehood:

\[
\mathsf{IML}_{G}(m)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(m).
\tag{IML-ML5}
\]

The earlier formulation is expired. Its blocked outcome cannot be relabelled as a new modal rival or repaired inside that lineage.

### 2.3 Structural/constitutive schemas

The available structural and constitutive expressions lack independently interpreted \(\Sigma\) or \(\sigma\), positive/negative cases, a non-gauge rule, and a compossibility rule. They therefore have the following status:

\[
\mathsf{Schema}(S)
\lor
\mathsf{Schema}(C)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SubmittedRivalEntryPacket}(F_R).
\tag{Schema-Not-Entry}
\]

### 2.4 Primitive actuality

The Aristotelian record establishes that nonreductive actuality need not be an empty roster label. Its local contrast is a protected resource. But its capacity/form/end inputs are not a public candidate-level admission interface. The PPA form is additionally noneligible because its frozen operative choice operation is undefined. Neither result authorizes a new primitive-actuality candidate without a separately declared operation and domain audit.

### 2.5 Ṣadrian intensity

The record preserves intensive ordering as a source-grounded resource. Yet its known public-effects externalization route did not supply a public rank, type, invariance, error, or extra-candidate procedure. It lacks the complete residual certificate required for \(K_{\rm new}\):

\[
\langle
\mathsf{Interface},
\mathsf{ResistedRole},
\mathsf{NonReductionArgument},
\mathsf{CandidateScope},
\mathsf{InvarianceClass},
\mathsf{PositiveCases},
\mathsf{NegativeCases},
\mathsf{TypeTreatment},
\mathsf{ErrorCondition},
\mathsf{ResidualBattery},
\mathsf{ClosureRoute},
\mathsf{Expiry}
\rangle.
\tag{Residual-Certificate}
\]

It is therefore protected and unclassified, not defeated and not ready.

### 2.6 Hybrid and previously closed candidate lines

ALM is a conjunction of several unproved bridges, not a single candidate-level interface. The WPI packets each retain their own finalized disposition. Reuse of either as a post-PPA rival would need a genuinely independent entry packet, not a change of title or a reinstatement of a closed component.

The strict nondual/pure-negation route remains current candidate-core research rather than an independently submitted rival; no internal comparative preference is inferred from PPA's closure.

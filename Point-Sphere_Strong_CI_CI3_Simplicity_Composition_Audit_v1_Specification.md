# Strong CI Adjudication — CI3 Simplicity / Composition Audit v1
## Formal specification

## Question

Does strongest complete intelligibility entail that the ultimate is absolutely simple, or does it more narrowly rule out an ultimate whose real composition has an unexplained unifying fact?

This audit applies CI0B and CI2. It does not assume that “complex,” “many,” “relational,” “finite aspect,” or “formally articulated” means “composed in the relevant ultimate-level sense.”

## 1. Required distinctions

| Type | Definition for this audit | CI consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Real constituent composition | \(G\) has distinct metaphysical constituents whose unity is an additional fact | Unity requires a non-brute account under SCI |
| Pseudo-composition | Apparent plurality is identity, conceptual distinction, gauge/descriptive redundancy, or a non-constituent relational description | Not composition merely by plurality of predicates or descriptions |
| Essential articulation | \(G\)'s profile includes distinct but inseparable explanatory moments under a claimed intrinsic unity principle | The unity principle itself must pass U1/U4; SCI does not reject it merely by label |
| Finite articulation/aspect | A finite content is claimed to be a real articulation of one non-structural ground, not a constituent possessed by \(G\) qua \(G\) | Requires a non-composition and genuine-aspect bridge at CI4; not settled at CI3 |
| External dependent effect | A finite entity is distinct from but dependent on \(G\) | Requires a creation/participation/emanation and selector account at CI4–CI5 |

## 2. Composition-reflection bridge

The strongest direct SCI result is conditional:

\[
\mathsf{CR}^{*}:
\quad
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\land
\mathsf{RealConstituents}_F(G)
\land
\mathsf{ResidualUnity}_F(G)
\Rightarrow
\bot.
\tag{CR*}
\]

where:

\[
\mathsf{ResidualUnity}_F(G)
:\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{Ultimate}_F(\mathsf{Unity}(G))
\land
\neg\exists e\,[
\mathsf{Expl}_F(e,\mathsf{Unity}(G))
\land
\mathsf{NonCircular}_F(e)
].
\]

\(\mathsf{CR}^{*}\) excludes a bare union of ultimate constituents. It does not establish:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}(F)\Rightarrow\neg\mathsf{EssentialArticulation}_F(G).
\tag{CI3-NE}
\]

To derive absolute simplicity requires the stronger **composition-reflection bridge**:

\[
\mathsf{CR}_{\rm strong}:
\quad
\mathsf{RealConstituents}_F(G)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ResidualUnity}_F(G).
\]

This bridge must rule out every proposed intrinsic account of essential unity without presupposing absolute simplicity. It is the contested positive argument, not a theorem of SCI alone.

## 3. Executable finite witness profiles

The audit uses four abstract profile types. They are not models of ultimate reality.

| Profile | Real constituents | Unity status | Intended role |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `simple` | no | no constituent unity fact | A simple-profile baseline |
| `bare_composite` | yes | primitive/unexplained union | Must fail SCI’s no-residue test |
| `essentially_articulated` | yes | claimed intrinsically explained unity | Shows U1/U3 alone do not label every articulation a failure; its unity claim remains independently held |
| `pseudo_distinct` | no | plurality is descriptive/identity-level only | Shows predicate plurality need not be constituent composition |

## 4. Assertions

The executable audit must establish:

1. A primitive unity fact in `bare_composite` is a named SCI residue.
2. The simple profile has no constituent-unity residue by construction.
3. The essentially articulated profile has real components but an explicitly typed internal unity explanation; a no-residue filter cannot reject it merely because its component count exceeds one.
4. The pseudo-distinct profile is not classified as composed simply because more than one description appears.
5. The structural no-residue classification therefore does not entail absolute simplicity.
6. The audit does not endorse the articulated profile’s self-intelligibility. It marks the claimed unity explanation as a CI2/CI3 bridge requiring a non-circular defense.
7. No conclusion is drawn about creator–creation, participation, emanation, real finite aspects, grammar, phenomenology, or physics.

## 5. Pass rule

CI3 passes as a typing/non-entailment audit if it establishes (CR*) and the distinction between unexplained composition and merely alleged/essential articulation. It fails if it assumes either that every plurality is a part-whole relation or that every internal unity claim automatically succeeds.

CI4 will apply the now-typed non-composition burden symmetrically to finite-reality relation architectures: external creation, participation, necessary emanation, immanent aspect/articulation, and holistic/structural realization.

# Corrigendum to AP2A: Structural Independence Is Not a Refutation of Conditional Identity

## What AP2A correctly established

The executed AP2A finite result remains correct in its stipulated formal scope. Given only:

- a gauge quotient;
- a supplied admissible set \(\mathsf A\); and
- invariance under a physical symmetry action,

both \(R=\varnothing\) and \(R=\mathsf A\) can be invariant. Thus **bare structural symmetry and bare admissibility alone** do not derive nonempty realisation.

## What its interpretation overstated

AP2A omitted the proposed core law that is supposed to connect the ground to aspects: a simple, complete, self-knowing aware ground has non-representational self-presentation. It was therefore wrong to state without qualification that necessary plenitude is simply “an explicit realisation axiom.”

The correct distinction is:

\[
\text{bare }(\mathsf A,H,K)\text{ formalism}
\not\models
R=\mathsf A,
\]

but

\[
\text{complete aware ground}
+\text{ valid aspect--self-presentation identity}
\models
R=\mathsf A
\]

for the specified domain of genuine intrinsic aspects.

The first result is a useful boundary: symmetry cannot substitute for the substantive metaphysical bridge. The second is the proposed conditional route to plenitude.

## Precise status

The question is not now whether a further symmetry premise must generate plenitude. The question is whether the following identity bridge is warranted:

> A genuine intrinsic aspect of a simple self-knowing ground is not a merely represented possibility; it is that ground’s actual nondual self-presentation.

If this bridge is constitutive of what the framework means by complete self-knowing awareness, plenitude is a conditional theorem within the framework rather than a free-standing extra axiom. If it is rejected, only bare AP2A remains and a separate realisation principle would be required.

## Programme correction

AP2B is redirected upstream. Its task is to assess the intrinsic-nature/aware-ground argument and the aspect--self-presentation identity against its serious rivals. It is not to attempt a second derivation of plenitude from orbit symmetry.

The AP1 orbit result remains valuable only as a consistency constraint: any proposed aspect domain must first distinguish non-gauge aspects from descriptions and show which symmetries are physically/metaphysically substantive.

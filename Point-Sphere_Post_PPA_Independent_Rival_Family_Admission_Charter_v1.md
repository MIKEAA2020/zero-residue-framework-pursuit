## 0. Purpose and non-recycling boundary

The bounded selector-residue result established only:

\[
\neg\exists\mathcal G\in\mathcal D_{\rm SRF1}\,
\mathsf{BridgeEligible}(\mathcal G).
\tag{SRF1-Result}
\]

It therefore blocks a PPA-9 draft **from the current material**, but it does not show that every possible nonbrute actuality account fails. The permitted constructive next act is a gate for independently specified rival-family work.

\[
\mathsf{NextActivity}
=
\mathsf{IndependentRivalFamilySpecification}.
\tag{Pivot}
\]

This charter is deliberately not a sixteenth-or-later reformulation of a whole-reality framework. It supplies no ontology, realization rule, PPA repair, score function, candidate roster, or verdict. Its sole function is to prevent a later proposal from being mistaken for an independently specified rival when it merely reuses the frozen PPA machinery.

## 1. PPA lineage exclusion

A proposed rival \(F_R\) is independent of the PPA line only if its operative actuality interface is not defined by the frozen PPA antecedent-actuality machinery or by an undeclared repair of it:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{IndependentOfPPA}(F_R)
\Longrightarrow
&\neg\mathsf{UsesAsOperativeInput}_{F_R}(
\mathsf{Act}_0^{\rm PPA},
\mathsf{Pred}^{\rm PPA},
\mathsf{Pulse}^{\rm PPA},
\mathsf{LexMax}^{\rm PPA})\\
&\land\neg\mathsf{ImportsUndefinedPPASelection}\\
&\land\neg\mathsf{TreatsPPA8LocalPassesAsRivalConfirmation}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{PPA-Independence}
\]

A proposal may address primitive actuality, modal actuality, production, relation, holism, presentation, or another interface. Shared vocabulary alone does not make it a PPA successor. What is excluded is reuse of PPA's frozen operative bridge without a separately declared, noncircular interface.

In particular, the following remains fixed:

\[
\neg\mathsf{Initiated}(F_{\rm PPA\text{-}9})
\land
\neg\mathsf{BatteryAuthorized}(F_{\rm PPA\text{-}9}).
\tag{PPA-Closed}
\]

## 2. Candidate-family map: research slots, not candidate endorsements

The available families below are intake locations derived from the A0 normal-form taxonomy. They are neither exhaustive nor ranked, and no slot is presently credited with a zero-residue pass.

\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
\textbf{slot} & \textbf{claimed immediate admission resource} & \textbf{first noncircular burden}\\
\hline
K_{\rm sem} & \text{semantic or essential determination} & \text{why semantics reaches actuality}\\
K_{\rm id} & \text{identity or individuation} & \text{why an actual profile obtains}\\
K_{\rm rel} & \text{dependence, role, or structure} & \text{relata without an actual roster}\\
K_{\rm modal} & \text{necessity, compossibility, or modal selection} & \text{possibility/necessity-to-actuality bridge}\\
K_{\rm prod} & \text{generation, derivation, or construction} & \text{actual base, operator, and output}\\
K_{\rm act} & \text{primitive or intensive actuality} & \text{public application and error rule}\\
K_{\rm norm} & \text{teleology, value, or fittingness} & \text{non-imported end and realization}\\
K_{\rm pres} & \text{presentation or acquaintance} & \text{access-to-membership bridge}\\

\[

The packet must make the following five roles noninterchangeable:

A source reconstruction, historical taxonomy, conditional escape card, or formal analogy can motivate a packet, but is not itself a packet.

## 4. Entry conditions

A candidate is admitted to an entry review only if all of the following are explicit and frozen.

### 4.1 Operative rule and noncircular application

The actualizing or admission operation must have declared arguments, output values, and application conditions. If it is defined only on an applicable field, the field must not be fixed by antecedent actualities that the operation is intended to determine:

\[
\mathsf{Applicable}_{F_R}(x)
\not\rightsquigarrow
\mathsf{Act}_{F_R}(y)
\quad
\text{for an undeclared antecedent }y.
\tag{Domain-G3}
\]

A total three-valued rule may instead use an exact `outside` value. Either form passes this entry condition only if:

\[
\mathsf{G3Pass}(F_R)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{DomainBoundaryIndependentOfActuality}(F_R)
\ \lor\ 
\mathsf{DeclaredNoncircularWellFoundedDischarge}(F_R).
\tag{G3-Condition}
\]

### 4.2 No hidden selector or possibility domain

The proposal must state what decides a nontrivial actuality difference, what makes its candidate/possibility domain available, and what would count as a tie, abstention, or error:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SelectorAudit}(F_R)	ext{ must decide whether }&\text{its output follows from an actual}\
&\text{rule, a primitive, a relation, a totality, or a declared residual.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Selector-Requirement}
\]

A mere label such as “necessary,” “fitting,” “self-present,” “actual,” or “maximal” does not close this burden.

### 4.3 Public adjudication without inventory loading

The candidate grammar, type rule, equivalence relation, and error rule must be declared before testing. In particular:

\[
\mathsf{Admit}_{F_R}(x)
\not\iff
\text{“}x\text{ belongs to the inventory affirmed by }F_R\text{.”}
\tag{No-Roster-Admission}
\]

The type rule must address, in its stated scope, the contrast among mode, property/attribute, independent substance, nonentity, and inapplicability. Presentation may count as defeasible evidence under a stated correction procedure; it is not thereby aspecthood, membership, or realization.

### 4.4 Comparative and adequacy discipline

An admissible entry must say how its rule differs from a relevant type-neighbor and must predeclare finite structural cases. Passing a local actuality test cannot compensate for an unclosed truth/error, phenomenal, normative, or physical bridge:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F_R;\mathcal B)
\iff
&\mathsf{Typed}
\land\mathsf{NonVacuousModel}
\land\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}
\land\mathsf{ProfileInvariant}\\
&\land\mathsf{ActualityBridgeClosed}
\land\mathsf{RealizationBridgeClosed}
\land\mathsf{TruthErrorBridgeClosed}\\
&\land\mathsf{PhenomenalBatteryPass}
\land\mathsf{NormativeBatteryPass}
\land\mathsf{PhysicalBatteryPass}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{Noncompensatory-ZR}
\]

No stage in this charter grants any of these conjuncts.

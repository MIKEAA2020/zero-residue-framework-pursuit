## Result

All registered checks passed.

| Stage | Grammar count |

The core’s finite-appearance requirement has real force in this implementation:

\[
\smile_U\quad\text{is excluded},
\]

because universal compatibility has only the whole vocabulary as a maximal context and hence yields no proper bounded finite mode.

The IAR-like overlapping-triple grammar survives. So do four other genuinely different structural families. Representatives include:

| Structural family | Maximal proper contexts |
|---|---|
| Star of pairs | three overlapping two-content contexts |
| Path-type pair family | three chained two-content contexts |
| Pair-plus-triple | one two-content and one three-content context |
| Four-cycle of pairs | four pair contexts in an overlap cycle |
| IAR-like diamond | two overlapping three-content contexts |

All five are non-label copies only in the sense that their internal labels can be permuted; they are distinct relation structures.

## What the four core constraints actually contribute

| Core constraint | Effect in the present formal language |
|---|---|
| Immanent, not higher-order | A typing constraint: no second observer/context is represented. It does not distinguish the five graph structures. |
| Partial/bounded | A real discriminator: excludes universal compatibility under the maximal-proper-context grammar. |
| Non-objectifying | A typing constraint: the simple graph has no subject/object or representation edge. It cannot yet distinguish structures. |
| Real boundary | A real discriminator against conventional labels: quotienting by vocabulary relabelling reduces 37 labelled grammars to five structural families. It does not select one family. |

This is the exact disciplined result. It is invalid to claim that immanence or non-objectification has selected IAR merely because every remaining graph can be interpreted immanently and non-objectively.

## Confirmation-bias safeguard applied

No additional condition was added merely because it would retain IAR. In particular, a stronger requirement such as “every content belongs to two contexts” would eliminate the IAR-like diamond family as well as several others. It cannot be added unless it has an independent argument from the core, phenomenology, or physics.

The appropriate test remains:

> Would the condition be accepted as a consequence of self-presentation even if it excluded IAR?

No such additional discriminator was used here.

## Current conclusion

The proposed identity-derived constraints produce a real, principled narrowing:

\[
37\ \text{labelled grammars}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
5\ \text{structural families}.
\]

They do not yet determine IAR uniquely. The answer is therefore currently a structural family—overlapping, bounded, nonexclusive context grammars—rather than a single relation.

To go further, the framework needs a richer formal language capable of representing immanent reflexivity, non-objectifying self-presence, and perhaps finite-horizon/retentional structure as more than untyped graph conditions. That is the next formal task. It must still meet the same no-bias safeguard.

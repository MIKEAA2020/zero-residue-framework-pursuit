## 0. What this audit does not contest

This audit does not decide whether the saturation calculus is internally well typed, whether \(\theta_1\) or \(\theta_2\) passes the irreducibility test, whether any trope rival is adequate, or whether later actual/public bridges exist. Those questions are assigned to unexecuted gates.

It also does not contest the candidate's source-level distinction:

\[
\mathsf{Unsaturated}_{\rm syn}(u)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NonentityProof}(u)
\lor
\mathsf{RealDeterminationProof}(u).
\tag{No-Shortcut-Conceded}
\]

The narrow issue is only the proposed further bridge:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{SemUnsaturated}(u)
\land
\mathsf{IrreducibleConstraint}(u)\\
&\land
\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\})\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{CandidateRealImmanentDetermination}_{10}(u,G).
\end{aligned}
\tag{CRP-Under-Test}
\]

## 1. Standard for an independent CRP warrant

For the CRP to be independently motivated, its support must satisfy both conditions:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{IndependentWarrant}(\mathsf{CRP})
\Longrightarrow{}&
\mathsf{GroundsForCRP}\text{ are statable without assuming real modehood}\\
&\land
\mathsf{FormalProfileData}\text{ cannot receive an equally adequate}\
\mathsf{NonrealistInterpretation}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{CRP-Independence-Standard}
\]

A principle may be contestable and still count as independently motivated. But it cannot gain independence merely by restating its conclusion with a new semantic label:

\[
\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\})
\iff
\mathsf{ProfileContainsRealDetermination}(u,G)
\tag{Forbidden-CRP-Restatement}
\]

would make CRP circular at the semantic level.

---

## 2. Formal facts do not by themselves yield modehood

The antecedent contains three kinds of formal or semantic fact:

\[
\mathsf{SemUnsaturated}(u),
\qquad
\mathsf{IrreducibleConstraint}(u),
\qquad
\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\}).
\tag{CRP-Antecedent-Components}
\]

The first says that \(u\) is an operation rather than a first-order profile value in the typed calculus. The second says that removal of the operation fails to preserve selected BaseQuery distinctions. Neither fact alone establishes that the operation is a real feature rather than a noneliminable constituent of a representation:

\[
\mathsf{SemUnsaturated}(u)
\land
\mathsf{IrreducibleConstraint}(u)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{RealImmanentDetermination}(u,G).
\tag{Formal-Features-Not-Reality}
\]

This conclusion is not a hostile inference. It is precisely the distinction maintained by WSP-10's own no-shortcut clause.

---

## 3. The adequacy dilemma

The term \(\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}\) has two possible readings in the packet's present resources.

### 3.1 Descriptive adequacy reading

If adequacy means only that the profile calculus compactly, minimally, and nonredundantly represents the relevant formal distinctions, then an instrumental or deflationary interpretation remains open:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{SemUnsaturated}(u)
\land
\mathsf{IrreducibleConstraint}(u)\\
&\land
\mathsf{DescriptivelyAdequate}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\})\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{RealImmanentDetermination}(u,G).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Descriptive-Adequacy-Rival}
\]

This is not a proof that the nonrealist reading is correct. It shows that formal irreducibility plus descriptive adequacy does not exclude it.

### 3.2 Reality-involving adequacy reading

If adequacy instead means that the calculus captures what is really intrinsic of \(G\), then the term already imports the bridge sought by CRP:

\[
\mathsf{RealityInvolvingAdequacy}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\})
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{RealImmanentDetermination}(u,G)
\quad\text{only by embedding the conclusion in adequacy.}
\tag{Reality-Import-Dilemma}
\]

The candidate does not provide a third independently articulated reading that both excludes the descriptive rival and avoids importing real modehood.

\[
\mathsf{NoArticulatedThirdAdequacyReading}_{\rm WSP10}
\neq
\mathsf{NoPossibleThirdAdequacyReading}.
\tag{No-Exhaustive-NoGo}
\]

---

## 4. Assessment of the two proposed motivation routes

### 4.1 Abductive route

An abductive defense would need a fixed explanandum, a specified class of rival ontologies, and a stated reason why real immanent determinations explain the profile facts better than a nonrealist or trope/item interpretation:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{AbductiveWarrant}(\mathsf{CRP})
\Longrightarrow{}&
\mathsf{IndependentExplanandum}
\land
\mathsf{RivalTheoryClass}\\
&\land
\mathsf{ComparativeExplanatoryCriterion}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{Abductive-Warrant-Requirements}
\]

WSP-10 supplies none of those ingredients. It supplies a formal profile calculus and a candidate semantic principle, but no independent explanandum whose best explanation is the real-mode reading and no comparative criterion against the descriptive or trope readings:

\[
\mathsf{AbductiveWarrant}_{\rm WSP10}(
\mathsf{CRP}
)
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}.
\tag{Abductive-Route-Result}
\]

### 4.2 Definitional/analytic route

A definitional route would set:

\[
\mathsf{RealImmanentDetermination}_{10}(u,G)
\iff
\mathsf{SemUnsaturated}(u)
\land
\mathsf{IrreducibleConstraint}(u)
\land
\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}(G,\mathbf S,\{u\}).
\tag{Definitional-CRP-Option}
\]

That move would remove the synthetic bridge by definition. But it would also change “real immanent determination” into a stipulated semantic label and no longer support the candidate's intended substantive ontological reading:

\[
\mathsf{DefinitionalCRP}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{NoIndependentOntologicalBridgeClaim}.
\tag{Definition-Does-Not-Establish-Reality}
\]

WSP-10 explicitly presents CRP as substantive and contestable, so it does not adopt this purely analytic route.

## 5. T3 disposition

WSP-10 successfully isolates the formal-to-ontological gap more precisely than the preceding packets. Its typed saturation distinction and irreducibility condition make clear what a bridge would have to connect.

The packet, however, has not independently motivated that bridge. \(\mathsf{IntrinsicProfileAdequacy}\) is either insufficiently strong to exclude a descriptive reading or strong only by importing the real-mode conclusion. Neither an abductive nor a definitional route presently supplies the needed independent warrant.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{T3Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}10})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm CRP\ is\ an\ explicit\ substantive\ principle;\\
\rm no\ independent\ warrant\ supplied\ for\ formal\ constraint\ \to\ real\ modehood
}}.
}
\tag{WSP10-T3-Result}
\]

This does not establish the contrary conclusion:

\[
\mathsf{T3Status}=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{TropeLike}(u,G)
\lor
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}10}).
\tag{T3-No-Overread}
\]

## 2. Decisive actuality/equivalence contradiction

### 2.1 Frozen clauses

The packet fixes canonical-only applicability:

\[
\mathsf{Applicable}(a_p)
\iff
\mathsf{Cand}(p)
\land p=\operatorname{can}(p)
\land\mathsf{RootedAtG}(p)
\land
\forall q\in\mathsf{Pred}(p)\,\mathsf{Act}_0(a_q).
\tag{APP}
\]

It also fixes:

\[
\mathsf{Act}_0(a_p)\to\mathsf{Applicable}(a_p),
\tag{APP-LIMIT}
\]

and carrier-renaming actuality invariance:

\[
p\approx_{\rm PPA}q
\to
[\mathsf{Act}_0(a_p)\leftrightarrow\mathsf{Act}_0(a_q)],
\tag{SA-2}
\]

where:

\[
p\approx_{\rm PPA}q
\iff
\operatorname{can}(p)=\operatorname{can}(q).
\tag{EQ}
\]

Finally, its own fixed primitive fixture interpretation declares:

\[
a_{p_{\rm act}}\in\mathsf{Act}_0^{\rm fixture},
\tag{Fixture-Act}
\]

with \(p_{\rm act}\) explicitly a canonical record.

### 2.2 Noncanonical equivalent representative

Apply a nonidentity permitted carrier-slot renaming \(\sigma\) to \(p_{\rm act}\), and let:

\[
q^\sigma=\sigma(p_{\rm act}).
\tag{Renamed-Code}
\]

Choose \(\sigma\) whose resulting raw encoding is not lexicographically least in its finite renaming orbit. Then:

\[
q^\sigma\neq\operatorname{can}(q^\sigma)=p_{\rm act},
\qquad
q^\sigma\approx_{\rm PPA}p_{\rm act}.
\tag{Equivalent-Noncanonical}
\]

By the fixture declaration and \((\mathrm{SA\text{-}2})\):

\[
\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{p_{\rm act}})
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{q^\sigma}).
\tag{Invariance-Actual}
\]

By \((\mathrm{APP\text{-}LIMIT})\), this entails:

\[
\mathsf{Applicable}(a_{q^\sigma}).
\tag{Required-Applicability}
\]

But \((\mathrm{APP})\) requires:

\[
q^\sigma=\operatorname{can}(q^\sigma),
\tag{Canonical-Requirement}
\]

which contradicts \((\mathrm{Equivalent\text{-}Noncanonical})\). Equivalently, the withholding rule gives:

\[
\neg\mathsf{Applicable}(a_{q^\sigma})
\to
\neg\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{q^\sigma}),
\tag{Withholding-Conflict}
\]

contrary to \((\mathrm{Invariance\text{-}Actual})\).

Thus the packet entails both:

\[
\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{q^\sigma})
\land
\neg\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{q^\sigma}).
\tag{Contradiction}
\]

## 3. Why the result is entry incoherence

The conflict is not a failed empirical fixture or an unproved metaphysical premise. The exact frozen candidate simultaneously requires:

\[
\text{only canonical raw codes are eligible for primitive actuality}
\tag{Canonical-Only}
\]

and:

\[
\text{primitive actuality is invariant across noncanonical carrier-renamed raw codes.}
\tag{Raw-Invariance}
\]

Because the packet declares an actual canonical fixture, the two clauses have an explicit contradiction witness. There is no nonvacuous model satisfying all frozen clauses and the fixture interpretation:

\[
\boxed{
\neg\exists\mathfrak M\,[
\mathfrak M\models
\mathsf{PPA1}_{\rm frozen}
\land
\mathsf{Act}_0(a_{p_{\rm act}})
].
}
\tag{No-Model}
\]

The primitive status of \(\mathsf{Act}_0\) is not itself the contradiction. It remains a substantive candidate cost that would require later selector/brute-residue assessment in a coherent fresh packet.

# Modal Monism — Intrinsic Limitation Test Protocol v1
## A standalone, expiry-limited test of whether finite plurality can arise without an external limiter or brute modal roster

## Status and separation

This is a **standalone constructive project** requested by the user. It does not amend, suspend, or supply a result for the active A0 Meta-Track Phase I. In particular, it does not alter the frozen M3 pilot preregistration.

The target is a modal-monist candidate, not a historical tradition and not an already established framework:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can one necessary, simple reality have genuine finite modal articulations}\
\text{through an intrinsic theory of limitation?}
}
\]

No answer is presupposed. A bare assertion that “the One limits itself,” “the Absolute appears,” or “all possibilities exist” is not a theory of limitation.

## 1. Target candidate and fixed distinction

Let \(G\) be a necessary, simple ground and let \(m\) range over proposed finite modal articulations. The candidate seeks all of:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Necessary}(G)\land\mathsf{Simple}(G);\\
&\mathsf{FiniteMode}(m)\land\mathsf{DependentOn}(m,G);\\
&\neg\mathsf{ExternalLimiter}(L,G);\\
&\neg\mathsf{BruteRoster}(\{m\});\\
&\mathsf{RealDistinctionWithoutIndependentSubstance}(m,G).
\end{aligned}
\]

“Modal” is not yet a solution. This protocol distinguishes:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\mathsf{ConceptualLimit} &: & \text{a description’s incompleteness};\\
\mathsf{EpistemicLimit} &: & \text{a finite knower’s restricted access};\\
\mathsf{OntologicalLimit} &: & \text{a real finite articulation or mode};\\
\mathsf{ExternalLimit} &: & \text{a distinct principle constraining \(G\)}.
\end{array}
\]

Only the third can supply the finite plurality required by the candidate. The fourth violates modal monism unless independently integrated.

## 2. Single preregistered intrinsic-limitation proposal

The sole formulation tested in this project is the **intrinsic partial-presentation schema**:

\[
\mathcal L_G=
\{\lambda:\mathcal I_G\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal I_\lambda\mid
\lambda\text{ is an admissible intrinsic partial presentation of }G\}.
\]

Here \(\mathcal I_G\) is not assumed to be a set of pieces in God. It is a declared interface for determinate intelligible content attributable to the candidate’s self-articulation; \(\lambda\) is a surjective loss-of-determination map to a finite interface \(\mathcal I_\lambda\).

A proposed mode is:

\[
m_\lambda=
\langle \mathcal I_\lambda,\rho_\lambda,\partial_\lambda\rangle,
\]

where \(\rho_\lambda\) is the finite structured profile retained under \(\lambda\), and \(\partial_\lambda\) states its dependence/interface condition.

The candidate admission schema is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{IML}_{G}(m)
\iff
\exists\lambda\in\mathcal L_G[
\mathsf{Admissible}_G(\lambda)
\land m\cong m_\lambda
\land\mathsf{NonTrivial}(\lambda)
\land\mathsf{Finite}(\lambda)
].
}
\tag{IML}
\]

This is a reconstruction to test, not a claim that any historical source uses this formalism.

## 3. Noncircularity conditions

The theory counts as intrinsic only if it supplies all of the following.

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
L_1 &: \mathsf{Admissible}_G(\lambda)\text{ is defined without enumerating desired modes};\\
L_2 &: \mathcal L_G\text{ is generated by a rule or closure condition internal to the proposal, not by fiat};\\
L_3 &: \lambda\text{ does not presuppose a second actual reality that limits }G;\\
L_4 &: \text{distinctness of }m_\lambda,m_\mu\text{ is given by a declared invariant, not names alone};\\
L_5 &: \text{the actual/modal status of each admitted }m_\lambda\text{ is publicly stated};\\
L_6 &: \text{the rule has an error, inapplicability, or rival-completion condition.}
\end{array}
\]

The following are explicitly disallowed as answers to \(L_1\text{--}L_5\):

\[
\text{“because the Absolute wills it,”}\quad
\text{“because that is the manifestation list,”}\quad
\text{“because it is intuitively a limitation,”}\quad
\text{or “because all descriptions are modes.”}
\]

## 4. Fixed test battery

| Test | Required showing |
|---|---|
| ML0 — intelligibility of the map | State what \(\mathcal I_G\), \(\lambda\), and “loss of determination” mean without treating \(G\) as a composite information store. |
| ML1 — internal generation | Give a non-roster generation/closure rule for \(\mathcal L_G\). |
| ML2 — no external limiter | Show that no distinct entity, lack, or alien constraint does the limiting work. |
| ML3 — simplicity compatibility | Explain how distinct \(\lambda\)-profiles do not partition, modify, or add accidents to \(G\). |
| ML4 — plurality and identity | State when \(m_\lambda=m_\mu\), when distinct, and how exact same-profile cases are handled. |
| ML5 — modal-to-actual bridge | Decide whether admissible modes are actual, merely possible, necessary, or selected; do not promote modal availability to actuality. |
| ML6 — type boundary | Distinguish a finite mode from a property, concept, fiction, independent substance, and the ground. |
| ML7 — profile invariance | Preserve the verdict under declared conservative re-encodings of interface, quotient, and profile. |
| ML8 — extra candidate | Admit or reject a proposed \(m_\nu\) without enlarging \(\mathcal L_G\) by stipulation. |
| ML9 — phenomenal bridge | If consciousness is claimed, give a separate non-identity bridge from mode/profile to finite experience. |
| ML10 — physical bridge | If emergent time/space/locality is claimed, give a separate QM/GR theory, recovery regime, and discriminator. |

## 5. Possible outcomes

### Limited intrinsic-limitation articulation pass

A limited pass requires a noncircular answer to ML0–ML8 in a stated finite scope, including:

\[
\mathsf{Admissible}_G,
\quad
\mathsf{ModeIdentity},
\quad
\mathsf{ActualityStatus},
\quad
\mathsf{TypeRule},
\quad
\mathsf{Invariant},
\quad
\mathsf{Err}.
\]

It would show only that the modal-limitation route is formally articulated within scope. It would not establish the truth of modal monism, phenomenal adequacy, physical adequacy, or a correct whole-reality framework.

### Structural limitation failure

A structural failure requires a demonstrated dilemma, not dissatisfaction:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
F_1 &: \text{an external limiter or unexplained contrast is necessary};\\
F_2 &: \text{the limitation is merely conceptual/epistemic and creates no ontology};\\
F_3 &: \text{real plurality compromises simplicity or becomes an undeclared plurality of fundamentals};\\
F_4 &: \text{the admissible set is a brute modal roster or does not yield actuality};\\
F_5 &: \text{mode/property/type and reformulation rules cannot be stated noncircularly.}
\end{array}
\]

A result about one formulation is not a result about every modal monism.

### Inconclusive

The project is inconclusive if the proposed map can be stated but the relevant notions of intrinsic determination, simplicity, or actualization have several materially distinct readings not resolved by the declared scope.

## 6. Adversarial rivals

The proposal must be compared with, not verbally assimilated to:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
R_{\rm ext} &: \text{external-limiter dualism};\\
R_{\rm conc} &: \text{real creator--creature distinction with dependent secondary causes};\\
R_{\rm abs} &: \text{appearance-only absolute monism};\\
R_{\rm plen} &: \text{plenitude without an intrinsic limitation rule};\\
R_{\rm mod} &: \text{modal availability without actual-mode admission.}
\end{array}
\]

A difference in vocabulary is not a discriminator. The relevant questions are domain, actualization, type, invariance, and error.

## 7. Expiry and non-proliferation

This project permits one intrinsic partial-presentation formulation, \((\mathrm{IML})\), and one complete ML0–ML10 application.

\[
\boxed{
\text{No successor limitation theory, revised definition of \(\lambda\), or extra modal}\
\text{selector may be introduced during this project.}
}
\]

If the formulation fails or remains inconclusive, the correct result is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{this intrinsic-limitation formulation has not closed modal monism's gap.}
}
\]

It is not:

\[
\neg\mathsf{ModalMonism}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\neg\exists\text{ an intrinsic theory of limitation}.
\]

## Nonclaim

This protocol does not assume that real God--creature distinction is a deficiency, that finite plurality is necessarily illusory, that all possibilities are actual, that the Absolute has parts or lacks, that information is fundamental, or that a theory of limitation yields emergent physics.

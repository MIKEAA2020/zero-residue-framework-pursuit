# AP4A Public Retentional Record Test v1: Executed Result

## Why this condition belongs to AP4, not AP3

AP3 concerns what a finite self-presenting mode can be. A mode may be private, bounded, and internally retentional without sharing a public history with every other mode.

AP4 asks the additional question of public record. Here a two-content overlap has independent work to do: one common content can be a shared datum, but cannot instantiate a jointly accessible ordered record of continuity/change. A minimal public retentional record requires two jointly accessible contents with a coherent internal order.

## Definitions

For contexts \(C,D\), their public interface is
\[
R_{CD}=C\cap D.
\]

- Elementary public datum: \(|R_{CD}|\ge1\).
- Public retentional record: \(|R_{CD}|\ge2\) and a shared pair has the same internal order in both contexts.

The local orders are the endogenous AP3N witnesses. No external time coordinate, physical dynamics, or preferred clock is introduced.

## What this establishes

This is the first independent downstream discriminator that selects one AP3 structural family without being smuggled into the AP3 definition of a mode.

> **Conditional result:** if finite appearance is required to support a public retentional record interface for every participating mode, then the IAR-like grammar is uniquely selected among the five minimal families.

The condition has an independent role: it is imposed for public records, not for preserving IAR.

## What it does not establish

The conclusion must remain restricted.

1. It does not prove that every finite mode must participate in public records. A private, isolated, dreamlike, or otherwise nonpublic mode is not thereby unreal.
2. It does not prove IAR as the unique grammar of all self-presentation.
3. It is a minimal finite combinatorial discriminator, not a physical derivation of measurement records, objectivity, redundancy, or intersubjective science.
4. The ordered relation remains an internal formal witness, not GR proper time, a physical arrow, or a solution to the Problem of Time.
5. AP3’s identity result remains conditional on the abductive aware-ground package; AP4A does not strengthen that premise.

# AP3G Intrinsic-Aspect Relation v1
## Conditional formal candidate for genuine-mode membership

## Status

AP3F found no existing genuine-mode membership law. This document proposes the closest continuation to the aware-ground core: a formal **Intrinsic-Aspect Relation** (IAR). It is a new explicit formalisation, not a claim that point/sphere imagery, symmetry, or awareness alone already supplied an executable membership law.

The test asks whether IAR can meet M1–M6 internally. It cannot by itself meet the physical-linkage requirement M7.

## Formal strata

The ground \(G\) is not a node, state, or element of the following finite structure. The structure represents only the typed content of a possible self-presentation.

Let \(\mathcal S\) be a finite vocabulary of presentation contents,
\[
\mathcal S=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v,q_s,q_v\}.
\]
Let \(\smile\) be a **self-presentability compatibility relation**. Its maximal proper compatible contexts are generated by
\[
C_P=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v\},
\qquad
C_Q=\{r_0,r_1,q_s,q_v\}.
\]
Contents from the P-exclusive and Q-exclusive pairs are not jointly self-presentable *within one finite context*. This is a context restriction, not a claim that P and Q cannot coexist as modes of the whole.

A local valuation satisfies:
\[
p_s=r_0,
\qquad
q_s=r_1,
\]
with \(r_0,r_1,p_v,q_v\in\{0,1\}\).

A redundant gauge label \(u\in\mathbb Z_2\) acts only on descriptions:
\[
k\cdot(C,\nu,u)=(C,\nu,u+k\!\!\pmod2).
\]
Gauge labels are not aspects/modes.

## IAR membership rule

A mode is the gauge equivalence class
\[
m=[C,\nu,u]_K
\]
where:

1. \(C\) is a maximal **proper** \(\smile\)-compatible context;
2. \(\nu\) is a constraint-satisfying valuation on \(C\);
3. \(C\) is proper in \(\mathcal S\), so the mode has bounded presentation rather than global access;
4. the identity of \(m\) includes its context and valuation, not the gauge label.

Thus
\[
\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}
=
\{[C,\nu,u]_K:C\in\operatorname{MaxPropCtx}(\mathcal S,\smile),\ \nu\in\operatorname{Sol}(C)\}.
\]

If the aware-ground identity package is accepted, the conditional phenomenality claim is:
\[
\operatorname{Phen}_G(m)
\Longleftrightarrow
m\in\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}\ \land\ \operatorname{SelfPresent}_G(m).
\]
This is not inferred by the finite model. It states where the metaphysical identity bridge enters.

Conditional P-internal plenitude then gives:
\[
\forall m\in\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}\;R(m).
\]

## Registered tests

1. Exactly \(C_P\) and \(C_Q\) are maximal proper compatible contexts.
2. Each context has eight constraint-satisfying valuations.
3. The 32 gauge-labelled descriptions quotient to 16 IAR modes, preventing gauge multiplication.
4. Every IAR mode has bounded presentation because its context is proper.
5. Cross-context modes can coexist when their public \((r_0,r_1)\) valuations agree; each such pair has a unique whole-content amalgamation.
6. Arbitrary nonmaximal or noncompatible subsets are excluded by the IAR membership rule.

## Interpretation rule

A passing test establishes a formally nontrivial membership rule with a gauge quotient, inclusion/exclusion, boundedness, and nonexclusive coexistence. It does **not** establish that the self-presentability relation \(\smile\) derives from awareness, that the modes are conscious, or that the finite vocabulary is physically real. \(\smile\) is the new substantive formal ingredient whose warrant must be audited.

# Aristotelian *Energeia* Actuality Reconstruction v1
## Source-grounded test of potentiality, activity, form, and public finite actuality

## Status

This dossier applies the predeclared protocol in `Point-Sphere_Aristotelian_Energeia_Actuality_Test_Protocol_v1.md`. It tests a serious primitive-actuality candidate without assuming that irreducibility is circularity.

The result is differentiated. Aristotle supplies a robust, non-roster contrast between potentiality and actuality and a substantive account of activity, form, capacity, change, and completion. It thereby gives the primitive-actuality route a stronger hearing than a bare \(A(x)\) predicate. But it does not provide a public candidate-level rule for genuine finite mode-membership, because the actuality verdict remains relative to an already typed bearer, capacity, form, and end.

This result does not show that Aristotelian actuality is incoherent, that primitive actuality is impossible, or that A0 is unsatisfiable.

## 2. Source architecture

The relevant claims are:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
A_1 &: \text{matter is potentiality and form is actuality};\\
A_2 &: \text{actuality is related to potentiality as seeing to having sight, waking to sleeping,}\
&\text{and shaped form to suitable matter (Met. \(\Theta\).6)};\\
A_3 &: \text{potentiality is intelligible through the corresponding actuality; actuality is prior}\
&\text{in account and in substance (Met. \(\Theta\).8)};\\
A_4 &: \text{a capacity is an originative source of change, active or passive (Met. \(\Theta\).1)};\\
A_5 &: \text{motion is actuality of the potential qua potential (Phys. III.1)};\\
A_6 &: \text{first actuality differs from second actuality, as possessing knowledge differs}\
&\text{from exercising it (De An. II.1, II.5)};\\
A_7 &: \text{the soul is first actuality/form of a natural organized body having life potentially};\\
A_8 &: \text{actuality, form, and end are connected in the explanation of natural change.}
\end{array}
\]

The account is not a bare present-tense predicate. *Energeia* is an analogically extended fundamental contrast with potentiality, and *entelecheia* emphasizes completion. Aristotle expressly treats the general contrast as grasped through cases rather than supplied by a reductive definition.

## 3. Strongest charitable actuality criterion

Let \(s\) be a bearer, \(p\) a capacity/potentiality of \(s\), \(F\) the relevant form or end, and \(e\) an exercise, process, or completion. The charitable reconstruction is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Actual}_{A}(e,F,s)
\iff
&\exists p\,[
\mathsf{CapacityFor}_{A}(p,F,s)\\
&\land\mathsf{ExerciseOrCompletionOf}_{A}(e,p,F,s)\\
&\land\mathsf{FormOrEnd}_{A}(F,s)
].
\end{aligned}
\]

It directs attention to a real difference:

\[
\mathsf{CanSee}(s)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{Seeing}(s),
\qquad
\mathsf{Buildable}(m)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{BuiltHouse}(m).
\]

Thus it is neither roster-defined nor equivalent to “currently observed.” It offers an intelligible primitive actuality contrast mediated by capacity, activity, form, and completion.

## 4. The first-/second-actuality complication

The proposed criterion cannot be simplified to “actuality = exercised capacity.” Aristotle distinguishes:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\text{first potentiality} &:& \text{being capable of acquiring a capacity};\\
\text{first actuality / second potentiality} &:& \text{possessed knowledge, faculty, or form};\\
\text{second actuality} &:& \text{the active exercise, e.g. contemplating or seeing.}
\end{array}
\]

A sleeper can possess knowledge actually in the first sense while not exercising it in the second. A soul is first actuality of a natural organized body rather than merely an ongoing exercise. Motion, by contrast, is an incomplete actuality of a potential as potential.

Accordingly:

\[
\mathsf{Energeia}_{A}
\notightsquigarrow
\text{one univocal actuality predicate applicable without respect to kind, bearer,}\
\text{capacity, and level of analysis.}
\]

This is a philosophical resource, not a defect. It prevents a simplistic primitive-actuality formulation from being attributed to Aristotle.

## 5. What the proposal genuinely achieves

### 5.1 Non-roster local actuality discrimination

For a specified form/capacity context, Aristotle can distinguish capacities from their exercise or completion:

\[
\mathsf{Potential}_{A}(p,F,s)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{SecondActuality}_{A}(s,F).
\]

This is an actual criterion of status within the hylomorphic setting, not a circular appeal to an inventory of actual things.

### 5.2 A substantial explanatory grammar

The account integrates material, formal, efficient, and final explanation. Actuality is not an isolated label but connects an entity’s potentiality to the relevant form/end and, in change, to an actualizing source.

### 5.3 A reason primitive actuality need not be arbitrary

The explanatory priority of actuality over potentiality gives the primitive route a serious content condition:

\[
\text{a capacity is a capacity for a determinate actuality.}
\]

This blocks the invalid inference:

\[
\text{non-reductive}
\Rightarrow
\text{arbitrary or merely roster-defined}.
\]

## 6. Why local actuality is not yet A0 admission

The criterion is relationally loaded. To apply it to a proposed \(x\), one must already identify:

\[
\langle s,p,F,e\rangle,
\]

where \(s\) is a bearer, \(p\) a genuine capacity, \(F\) the relevant form/end, and \(e\) an actual exercise/completion.

The source gives no finite public procedure, independent of its substance/nature/kind inventory, to establish these inputs for an arbitrary proposed candidate. In particular:

\[
\mathsf{CapacityFor}_{A}(p,F,s)
\quad\text{is not settled merely by calling }p\text{ a capacity,}
\]

and:

\[
\mathsf{ExerciseOrCompletionOf}_{A}(e,p,F,s)
\quad\text{is not settled merely by calling }e\text{ actual.}
\]

The source can explain why a *known natural substance* is actual in a specified respect. It does not thereby decide whether a proposed finite item is a genuine metaphysical mode, a property, a substance, a process, an abstraction, or a nonentity.

## 7. Predeclared structural battery

| Test | Charitable Aristotelian result | A0 assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **T0** no finite candidate | The metaphysics includes eternal actuality and a rich natural ontology; natural change is oriented to actualization. | The theory does not use *energeia* to derive a publicly specified finite population. Its commitments about substances/natures do not decide a finite-empty candidate field without its wider ontology. |
| **T1** one candidate | Given a bearer, capacity, and form/end, it can distinguish actual exercise/completion from mere potentiality. | This is a genuine local success. It does not establish that a proposed \(x\) is the relevant bearer, capacity, exercise, or finite mode before the Aristotelian inventory is applied. |
| **T2** symmetric pair | Two symmetric exercises may both be actual; the distinction is not meant to select one arbitrary token. | *Energeia* does not by itself give identity/particularity conditions for the pair. Underlying substances, matter, motion, or histories must do that work. |
| **T3** property candidate | Aristotle distinguishes substance, form, capacity, activity, and accidental predicates in his broader category theory. | It does not yield one public mode/property boundary: actuality may be form, a first actuality, a second activity, or a process, and finite modes are not the theory’s uniform target type. |
| **T4** independent candidate | Aristotle admits finite primary substances; actuality does not entail dependence on a single ultimate substance. | This usefully separates actuality from dependence, but means the criterion does not decide the target non-substantial-mode relation. |
| **T5** conservative re-encoding | The account has structured analogies and explanatory roles. | No common profile extractor or invariance theorem preserves capacity/form/end verdicts across conservative rival formulations. |
| **T6** extra candidate | A new item would need a genuine capacity and its exercise/completion. | Adding a new capacity, bearer, kind, or end changes the actual inventory. The criterion supplies no noncircular public rule for whether the additions are genuine. |

## 8. \(P_1\)–\(P_3\) actuality-input audit

| Burden | Source resource | Unclosed issue |
|---|---|---|
| \(P_1\): real capacity | Capacities are roots of change and are intelligible relative to their activities. | Whether a proposed capacity is a real capacity relies on the relevant substance, nature, or form. |
| \(P_2\): actual exercise/completion | Seeing, building, thinking, and living are standard cases of actualization. | The source distinguishes exercises once identified; it does not give framework-independent admission of a candidate event/state as the relevant exercise. |
| \(P_3\): form/end | Form and end organize potentiality and explanation; actuality is prior in account and substance. | The form/end is supplied through the relevant kind/substance account. No profile-neutral rule fixes the kind or end for an arbitrary candidate. |

## 9. Articulation verdict

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{Aristotle supplies a nontrivial, non-roster, error-sensitive local contrast}\
\text{between potentiality and actuality.}\\[1mm]
\text{He therefore shows that primitive actuality cannot be dismissed as bare stipulation.}\\[1mm]
\text{But *energeia* does not yet pass A0 as a public candidate-level criterion}\
\text{of genuine finite mode-membership and actuality.}
\end{gathered}
}
\]

The failure is structural under the protocol, though it is not a claim of general incoherence:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
F_1 &: \text{capacity/application presupposes a relevant bearer and actual capacity};\\
F_2 &: \text{exercise/completion is adjudicated only relative to an already identified capacity/form setting};\\
F_3 &: \text{form, natural kind, and end are inputs to the verdict rather than a public admission rule};\\
F_4 &: \text{the target mode/property/substance boundary is not recovered as a uniform criterion};\\
F_5 &: \text{no profile-conservative invariance or public error protocol is supplied.}
\end{array}
\]

The central implication fails:

\[
\mathsf{Energeia}_{A}(x,F,s)
\notightsquigarrow
\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicatedActualFiniteMode}(x).
\]

## 10. Consequence for the escape-class test

Aristotle does not vindicate the hoped-for primitive actuality escape in the required A0 form. But neither does he validate an argument that primitive actuality is empty, circular, or unintelligible. The correct result is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{An intelligible primitive actuality contrast can remain local and kind-relative}\
\text{without becoming a public finite ontology-admission criterion.}
}
\]

This gives defeasible support only to the restricted Aristotelian/hylomorphic subfamily of \(\mathcal K_{\rm prim}\). It does not show:

\[
\forall C\in\mathcal K_{\rm prim}\;\mathsf{BridgeFail}(C).
\]

The conditional A0 no-go schema remains open because its reduction and exhaustiveness premises remain unproved, and because modal and constitutive routes remain untested.

## 11. Downstream bridges

### Phenomenal bridge

Aristotle’s psychology and account of perception should not be conflated with a Point–Sphere phenomenal bridge. They yield no metaphysical certification from experience and no entry into the frozen CFD corpus.

### Physical bridge

Aristotle’s natural philosophy supplies historical accounts of change and nature. It supplies neither a recovery theorem for quantum mechanics/general relativity nor a modern discriminating observable. It therefore does not discharge the physical-adequacy condition.

## Nonclaim

This reconstruction does not show that Aristotle’s account of change, form, or life is false; that his actuality/potentiality distinction is merely verbal; that no primitive actuality predicate can meet A0; or that A0 is unsatisfiable. The correct whole-reality framework remains unidentified.

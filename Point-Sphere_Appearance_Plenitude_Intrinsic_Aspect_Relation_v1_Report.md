# AP3G Intrinsic-Aspect Relation v1: Executed Result

## Aim

AP3F found that plenitude needs a membership law: it can realise every genuine mode, but does not by itself identify which formal candidates are genuine. AP3G implements the closest candidate to the aware-ground core: an **Intrinsic-Aspect Relation** (IAR).

IAR does not select one perspective. It defines a family of proper maximal self-presentable contexts; conditional plenitude may then realise every gauge-inequivalent member of that family.

## Frozen relation

The finite presentation vocabulary was
\[
\mathcal S=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v,q_s,q_v\}.
\]
A stipulated self-presentability relation had exactly two maximal proper contexts:

\[
C_P=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v\},
\qquad
C_Q=\{r_0,r_1,q_s,q_v\}.
\]

The local constraints were
\[
p_s=r_0,
\qquad q_s=r_1.
\]

A mode was a gauge-equivalence class of a constraint-satisfying valuation on a maximal proper context. A redundant \(\mathbb Z_2\) label was explicitly quotiented, so descriptions were not multiplied into modes.

## What IAR achieves

IAR is more than the earlier arbitrary-subset model. It supplies a typed candidate answer to the membership question:

\[
\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}
=
\{[C,\nu]_K:C\in\operatorname{MaxPropCtx}(\mathcal S,\smile),\;\nu\in\operatorname{Sol}(C)\}.
\]

It passes the following formal portions of the AP3F audit:

- **M1, domain:** contexts plus valuations;
- **M2, gauge:** explicit quotient;
- **M3, inclusion/exclusion:** maximal proper self-presentable contexts only;
- **M4, formal noncircularity:** membership is not defined as consciousness, actuality, or unique selection;
- **M5, coexistence:** multiple public-compatible modes coexist without independent substance status.

Conditional on accepting IAR as the intended formal grammar of self-presentation, the nondual plenitude claim has a clear target:

\[
\forall m\in\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}\;R(m).
\]

## What IAR does not achieve

The key caution is equally exact:

\[
\smile\quad\text{is newly stipulated.}
\]

The finite test does not derive its self-presentability compatibility relation from awareness, simplicity, symmetry, point/sphere imagery, physics, or the earlier graph models. It therefore fails the substantive part of the audit unless one explicitly adopts it as a formal intrinsic-aspect law.

It also does not supply:

- **M6 phenomenality:** the bridge
  \[
  \operatorname{Phen}_G(m)
  \Longleftrightarrow
  m\in\mathsf M_{\rm IAR}\land\operatorname{SelfPresent}_G(m)
  \]
  remains conditional on the aware-ground identity package; and
- **M7 public/physical linkage:** no records, locality, observables, dynamics, probability, or empirical discriminator are derived.

## Honest status

IAR is an explicit **option-3 candidate** from AP3F: a formal intrinsic-aspect relation. It is not a theorem extracted from the pre-existing framework.

There are two legitimate ways to proceed:

1. **Core-formalisation route:** declare that the self-presentability relation is constitutive of the intended aware ground. Then IAR is a protected conditional grammar, and the programme can derive its metaphysical consequences while clearly labelling the move.
2. **Public-derivation route:** retain IAR as revisable and seek an independent physical, phenomenological, or formal argument for \(\smile\). Until such an argument exists, no downstream claim is publicly derived.

The execution itself cannot decide between those routes. It shows exactly what must be accepted or justified.

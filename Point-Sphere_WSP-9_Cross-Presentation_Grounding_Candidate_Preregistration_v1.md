## 3. Total cross-presentation profile algebra

Each anchor-role token has the same four-position chain algebra:

\[
P_g=P_h=\{\bot,j_1,j_2,\top\},
\qquad
\bot<j_1<j_2<\top,
\tag{WSP9-Profile-Carrier}
\]

with \(\wedge=\min\), \(\vee=\max\), and the identity as its sole order automorphism. The profile effects are total maps on the full carrier:

\[
\Theta_{a,\mu_a}(x):=x\vee j_1,
\qquad
\Theta_{a,\nu_a}(x):=x\vee j_2,
\qquad
(a\in\{g,h\},\ x\in P_a).
\tag{WSP9-Total-Profile-Effects}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\Theta_{a,\mu_a}(\bot)=j_1,
\qquad
\Theta_{a,\nu_a}(\bot)=j_2,
\tag{WSP9-Effect-at-Bottom}
\]

and values at every other profile position are determined by the same total clauses.

The base-query language is the fixed two-bit evaluation map:

\[
\mathsf{BaseQuery}(x)
:=
\langle
\mathbf 1[x\geq j_1],
\mathbf 1[x\geq j_2]
\rangle
\in\{0,1\}^2.
\tag{WSP9-Base-Query}
\]

The preservation maps are exactly the bounded-lattice endomorphisms:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{BaseEnd}(f,P_a)
\iff{}&
f:P_a\to P_a
\land f(\bot)=\bot
\land f(\top)=\top\\
&\land\forall x,y\in P_a\,[
 f(x\wedge y)=f(x)\wedge f(y)\\
&\hspace{38mm}\land f(x\vee y)=f(x)\vee f(y)].
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSP9-Base-Endomorphism}
\]

A nonbottom position is noneliminable precisely when every such base-profile compression that collapses it with bottom also collapses a distinct base-query pair:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{NonEliminable}(j,P_a)
\iff{}&
j\in\{j_1,j_2\}
\land
\forall f\,[
\mathsf{BaseEnd}(f,P_a)\land f(j)=f(\bot)\\
&\Longrightarrow
\exists x\neq y\in P_a\,[
\mathsf{BaseQuery}(x)\neq\mathsf{BaseQuery}(y)
\land f(x)=f(y)]].
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSP9-Noneliminability}
\]

The chains for \(g\) and \(h\) are connected by the unique order isomorphism \(\iota_{g,h}\) that maps each height to itself:

\[
\iota_{g,h}(\bot)=\bot,
\quad
\iota_{g,h}(j_1)=j_1,
\quad
\iota_{g,h}(j_2)=j_2,
\quad
\iota_{g,h}(\top)=\top.
\tag{WSP9-Cross-Presentation-Isomorphism}
\]

The operational-profile lift is:

## 4. Proposed invariant-grounding semantic lift

The new semantic proposal is not that all formal invariants are real. It requires a cross-presentation invariant to be an exact constraint in the least intrinsic grounding representation of the candidate first-slot token.

Let \(\mathsf{IGRep}(a,\mathcal P_a)\) mean: \(\mathcal P_a\) is a **minimal intrinsic grounding representation** for the intrinsic-profile ascriptions of \(a\); removing one of its noneliminable positions fails to preserve the fixed BaseQuery distinctions. This is a new primitive application condition, not a public truth/error bridge or actual-world claim.

\[
\mathsf{IGRep}(a,\mathcal P_a)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PublicTruthError}
\lor
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentPossibilityDomain}.
\tag{WSP9-IGRep-Scope-Limit}
\]

The **Cross-Presentation Grounding Principle** is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{CrossInvariantLift}(q@a,j)
\land
\mathsf{IGRep}(a,\mathcal P_a)\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{CandidateIntrinsicConstraint}(q@a,j).
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{CPGP}
\]

The intended rationale is that an unlabelled, noneliminable structural constraint earns candidate intrinsic significance only if it recurs in every admissible minimal presentation of the same intrinsic grounding profile—not merely in one stipulated description.

The candidate does not equate candidate intrinsic constraint with an entity or a mode. It proposes a further, defeasible interpretation step:

\[
\mathsf{CandidateIntrinsicConstraint}(q@a,j)
\land
\mathsf{NoSeparateCarrierTrack}(q@a)

\mathsf{CandidateRealImmanentDetermination}(q@a).
\tag{WSP9-Reality-Proposal}

A separate carrier track is defined operationally as an independently closed, countable, and renamable trace for the same token across a change of its partner:

\[

\exists q',b\,[b\neq a

&\land
\mathsf{cl}(q')=\mathsf{ct}(q')=\mathsf{rn}(q')=1\\

\end{aligned}

# Strong CI Adjudication — PC Contrast-Legitimacy Corrigendum v1
## Scope of Independence and what the CL audit actually exhausts

## Correction

The contrast-legitimacy audit correctly shows that the presently audited local presupposition challenges do not establish their candidates’ exact ultimate profiles. It must not be read as proving that every presupposition challenge is structurally circular or that all bridge work is exhausted.

## 1. Two senses of independence

A strict reading of \(\mathsf{Independence}(\neg d,p)\) as “logically unrelated to \(p\)” is too strong. Any genuine explanation of a profile will be relevant to that profile.

The required sense is **non-question-begging independence**:

\[
\mathsf{NQBInd}(r,p)
:=
\text{the argument for \(r\) does not assume \(p\), its exact extension, or a synonym for it as an unargued premise.}
\]

An independently defended ontology may be relevant to \(p\). It is not disqualified merely because it bears on \(p\); it must itself be assessable without simply presupposing the profile it is invoked to establish.

## 2. Conditional local result versus global selection

The following can be a legitimate **conditional local result**:

\[
\mathsf{EE}(G)
\Rightarrow
\neg Q_{\rm add}.
\]

It says: if the EE profile is correct, a question that presupposes essence–existence composition is ill-typed. The analogous conditional results for whole-first and nondual profiles are also legitimate.

What does not follow is:

\[
\mathsf{EE}(G)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EE}(G),
\quad\text{therefore EE is selected.}
\]

A local presupposition challenge fails as a **global selection argument** when the profile used to establish the local result has not itself been non-question-beggingly derived or defended against the remaining exact-profile question.

## 3. Narrow exhaustion result

The earned closure is limited to:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No presently audited candidate may use a local category-error diagnosis,}\
\text{derived only from its asserted core profile, as a proof of that profile.}
}
\]

It does not establish:

\[
\boxed{
\text{No candidate can ever make a non-question-begging presupposition challenge.}
}
\]

The former is a result; the latter remains unproved.

## 4. Conditions for a new direct or transcendental route

A direct condition \(X\), or a transcendental argument from intelligible discourse/explanation, may be admitted only if it is predeclared with:

1. **profile-neutral formulation:** \(X\) does not include the selected profile, its exact output, or a disguised equivalent;
2. **argument for adoption:** the rationale for \(X\) is stated rather than inherited from strongest CI by name;
3. **typed bridge:** an explicit reason why satisfying \(X\) is ontological/grounding evidence rather than merely a formal, semantic, pragmatic, or epistemic condition;
4. **adversarial cases:** at least one positive and one negative candidate profile whose treatment is identifiable before the verdict; and
5. **expiry condition:** failure to discriminate or failure of the typed bridge closes the route.

A transcendental argument especially cannot move from a condition of intelligible thought or discourse to the ontology of reality without the typed bridge in item 3.

## 5. Least-residue status

If no candidate in a sufficiently specified, fairly tested field supplies a strongest-CI closure, then a least-residue programme becomes a live **methodological alternative**. That does not show strongest CI is false or unsatisfiable in all possible architectures; it records that it has no supplied satisfier in the audited field.

## Nonclaims

This corrigendum neither selects a framework nor reopens generic CI rhetoric. It clarifies the type of future bridge deliverable permitted after the local-presupposition result.

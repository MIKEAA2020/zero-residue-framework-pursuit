# Strong CI Adjudication — ET Necessary-Output Comparison Dossier v1
## Necessary emanation/plenitude versus nondual genuine-aspect realization

## Status

**ET comparison complete at the current resource level. Neither route has a public exact-totality pass.** The comparison is symmetric: a framework may stipulate rich core content and derive consequences conditionally, but it may not advertise that core content as generated by a thinner theory which omits it.

## 1. Exact-totality pass condition

Let \(U\) be the candidate ultimate profile, \(\Omega\) a candidate-description space, \(\mathcal A\) a non-gauge genuine finite-content domain, \(\mathcal C\) a compossibility/context structure, and \(R\) realization.

A public SCI exact-totality pass requires a non-circular map:

\[
\mathsf{Ess}(U)
\Longrightarrow
\langle
\mathcal A,
\mathcal C,
R
\rangle
\tag{ET-map}
\]

such that:

1. \(\mathcal A\) is typed independently of \(R\), grammar membership, and coherence alone;
2. inclusion and exclusion in \(\mathcal A\) have a stated non-circular semantic ground;
3. \(\mathcal C\) handles incompatibility without denying finite reality or conjoining incompatible contents in one finite context;
4. \(R\) follows from the stated essence/domain relation without a later selector;
5. formal/gauge descriptions do not multiply contents; and
6. at least one positive and one negative candidate case are independently identifiable.

A framework may instead make \(\langle\mathcal A,\mathcal C,R\rangle\) protected **core** content. That yields a conditional internal framework, not a public derivation of exact output from a thinner essence description.

## 2. Necessary emanation/plenitude route

### Candidate claim

\[
\mathsf{PerfectGood}(U)
\land
\mathsf{NecessarilyDiffusive}(U)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Plenitude}(\mathcal A)
\Longrightarrow
\forall a\in\mathcal A\;R(a).
\tag{NEP}
\]

### Required bridge ledger

| Required link | What must be supplied | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Goodness to diffusion | Why perfect goodness is necessarily self-communicative rather than fully complete without finite expression | Unproved bridge |
| Diffusion to candidate domain | Why diffusion determines all and only a specified \(\mathcal A\), not an arbitrary hierarchy, one grade, or another exact totality | Unproved bridge |
| Possibility to genuine aspect | What makes a possibility a genuine finite determination rather than a description, abstraction, incompatible state, or duplicate | Unproved bridge |
| Plenitude to compossibility | Why all relevant contents coexist, or how context-indexed realization preserves the claim | Unproved bridge |
| Hierarchy/gradation | Why this exact order, degrees, and exclusions follow from the source | Unproved bridge |
| Non-composition | Why derivative multiplicity neither parts the source nor becomes a second ungrounded reality | Unproved bridge |

The expression “a perfect ground is limited by unactualized possibilities” cannot supply these links by itself. It presupposes a typed possibility space and a principle that non-realization of a member counts as a defect rather than as the absence of any genuine determination.

### ET result

Necessary emanation/plenitude directly addresses contingent selector debt, but it currently supplies no public \((\mathrm{ET\text{-}map})\). It is therefore **held**, not eliminated and not selected.

## 3. Nondual genuine-aspect route

### Conditional identity theorem

The protected non-representational identity package gives:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(a)
\Rightarrow
R(a).
\tag{NDI}
\]

This is a valid internal omission/actualization theorem. It avoids a subsequent external selector once genuine finite content is already given.

### Two distinct versions

| Version | Claim | ET status |
|---|---|---|
| Rich protected nondual core \(P_{\rm ND}\) | Real finite modes and the mode/aspect/self-knowledge identity are core content | Non-vacuity is internal to the core, not derived from a thin theory; conditional internal result only |
| Thin self-knowledge core \(K_0\) | Complete self-knowledge, simplicity, and non-representational identity without a real-mode articulation principle | Does not entail a finite differentiated domain; bare self-presence remains a model |

The thin-core result is:

\[
K_0
\not\models
\exists a\,[
\mathsf{Finite}(a)
\land
\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)
].
\tag{K0-ET}
\]

It does not refute \(P_{\rm ND}\). It shows only that a derivation cannot remove real finite articulation from the premises and recover it for free.

### Public exact-totality burden

Even with \(P_{\rm ND}\), the public comparison still needs a non-circular account of:

\[
\mathcal A_{\rm genuine}:
\quad
\text{all and only genuine finite determinations of the one reality.}
\]

GA1 establishes that the structural filters do not determine this domain:

\[
\mathsf{Coh}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NG}(a)
\land
\mathsf{Ctx}(a)
\not\models
\mathsf{Det}_G(a).
\]

The core-relative criterion \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\leftrightarrow\mathsf{Req}^{P_{\rm ND}}_M(a)\) is an internal identity articulation, not yet a public \((\mathrm{ET\text{-}map})\) which classifies independent formal candidates. Grammar remains target-relative description rather than ontology-by-formalization.

### ET result

The nondual route has a stronger **conditional actualization** theorem than the emanation route once its domain is fixed. It does not yet provide a public exact domain/classification map. It is therefore **conditionally promising but held**.

## 4. Symmetry check

The two routes have parallel status:

| Question | Necessary emanation/plenitude | Nondual genuine-aspect identity |
|---|---|---|
| Can its rich relation/domain be stipulated as core? | Yes, conditionally | Yes, conditionally |
| Does a thin essence description derive its full finite domain? | Not yet | No, not from \(K_0\) |
| Does it avoid a later contingent selector if its domain is fixed? | Proposed, but realization bridge still needed | Yes internally via NDI |
| Does it publicly classify all and only genuine finite contents? | No | No |
| Does it have a public exact-totality pass? | No | No |

Neither candidate may reject the other for possessing substantive core content while protecting its own content from the same question.

## 6. Expiry condition and next work

The generic exact-totality route is closed. New work must supply one concrete bridge:

1. **Emanation bridge:** a typed possibility/admissibility/compossibility semantics plus derivation of diffusion and exact hierarchy; or
2. **Nondual bridge:** a realization-independent public semantics of genuine finite determination with positive/negative cases, not merely a formal grammar; or
3. **Alternative candidate:** a fully specified ET-map meeting the same six conditions.

No further assertion that “all possibilities must be realized” or “modes are already core” counts as a public SCI derivation without one of these deliverables.

## Nonclaims

This comparison does not reject either framework, strip protected core content from a conditional framework, prove a public aspect criterion impossible, or establish creation, emanation, plenitude, phenomenology, or physics.

# Perspective Individuation and Public Structure v1
## C2: finite graph architecture under the active hard-core + belt programme

## Scope box

**Assumed:** real differentiated dependent creation, the C1 typed concurrence target, and the distinction between a created causal model and ontological sustenance.

**Question:** can finite perspective-carrier candidates be structurally individuated while sharing a public world, remaining partial, and retaining independent causal/intervention roles?

**Not claimed:** that any graph carrier is conscious, that the graph solves the combination/decombination problem, or that it establishes personal identity. The P belt still requires a phenomenal bridge.

---

## 1. Perspective record

A finite perspective record is a tuple
\[
P_i=(C_i,I_i,K_i,A_i,\tau_i),
\]
where:

- \(C_i\) is a carrier candidate: a structured set of created variables;
- \(I_i\) is its public-world interface, not automatically a member of the carrier;
- \(K_i\) is its finite information/access state;
- \(A_i\) is an action/intervention variable; and
- \(\tau_i\) is a temporal/history scope.

This separates three relations often conflated: carrier membership, interface access, and temporal scope.

## 2. Frozen finite graph

The graph contains a public state \(W\), and for each \(i\in\{1,2\}\):

\[
W\to S_i\to M_i\to A_i,\qquad
A_i\to H_i\to M_i,\qquad
A_i\to W^+.
\]

\(S_i\) is a noisy finite sensor, \(M_i\) local memory/access, \(A_i\) a finite action, and \(H_i\) a history/feedback variable. Both agents affect the same future public state:
\[
W^+=W\oplus A_1\oplus A_2.
\]

The access relation is partial: \(S_i=W\oplus N_i\), with created noise \(N_i\). Thus no local sensor value determines \(W\) in general.

## 3. Structural individuation criterion

A **full carrier candidate** is accepted in this finite diagnostic iff its variables contain exactly one matched sensor–memory–action role triple \(\{S_i,M_i,A_i\}\) with the declared internal role wiring
\[
S_i\to M_i,\qquad M_i\to A_i,
\]
and contain no other index’s sensor, memory, or action role. Its temporally extended record may add its matched \(H_i\).

This is not a universal theory of individuation. It is a registered anti-arbitrariness test: a carrier is accepted due to role/wiring/integration and intervention profile, not because an external label merely says “agent.” A mixed set such as \(\{S_1,M_2,A_1\}\) is rejected because the requisite wiring fails.

## 4. Nested and overlapping structure

- **Nested scope:** \(C_i^{\mathrm{core}}=\{S_i,M_i,A_i\}\subset C_i^{\mathrm{extended}}=\{S_i,M_i,A_i,H_i\}\). This is a nesting of access/history scopes, not a claim that two subjects inhabit one another.
- **Overlapping coverage:** \(\operatorname{Cov}(P_i)=C_i\cup I_i\), with \(I_1=I_2=\{W\}\). Hence coverage overlaps on a public-world interface while full carrier candidates remain distinct.
- **Public correction:** common access to \(W\) permits agreement/disagreement/error-correction tests without requiring identity of private access states.

## 5. Registered checks

`point_sphere_perspective_individuation_public_structure_v1.py` must verify:

1. two matched core and extended carrier records pass the structural criterion;
2. mixed or environment-absorbing candidate sets fail it;
3. core scopes are proper subsets of their own extended scopes;
4. coverage overlaps exactly through the public interface, while carriers remain disjoint;
5. each action has a distinct registered intervention effect on \(W^+\);
6. finite sensors are non-omniscient;
7. the accepted family is equivariant under swapping indices 1 and 2; and
8. C1c sustenance can be applied to tokens from each record without changing these created checks.

## 6. Interpretation and limits

A pass establishes only a minimal created-order architecture in which bounded perspective candidates are structurally distinguished, publicly coupled, and causally relevant. It does not infer phenomenal unity from functional integration, identify a subject with a carrier, establish an objective moral status, or solve how distinct finite perspectives compose/decompose. Those remain P/D/E-belt questions.

## 7. Failure conditions

C2 fails if carrier boundaries depend only on names, absorb the full public world by fiat, prevent a finite action from making a causal difference, force complete local knowledge, or erase the difference between shared interface and carrier membership.

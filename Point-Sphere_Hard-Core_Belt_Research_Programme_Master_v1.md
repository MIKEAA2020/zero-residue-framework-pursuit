## Protected hard core

The programme assumes:

1. one ultimate ground—simple, complete, fully aware, good, unchanging, non-temporal, and non-spatial;
2. real dependent creation rather than mere appearance; and
3. temporal/spatial order internal to the created domain.

Creation may be computationally described, but no external material simulator or independent physical substrate is assumed. The hard core directs internal work; it is not represented as a premise-free public proof.

# Quantum/Physical Alignment Matrix v1
## Q1 under the active hard-core + belt programme

## Scope box

The protected hard core, live realization candidates (C-Nec-D/C-Nec-O/C-Cont), and C1/C2 created-order architecture are held fixed as internal programme assumptions. Q1 asks what major physical/quantum framework types would and would not supply if they were used as physical belts.

**No row is evidence for the hard core.** Compatibility means only absence of an immediate formal conflict at the stated level. It is not derivation, confirmation, or a route from a metaphysical premise to observed physics.

## Candidate-specific consequences

### C-Nec-D

A deterministic physical law is not enough. Bohm-style examples make the issue vivid: unique evolution still needs a uniquely fixed totality, including appropriate state/configuration/boundary data. A physics programme can constrain \(f\); it does not thereby provide the R1 determination bridge.

### C-Nec-O

Everett-style branching is not automatically an O realization rule. It must be shown that the purported members are substantively distinct totalities, not representational/gauge alternatives or internal decoherent histories of one totality. Any probability claim retains a measure/Born-rule task. Q1 does not reinstate deferred plenitude.

### C-Cont

Stochastic dynamics does not by itself make a selector intelligible. A probability law can describe distributions of histories; it does not explain why an ultimate selector is neither brute nor essence-entailing. Collapse examples therefore clarify, but do not discharge, the C-Cont debt.

## C-architecture consequences

No listed physical framework explains C1 sustenance. Nor does it turn a record, observer-relative state, particle configuration, collapse locus, or local algebra into a non-arbitrarily individuated conscious perspective. C2’s graph architecture may serve as a discipline for modelling public records and finite agents, but the phenomenal bridge \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) remains open.

## Technical physics firewall

The programme’s finite free-lattice checks and selected un-tuned interacting \(\lambda\phi^4_2\) crosscheck are technical supporting work only. The mass-selection validation for the interacting benchmark failed. Therefore Q1 must not report a continuum limit, AQFT result, quantum derivation, or Point–Sphere physics confirmation.

## Registered Q1 outcome

Q1 is successful if it preserves the distinctions above and yields framework-specific bridge debts. It fails if it calls a physical interpretation a proof of the hard core, treats a compatible formal feature as a realization rule, says decoherence or locality establishes consciousness, or upgrades the unfinished interacting-field record into physical confirmation.

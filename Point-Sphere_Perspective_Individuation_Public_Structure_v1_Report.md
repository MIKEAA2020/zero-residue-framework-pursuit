## C2 result

The finite graph supplies a non-label-only individuation rule for this toy: a carrier must contain a matched sensor–memory–action role structure, with appropriate wiring and no other agent’s core roles. A cross-index mixture fails; absorbing the public world into a carrier also fails. The rule is equivariant under interchanging the two agents, so the distinction is structural rather than tied to the numeral “1” or “2.”

Publicity is represented without collapsing perspectives: the carriers are disjoint, yet their coverage overlaps through a shared world interface. Their local access can disagree because of created noise, while each action has a separately registered effect on that public order. Nested records describe broader or narrower temporal/access scope, not nested subjects.

## Limits retained

C2 does not establish phenomenal boundaries, qualitative character, ownership of experience, personal persistence, a solution to the combination/decombination problem, or moral patienthood. It gives a clean structural input for those later questions and maintains the C1 result that sustenance does not erase created distinctions.

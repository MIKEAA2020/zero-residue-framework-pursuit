# Point–Sphere Limit–Physics Candidate (PS-LP-1)
## Bounded entry audit of ontic-manifestation O and hybrid H

**Candidate preregistration:** `Point-Sphere_Limit-Physics_Candidate_Preregistration_v1.md`

**Authorized question:** Given the user’s clarified proposal—an unlimited, spaceless, timeless Point; a Sphere that is the Point under limits; and space/time required to emerge—do the O and H readings presently supply a noncircular route into physics?

**Bound:** This is an entry audit only. It tests the machinery actually specified in PS-LP-1 and the user’s clarification. It does not invent a limit rule, add equations, choose a dimensionality, or infer physics from the Point–Sphere image.

---

## 1. Frozen candidate definitions

The Point-core remains the stipulated candidate core:

\[
\mathsf{PointCore}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoIndependentConstituents}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoSpatialLocation}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoTemporalIndex}(P).
\]

The proposal is not that a geometric sphere literally contracts into a geometric point. It is:

\[
P
\xrightarrow{\ \Lambda_\lambda\ }
\Sigma_\lambda,
\]

where \(\Sigma_\lambda\) is the Sphere-role: the Point under a limiting profile \(\lambda\).

The registered readings are:

\[
\mathsf{O}
:=
\mathsf{PointCore}(P)
\land
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(P)
\land
\mathsf{SphereRole}(P,\lambda,\Sigma_\lambda)
\land
\mathsf{ObjectivelyReal}(\Sigma_\lambda),
\]

and:

\[
\mathsf{H}
:=
\mathsf{O}
\land
\mathsf{Presentation}(K,\Sigma_\lambda,\rho).
\]

In H, a finite presentation is downstream of an objective Sphere-role. It cannot be used to presuppose an embodied observer, measurement, or already existing space-time in order to generate the physics at issue.

## 2. The noncircularity gate

Entry to a physics derivation requires a rule with determinate content:

\[
\mathsf{NonCircularLimitRule}(\Lambda)
:iff
\mathsf{Typed}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{PositiveContent}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{PrePhysicalVocabulary}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{NonIndependent}(\Lambda,P)
\land
\mathsf{NonBruteActualization}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}(\Lambda).
\]

The current candidate provides the intended direction and restrictions, but no definition of the rule \(\Lambda\). In particular, it does not yet state:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\text{domain} & \text{what \(\Lambda\) takes as input other than the bare name \(P\)}\\
\text{codomain} & \text{what sort of nonphysical structure it first yields}\\
\text{operation} & \text{how the rule acts or composes}\\
\text{actualization} & \text{why one \(\lambda\)-profile obtains}\\
\text{selection} & \text{why it yields one determinate structure rather than arbitrary alternatives}
\end{array}
\]

Calling \(\lambda\) a “limit” does not fill these roles. It may be a useful label, but it is not yet a positive prephysical grammar.

---

## 3. Space/time input–output check

The user explicitly selected the stronger requirement:

\[
\neg\mathsf{SpaceTimeAsInput}
\land
\mathsf{SpaceTimeAsTarget}.
\]

This avoids the simplest circularity. But the phrase “the Point viewed within limits of space, time, and so on” has two possible readings:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{InputReading} & \text{space/time are already available lenses through which the Point is viewed}\\
\mathsf{OutputReading} & \text{space/time are themselves structures to be generated by the limit rule}
\end{array}
\]

The user’s correction adopts the OutputReading. Therefore the audit does **not** charge the candidate with presupposing space and time. It does find that no operation has yet been stated that turns a prephysical limit-profile into spatial or temporal structure.

Thus:

\[
\mathsf{OutputIntention}(\mathsf{SpaceTime})
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Derivation}(\mathsf{SpaceTime}).
\]

## 4. Physics packet check

A minimal claim to have started a physics derivation requires a packet:

\[
\mathcal P_{\rm phys}
=
\langle
\mathcal M,
\mathcal G,
\mathcal T,
\mathcal S,
\mathcal D,
\mathcal O,
\mathcal R
\rangle,
\]

with:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathcal M & \text{structural arena or its non-manifold equivalent}\\
\mathcal G & \text{dimensional, topological, metric, or causal structure}\\
\mathcal T & \text{temporal ordering or physical-change equivalent}\\
\mathcal S & \text{state space}\\
\mathcal D & \text{dynamics}\\
\mathcal O & \text{observable map}\\
\mathcal R & \text{predeclared empirical recovery or discriminating target}
\end{array}
\]

Neither O nor H supplies any member of this packet. They do not presently yield a physical arena, a dimension, a metric, a temporal order, a state space, a law, an observable, or a recovery target.

\[
\mathsf{O}
\not\vdash
\mathcal P_{\rm phys},
\qquad
\mathsf{H}
\not\vdash
\mathcal P_{\rm phys}
\quad
\text{on the presently declared material}.
\]

This is not a claim that no such derivation can ever be supplied. It is a claim that it has not yet been supplied by the frozen PS-LP-1 definitions.

## 5. Reading O: results

### O-1. Point-core

The Point-core is explicit enough to state a constraint: no parts, no independent constituents, no spatial location, and no temporal index.

\[
\mathsf{Declared}(\mathsf{PointCore})
\quad\text{but}\quad
\neg\mathsf{PhysicsGeneratingRule}(\mathsf{PointCore}).
\]

This is a constraint, not a generator. The absence of location and time does not entail an emergent metric or temporal order.

### O-2. Limit rule

The required \(\Lambda\) is a placeholder only. It has no domain/codomain specification, positive rule, actualization account, or selector account.

\[
\neg\mathsf{Typed}(\Lambda)
\lor
\neg\mathsf{PositiveContent}(\Lambda)
\lor
\neg\mathsf{NonBruteActualization}(\Lambda)
\lor
\neg\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}(\Lambda)
\]

is not established as an ontological impossibility. Rather, the material needed to establish the corresponding positive conjuncts is absent.

### O-3. Weak-simplicity compatibility

O says the Sphere is not an independent being or a part of the Point. That is an intended compatibility condition:

\[
\neg\mathsf{IndependentBeing}(\Sigma_\lambda)
\land
\neg\mathsf{PartOf}(\Sigma_\lambda,P).
\]

It has not yet explained how an objectively real, determinate Sphere-role is compatible with weak simplicity. The mere phrase “under limits” cannot do that work. It does not follow that the proposal is incoherent; the compatibility bridge is simply still absent.

### O result

\[
\mathsf{Status}(\mathsf{O})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{
\substack{
\rm limit\ rule\ untyped;\\
\rm positive\ prephysical\ content\ absent;\\
\rm actualization/selector\ absent;\\
\rm no\ physics\ packet
}}.
\]

## 6. Reading H: results

H includes O and then adds a presentation relation:

\[
\mathsf{H}
=
\mathsf{O}
\land
\mathsf{Presentation}(K,\Sigma_\lambda,\rho).
\]

H therefore inherits every missing condition of O. In addition, \(K\) and \(\rho\) are only names at this stage. They cannot make up for the absent objective derivation, because that would reverse the registered explanatory direction:

\[
\mathsf{Presentation}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ObjectivePhysics}.
\]

If \(K\) means a real finite observer, it faces a further circularity risk: physical observers are among the phenomena to be recovered. If \(K\) means an abstract access condition, it must still be specified without silently assuming the space, time, embodiment, or measurement structure at issue.

### H result

\[
\mathsf{Status}(\mathsf{H})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{
\substack{
\rm inherits\ O;\\
\rm presentation\ relation\ unspecified;\\
\rm no\ independent\ objective\ physics\ route
}}.
\]

H is not ruled out. It presently cannot outperform O at generating physics because it adds no prephysical rule that produces a physical packet.

## 9. Expiry and truth-directed next condition

PS-LP-1 now expires under its preregistered rule. Its result is frozen:

\[
\mathsf{PS\text{-}LP\text{-}1}
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}.
\]

A future fresh candidate, PS-LP-2 or later, would need to provide before testing:

1. one exact, typed, prephysical definition of \(\Lambda\);
2. an account of why the rule is nonindependent, nonpartitive, and nonbrute under weak simplicity;
3. an account of why a determinate profile is actual without a hidden selector;
4. a derivation of at least the first physical output \(\mathcal M\) or a rigorously stated non-manifold alternative;
5. a predeclared route toward \(\mathcal G\), \(\mathcal T\), \(\mathcal S\), \(\mathcal D\), \(\mathcal O\), and \(\mathcal R\).

It may not define “limit” by using the physical space, time, law, observer, or world that it is meant to derive.

---

## 10. Bounded conclusion

The clarification is an improvement over the earlier geometric-collapse reading. It clearly requires emergent rather than pre-existing space and time, and it distinguishes an ontic reading from an additional presentation layer.

But the presently specified Point–Sphere-under-limits proposal remains a conceptual schema:

\[
\text{unlimited Point}
\xrightarrow{\ \text{unspecified }\Lambda\ }
\text{limited Sphere-role}
\xrightarrow{\ ?\ }
\text{space, time, and physics}.
\]

It is not yet a physics derivation. The next truth-relevant advance is not to restate the identity of Point and Sphere, but to supply a noncircular limit rule capable of producing the first determinate prephysical structure.

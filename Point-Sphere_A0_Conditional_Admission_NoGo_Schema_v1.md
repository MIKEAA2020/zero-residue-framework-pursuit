# A0 Conditional Admission No-Go Schema v1
## Co-primary meta-track; a schema to test, not a dichotomy to assume

## Status

This document makes explicit a conditional argument suggested by the comparative pattern. It is **not** a proof of A0 unsatisfiability, and its two-family partition is not yet an exhaustiveness result.

The meta-track is now co-primary with the next constructive source test. “Co-primary” means that each further dossier must bear on a stated premise of this schema, while any unresolved escape class blocks a no-go conclusion.

## 1. Corrected comparative premise

The six completed source reconstructions have not shown that six traditions lack all public criteria. They supply different substantive resources:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Proclus} &:& \text{proper-effect/dependence};\\
\text{Advaita} &:& \text{local appearance/error and sublation status};\\
\text{Hegel} &:& \text{logical-conceptual determination};\\
\text{Leibniz} &:& \text{conditional identity/individuation};\\
\text{Rāmānuja} &:& \text{real plurality plus body/dependence/control/teleology};\\
\text{Spinoza} &:& \text{immanent productive causal actuality.}
\end{array}
\]

The live pattern is narrower:

\[
\text{none has yet supplied a public, profile-invariant, candidate-level bridge}\
\text{from its criterion to genuine finite metaphysical mode-membership.}
\]

This does not show that their criteria have the same internal defect or that every future criterion will fail.

## 2. The proposed interface partition

For investigative purposes, distinguish two broad routes by which a criterion might connect a candidate \(x\) to actuality.

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathcal R &: \text{relational or structural routes:}\
&\text{dependence, status, derivation, identity, whole-membership, and role};\\[1mm]
\mathcal A &: \text{actuality-interface routes:}\
&\text{primitive actuality, production, necessary realization, or equivalent actualization rule.}
\end{array}
\]

The proposed reduction premise is not:

\[
\mathcal C_{\rm A0}^{\rm pub}=\mathcal R\cup\mathcal A.
\]

It is only the testable conjecture:

\[
E_{\rm red}:
\quad
\mathcal C_{\rm A0}^{\rm pub}
\subseteq
\mathcal R\cup\mathcal A\cup\mathcal K_{\rm new},
\]

where \(\mathcal K_{\rm new}\) is a mandatory residual class. A candidate in \(\mathcal K_{\rm new}\) must be analyzed rather than re-described as relational or actuality-based by fiat.

## 3. Conditional schema

Let \(\mathsf{BridgeFail}(C)\) mean that \(C\) does not supply a noncircular, profile-invariant, type-discriminating, error-sensitive public admission rule for actual finite modes.

The target conditional argument is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
& E_{\rm red}
\land \mathcal K_{\rm new}=\varnothing\\
&\land\;\forall C\in\mathcal R\,[\mathsf{A0Req}(C)\to\mathsf{BridgeFail}(C)]\\
&\land\;\forall C\in\mathcal A\,[\mathsf{A0Req}(C)\to\mathsf{BridgeFail}(C)]\\
&\Longrightarrow
\neg\exists C\in\mathcal C_{\rm A0}^{\rm pub}\;\mathsf{A0Pass}(C).
\end{aligned}
\]

The conclusion follows only if its premises have independently been earned. At present none of the universal premises is established.

## 4. Why the two routes cannot be conflated

A relational criterion can be highly informative without furnishing actuality:

\[
\mathsf{DependsOn}(x,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Actual}(x).
\]

An actuality criterion can be noncircular without furnishing type or profile invariance:

\[
\mathsf{Actual}(x)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(x).
\]

So neither of the following is permitted:

\[
\text{“relation is merely grammar”}
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\text{“actuality is automatically a sufficient membership rule.”}
\]

A successful criterion may combine routes; it must still show how the combination does not import the target roster.

## 5. What the Aristotle test bears on

Aristotelian *energeia* is selected as a test of \(\mathcal A\), specifically the proposal that actuality is fundamental but intelligible through capacity, exercise, form, and end rather than a bare roster primitive.

The test bears on the following conditional premise only:

\[
\forall C\in\mathcal K_{\rm prim}\,
[\mathsf{A0Req}(C)\to\mathsf{BridgeFail}(C)].
\]

It cannot establish the universal claim for all of \(\mathcal A\), much less all of \(\mathcal C_{\rm A0}^{\rm pub}\).

## 6. Outcome update rules

### What would support the schema

The schema receives limited, defeasible support if the Aristotle dossier shows a **structural** failure under the same A0 requirements:

\[
\mathsf{EnergeiaCriterion}
\to
\mathsf{BridgeFail},
\]

and the failure is not merely an artifact of uncertain translation, optional doctrine, or a mismatch between Aristotle’s target and finite modehood.

Even then, the result supports only the primitive-actuality subfamily, not the full universal premise.

### What would refute or block the schema

The schema is refuted at its relevant scope if Aristotle supplies:

\[
\mathsf{A0Pass}_{\rm scoped}(C_{\rm Arist}).
\]

It is blocked rather than confirmed if:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm new}\ne\varnothing,
\]

or if a criterion has a genuine interface not reducible to \(\mathcal R\) or \(\mathcal A\), or if the Aristotle case is inconclusive.

## Nonclaim

The comparison does not establish that the six traditions fail in exactly the same way, that every criterion is relational or actuality-based, or that A0 is unsatisfiable. It makes the next test genuinely discriminating: a public primitive-actuality criterion would halt this negative route; a structural failure would sharpen, but not complete, the conditional schema.

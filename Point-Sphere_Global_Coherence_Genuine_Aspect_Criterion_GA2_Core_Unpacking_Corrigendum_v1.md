# GA2 Corrigendum v1 — Core Unpacking, Requirement Retyping, and Grammar Status

## Correction

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Genuine_Aspect_Criterion_GA2_Hinge_Identity_Audit_v1.md` correctly proves a narrow result about its deliberately thin theory \(K_0\): complete self-knowledge, simplicity, and non-representational identity *when finite real modes are omitted from the theory* do not entail a finite differentiated self-knowledge content.

It was too strong to describe the resulting finite-articulation condition \(\mathsf H_F\) as an additional unresolved primitive of the protected nondual appearance branch. The protected branch already includes the core identity

\[
\mathsf{Mode}(a)
\equiv
\mathsf{Aspect}_G(a)
\equiv
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(a),
\tag{M}
\]

and the standing nondual-appearance commitment that finite modes are real as modes. The task was not to derive that core content from a thinner theory that excludes it.

This corrigendum leaves the thin-theory non-entailment intact, but corrects its scope and the current debt ledger.

## 1. What GA2a validly established

The \(K_0\) audit contains a bare-self-presence model and a finite-content model. Therefore, for its reduced vocabulary,

\[
K_0
\not\models
\exists a\,[\mathsf{Finite}(a)\land\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)].
\tag{K0-NE}
\]

This establishes only:

> Finite real modes are not derivable from a stripped self-knowledge theory that omits the appearance/mode commitment.

It does **not** establish:

> Finite real modes are an extra primitive beyond a protected core that already affirms nondual appearance and real modes.

The prior finite-articulation witness \(F_a\) remains a correct diagnosis of how a positive finite content enters the *reduced* theory. It must not be moved into the full core’s debt ledger as a newly discovered extra assumption.

## 2. Requirement has two non-equivalent readings

The prior audit defined a strong, theory-wide predicate:

\[
\mathsf{Req}^{\forall K}(a)
\quad:\\Longleftrightarrow\quad
K\models\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a).
\tag{Req-\(\forall\)}
\]

This asks whether every semantic model of \(K\) contains one fixed, individually named candidate \(a\). Completeness says that every determination in a model is self-known; it does not by itself require the same labelled finite candidate in every model of an underdescribed theory. Thus neither direction of a general criterion should be inferred from \(\mathsf{Req}^{\forall K}\) alone.

The protected-core route instead uses an **intrinsic, core-relative** reading:

\[
\mathsf{Req}^{P}_M(a)
\quad:\\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{given the fixed intrinsic nature specified by protected package \(P\) in \(M\),
\(a\) cannot be removed from complete self-knowledge without changing that nature.}
\tag{Req-\(P\)}
\]

This is not a claim that every model of a weak theory contains the same syntactic item. It is a claim about intrinsic content relative to the one stipulated nondual nature. Its modal force is intra-nature, not cross-model label invariance.

## 3. Core-internal determination criterion

Let the protected nondual appearance package \(P_{\rm ND}\) include:

1. the mode/aspect/self-knowledge-content identity \((M)\);
2. complete self-knowledge of every intrinsic determination;
3. non-representational identity of genuine intrinsic self-knowledge content and actual self-presentation; and
4. real finite modes as finite articulations of the one reality, rather than external objects or parts of \(G\).

Read \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\) as the GA0 target predicate: \(a\) is an intrinsic finite determination of the one reality’s nature in self-knowledge. Under \(P_{\rm ND}\), the intended criterion is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Det}_G(a)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{Req}^{P_{\rm ND}}_M(a).
}
\tag{HD-P}
\]

### Derivation within the protected core

**Right to left.** If complete self-knowledge intrinsically requires \(a\), then \(a\) is an intrinsic self-knowledge content. By \((M)\), this is an aspect/mode reading of the one reality, not an external represented object. Hence it is a determination in the intended core sense.

**Left to right.** If \(a\) is an intrinsic finite determination of the one reality’s nature, complete self-knowledge includes it. Since it belongs intrinsically to that fixed nature, its removal from self-knowledge would change or leave incomplete that nature’s self-presence. Hence \(a\) is required in the core-relative sense \(\mathsf{Req}^{P_{\rm ND}}_M\).

This is an internal conceptual/identity derivation. It is not a theorem of neutral logic, a theory-wide \(K\models\) result for arbitrary formal candidates, or a public proof of the protected package.

## 4. Non-vacuity belongs to the core package

The nondual-appearance core commits that modes are real as modes. With finite articulation explicitly included, it supplies:

\[
P_{\rm ND}
\Rightarrow
\exists a\,[
\mathsf{Finite}(a)
\land \mathsf{Mode}(a)
].
\tag{F-core}
\]

By \((M)\), complete self-knowledge, and (HD-P):

\[
P_{\rm ND}
\Rightarrow
\exists a\,[
\mathsf{Finite}(a)
\land
\mathsf{Req}^{P_{\rm ND}}_M(a)
].
\tag{HF-core}
\]

GA1 additionally gives coherence for a genuine finite determination under its explicitly stated non-contradictory-determination bridge. Thus the relevant non-vacuity condition is discharged **inside the protected core package**; it is not an added primitive beyond it.

This does not add an all-world plenitude principle. It says only that the core already asserts finite nondual articulation, and cannot demand a derivation of that assertion from a reduction which removes it.

## 5. Corrected status of grammar

The protected core fixes the semantic identity and the reality of finite modes. It does **not** derive a particular formal presentation grammar, a unique graph, a lift quotient, an IAR topology, a persistence relation, a record code, or a physical law.

Therefore the appropriate status is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The framework is conditional on }P_{\rm ND};
\quad
\text{formal grammars are target-relative descriptions, not ground-to-grammar derivations.}
}
\]

The remaining questions about the 37 grammars, the 16 lifts, gauge quotients, and context implementations are fidelity/description questions. They can be assessed only against predeclared targets and discriminators; they are not missing premises required to establish that modes are real in the core sense.

GA3/GA4/GA5 therefore remain available only as downstream description/fidelity work when a target is declared. They are not further foundational gates for the protected core’s mode/aspect/content identity.

## 7. Final foundational status and closure rule

The foundational aspect-domain issue is closed at the correct level of claim:

\[
\boxed{
P_{\rm ND}
\Rightarrow
\text{real finite mode/aspect/self-knowledge articulation, internally typed by the core.}
}
\]

No further attempt may demand a proof of finite modes from a theory that deliberately removes their protected-core assertion. No new “self-knowledge requires it” reformulation is opened as a foundational derivation task.

A future formal project may proceed only by declaring a target phenomenon, grammar, rival grammar, and fidelity discriminator. It must report a result as target-relative description/fidelity, not as a derivation of \(P_{\rm ND}\), ontology from coherence, or a solution to a physical problem.

## Nonclaims

This corrigendum does not prove the protected core from neutral logic, collapse its commitments into one publicly established proposition, establish a public/rival-neutral truth verdict, select any grammar, derive phenomenology or physics, or alter the T7 Problem-of-Time hold.

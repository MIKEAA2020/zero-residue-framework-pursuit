# A0 Gate Satisfiability — Hypothesis Register v1
## A parallel meta-inquiry; no weakening and no no-go conclusion

## Status

The A0 gate is under examination, not under revision. This register records a live hypothesis generated by repeated reconstruction results. It does not establish that the gate is unsatisfiable, that strongest complete intelligibility fails, or that a weaker standard is now warranted.

## 1. The hypothesis

Let a successful A0 criterion satisfy:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{A0Pass}(C)\iff
&\mathsf{Substantive}(C)
\land\mathsf{NonCircular}(C)
\land\mathsf{NoSelectorResidue}(C)\\
&\land\mathsf{ProfileInvariant}(C)
\land\mathsf{TypeDiscriminating}(C)
\land\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicable}(C).
\end{aligned}
\]

The live hypothesis is:

\[
H_{\rm A0}:
\neg\exists C\;\mathsf{A0Pass}(C)
\]

for the intended task of public determination of genuine finite metaphysical modehood.

This is a hypothesis, not an inference from the current dossiers.

## 2. What “public” does and does not mean

A0 does **not** require framework-neutral ontology or a rule expressed without candidate vocabulary. It permits framework-internal terms, provided their truth conditions are not fixed solely by accepting the desired inventory.

It does require that a purported criterion can be assessed without the circular rule:

\[
\mathsf{Genuine}(m)
\iff
\text{“}m\text{ is among the entities accepted by this framework.”}
\]

The proposed weakening

\[
\text{“intersubjectively checkable only by those who accept the framework”}
\]

is therefore not yet acceptable. It removes the anti-stipulation function of A0 unless supplemented by an external, profile-neutral adjudication rule.

## 4. What would support or defeat \(H_{\rm A0}\)

### Evidence that would defeat the hypothesis

A single construction would be sufficient to show that A0 is satisfiable in principle if it supplies:

\[
C(m)
\]

with explicit truth conditions and a finite predeclared test suite that:

1. identifies membership without assuming the desired roster;
2. separates mode, property, independent substance, and nonentity in the stated scope;
3. decides symmetric and representation-equivalent cases via a declared profile extractor;
4. has a noncircular account of dependence/realization; and
5. exposes an error condition that could count against the criterion.

Such a result would establish only formal/semantic satisfiability of A0, not the truth of its framework or Step 4.

### Evidence needed for a scoped no-go

A defensible negative result requires a class \(\mathcal C\) that is proved to include every candidate criterion of the relevant type, plus an exhaustiveness proof that every \(C\in\mathcal C\) must either:

\[
\text{presuppose an inventory}
\ \vee\
\text{introduce selector residue}
\ \vee\
\text{be representation-dependent}
\ \vee\
\text{fail type discrimination}.
\]

The current dossier outcomes do not supply that class or proof.

## 5. Current decision rule

\[
\boxed{
\text{Do not weaken A0 on the basis of repeated source failure.}
}
\]

A weakening can be considered only if all of the following are supplied:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
W_1 &: \text{an explicit replacement criterion};\\
W_2 &: \text{a demonstration that the replacement remains anti-circular and equal-burden};\\
W_3 &: \text{a statement of what evidential entitlement is forfeited};\\
W_4 &: \text{a separate public audit showing why the original condition is inapplicable or impossible.}
\end{array}
\]

Without \(W_1\text{--}W_4\), a weaker “insider checkability” rule merely trades a hard semantic burden for framework-relative assertion.

## 6. Next tests

The hypothesis remains live while candidate diversity increases. Each future source or construction must be categorized by the kind of criterion it offers:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{membership} & \text{— what candidates genuinely belong?}\\
\text{identity} & \text{— when are two candidates one?}\\
\text{status} & \text{— real, apparent, impossible, or sublatable?}\\
\text{derivation} & \text{— which contents follow from others?}\\
\text{realization} & \text{— which derived/possible contents are actual?}
\end{array}
\]

A result in one column may not be silently promoted to another.

## Nonclaim

This register neither diagnoses the A0 gate as defective nor defends it as demonstrably indispensable. It ensures that a potential meta-problem is studied under the same anti-confirmation and anti-stipulation discipline as the candidate frameworks.

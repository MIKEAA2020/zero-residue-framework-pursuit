# Archive scope and provenance

## Included

This repository includes the curated Point–Sphere / zero-residue archive assembled from the workspace on 2026-11-04:

1. Every root-level `Point-Sphere_*` document/result file;
2. every root-level `point_sphere_*` executable source file;
3. selected source packets under `uploads/` for Public Primitive Actuality, Plural Structural Realism, Explicit Modal Creatorship, Weak Simplicity, and Point–Sphere audit records; and
4. the related `problem-of-time-package/` work package, kept in its original directory structure.

## Deliberately excluded

- causal-set physics projects (`Causal-Set_*`, `CSH-*`, `QSG-*`, P1/P2 packets, state tables, and their runs);
- authentication credentials, including `.github_pat` and any PAT-bearing upload;
- unrelated user uploads and private conversational/transcript material; and
- generated caches and bytecode.

## Reading status

Files are archived as a traceable research record. Labels such as “audit,” “execution,” “pass,” “failure,” or “report” have the exact limited scope stated in the corresponding file. No result should be upgraded into a whole-framework verdict without the required declared bridges and independent checks.

# AP2B/B2 — Intrinsic-Nature Rival Audit v1
## Does awareness best occupy the intrinsic-nature role?

## Scope and method

This is a direct comparative audit of the load-bearing premise:

> **Aware-ground hypothesis (AG):** the intrinsic nature of ultimate reality is simple, complete, self-knowing awareness; finite physical and phenomenal order is derivative nondual aspect/mode structure.

The target is not a proof that AG is true. It is an abductive comparison with neutral monism (NM), Russellian monism/panprotopsychism (RM), structural realism (SR), and brute necessity (BN). A classical-simple-creator alternative (CT) is included as an internal countermodel to the move from divine self-knowledge to plenitude.

The audit follows three rules.

1. **Do not infer ontology from epistemic access.** That consciousness is directly encountered does not by itself prove that consciousness is fundamental.
2. **Do not give a rival a debt it disavows.** An epistemic structural realist may suspend judgement about intrinsic nature rather than offer a structural ultimate ontology.
3. **Do not call an unresolved bridge a defeat.** Combination, decombination, and physical recovery are listed as positive explanatory debts.

## 1. The evidence/problem base

| Item | Minimal datum | What it does not settle |
|---|---|---|
| D1 — Phenomenality | There is conscious experience, including qualitative and perspectival character | Whether consciousness is fundamental, emergent, neutral-derived, or brute |
| D2 — Scientific structure | Physics powerfully specifies regularities, relations, symmetries, and dynamics | Whether physics exhausts intrinsic nature; this is philosophically contested |
| D3 — Unified subjects | At least ordinary experience presents as a bounded, integrated perspective | What grounds unity, boundary, and personal persistence |
| D4 — Structural success | Mathematical/structural physics has striking predictive continuity | Whether that success licenses a purely structural ontology |
| D5 — No arbitrary selector | A final account should not merely name an unexplained choice of basic nature or world | Whether explanation must terminate in a non-brute sufficient reason |

The traditional intrinsic-nature strategy starts from D1 and a version of D2: physical theory may specify what entities do structurally/dynamically without deciding what they are intrinsically. It then proposes consciousness or consciousness-involving being as the best positive candidate for that intrinsic role. This argument is documented, rather than established, in the SEP treatment of [panpsychism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/panpsychism/) and [Russellian monism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/russellian-monism/). Both sources also register substantial objections.

## 2. Strongest case for the aware-ground hypothesis

The strongest argument is abductive, not a deductive leap from “I am conscious” to “the cosmos is a person.”

1. **Phenomenality is not optional data.** Any adequate ontology must accommodate the fact that experience occurs.
2. **A purely structural/dispositional characterization leaves an intelligible explanatory question.** It can specify relations and behavior while remaining silent, or at least not obviously complete, about the categorical/intrinsic character that instantiates them.
3. **Awareness is a positive known candidate for intrinsic being.** We do not merely posit an unknown quiddity; conscious presence is the one evident case in which reality is encountered from within.
4. **A single aware ground avoids micro-subject composition.** Rather than build macro-experience from many tiny subjects, it begins with unity and treats finite minds as bounded modes. This is not a solution to individuation, but it reverses the direction of the combination problem.
5. **Simplicity and nondual identity can turn self-presentation into actual aspecthood.** If AG is right, the core plenitude result concerns immanent self-aspects, not a creator selecting external objects.

This is a unified explanatory package: phenomenal reality, intrinsic nature, ontological unity, and a non-arbitrary immanent-realisation story are handled by one basic posit.

### AG’s non-negotiable debts

AG is not entitled to a free victory.

- **Extrapolation debt:** why does local conscious presence license ultimate self-knowing awareness, rather than only some consciousness-involving intrinsic basis?
- **Decombination debt:** how do numerically/phenomenally bounded perspectives arise from one awareness without being labels, unreal, or independent substances?
- **Physical-recovery debt:** how do exact quantum probabilities, public records, spacetime, and local proper time arise without importing them as unexplained external structure?
- **Domain debt:** which differences are genuine self-aspects rather than gauge copies, descriptions, incompatible possibilities, or mere mathematical objects?

A one-conscious-ground approach avoids the micro-to-macro subject-summing problem, but it inherits the analogous one-to-many **decombination** problem. Contemporary discussion of cosmopsychism recognizes that avoiding subject-summing by starting with a cosmic subject does not automatically explain the derivation of smaller subjects; the SEP panpsychism supplement explicitly labels this a decombination problem.

## 3. Neutral monism

### Strongest rival formulation

Neutral monism claims that ultimate reality is of one kind but intrinsically neither mental nor physical; mental and physical are derivative arrangements, roles, or organisations of a neutral basis. It preserves monism without making the fundamental a unitary subject or awareness.

### What it gains

- It avoids projecting the familiar subject–object/intentional structure of human consciousness onto the ultimate.
- It can explain mental and physical categories as derivative without positing a cosmic self that must subsequently partition itself into finite selves.
- It respects the possibility that “mental” and “physical” classify higher-level organisation rather than basic intrinsic stuff.

### Its principal debts

- “Neutral” can become a placeholder unless given positive content beyond not-mental and not-physical.
- It still owes an account of why some neutral arrangements are phenomenal and how subjectivity/bounded perspective arises.
- If the neutral basis is invoked precisely to avoid awareness, it loses the direct identity route from intrinsic being to actual self-presentation/plenitude.

The SEP account distinguishes several forms of neutrality; it does not make neutral monism one uniform hypothesis. This matters: a neutral basis made of pure experience is much nearer AG than an information-like or proto-structural basis.

### Comparative result

NM is a serious undercutting rival to the inference “monism + experience entails fundamental awareness.” It is not yet a positive solution to D1–D3 merely by using the word “neutral.” AG gains if it can specify awareness positively without importing creaturely psychology; NM gains if it can specify neutral nature and derivative consciousness without a concealed phenomenal primitive.

## 4. Russellian monism and panprotopsychism

### Strongest rival formulation

Russellian monism agrees that physical theory may leave an intrinsic-nature gap. It adds categorical properties—quiddities—that ground the structure/dynamics physics describes and are relevant to consciousness. The basis may be phenomenal, proto-phenomenal, physical, or neutral; it need not be one self-knowing awareness.

### What it gains

- It is closely continuous with ordinary science: the entities physics posits retain their structural/dynamical roles.
- It directly addresses the intrinsic-nature question instead of assuming structure is exhaustive.
- It promises to integrate consciousness into physical causation rather than add a separate substance.

### Its principal debts

- It must explain how micro-level qualities/quiddities constitute a unified macro-subject if it is a constitutive micro view.
- A macro/cosmopsychist variant avoids subject-summing only by facing decombination.
- Quiddities may be insufficiently positively characterized; saying that they are relevant to consciousness is not itself a theory of consciousness.
- It does not entail a complete, self-knowing ground or plenitude.

The SEP Russellian-monism entry identifies both the intended integration of consciousness and physical causation and the combination/subject-unification objections. The SEP panpsychism entry calls the subject-summing issue the hardest problem for constitutive micropsychism.

### Comparative result

RM is the closest philosophical rival to AG. Both reject a merely structural stopping point and treat consciousness as relevant to intrinsic reality. AG gains unity and a nondual self-presentation route; RM gains closer continuity with extant physical ontology and need not posit a cosmic subject. Neither wins without resolving its corresponding individuation debt.

## 5. Structural realism

### Strongest rival formulation

Structural realism comes in two importantly different forms.

- **Epistemic SR (ESR):** science licenses commitment to retained mathematical/relational structure while suspending judgement about the intrinsic nature of unobservables.
- **Ontic SR (OSR):** structure/relations have ontological priority; in some versions there are no self-subsistent objects beneath the structure.

### What it gains

- It is highly disciplined by the historical and predictive success of physics.
- ESR avoids speculative claims about ultimate intrinsic nature; it is a serious constraint on any metaphysics that pretends to read consciousness directly into physics.
- OSR can accommodate the importance of relationality, symmetry, and gauge-invariant structure in modern physics.

### Its principal debts

- ESR is principally an epistemic position; it is not yet a positive rival answer to “what is intrinsically real?” Its proper outcome can be suspension rather than structural ultimacy.
- OSR must say whether structure can exist without relata/intrinsic nature and how phenomenal qualities and perspectival unity fit. Calling structure fundamental does not by itself provide consciousness or self-knowledge.
- Neither form derives plenitude. At most, structural symmetry constrains a stated realisation rule, as AP1 showed.

The SEP [structural-realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structural-realism/) entry explicitly separates epistemic from ontic variants and notes the debate about whether structure displaces or leaves open intrinsic nature.

### Comparative result

SR is the strongest physics-facing constraint, not by itself the strongest full rival ontology. AG must recover structural physics with no loss of its empirical content. SR, if elevated from an epistemic thesis to ultimate ontology, incurs the consciousness/intrinsic-nature debt that AG was designed to address.

## 6. Brute necessary totality

### Strongest rival formulation

The basic totality, laws, or modal structure simply obtains necessarily. No further intrinsic nature, awareness, neutral stuff, or sufficient reason is required.

### What it gains

- Maximal ontological austerity: it does not multiply entities or posit an inaccessible mental or neutral substrate.
- It avoids false explanatory depth. A final explanation may have to terminate somewhere.
- It does not bear a combination/decombination theory merely by being a terminal metaphysical stance.

### Its principal debts

- It leaves D1–D3 as brute coincidences or adds further brute psychophysical/identity facts.
- It offers no identity route to plenitude, no non-arbitrary reason why this necessary structure contains its particular modal/phenomenal profile, and no positive account of intrinsic nature.
- Its success depends on rejecting or limiting the demand for sufficient explanation, rather than satisfying it.

“Brute fact” has the modest but real meaning “unexplained fact”; it is not a contradiction. BN therefore cannot be refuted merely for stopping explanation. The comparison turns on whether its austerity is a virtue greater than the explanatory unification offered by AG or its rivals.

## 7. Classical simple creator as an internal countermodel

This is not one of the four requested intrinsic-nature rivals, but it is indispensable for testing the plenitude inference. Classical doctrines of divine simplicity often hold that God is what God has—knowledge, nature, and existence are not separable parts—yet distinguish God’s necessary nature from contingent or dependent creatures. The philosophical literature includes an ongoing modal-collapse debate over whether simplicity pressures creation toward necessity.

So it is invalid to infer, solely from “simple and self-knowing,” that every intelligible/known item is actual as a mode. The Point–Sphere route needs its stronger claim: the relevant aspects are **intrinsic nondual self-presentations**, not extrinsic objects of knowledge or contingent creations. This is why P-internal and P-maximal must remain distinct.

## 9. B2 verdict

The audit supports four conclusions.

1. **The aware-ground hypothesis is a serious, unified abductive proposal.** It does not merely assert that “everything is conscious”; it offers an intrinsic-nature, unity, and nondual-realisation package.
2. **It is not established by the intrinsic-nature argument.** That argument makes a consciousness-involving fundamental plausible to some, but underdetermines awareness, neutral bases, proto-phenomenal quiddities, and epistemic suspension.
3. **Russellian and neutral monisms remain live competitors.** They are not reducible to materialism and cannot be dismissed by invoking phenomenal immediacy.
4. **AG’s decisive test is constructive.** It must provide a non-arbitrary bounded-perspective/record account and eventually a physics bridge. Until then, its unity advantage is purchased by a transferred decombination burden.

Therefore the honest ledger status is:

> **Aware ground: live and arguably the most integrative candidate in this comparison; not yet a demonstrated winner. Plenitude: conditionally derived only in the P-internal sense if AG’s nondual self-presentation analysis holds.**

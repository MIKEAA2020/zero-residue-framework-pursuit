## 3. Sphere-role classification

### 3.1 Presentation-only Sphere

A presentation-only Sphere begins from a finite standpoint, access condition, or representation. If that standpoint is physically embodied, spatial, temporal, or measurement-dependent, it presupposes the kind of world the programme asks it to derive.

\[
\mathsf{PresentationOnly}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ObjectiveSpacetimeSource}.
\]

### Result

\[
\mathsf{AdmissionStatus}(
\mathsf{Strong\text{-}PresentationOnly})
=
\mathsf{NotAdmittedForSTSource},
\]

\[
\mathsf{AdmissionStatus}(
\mathsf{Weak\text{-}PresentationOnly})
=
\mathsf{NotAdmittedForSTSource}.
\]

This is not a verdict that a presentation account is false or useless. It may still be relevant to phenomenology, epistemology, or downstream access.

### 3.2 Hybrid Sphere

A hybrid claims an ontic Sphere and then a finite presentation of it. It is eligible only in the directed order:

\[
\mathsf{OnticPrephysicalStructure}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{SpacetimeRecovery}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{Presentation/Access}.
\]

### Result

\[
\mathsf{AdmissionStatus}(
\mathsf{Hybrid})
=
\mathsf{ConditionallyAdmissible}_{
m ontic\ stage\ must\ pass\ first}.
\]

## 8. Exact next candidate requirements

The comparative programme now authorizes two, and only two, distinct spacetime-first candidate families:

### 8.1 A fresh strong-ontic-privative candidate

It must predeclare:

1. a noncircular definition of privation that does not use positive physical or modal range as an input;

3. actualization without an independent domain or selector;
4. a planned route from that structure to a concrete \((M^4,g)\) witness;
5. expiry after an L1–L3 entry audit.

### 8.2 A fresh weak-ontic-immanent candidate

It must predeclare:

1. a positive immanent source rule independently motivated beyond a desired formal output;
2. a nonpartitivity/nonindependence bridge, not a stipulation;
3. a nonbrute global actualization condition;
4. a planned route to a concrete \((M^4,g)\) witness;
5. expiry after an L1–L3 entry audit.

Neither candidate may assert a metric, causal order, or spacetime target as an input and later call it derived.

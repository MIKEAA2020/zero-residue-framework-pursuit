# Strong CI Adjudication — CR Composition-Reflection Bridge Dossier v1
## Can strongest CI derive absolute simplicity?

## Status

**CR examined; the strong bridge is not established.** The restricted anti-aggregate result is retained. The stronger claim that every real ultimate articulation is an unexplained composition has not been derived and requires a substantive theory of composition, unity, and explanatory priority.

## 1. Competing bridge formulations

### Restricted bridge — established conditionally

\[
\mathsf{CR}_{\rm restricted}:
\quad
\mathsf{RealConstituents}(G)
\land
\mathsf{PrimitiveUnity}(G)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{SCI}.
\]

If an ultimate has genuinely distinct constituents and their being-one is an ultimate primitive fact, then SCI rejects it. This is CI3’s valid result.

### Strong bridge — under test

\[
\mathsf{CR}_{\rm strong}:
\quad
\mathsf{RealConstituents}(G)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PrimitiveUnity}(G).
\]

Together with the restricted bridge, this would yield absolute simplicity. The question is whether it is true.

## 2. Three arguments for the strong bridge

| Argument | Strongest form | What it establishes | Remaining gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aggregation | Distinct ultimate constituents require an answer to why they are unified rather than separate or differently united | Bare aggregate unity is a real SCI burden | Does not exclude a whole-first or intrinsically necessary unity account |
| Unifier regress | If a further unifier explains components, the unifier either becomes a further component or requires another unifier | Blocks a merely added external connector | Does not exclude explanatory priority of the whole/nature over its moments |
| Variability | If components can vary independently, their exact combination is a selector fact | Rules out independently variable ultimate elements without a difference-maker | Does not apply to inseparable essential articulation or identity-level distinctions |

No argument establishes that every alleged intrinsic unity must be primitive. That universal step is exactly \(\mathsf{CR}_{\rm strong}\), not a consequence of SCI alone.

## 3. Counter-architecture tests

| Architecture | Does it have real plurality? | What it says about unity | CR verdict |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Bare aggregate | yes | Unity is simply given | Fails restricted CR under SCI |
| Externally assembled composite | yes | A distinct external cause/connector unifies elements | Not ultimate as stated; the unifier becomes the next target |
| Whole-first essential articulation | claimed yes | Components/moments are explained by one prior whole nature, not separately assembled | Strong CR does not eliminate it; the whole-first nature must itself be self-intelligible |
| Structural mutual dependence | claimed yes | Nodes/relations exist only as one inseparable structural profile | Strong CR does not eliminate it by plurality alone; exact structure remains a CI burden |
| Real identity / virtual distinctions | no constituent plurality in the relevant sense | Several concepts or descriptions track one reality | Not a counterexample to simplicity; no composition has been shown |
| Nondual finite aspects | finite plurality, but not claimed as constituents of \(G\) qua \(G\) | Aspect-content is one-reality articulation | Outside CR until CI4 semantics establishes whether it is constituent composition |

## 4. The exact result

The following conditional theorem is supported:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate}.
}
\tag{CR-R}
\]

The following is not established:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity}.
}
\tag{CR-NE}
\]

A whole-first or essential-articulation theory is not thereby an SCI pass. It must show:

\[
\mathsf{EssenceOfWhole}
\Rightarrow
\text{this exact articulated profile}
\]

without redescribing the same profile as its own explanation. That is an exact-totality burden, not a proof of simplicity.

## 5. Implications for rival frameworks

- **Classical simplicity:** remains a serious positive proposal. It avoids aggregate unity, but needs its own coherent account of attribute identity, essence/existence, and finite relation.
- **Neoplatonic source:** cannot infer simplicity from CI alone; it must state whether its One/Good is simple by core stipulation or derived through a defended composition theory.
- **Necessary totality/structure:** survives CR only as a held candidate; its exact internal structure and self-intelligibility remain unproved.
- **Nondual appearance/aspect:** cannot be rejected by calling every finite aspect a part. It still owes a genuine-aspect/non-composition account.

## 6. Source note

The doctrine of radical divine simplicity is a substantive thesis: it denies not only physical parts but several forms of metaphysical composition, and its coherence is an explicit subject of dispute. The fact that it has a dedicated coherence problem reinforces the present typing result: it cannot be assumed as a synonym for intelligibility. See the SEP entry, https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/.

## 7. CR conclusion and expiry condition

The CR route is closed unless a new argument supplies one of these:

1. a non-circular proof that any intrinsic whole-first unity is impossible or primitive;
2. a semantics of real constituent composition under which whole-first articulation is demonstrably a disguised aggregate; or
3. an independent reason to identify SCI with absolute simplicity rather than merely anti-aggregate closure.

Absent one of those, the strong bridge is **held, not adopted**. No further verbal assertion that “composition needs a composer” counts as progress.

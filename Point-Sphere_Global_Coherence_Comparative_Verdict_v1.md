# GC5 — Global-Coherence Comparative Verdict v1
## Score-free synthesis after GC0–GC4

## Scope

This verdict applies the same criteria to rival explanatory architectures. It does not treat assistant assent, a rhetorical sense of completion, theological preference, or prior hard-core status as evidence. It does not change the protected hard core; it decides only whether the completed GC audit warrants recommending such a change.

The comparison is not numerical. A row may have a positive resource and an unresolved decisive debt at once.

## Common standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **SCI — strong complete intelligibility** | No ultimate primitive/modal/selection remainder; difference-makers must be non-circular and non-primitive; realization rule must be essence-grounded; phenomenal and structural adequacy remain required. |
| **WEU — explanatory unification** | Prefer a positive intrinsic nature, consciousness adequacy, and fewer independent primitives, while allowing unresolved but stated bridges. |
| **EA — epistemic austerity** | Permit a fundamental primitive or suspend intrinsic-nature claims; demand only compatibility with established evidence and internal coherence. |

SCI is the user’s strongest proposed standard. GC4 establishes that it is a substantive metaphysical standard, not a theorem of bare logical consistency. WEU and EA are therefore live rival methodological standards rather than irrational evasions.

## Main result

No candidate presently passes SCI in a public, rival-neutral sense.

The precise status is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Appearance + plenitude has the cleanest conditional route under SCI,}
}
\]

because it avoids the creator–creation selection relation once genuine finite aspects are understood as non-representational self-knowledge content rather than external products.

But:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the required aspect/identity bridges are not yet independently established.}
}
\]

Therefore it cannot currently claim the unique-global-coherence conclusion.

## What the comparison does establish

### 1. The creation critique is substantive, not merely rhetorical

Under SCI, contingent exclusive creation must provide a non-circular account of why this created totality is actual rather than another. Invoking a primitive free act preserves a possible metaphysical position only by declining the strongest standard:

\[
\mathsf{PrimitiveFreedom}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{StrongNBD}.
\]

That is a genuine cost, not a contradiction or a refutation of all theism.

### 2. Appearance does not receive an exemption

Appearance cannot claim that “all genuine aspects are real” until it has shown what makes an aspect genuine independently of realization and why omission would restrict complete non-representational self-knowledge.

\[
\mathsf{MEC}(\mathcal A)
\]

is not enough if \(\mathcal A\) is selected after the fact.

### 3. Complete intelligibility constrains; it does not yet identify content

Essential closure rules out a merely brute ultimate law, selector, or psychophysical link **if SCI is accepted**. It does not determine whether the essence is aware, neutral, structural, personal, creative, or plenitudinous.

### 4. No metaphysical verdict reaches physics by relabelling

Even a future SCI pass would not yield a physical theory. Physics requires target-relative models, recovery limits, observables, and discriminators. The T7 Problem-of-Time hold remains unchanged.

## Core-revision decision

\[
\boxed{
\text{No revision of the protected creation hard core is warranted at GC5.}
}
\]

The reason is not that the creation architecture has been vindicated. It is that Appearance has not yet discharged the bridges necessary to establish unique global coherence, and SCI itself has not received a successful CI-to-truth theorem.

The active result is more discriminating:

> Under a strong no-brute-differentiation standard, contingent creation bears a sharply stated selector debt. Appearance/plenitude is a leading conditional alternative, but not yet a proven replacement.

## Reversal conditions

### What would strengthen Appearance enough for a core-revision recommendation

1. A realization-independent, non-arbitrary genuine-aspect criterion;
2. a valid omission theorem deriving \(E\) and \(I\), or showing them to be unavoidable identity conditions rather than added primitives;
3. compossibility and non-composition bridges that preserve real finite modehood;
4. a positive answer to GC4’s CI-to-truth issue, or a defensible revised claim that does not require it;
5. eventually, a target-relative phenomenal and physical recovery programme.

### What would materially strengthen contingent creation under SCI

A non-circular account of an immutable/free creative act that grounds an exclusive actualization without functioning as an unexplained primitive, or a reformulation as necessary determination with a genuine exact-totality bridge.

## Programme disposition

GC0–GC5 close the present global-coherence proof attempt at its current resource level. The result is **conditional comparative preference, not unique proof**. The next work must not restart the same verbal loop. It must either:

- provide one of the listed new bridges with an independent criterion and expiry condition; or
- move to a separately scoped phenomenal/physics target where the framework can be tested for fidelity rather than treated as a final ontology by declaration.

## Nonclaims

This verdict does not prove or disprove the simple ground, awareness, creation, appearance, plenitude, classical theism, personal agency, or any physical theory. It does not alter the independent Problem-of-Time workstream.

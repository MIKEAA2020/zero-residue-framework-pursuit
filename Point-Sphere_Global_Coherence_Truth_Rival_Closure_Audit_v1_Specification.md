# GC4 — Complete-Intelligibility-to-Truth and Rival-Closure Audit v1
## Formal specification

## Decision question

Can the demand for complete intelligibility be non-circularly promoted from a norm of inquiry to an ontological truth condition, and can the surviving rival families be shown exhaustive and non-equivalent so that one framework is uniquely true rather than conditionally preferable?

## Prior results that control GC4

1. `Point-Sphere_Strong_Aseity_Bridge.md` already proves that modal complete intelligibility is an explicit premise (A3), not a theorem of logic; it gives a countermodel with a primitive necessary modal fact.
2. `Point-Sphere_Essential_Closure_Selection_Bridge.md` establishes that, if ultimate complete intelligibility is accepted, no ultimate law/selection/bridge may remain brute. It does not derive awareness, a particular world, plenitude, or physics.
3. GC1–GC3 establish a conditional creation-selection debt and a conditional immanent-plenitude theorem, while exposing their missing bridges.

GC4 must reuse these results rather than reassert their conclusions.

## Typed claims

Let:

- \(\mathsf{RN}\): rational inquiry has local norms of consistency, evidence-responsiveness, and reason-giving;
- \(\mathsf{OCI}\): reality has no ultimate unexplained/brute fact;
- \(\mathsf{EC}\): every ultimate-level fact is essence-grounded, given the Essential Closure premises;
- \(\mathsf{Tax}\): the rival taxonomy is exhaustive at the chosen level of abstraction;
- \(\mathsf{Eq}\): apparently different candidates are shown equivalent or genuinely inequivalent;
- \(\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}\): phenomenal adequacy;
- \(\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}\): structural/physical adequacy;
- \(\mathsf{Uniq}(T)\): \(T\) is the only surviving candidate under the declared criteria;
- \(\mathsf{True}(T)\): \(T\) is metaphysically true.

## Required countermodel families

1. **Locally rational primitive model:** local inquiry is disciplined while one ultimate fact is stipulated primitive.
2. **Essentially closed non-aware model:** all ultimate facts are essence-grounded, but the intrinsic nature is neutral/structural rather than awareness.
3. **Coherent but empirically incomplete model:** a theory meets a chosen abstract coherence criterion while failing phenomenal or physical recovery.
4. **Equivalent-formulation model:** two surface frameworks share the same structural/empirical content under a translation, so verbal uniqueness is not metaphysical uniqueness.
5. **Taxonomy-extension model:** a new hybrid family falls outside a predeclared named-rival list while still fitting the more abstract explanatory axes.

## Rival-closure method

Named traditions are not assumed exhaustive. The taxonomy must first classify theories by three orthogonal axes:

| Axis | Exhaustive typed alternatives for this audit |
|---|---|
| Ultimate explanatory status | primitive/brute; external ground; intrinsic/essence grounding; regress/plural foundation |
| Intrinsic-nature proposal | awareness; neutral/quiddity; structure; unspecified/deflationary |
| Realization architecture | contingent selector; necessary determinate output; substantive orbit/plurality; immanent aspect identity; no realization claim |

This matrix is a disciplined comparison space, not a proof that every future vocabulary is already known. A rival not named in the literature must be located on the axes or shown to add a fourth explanatory status, intrinsic-nature type, or realization architecture.

## Pass conditions

GC4 passes as a positive CI-to-truth theorem only if it proves B1–B4 without defining \(\mathsf{OCI}\), adequacy, or truth in a way that preselects Appearance.

It passes as a closure audit if it determines exactly which bridges are conditional and whether the taxonomy can be used for a comparative verdict.

## Failure conditions

- Treating a methodological commitment to explanation as an ontological theorem without a transcendental argument;
- treating “at most one incompatible framework can be true” as proof that a candidate is true;
- treating internal coherence as phenomenal or physical adequacy;
- calling a named list of rivals exhaustive without an abstract classification rule;
- ignoring the countermodel in the Strong Aseity Bridge.

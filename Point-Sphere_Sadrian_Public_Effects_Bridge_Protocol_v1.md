# Ṣadrian Public-Effects Bridge Protocol v1
## A one-use, expiry-limited A0 test of publicizing intensive order through effects

## Status

This protocol accepts one focused constructive challenge suggested by the direct *Kitāb al-Mashāʿir* collation:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can Ṣadrian intensive order be publicly adjudicated through a}\
\text{profile-invariant pattern of effects and consequences?}
}
\]

It is a bridge audit, **not** a replacement Sadrian ontology, a new general A0 gate, or a verdict on Ṣadrā. It preserves the prior result:

\[
\mathsf{SadrianIntensiveA0Result}=	ext{inconclusive before this audit.}
\]

The audit is deliberately narrow. Its purpose is to test whether the source’s effects-and-consequences language can externalize the relevant intensive criterion without turning a proposed ontology into its own admission list.

## 2. Hypothesis under test

The charitable bridge hypothesis is:

\[
H_{\rm PE}:
\quad
\text{a finite candidate's extra-mental actuality and intensive place can be}\
\text{publicly adjudicated by an invariant profile of its effects/consequences.}
\]

The intended route is not a modern numerical theory:

\[
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\not=
\text{a cardinal quantity inferred from a count of effects}.
\]

The candidate must instead make public use of the source’s qualitative contrasts:

\[
\mathcal D_S=
\{\mathsf{Priority},\mathsf{Posteriority},\mathsf{Completion},\mathsf{Deficiency},
\mathsf{Independence},\mathsf{Need},\mathsf{Strength},\mathsf{Weakness}\}.
\]

The protocol does not assume that a profile of effects actually determines degree. That is precisely the question.

## 3. Typed public dossier

For this test only, a public dossier for a candidate is:

\[
\mathcal P(x)=\langle \mathsf{Ref}(x),\mathsf{Type}(x),\mathsf{Traces}(x),
\mathsf{Asym}(x),\mathsf{Alt}(x),\mathsf{Enc}(x)\rangle.
\]

These are methodological inputs, not primitive elements of Ṣadrā’s ontology:

| Field | Minimal requirement | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| \(\mathsf{Ref}(x)\) | identifies whether the proposed referent is an act, intentional content, property, individual, relation, or alleged external correlate | treating a name or description as a referent already admitted to reality |
| \(\mathsf{Type}(x)\) | keeps mental act, mental content, extra-mental candidate, property, and cause distinct | inferring type merely from the preferred ontology |
| \(\mathsf{Traces}(x)\) | shared descriptions of alleged effects/consequences not written as “\(x\) is an actual degree” | calling a stipulated effect an independent trace |
| \(\mathsf{Asym}(x)\) | the proposed public direction of dependence, completion/deficiency, or strength/weakness | reading the direction from an already accepted Ṣadrian hierarchy |
| \(\mathsf{Alt}(x)\) | at least one non-Sadrian account of the same trace pattern | treating the absence of an alternative description as proof |
| \(\mathsf{Enc}(x)\) | an explicit re-encoding preserving the declared dossier fields | preserving only labels or a friendly translation |

“Public” is restricted to **shareable, stated, corrigible dossier conditions**. It does not presuppose a second realm of public objects, an external time, a physicalist theory of causation, or that these data establish metaphysical truth.

## 4. Attempted effects bridge

The strongest effects-based reconstruction is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{PEI}_{S}(x;\mathcal P)
\iff{}&
\mathsf{Contingent}_{S}(x)
\land \mathsf{TypedExtraMentalReferent}(x;\mathcal P)\\
&\land\mathsf{NonStipulativeTrace}(x;\mathcal P)
\land\mathsf{IntensiveAsymmetry}_{S}(x;\mathcal P)\\
&\land\mathsf{IndividuationWitness}_{S}(x;\mathcal P).
\end{aligned}
\]

where:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{IntensiveAsymmetry}_{S}(x;\mathcal P)
\Rightarrow{}&
\exists y\,[\mathsf{Asym}(x,y)\text{ is classified, without numerical reduction, as}\\
&\mathsf{Priority/Posteriority},\mathsf{Completion/Deficiency},\mathsf{Need/Independence},\text{ or }\mathsf{Strength/Weakness}],\\
\mathsf{IndividuationWitness}_{S}(x;\mathcal P)
\Rightarrow{}&\text{the dossier distinguishes the alleged individual, not merely a universal quiddity or name.}
\end{aligned}
\]

This is intentionally more demanding than `has some effects`. A mental act can have effects without its intentional object having extra-mental actuality; a property can be causally relevant without being the required finite mode; and a causal pattern can be redescribed without an intensive rank following.

## 5. Independence and noncircularity rules

The bridge counts as noncircular only if all of the following hold.

1. **No roster loading.** Neither \(\mathsf{Type}\), \(\mathsf{Trace}\), \(\mathsf{Asym}\), nor \(\mathsf{IndividuationWitness}\) may be certified merely because \(x\) already appears in the asserted Ṣadrian hierarchy.
2. **No flat effect inference.** \(\mathsf{Effects}(a)\) for a mental act \(a\) may not be transferred to its intentional content \(c\).
3. **No causal-label substitution.** Renaming a relation “causal poverty,” “emanation,” or “intensity” does not establish a public direction or rank.
4. **No quantitative surrogate.** Number, magnitude, duration, spatial extent, or practical salience cannot be silently substituted for existential intensity.
5. **No first-person escape.** A report of direct witnessing is admissible as a source-relevant datum, but does not by itself satisfy the public bridge. A pass needs public error/correction conditions for its application.
6. **No causation import.** An outside causal model may be stated as a rival or evidence policy; it may not be announced as metaphysical proof of the Ṣadrian order.

## 6. Fixed adversarial docket

No cases may be added, removed, or redefined after the outcome conditions below are applied.

| Case | Dossier situation | Required result for the bridge |
|---|---|---|
| PE0 — bare posit | a name and quiddity-like description with no nonstipulative trace | reject, without calling mere lack of a name nonexistence |
| PE1 — mental act/content | a mental act with ordinary consequences; its intentional content lacks an established external correlate | classify the act and content differently; no transfer of effects from act to content |
| PE2 — publicly traced finite candidate | a putative finite external candidate with a shared consequence pattern | state what establishes intensive asymmetry rather than extra-mentality alone |
| PE3 — horizontal pair | two candidates have a common alleged cause and neither is publicly ordered above the other | say whether there are two degrees, equal degrees, or no admissible pair without an undeclared selector |
| PE4 — profile twin | two putative candidates share every declared trace and intensive-asymmetry field | decide identity/distinctness, or state a public indeterminacy rule, without self-individuation being a bare label |
| PE5 — accident/property | a dependent property or accident has an alleged trace profile | decide its type relative to the A0 finite-mode target |
| PE6 — re-encoding | a stated transformation preserves \(\mathsf{Ref},\mathsf{Type},\mathsf{Traces},\mathsf{Asym}\) and \(\mathsf{Alt}\), but changes description/segmentation | preserve the verdict or state a non-question-begging reason it changes |
| PE7 — extra candidate | an additional candidate is furnished with a proposed trace and rank | admit or reject without stipulating a new hierarchy member or accepting the alleged rank by description |

## 7. Fixed success and failure conditions

### Limited public-effects success

This focused bridge succeeds only if the attempted rule gives a source-faithful and noncircular verdict for **every** PE0–PE7 and satisfies all of the following:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{a typed, transferable error condition for mental content, fiction, and property cases;}\\
&\text{a public qualitative test of intensive asymmetry, not merely a trace of extra-mentality;}\\
&\text{a public response to profile twins and horizontal pairs;}\\
&\text{a verdict invariant under PE6;}\\
&\text{a non-roster treatment of PE7;}\\
&\text{and a specified way the bridge could be shown wrong on the stated dossier.}
\end{aligned}
\]

A limited success would be evidence for a **scoped** Sadrian A0 bridge. It would not demonstrate that Ṣadrā is true, establish physical or phenomenal adequacy, or solve the full A0 problem outside the stated domain.

### Targeted bridge failure

The effects route fails if any condition necessary to its operation requires one of the following:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
F_1 &: \text{prior admission of the alleged candidate or its rank to establish its effects profile};\\
F_2 &: \text{an untyped transfer from a mental act to intentional content};\\
F_3 &: \text{a causal/effect profile that establishes extra-mentality but not intensive order};\\
F_4 &: \text{an undeclared haecceity, hierarchy, or profile-breaking feature for PE3 or PE4};\\
F_5 &: \text{an unadjudicated property/mode boundary or a verdict that changes under PE6};\\
F_6 &: \text{mere stipulation when PE7 is added};\\
F_7 &: \text{private witnessing as the only means of fixing a candidate-level verdict.}
\end{array}
\]

This is a failure of **the public-effects bridge**, not automatically of Ṣadrian ontology, all intensive criteria, or A0 satisfiability.

### Inconclusive audit

The audit is inconclusive only if the source base cannot determine whether its effects language applies to candidate admission at all, or if the fixed cases cannot be given source-faithful interpretations. Mere lack of a complete A0 pass is not enough for a targeted failure.

## 8. Handling the direct-witnessing passage

Paragraph 92 must neither be ignored nor made into a blanket disqualification.

A targeted failure under \(F_7\) is available only if, after the effects route is stated, the needed particularity/rank cannot be fixed from the public dossier and the source-relevant way of fixing it is direct witnessing. The passage may not be used to conclude that every Ṣadrian claim is in principle private, that all public reasoning fails, or that non-discursive knowledge is irrational.

Conversely, a successful route must show how its public conditions determine the **same candidate-level intensive verdict** rather than merely providing publicly visible signs whose metaphysical interpretation still awaits direct witnessing.

## 9. Expiry and non-proliferation rule

This protocol expires on completion of one written application of PE0–PE7.

\[
\boxed{
\text{No successor effects formulation, relaxed effect criterion, or additional}\
\text{Sadrian “publicization” reformulation may be introduced from this audit.}
}
\]

A later reopening requires a newly recovered, passage-level primary-text rule that explicitly supplies an independently applicable public condition for rank, individuation, or error. Better rhetoric, a new example, or rephrasing this rule is not enough.

On failure, the constructive Sadrian route remains a protected residual, but the meta-track may become the programme’s **primary** inquiry priority. That priority shift is evidential and methodological; it is not a proof of \(H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}\).

## Nonclaim

This protocol does not declare effects sufficient for reality, identify intensive order with ordinary causation, treat empirical causal evidence as metaphysical proof, dismiss direct witnessing, collapse Ṣadrā into a previously tested tradition, or authorize a whole-reality verdict.

# Strong Complete-Intelligibility Whole-Reality Adjudication Methodology v1
## A controlled programme for asking which framework, if any, survives the strongest CI demand

## Status

**Methodology adopted; no concrete framework has been selected.** This programme treats the updated `uploads/Uniqueness-not-proved.txt` transcript as adversarial input, not as proof. It preserves the corrected GC4 result: complete intelligibility (CI) is an argument-supported substantive commitment, not a theorem of bare formal logic.

The decision target is deliberately stronger than internal consistency and weaker than an oracle stipulation that one theory is true by definition.

## 1. Decision question

Let \(F\) range over fully specified whole-reality frameworks and let \(\simeq\) mean genuine equivalence of explanatory and ontological content, not mere verbal resemblance. The target claim is:

\[
\exists! [F]_{\simeq}\;
\bigl[
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\land
\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}(F)
\land
\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}(F)
\bigr].
\tag{Target}
\]

where:

- \(\mathsf{SCI}(F)\): \(F\) meets the declared strongest complete-intelligibility standard;
- \(\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}(F)\): \(F\) has a non-question-begging account of phenomenal/first-person data;
- \(\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}(F)\): \(F\) is compatible with, and eventually recoverable against, physical evidence and theory constraints.

The programme permits four outcomes:

1. one framework conditionally passes within a closed, defended rival space;
2. multiple non-equivalent frameworks remain held;
3. every available framework fails a stated SCI gate;
4. the candidate space or the SCI criterion itself remains underdetermined.

It must not turn “at most one true framework” into “one identified framework exists.”

## 2. Strong CI, made testable

Strong CI is a substantive standard adopted **for this comparison**, not a consequence of the proof calculus.

\[
\mathsf{SCI}(F) := \mathsf{U0}\land\mathsf{U1}\land\mathsf{U2}\land\mathsf{U3}\land\mathsf{U4}.
\]

### U0 — explicit explanatory language

The framework states separately:

- formal consequence \(\vdash_L\);
- metaphysical grounding/explanation \(\prec_F\);
- dependence/realization relations;
- identity/equivalence relations; and
- the relevant modal or necessity operator.

No inference may silently substitute logical entailment for grounding, or “necessary” for “explained.” The logic \(L\) is declared; PSR/CI is listed as a metaphysical standard, not smuggled in as a theorem of formal logic.

### U1 — ultimate non-bruteness

For every fact the framework classifies as ultimate,

\[
\forall p\,[\mathsf{Ultimate}_F(p)\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Brute}_F(p)].
\]

An explanation must be non-circular at the relevant type level. Merely relabelling a posit “necessary,” “primitive freedom,” “nature,” or “self-grounding” does not pass U1.

### U2 — totality and regress closure

An explanatory chain, circle, plurality, law, or infinite structure is permitted only if the framework supplies an account of why that whole structure obtains which is not simply the same unexplained fact restated.

This does **not** ban infinity as such. It rejects an infinite or circular explanatory account only where the framework cannot non-circularly explain the whole pattern.

### U3 — difference and selector closure

Every fundamental asymmetry, exact domain restriction, realization rule, primitive law, or world-selecting difference must be grounded in the framework’s stated ultimate nature rather than inserted after the fact:

\[
\mathsf{FundDiff}_F(x,y)
\Rightarrow
\exists e\,[e\prec_F \mathsf{FundDiff}_F(x,y)]
\land \mathsf{NonCircular}_F(e).
\]

This applies uniformly to creator selection, necessary determinate orders, plenitude domains, aspect criteria, physical laws, and grammar-selection claims.

### U4 — self-application and semantic non-disguise

The framework must state why its own alleged explanatory principles, individuation conditions, and relation between ultimate and finite are not ungrounded additions. A conclusion cannot be hidden in a definition:

- “all finite contents are genuine aspects” cannot be assumed to prove aspecthood;
- “the actual order is necessary” cannot be assumed to explain why that exact order is necessary;
- “goodness is self-diffusive” cannot count as explanation unless its role and grounds are independently stated;
- “appearance is the ground appearing” cannot be called derived if that identity is the substantive core stipulation.

This is an audit of **premise location**, not a prohibition on protected or substantive metaphysical commitments. A framework may have a primitive core, but it cannot advertise core content as a deduction from a thinner core that omits it.

## 3. Logic, CI, truth, and identification remain distinct

The following levels must never be collapsed:

| Level | Claim | What would establish it |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Formal/model consistency | A declared semantics and a model or proof check |
| L1 | Conditional explanation theorem | Transparent premises plus valid derivation |
| L2 | SCI pass | Every stated U0–U4 burden discharged relative to a fixed framework |
| L3 | Comparative unique survival | Exhaustive/equivalence-controlled rival matrix plus eliminations |
| L4 | Truth of the framework | L3 plus phenomenal and physical adequacy, and a defensible truth/evidence bridge |

Thus:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}(F)\not\Rightarrow\mathsf{True}(F),
\qquad
\mathsf{Uniq}(F)\not\Rightarrow\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}(F),
\qquad
\mathsf{Uniq}(F)\not\Rightarrow\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}(F).
\]

A claim of exact uniqueness must prove both existence and exclusion:

\[
\underbrace{\exists F\;\mathsf{SCI}(F)}_{\text{non-vacuous pass}}
\quad\land\quad
\underbrace{\forall F,G\,[\mathsf{SCI}(F)\land\mathsf{SCI}(G)\Rightarrow F\simeq G]}_{\text{uniqueness up to equivalence}}.
\]

## 4. Candidate-space protocol

Named schools are not exhaustive. Candidate closure occurs at the level of explanatory architecture. Every candidate must be located on this matrix or establish a new typed architecture.

| Axis | Required alternatives to test |
|---|---|
| Ultimate status | brute/primitive; self-intelligible intrinsic nature; necessary totality; infinite or circular explanatory structure; plural roots |
| Intrinsic nature | aware/self-knowing; personal/agentive; neutral/quiddity; structural; non-attributive/deflationary |
| Finite-reality status | external created order; necessary emanation/determination; real immanent aspect/articulation; illusion/derivative representation; no robust finite ontology |
| Realization rule | contingent selection; reason-guided but non-determining selection; necessary exact output; unique-best selection; plenitude; no realization rule |
| Difference treatment | grounded internal differentiation; extrinsic relation; gauge/description equivalence; primitive individuation; domain restriction |
| Ultimate–finite relation | efficient creation; participation; emanation; constitutive grounding; identity/aspect; structural realization |

Initial families to enter the matrix are: contingent creator–creation; necessary determinate creation; Neoplatonic/emanationist plenitude; Leibnizian unique-best selection; nondual real-aspect/plenitude; neutral/Russellian monism; structuralism; necessary holistic totality; and principled infinitist/coherentist accounts. This list is provisional, not privileged.

## 5. Equal-burden dossier required for every candidate

A candidate cannot pass because its difficult premise is called “nature,” “necessity,” or “core.” Each dossier must contain:

1. **Typed core:** entities/facts/relations the candidate treats as basic;
2. **Exact ultimate facts:** a finite, explicit ledger of what it claims is fundamental;
3. **Explanation map:** source, target, relation type, and termination condition for each ultimate and derivative fact;
4. **Difference ledger:** every actual/nonactual, finite/infinite, one/many, law/alternative, and aspect/description asymmetry;
5. **Selector audit:** why this exact totality/domain/relation rather than a non-equivalent alternative;
6. **Non-composition or externality account:** how finite differentiation is real without illicitly changing the alleged ultimate;
7. **Phenomenal target:** what concrete phenomenal datum the candidate accounts for and what would count against it;
8. **Physical target:** at minimum compatibility constraints; a physics claim requires independent equations, recovery limits, observables, and discriminators;
9. **Rival challenge:** the strongest available countermodel or alternative and its claimed pass condition;
10. **Verdict type:** pass conditional, held, eliminated under stated SCI premises, equivalent, or outside current scope.

A “residue” verdict must name a particular fact \(p\), show it is fundamental in that candidate, and show why all offered explanations are circular, external in the wrong direction, or merely primitive. It cannot be inferred from unfamiliarity or rhetorical dissatisfaction.

## 6. Gate sequence

### CI0 — Vocabulary and equivalence freeze

Freeze \(L\), grounding vocabulary, explanation adequacy, framework equivalence \(\simeq\), and what counts as a genuine rival. **Failure:** equivocation between logic, grounding, necessity, identity, and representation.

### CI1 — Terminal-structure audit

Test brute, infinite, circular, plural, self-intelligible, and totality architectures. **Required result:** no type of terminal structure is rejected merely by label; any failure is witnessed by a named unexplained fact.

### CI2 — Intrinsic-nature audit

Test whether SCI selects awareness, personhood, neutrality, structure, or a non-attributive absolute. **Failure:** infer mind, goodness, or simplicity merely from the word “intelligible.”

### CI3 — Simplicity/composition audit

Test whether composition, multiplicity, potency, limitation, or change necessarily introduce an ultimate residue. **Failure:** assume that any distinction needs an external composer without a composition-reflection bridge.

### CI4 — Finite-reality relation audit

Test creator–creation, participation, emanation, immanent aspect, and totality models under the same non-composition and reality conditions. **Failure:** use the failure of a spatial analogy as a metaphysical elimination.

### CI5 — Exact-totality / selector audit

Test contingent selection, necessary determination, unique-best, and plenitude routes. **Failure:** call a domain or realization rule explained merely by naming it “nature,” “goodness,” or “all possibilities.”

### CI6 — Phenomenal and physical adequacy gates

Require predeclared targets and defeaters. **Failure:** treat metaphysical coherence as a solution to consciousness or physics.

### CI7 — Rival closure and conclusion

Only after CI0–CI6 can the programme claim conditional unique survival. The final conclusion must identify which level L0–L4 was reached and preserve every antecedent.

## 7. Anti-confirmation-bias controls

1. **Symmetric burden rule:** an objection counts against a candidate only if an equivalent objection is applied to all rivals.
2. **No victory by relabelling:** “core,” “necessity,” “nature,” “goodness,” “freedom,” and “appearance” do not automatically explain what they name.
3. **Countermodel first:** before a positive derivation, build or cite the nearest coherent rival model that denies its conclusion.
4. **No hidden exhaustiveness:** every candidate taxonomy has an extension test.
5. **Equivalence before elimination:** representation, gauge, duality, or level differences cannot be counted as distinct ontologies before an equivalence test.
6. **No penalty for stipulated core alone:** the issue is not whether a framework has commitments, but whether it honestly locates them and can meet SCI’s own self-application demand.
7. **Independent adequacy:** phenomenal and physical targets remain separate from metaphysical elegance.

## 8. Evidence standards and stopping conditions

A successful argument may establish only its announced result:

| Evidence | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|
| Finite model/countermodel | Non-entailment from declared premises |
| Formal proof | Conditional theorem in stated system |
| Conceptual bridge argument | Premise-priced comparative support |
| Phenomenal source/audit | Limited target fidelity or constraint |
| Physical calculation/data | Model-specific empirical adequacy or discrimination |

The programme stops, rather than inventing another reformulation, when a candidate’s crucial bridge is either: (a) independently stated and conditional; (b) shown to be a restatement of its core; or (c) unable to meet its expiry condition. A future reopening requires a new relation type, semantics, and falsification/discriminator condition.

## Nonclaims

This methodology does not assume that SCI is true, that a SCI-passing framework exists, that a unique framework will be found, that any historical system is the answer, or that metaphysics alone yields a physical theory. It is a disciplined way to determine what follows if the strongest CI demand is provisionally accepted and applied uniformly.

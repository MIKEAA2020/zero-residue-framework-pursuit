# Strong CI Adjudication — ET Nondual Public-Domain Bridge Audit v1
## Can nondual identity publicly determine its genuine finite aspect domain?

## Status

**The nondual public-domain bridge is not yet supplied.** The audit preserves the internal non-representational actualization theorem while distinguishing it from a public semantics that classifies independent finite candidates as genuine aspects.

## 1. The internal theorem

Under the protected non-representational identity package:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(a)
\Rightarrow
R(a).
\tag{NDI}
\]

This theorem is valid conditional on its identity premises. It establishes:

> A genuine finite aspect cannot subsequently be omitted by a separate realization selector.

It does not establish:

\[
\mathsf{FormalCandidate}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a).
\]

The public exact-totality problem concerns the latter implication.

## 2. Public-domain pass condition

A nondual candidate obtains a public domain pass only if it supplies a criterion \(\mathsf{PubDet}_G(a)\) such that:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{PubDet}_G(a)
\land
\mathsf{Coh}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NG}(a)
\land
\mathsf{Ctx}(a),
\tag{PD}
\]

where \(\mathsf{PubDet}_G\):

1. does not contain \(R(a)\), actual modehood, or a synonym for already-realized self-presentation;
2. is not mere grammar membership, gauge class, coherence, or context membership;
3. has explicitly stated semantics not treating a formal graph as ground structure;
4. identifies at least one positive candidate and excludes at least one coherent, non-gauge, context-admissible negative candidate; and
5. survives an adversarial rival expansion with the same structural data.

The final condition is essential because GA1 already establishes:

\[
\mathsf{Coh}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NG}(a)
\land
\mathsf{Ctx}(a)
\not\models
\mathsf{Det}_{G}(a).
\tag{GA1}
\]

## 4. Thin-core and rich-core distinction

The public bridge must not make either of these invalid moves.

### Invalid thin-core recovery

\[
K_0
\not\models
\exists a[
\mathsf{Finite}(a)
\land
\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)
].
\]

A stripped theory of self-knowledge that omits real finite articulation cannot derive it merely by restating completeness.

### Invalid core-to-grammar promotion

\[
\mathsf{RealFiniteModes}_{P_{\rm ND}}
\not\models
\mathsf{FaithfulGrammar}(\mathcal M).
\]

A protected core may contain real finite modes, but that does not turn every available formal grammar, component, lift, or context into a genuine mode.

## 5. Exact result

The nondual candidate currently has these asymmetric resources:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{NDI}:
\quad
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow R(a)
\quad\text{is internally established.}
}
\]

But it lacks:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{PubDet}_G(a)
\quad\text{for independent finite candidates.}
}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Nondual identity conditionally solves actualization after domain membership;}
\quad
\text{it does not yet publicly solve exact domain membership.}
}
\]

This is not a refutation of the framework. It is its current exact SCI burden.

## 6. Why no further verbal route is opened

The following formulations are extensionally insufficient unless given new semantics and cases:

- “a genuine aspect is what self-knowledge requires”;
- “a genuine aspect is self-presentable”;
- “a genuine aspect is a coherent non-gauge context”; and
- “a genuine aspect is one of the real modes.”

The last is circular for public classification; the first three either move the untyped term into a new label or collapse into GA1’s structural non-entailment. A new route must specify its semantic relation type, positive and negative cases, and an expiry test before it is admitted.

## 8. Expiry condition and next work

The nondual public-domain route advances only with one of:

1. a realization-independent semantic criterion meeting (PD), with positive and negative cases;
2. a proof that the proposed nondual core necessarily supplies such a criterion without defining it by realization or grammar; or
3. a target-relative fidelity result that justifies a restricted grammar-to-mode mapping without claiming unrestricted ontology.

Absent such a deliverable, no additional restatement of the mode/aspect/self-knowledge identity is a public exact-totality solution.

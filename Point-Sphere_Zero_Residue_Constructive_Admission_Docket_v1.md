## Stage ZR-0: disciplined intake after A0 Phase-I expiry

**Precondition:** `Point-Sphere_A0_MetaTrack_M4_Closure_Audit_v1.md`  
**Status:** Intake protocol only; no candidate is admitted to a zero-residue battery, selected, or rejected by this docket.  
**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Purpose:** Operationalize the research aim of finding, if there is one, a unique framework up to essential equivalence with no unresolved residue, without mistaking a protected core, formal analogy, historical vocabulary, or local bridge result for a whole-framework victory.

## 0. Target and noncompensatory standard

The research target is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Goal}_{\rm ZR!}:
\qquad
\exists![F]_{\simeq}\,\mathsf{ZeroResidue}(F).
}
\tag{ZR!}
\]

The target is not a presupposition that any currently named framework already satisfies it. A candidate is not selected by a weighted tradeoff between elegance and adequacy. It must close each required bridge:

\[
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F;\mathcal B)
\iff
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Typed}
\land
\mathsf{NonVacuousModel}
\land
\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}
\land
\mathsf{ProfileInvariant}\\
&\land
\mathsf{ActualityBridgeClosed}
\land
\mathsf{RealizationBridgeClosed}
\land
\mathsf{TruthErrorBridgeClosed}\\
&\land
\mathsf{PhenomenalBatteryPass}
\land
\mathsf{NormativeBatteryPass}
\land
\mathsf{PhysicalBatteryPass}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{ZR-Pass}
\]

No positive result on one conjunct compensates for a failed or unprovided conjunct.

## 1. Intake rule

A candidate may enter a constructive zero-residue test only through a complete, frozen interface packet:

\[
\mathsf{EntryPacket}(F,\mathcal I)
=
\langle
\mathsf{Scope},
\mathsf{Ontology},
\mathsf{Signature},
\mathsf{CandidateGrammar},
\mathsf{ActualityRule},
\mathsf{PresentationRule},
\mathsf{TruthErrorRule},
\mathsf{TypeRule},
\mathsf{Equivalence},
\mathsf{AntiRosterAudit},
\mathsf{FiniteBattery},
\mathsf{Expiry}
\rangle.
\tag{Entry}
\]

The packet must distinguish, without definitional collapse:

\[
\text{candidate code},
\quad
\text{actual articulation},
\quad
\text{actual presentation},
\quad
\text{correct/error claim},
\quad
\text{actual finite entity}.
\tag{Five-Way}
\]

It must also state whether it claims real finite entities. A strict-Appearance candidate is not penalized for declining that claim, but it must not silently use a real-mode bridge while denying one.

The allowable intake determinations are exactly:

\[
\mathsf{AdmittedForBattery},
\qquad
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete},
\qquad
\mathsf{EntryIncoherent},
\qquad
\mathsf{WithdrawnByScope}.
\tag{Intake}
\]

An incomplete entry is neither rejected ontology nor evidence for a rival. It simply cannot be tested as a zero-residue candidate.

## 2. Anti-circularity and anti-selector requirements

Before any battery is encoded, an entry packet must show all of:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{NoTruthByDeclaration}:&&
\text{correctness is not asserted by the candidate's claim label;}\\
&\mathsf{NoFitByToken}:&&
\text{presentation-tokenhood does not entail fit by definition;}\\
&\mathsf{NoRosterActuality}:&&
\text{actuality is not inferred merely from a listed candidate inventory;}\\
&\mathsf{NoExternalDecoder}:&&
\text{no post hoc interpreter, intended model, or selected world fixes the verdict;}\\
&\mathsf{NoSilentRealization}:&&
\text{a formal pair, code, or diagram is not silently treated as a real mode or entity;}\\
&\mathsf{InvarianceDeclared}:&&
\text{the profile-preserving equivalence relation is fixed before cases.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Firewall}
\]

A formal mathematical model can serve as a consistency or non-entailment certificate. It cannot be selected afterwards as an intended reference world that makes a presentation true.

## 3. Intake queue: current serious candidate cores

This docket freezes only the current intake statuses. It does not define new ontologies or restrict future entrants that independently satisfy \((\mathrm{Entry})\).

\[
F_{\rm NP}^{\sharp}:
\qquad
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{StrictNonDuality}(G)

\mathsf{MPlen}^{\rm fin}_{G}.

**What is available.** The candidate has a clear anti-reification discipline and a conditional internal theorem:

\[
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{StrictNonDuality}(G)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PositiveImmanentFormalMode}(G).
\tag{NP-Core}
\]

It has also supplied candidate grammars, formal context machinery, and restricted manifestation/presentation constructions.

**Why entry is incomplete.** The frozen projects have not supplied an actual truth/error bridge:

\[
\mathsf{Core}_{\rm NP}
\not\models
\mathsf{GroundedActualFit}.
\tag{NP-Fit-Gap}
\]

The strict-presentation audit left truth/error, privation, normativity, and horror/evil unproved; the semantic successor stopped at a restricted correctness/error failure; and the immanent-entailment successor failed its ordinary-profile control through automatic formal correctness.

No new bridge basis \(\mathcal E\) with independently stated application conditions is presently frozen. Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Intake}(F_{\rm NP}^{\sharp})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}.
}
\tag{NP-Intake}
\]

This is not a refutation of the strict core.

### 3.2 Weak simplicity plus positive-immanent formal differentiation

\[
F_{\rm PI}^{\rm weak}:
\qquad
\mathsf{WeakSimplicity}(G)

The HoTT proposal is a formal analogy, not a completed EntryPacket.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Intake}(F_{\rm PI}^{\rm weak})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}.
}
\tag{PI-Intake}
\]

This is not a refutation of weak simplicity or a proof that positive differentiation is partitive.

The M2 interface cards and the historical dossiers are not admitted as whole-framework candidates merely by occurring in the earlier records. They are either conditional interface possibilities, protected resources, or unclassified packages. Each would need its own \((\mathrm{Entry})\) packet.

## 4. One-at-a-time execution rule

The docket permits no battery execution for an entry marked \(\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}\). A candidate becomes \(\mathsf{AdmittedForBattery}\) only after its entry packet is complete and frozen.

For an admitted candidate \(F\), the execution order is:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
Z0&:&\text{validate the typed signature, grammar, and nonvacuous model;}\\
Z1&:&\text{audit actuality, presentation, and realization separately;}\\
Z2&:&\text{run the fixed truth/error and invariance battery;}\\
Z3&:&\text{run the declared phenomenal, normative, and physical batteries;}\\
Z4&:&\text{issue a scoped pass, failure, or unproved result; no in-project repair.}
\end{array}
\tag{ZR-Order}
\]

A failure at any stage closes only the frozen bridge claim. A repair must be entered as a new packet, not appended to the failed candidate.

---

## 5. Comparative and uniqueness rule

No candidate receives a comparative rank before it completes \((\mathrm{ZR\text{-}Order})\). A candidate with fewer articulated commitments is not thereby superior to one with more articulated commitments; conversely, expressive capacity is not adequacy.

Only a candidate that has completed the full battery can become a provisional survivor:

\[
\mathsf{ProvisionalZRSurvivor}(F)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F;\mathcal B).
\tag{Survivor}
\]

Uniqueness requires, for each serious rival, an explicit equivalence or failure certificate:

\[
\mathsf{UniqueZR}(F^*)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F^*)
\land
\forall F[
\mathsf{SeriousRival}(F)
\to
(F\simeq F^*\lor\neg\mathsf{ZRPass}(F))
].
\tag{Unique}
\]

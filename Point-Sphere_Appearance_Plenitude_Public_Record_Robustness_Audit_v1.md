# AP4B Public Record Robustness Audit v1
## Why a shared ordered interface is not yet a robust public record

## Scope

AP4A showed that, in the minimal family, the IAR-like grammar uniquely gives every context a two-content order-coherent public interface. AP4B asks whether this rises to a robust public record rather than a single shared combinatorial overlap.

A robust public record implementation requires:

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | At least two independently specified record witnesses or a precise non-arbitrary redundancy criterion |
| R2 | Counterfactual stability: a stated perturbation/constraint change preserves the record relation in the relevant regime |
| R3 | Gauge-invariant agreement: the record algebra and its order relation have stated transformation status |
| R4 | No circular fragment/factorisation assumption: witnesses are not defined by the very mode partition the record is meant to validate |
| R5 | No external background time when the order is intended to be fundamental |

## Audit

| Available resource | R1 | R2 | R3 | R4 | R5 | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| AP4A IAR-like two-context interface | fail | untested | untested | pass only combinatorially | pass | one shared ordered interface, not redundant public recording |
| AP3N internal order witness | fail | fail | untested | pass as formal witness | pass | no perturbation law or gauge action for physical records |
| C2 public-world graph | partial/stipulated | stipulated toy interventions | toy-equivariant | fail for derivation | temporal toy | created-order diagnostic, not a self-presentation record law |
| T3 WKB/decoherence | conditional | conditional | partial | fail | approximate/emergent | system/environment/coarse graining assumed |
| T5 redundancy/articulation | conditional | conditional | partial | fail | no orientation law | environment fragments/factorisation assumed; branch-neutral diagnostic cannot supply orientation |

## Result

No current component meets R1–R5.

The AP4A result remains valid and useful:

> A minimal public retentional interface conditionally discriminates the IAR-like grammar among the five finite families.

But it does not establish public objectivity, redundant records, physical stability, or a gauge-invariant record algebra.

The limiting issue is again structural:

\[
\text{record robustness}
\quad\text{requires a witness/factorisation/interaction structure}
\]

that is not yet derived from the mode grammar and cannot be assumed without circularity.

## Next gate

AP4C requires an explicit record law containing:

\[
(\mathcal A_{\rm record},\;\text{gauge action},\;\text{constraint or dynamics},\;\text{stability notion},\;\text{redundancy/witness rule}).
\]

It may be a conditional formal law or a physical law, but it must say which variables are records and why without importing the desired factorisation. Until then, AP4 remains a minimal interface discriminator, not a public-record derivation.

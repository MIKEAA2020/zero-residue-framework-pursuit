# Point–Sphere Limit–Physics Candidate (PS-LP-1)
## Preregistration, fresh-candidate definition, scope controls, and expiry

**Status:** Preregistered only. This document does not claim a derivation of space, time, physics, finite modes, public truth/error, or the truth of Point–Sphere monism.

**Purpose:** The user clarified a new proposal: the Point is limitless, spaceless, and timeless; the Sphere is the Point under limiting conditions. Space and time are required to emerge rather than serve as a pre-existing container or lens. The present document preregisters a bounded entry test of two readings:

- **O — ontic-manifestation:** the Sphere is objectively real as a manifestation of the Point, while not being a second independent being or a part of the Point.
- **H — hybrid manifestation-and-presentation:** O holds, and finite knowers may also apprehend the manifestation through finite presentations. The presentation layer may not generate the objective physical structure.

This is **PS-LP-1**, a fresh candidate. It is not an amendment to strict-simplicity Point–Sphere/Appearance, PSR-5, PPA, EMC, WSP-1 through WSP-11, or PED-1.

## 1. Claim discipline

The target is truth-directed, physics-facing adequacy. Traditional resonance, private experience, practical fruitfulness, and elegant vocabulary are not truth selectors.

\[
\mathsf{TraditionalCoherence}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Truth},
\qquad
\mathsf{PhenomenologicalFit}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Truth},
\qquad
\mathsf{PragmaticUsefulness}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Truth}.
\]

Nor would recovery of known physics, if it occurred, uniquely establish monism:

\[
\mathsf{RecoverKnownPhysics}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{MonismTrue}.
\]

It would, however, be more truth-relevant than a merely verbal correspondence, provided the recovery is independently specified, noncircular, and empirically discriminating.

## 3. Candidate core and typed vocabulary

Let \(P\) denote the proposed Point. For PS-LP-1 only, the Point-core is stipulated as follows:

\[
\mathsf{PointCore}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoIndependentConstituents}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoSpatialLocation}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoTemporalIndex}(P).
\]

“Limitless” is not treated as a magic derivation rule. At this stage it means only that the candidate may not assume \(P\) is bounded by an independently existing space, time, or external domain. It does **not** imply that all structures, laws, possibilities, or physical facts have been derived from \(P\).

Weak simplicity is fixed as:

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoIndependentConstituents}(P).
\]

It may permit a positive immanent formal differentiation, but does not by itself entail one:

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(P)
\not\models
\mathsf{ActualImmanentDifferentiation}(P).
\]

Let \(\Lambda\) be a proposed limit-forming relation and \(\Sigma_\lambda\) the associated Sphere-role:

\[
\mathsf{SphereRole}(P,\lambda,\Sigma_\lambda)
:\iff
\Sigma_\lambda = \Lambda_\lambda(P).
\]

This notation does **not** assert literal numerical identity between a bearer and a role/profile. The intended nonduality claim is instead:

\[
\neg\mathsf{IndependentBeing}(\Sigma_\lambda)
\land
\neg\mathsf{ExternalContainerOf}(\Sigma_\lambda,P).
\]

The term \(\lambda\) is presently a typed placeholder, not a completed explanatory resource. It cannot be left as a brute selector, an independent possibility-domain, a hidden substance, a physical law under another name, or a preexisting spatial/temporal framework.

## 4. The two readings to be tested

### 4.1 O: ontic-manifestation reading

Under O, the Sphere is an objectively real manifestation-profile of the Point:

\[
\mathsf{O}
:=
\mathsf{PointCore}(P)
\land
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(P)
\land
\mathsf{SphereRole}(P,\lambda,\Sigma_\lambda)
\land
\mathsf{ObjectivelyReal}(\Sigma_\lambda).
\]

The objective-reality predicate is deliberately weak: O may not infer all or any actual finite manifestations from the bare vocabulary. Its entry burden is to state what makes a \(\lambda\)-profile actual and determinate without introducing an external selector or an independent constituent.

### 4.2 H: hybrid manifestation-and-presentation reading

Under H, O is retained, while finite presentation is a further, downstream relation:

\[
\mathsf{H}
:=
\mathsf{O}
\land
\mathsf{Presentation}(K,\Sigma_\lambda,\rho).
\]

Here \(K\) and \(\rho\) denote, respectively, a candidate finite knower/access condition and a presentation relation. H is allowed only if it observes the direction of explanation:

\[
P
\longrightarrow
\Sigma_\lambda
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{Presentation}(K,\Sigma_\lambda,\rho),
\]

not:

\[
\mathsf{AlreadyPhysicalObserver}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{SpaceTime}
\longrightarrow
\Sigma_\lambda.
\]

Thus H may address access to an already objectively derived physical structure. It may not use finite observers, their embodiment, their measurement apparatus, or an already physical space-time to generate that structure.

## 5. Noncircularity definition

A proposed \(\Lambda\) qualifies for the entry test only when all six conditions hold.

\[
\mathsf{NonCircularLimitRule}(\Lambda)
:iff
\mathsf{Typed}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{PositiveContent}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{PrePhysicalVocabulary}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{NonIndependent}(\Lambda,P)
\land
\mathsf{NonBruteActualization}(\Lambda)
\land
\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}(\Lambda).
\]

The conditions mean:

1. **Typed:** domain, codomain, and composition or application conditions are explicit.
2. **Positive content:** \(\Lambda\) says more than “the Point appears finite.”
3. **Prephysical vocabulary:** the definition does not invoke spatial extension, duration, causal succession, measurement, matter, energy, particle, field, observer, consciousness, a coordinate system, a metric, or a known physical law as an input.
4. **Nonindependent:** \(\Lambda\) does not add a second substance, part, externally existing realm, or independent possibility-space alongside \(P\).
5. **Nonbrute actualization:** the candidate identifies why a particular profile occurs rather than merely positing its occurrence.
6. **No hidden selector:** the candidate does not choose one physical world, dimension, metric, law, or initial condition from prelisted alternatives without accounting for the selection.

These are symmetric controls. A privative account of limits must have determinate grammar without a hidden positive range, independent possibility-domain, selector, or brute fact. A positive-immanent account must be positive, nonpartitive, nonindependent, nontemporal, nonspatial, and nonbrute.

## 6. Physics-generation target

The user’s stipulated direction is that space and time must emerge; they are not permitted as ambient inputs.

\[
\neg\mathsf{SpaceTimeAsInput}
\land
\mathsf{SpaceTimeAsTarget}.
\]

“Physics” is decomposed into progressively stronger targets rather than treated as a single word:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{Q0} & \text{typed, noncircular prephysical limit rule}\\
\mathsf{Q1} & \text{derived differentiable or equivalent structural arena }\mathcal M\\
\mathsf{Q2} & \text{derived dimensionality, topology, and nondegenerate metric/causal structure}\\
\mathsf{Q3} & \text{derived temporal ordering or an equivalent account of physical change}\\
\mathsf{Q4} & \text{derived state space }\mathcal S\text{ and dynamics }\mathcal D\\
\mathsf{Q5} & \text{derived observable map }\mathcal O\text{ and an empirical recovery target }\mathcal R
\end{array}
\]

A minimal recovery packet is:

\[
\mathcal P_{\rm phys}
=
\langle
\mathcal M,
\mathcal G,
\mathcal T,
\mathcal S,
\mathcal D,
\mathcal O,
\mathcal R
\rangle,
\]

where \(\mathcal G\) is geometric/causal structure and \(\mathcal T\) gives temporal ordering or its physical equivalent. A candidate that provides a verbal unity claim but no packet does not reach even a minimal physics derivation.

## 7. Staged audit and result vocabulary

The authorized next action is limited to an entry audit. It asks whether O and H provide enough prephysical machinery to enter Q0–Q5; it does not invent missing equations or supply physics from analogy.

\[
\mathsf{EntryPass}(C)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Q0}(C),
\]

but:

\[
\mathsf{EntryPass}(C)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Q1}(C)
\lor
\mathsf{Q2}(C)
\lor
\mathsf{Q3}(C)
\lor
\mathsf{Q4}(C)
\lor
\mathsf{Q5}(C).
\]

Permitted results are:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete} & \text{required prephysical machinery is unspecified}\\
\mathsf{EntryBlocked} & \text{the formulation uses forbidden physical inputs or a circular bridge}\\
\mathsf{QnPass} & \text{only the named target Qn is established under stated checks}\\
\mathsf{Underdetermined} & \text{multiple incompatible structures fit the same core}\\
\mathsf{NoConclusion} & \text{available material does not decide the condition}
\end{array}
\]

No result may be translated into composition, independent beings, refutation of weak simplicity, proof of monism, or a complete physics derivation without a separately defended bridge.

## 8. Prohibited moves

The following are prohibited in PS-LP-1:

- treating the metaphor of a sphere “inside” a point as geometry;
- presupposing space, time, observers, measurement, physical fields, or known laws as the very limits allegedly derived;
- equating “viewed” with an already embodied observer outside the system;
- treating a formal map as an ontological production rule merely because it is formal;
- stipulating a preferred dimension, signature, law, state, or world and calling the stipulation a derivation;
- treating an abstract continuum, symmetry group, algebra, or differential manifold as physics without a physical bridge;
- promoting Q0, Q1, or Q2 to Q5;
- taking empirical recovery, if supplied, as unique proof that this metaphysical core is true.

PS-LP-1 expires after **one bounded entry audit** of O and H against Sections 5–7.

\[
\mathsf{Expiry}(\mathsf{PS\text{-}LP\text{-}1})

\mathsf{Complete}(

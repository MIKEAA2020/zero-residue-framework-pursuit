# AP3B Perspective Gate v1: Executed Result

## Why AP3A was not enough

AP3A established that local profiles can be distinct, bounded, publicly compatible restrictions of one global formal state. It did not establish that those profiles possess any internal unity sufficient to count as modes, subjects, or phenomenal fields.

AP3B introduced a necessary integration gate before any such promotion is allowed.

## Gate

For P and Q to be minimally eligible as local mode candidates, their exclusive content must be informationally integrated with their shared/public carrier content:

\[
I(X_{V_i\setminus O};X_O)>0.
\]

This is a necessary proxy only, not a consciousness measure. It blocks calling an arbitrary projection of independent variables a perspective merely because it has less-than-global access.

A separate boundary check required:

\[
I(X_{V_P\setminus O};X_{V_Q\setminus O}\mid X_O)=0.
\]

This makes the two exclusive local contents conditionally screened by the public interface, rather than treating any overlapping fragments as distinct modes.

## Corrected AP3 status

AP3A’s profiles are formally coherent partial descriptions. They are **not licensed as modes or perspectives** under the newly explicit criterion, because their exclusive coordinate is independent of the rest of the carrier under the frozen product measure.

This is a useful failure, not a failure of the wider framework. It prevents a category error:

\[
\text{bounded restriction}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{integrated mode}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{phenomenal subject}.
\]

## The conditional phenomenality bridge

The strongest available nondual formulation is:

\[
\operatorname{Phen}(i)
\Longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{Mode}(i)
\land
\operatorname{SelfPresent}_G(i).
\]

Within the aware-ground package, the second conjunct is intended as non-representational self-presentation, not an extra externally caused mental state. But the graph/measure model does not establish that bridge. It must remain conditional and explicit.

## Next node: AP3C

AP3C must construct a global correlated or constraint-governed model in which all of the following obtain together:

1. structurally noninterchangeable local carriers;
2. bounded local access;
3. stable public overlap and global amalgamation;
4. positive local integration;
5. a justified boundary criterion that prevents the whole global state or arbitrary fragments from automatically becoming the only or every perspective.

Even a passing AP3C result would remain a mode-architecture result. It would not demonstrate consciousness, solve decombination, or derive physics.

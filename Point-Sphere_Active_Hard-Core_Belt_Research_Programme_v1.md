## Governing decision

The user’s hard-core + belt research programme is now the **active organising architecture**. The earlier merged conditional lineage remains an archive of arguments, countermodels, corrections, and public-facing premise prices. It is not the default question-generator and is not to be re-run before ordinary belt work.

Routine work must begin from the hard core, move through the belt in dependency order, and use legacy material only when a belt task needs a recorded distinction, countermodel, or failure condition.

## Protected hard core: assumed in this programme

1. One ultimate ground: simple, complete, fully aware, good, unchanging, non-temporal and non-spatial.
2. Real dependent creation over mere appearance: finite beings and temporal order are real, derivative, and asymmetrically dependent.
3. Creation may be described computationally, but there is no external material simulator or independent physical substrate beyond the ground.
4. Finite truth is perspectivally distributed; research therefore synthesizes constrained contributions from many disciplines rather than treating one source as exhaustive.

The hard core is not reopened by an ordinary belt failure. It is not presented in public outputs as a premise-free deduction.

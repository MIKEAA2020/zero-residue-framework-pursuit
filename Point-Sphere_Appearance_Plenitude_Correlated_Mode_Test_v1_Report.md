## Frozen construction

\[
X=\{(o_0,o_1,o_2,o_3,\;o_0,e,\;o_1,f):o_j,e,f\in\{0,1\}\}.

P and Q shared four public coordinates \(O=\{0,1,2,3\}\). P’s exclusive pair was \((X_4,X_5)=(o_0,e)\); Q’s was \((X_6,X_7)=(o_1,f)\). Each pair therefore contains:

- a local signal constrained by public content; and
- a local variation available at fixed public content.

The global relational graph was rigid, and P/Q’s carrier graphs were nonisomorphic.

## Restricted result

AP3C passes a stronger result than AP3A:

> A single constrained global support can contain structurally distinct, bounded, publicly compatible, non-independent **minimum correlated mode candidates**, whose exclusive local content is positively integrated with their carrier yet conditionally screened from each other by a public interface.

This avoids treating every arbitrary bounded data projection as a perspective. The product-state AP3A profiles failed the integration gate; the AP3C profiles pass it.

## Limits

The remaining limits cannot be softened.

1. The support, graph, carrier sets, and signal constraints were stipulated. They are not derived from simple awareness, completeness, or plenitude.
2. The integration proxy is necessary but nowhere near sufficient for consciousness, a unified subject, ownership, agency, or moral status.
3. The model does not prove that P/Q are the uniquely correct boundaries. A global state may contain other integrated subsets; a general boundary-selection theorem is absent.
4. \(X\) is structured and composite. It must not be identified with a simple ground; it is only a formal analogue of the structured content of a mode/aspect.
5. No dynamics, quantum probability, spacetime, or public physical measurement has been derived.

Thus AP3C is a successful **mode-architecture** result, not a solution to decombination or phenomenality.

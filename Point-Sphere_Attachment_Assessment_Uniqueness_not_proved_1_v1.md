## 3. Claims in the attachment that are not earned

### A. Comparative “best odds” claim

The attachment repeatedly calls nondual “appearance” more likely to pass or the best candidate on the selector axis. No equal-burden theorem, common metric, or non-arbitrary tractability measure is supplied.

\[
\mathsf{NoExternalSelectorAfterGenuineDomain}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{MoreLikelyToPassSCI}.
\]

The nondual actualization theorem is a real conditional resource. It does not rank total burdens across candidates. A comparative ranking would require explicitly weighted, symmetrically applied measures of bridge difficulty and adequacy; none is given.

### B. Conflation of protected \(P_{\rm ND}\) with unrestricted plenitude

Protected \(P_{\rm ND}\) contains real finite modes/aspects as core content and gives the conditional result:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)
\Rightarrow R(a).
\]

It does **not** thereby establish:

\[
\forall a[
\mathsf{PossibleContent}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow R(a)].
\]

“Every possible self-knowledge content is a genuine aspect” is a new plenitude bridge. It must state possibility semantics, aspect typing, compossibility, non-gauge identity, and exact-domain consequences. It cannot be counted as a supplied \(\mathsf{PubDet}_G\).

### C. The alleged determination criterion is a placeholder

The attachment proposes:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{PossibleContentOfSelfKnowledge}(a).
\]

This does not pass the public-domain condition until “possible content” is given realization-independent semantics and positive/negative cases. Otherwise it merely transfers the genuine-aspect problem:

\[
\text{Which candidates are genuine aspects?}
\quad\leadsto\quad
\text{Which candidates are possible self-knowledge contents?}
\]

No public classification has yet been supplied.

### D. Possibility/compossibility is not avoided

“All possible contents are realized” does not remove incompatibility questions. It introduces them in concentrated form:

\[
\mathsf{AllPossibilityRealization}
\Rightarrow
\text{typed possibility + compossibility + duplicate/gauge treatment are owed.}
\]

A claim that logical possibility includes contradictions is incorrect under the standard use of logical possibility; contradictions are normally excluded. But excluding contradictions alone still leaves modal, metaphysical, aspect, perspective, and compossibility questions.

### E. Exact-totality does not disappear

Plenitude cannot simply be contrasted with exact-totality as though it were a shortcut around it. It requires an account of why this exact possibility space, typing, realization relation, and context structure obtain:

\[
\mathsf{Plenitude}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactTotalityExplained}.
\]

The burden is transformed, not discharged.

### F. Moral/phenomenal burden must be symmetrically stated

The attachment’s suffering/evil discussion identifies a potentially serious phenomenal, ethical, and normative adequacy question. It is not itself a CI refutation, and it must be applied symmetrically to creation, emanation, nondual, totality, neutral, and other candidates. It is a distinct adequacy interface, not an automatic decisive objection to one candidate.

## 4. Fair reconstruction of the “nondual plus plenitude” profile

Let:

\[
\mathsf{NDP}(G):=
\mathsf{NondualIdentity}(G)
\land
\mathsf{PlenitudeOverGenuineAspects}(G).
\]

Its strongest earned conditional resource is:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow R(a).
\]

Its newly added bridge burden is:

\[
\mathsf{PlenitudeOverGenuineAspects}(G)
\Rightarrow ?
\langle\mathcal A,\mathcal C,R\rangle,
\]

where it must define a genuine domain \(\mathcal A\), compatibility relation \(\mathcal C\), realization semantics \(R\), and exact inclusion/exclusion. It also retains the whole-profile/self-intelligibility burden and its phenomenological/physical adequacy burdens.

Thus:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{NDP}\text{ is a live enriched candidate, not a public exact-totality pass.}
}
\]

## 5. Net effect

No protected commitment or canonical conclusion is revised by the attachment.

The attachment strengthens the case for treating unrestricted nondual plenitude as a separately named candidate profile rather than silently folding it into protected \(P_{\rm ND}\). Its precise next admissible deliverable is a typed, realization-independent possibility/aspect semantics with positive and negative cases and a declared compossibility rule.

## Nonclaims

This assessment does not refute nondual identity, plenitude, appearance-language, or necessary emanation. It does not deny that the nondual route has a real conditional no-later-selector advantage. It rejects only the attachment’s premature comparative ranking and its treatment of possibility-content plenitude as an already supplied determination criterion.

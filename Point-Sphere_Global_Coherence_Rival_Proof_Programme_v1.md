# Point–Sphere Global-Coherence Rival Proof Programme v1
## Pre-core-revision audit — topological order, feasibility priority, adversarial control

## Objective

Determine whether the following can be established rigorously, rather than asserted rhetorically:

\[
\text{simple ground + nondual appearance + plenitude}
\]

is the unique framework satisfying a precisely defined **global coherence / complete intelligibility** condition, and whether classical contingent creation necessarily fails that condition through an unexplained selection remainder.

This programme is not a replacement of the active creation hard core. It is a controlled audit of whether the appearance branch supplies grounds for such a revision. It is also not a physics programme: no result below licenses a time law, quantum-gravity claim, or empirical prediction without the separate T7 requirements.

## Rules

1. **No agreement laundering.** Prior assistant assent, religious affiliation, rhetorical force, or a metaphor is not evidence.
2. **No equivocation.** Keep logical consistency, explanatory adequacy, global coherence, metaphysical truth, and physical adequacy distinct.
3. **No special pleading.** Apply the same no-hidden-selector and no-brute-remainder tests to Appearance and every rival.
4. **No reification.** \(G\) is non-structural; a formal model is never identified with the ground.
5. **No ontology by grammar.** Coherent descriptions, group orbits, and maximal contexts do not automatically become genuine aspects.
6. **Anti-confirmation condition.** Every positive lemma must predeclare a serious countermodel attempt and a rival-favoring outcome.
7. **Topological order first; feasibility second.** Execute a node only after predecessors pass. Among ready nodes, take the highest-feasibility decisive node.

## GC1 specification and executed controls

### 1. Required definitions

GC1 must state a theory-neutral language for:

\[
\mathsf{CI}(T),\quad
\mathsf{NBD},\quad
\mathsf{DifferenceMaker},\quad
\mathsf{Primitive},\quad
\mathsf{Explanation},\quad
\mathsf{Truthmaker},\quad
\mathsf{Adequacy}.
\]

It must distinguish an ontological difference-maker from:

- an epistemic reason to believe a theory;
- a merely verbal label;
- a gauge coordinate;
- a descriptive representation choice; and
- a postulated axiom whose status has not been justified.

### 2. Required adversarial tests

GC1 must include at least these candidates:

1. an ultimate primitive/brute-totality model;
2. an infinite-derivative-chain model;
3. a necessary-determinate-totality model;
4. an orbit/maximal-expression model;
5. a contingent-creator selector model;
6. an appearance-plus-conditional-plenitude model.

A pass requires that the standard not prejudge any candidate by definition.

### 3. Possible outputs

- **Theorem:** NBD follows from a rival-neutral CI definition.
- **Conditional theorem:** NBD follows only after a named transcendental/identity premise.
- **Underdetermination:** at least two non-equivalent models satisfy all stated premises.
- **No-go:** CI cannot be made non-circular under the available vocabulary.

The programme may not call a conditional theorem a proof of framework truth.

## GC2 specification — creator-selection lemma

For \(w_1\neq w_2\), formalize the claim:

\[
\mathsf{Act}(w_1)\land\neg\mathsf{Act}(w_2)
\Rightarrow
\exists\delta\,\Delta(w_1,w_2;\delta).
\]

Then test four rival replies:

1. \(\delta\) is an intrinsic differentiator in \(G\);
2. \(\delta\) is an extrinsic relation of a single immutable act;
3. \(\delta\) is primitive agent causation/free choice;
4. actuality is necessary, not contingent.

The target is not to call a reply “unsatisfying.” It is to prove either incompatibility with the frozen simplicity/CI conditions or a residual primitive that violates them.

## GC2 completed result

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Creator_Selection_Audit_v1_Report.md` completes the creator-selection case audit. Under strong NBD, no declared response preserves frozen strict simplicity and contingent exclusive actualization: intrinsic differentiation violates the frozen SSS typing; primitive or extrinsic single-act replies preserve only weak NBD while retaining a terminal selector debt; necessary determination leaves the contingent-creation class and owes an exact-totality bridge. This is a conditional rival result, not a proof of Appearance. GC3 is now the highest-feasibility ready node.

## GC3 specification — maximal-expression hinge

GC3 must not begin with “all possible worlds are real.” Its domain is restricted to independently established genuine aspect-content:

\[
\mathsf{MEC}:
\forall a\in\mathcal A\,
\bigl(\mathsf{GenuineContent}_G(a)\Rightarrow R(a)\bigr).
\]

Required subgates:

- **GC3a — aspect criterion:** define \(\mathcal A\) independently of \(R\) and of arbitrary formal describability;
- **GC3b — omission theorem:** show why \(\neg R(a)\) for genuine \(a\) is a restriction of self-knowledge rather than a harmless nonactual possibility;
- **GC3c — compossibility:** state how incompatible finite contents are realized without joint contradiction, preserving the prior maximal-proper-context discipline;
- **GC3d — non-composition:** prove that the account does not make finite distinctions parts or changing states of \(G\).

A failure at any subgate retains conditional plenitude; it does not confirm a rival.

## GC3 completed result

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Maximal_Expression_Audit_v1_Report.md` completes the maximal-expression hinge audit. It confirms the valid conditional P-internal theorem from explicit exhaustive non-representational self-presentation, but finds that complete self-knowing awareness alone does not entail a nonempty finite aspect domain, exhaustivity, or the identity bridge; representational knowledge leaves a possible/actual gap; MEC can conceal a restricted aspect domain; and MEC does not settle compossibility. Appearance therefore retains a concrete bridge debt rather than a completed plenitude proof. GC4 is next.

## GC4 specification — CI-to-truth and rival closure

GC4 has two distinct burdens:

\[
\mathsf{CI}\text{ is required for rational assertion}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{RealitySatisfiesCI}
\]

without a transcendental bridge; and

\[
\text{at most one incompatible theory is true}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{one candidate has been proved true}.
\]

GC4 must either prove the missing bridges or label the result as comparative/conditional. Its rival taxonomy must include at minimum: brute necessary totality, structural realism, Russellian/neutral monism, classical contingent creation, necessary determinate creation, necessary orbit/plenitude, and nondual appearance.

## GC4 completed result

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Truth_Rival_Closure_Audit_v1_Report.md` completes GC4 as a non-entailment/closure audit. It finds that rational inquiry does not formally entail ontological complete intelligibility; essential closure does not select awareness or plenitude; internal coherence/unique survival cannot replace phenomenal and physical adequacy; and named rival lists need extension/equivalence controls. Thus no CI-to-truth theorem has been earned. GC5 may now issue only a conditional comparative verdict.

`Point-Sphere_Global_Coherence_Comparative_Verdict_v1.md` completes GC5. The remaining upstream debt is a realization-independent genuine-aspect criterion. No candidate presently wins in a public, rival-neutral sense; no protected-core revision is recommended.

## Stop and revision rules

1. No metaphysical position gains confirmation from a rival’s failure alone.
2. A countermodel that preserves the declared premises blocks the proposed theorem, even if it is aesthetically inferior.
3. If GC1 or GC3 returns underdetermination, retain the current conditional status and do not start a new nineteenth verbal derivation.
4. A core-revision recommendation is permitted only after GC5; it must state what would reverse it.
5. No GC result transfers to Problem-of-Time physics without a separately accepted T7-quality physical postulate.

## GC programme disposition

GC0–GC5 are complete at the current resource level. The global-coherence route is now held rather than repetitively reformulated. It may reopen only for a predeclared new bridge with an independent criterion and expiry condition: an aspect-domain/omission/non-composition bridge, a successful CI-to-truth argument, a non-circular contingent-creation selector account, or a target-relative phenomenal/physical fidelity construction. The separate Problem-of-Time T7 hold remains unchanged.

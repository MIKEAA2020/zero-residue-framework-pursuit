# AP3P2E Ground–Appearance Valuation Quotient Audit v1
## Does one timeless/nondual ground derive full Q-coordinate gauge?

## Claim tested

A proposed appearance/ground reframing says that the 16 trace-preserving lifts are many appearance frames on one ground structure, so full \(H_{\rm full}\cong(\mathbb Z_2)^4\) should be gauge.

This audit grants the strongest relevant premise:
\[
\forall \ell\in L,\quad \operatorname{Ground} (\ell)=G,
\qquad |L|=16,
\]
and asks whether it entails:
\[
\forall \ell,\ell'\in L,\quad \ell\sim_{\rm desc}\ell'.
\]

## Level distinction

The protected core identifies a genuine mode with an aspect/content *of* the one ground; it does not say that two contents of the same ground are the same content. Therefore:
\[
\operatorname{Ground}(x)=\operatorname{Ground}(y)=G
\not\Rightarrow x=y
\quad\text{or}\quad x\sim_{\rm desc}y.
\]
This is the difference between **one ground** and **one aspect**. Collapsing every distinction merely because it has one ground would also collapse the framework's protected finite-aspect multiplicity.

## Quotient models compatible with common grounding

With all 16 lifts grounded in \(G\) and sharing AP3P2 trace data, consider three equivalence relations:

1. \(\sim_{\rm id}\): identity only — 16 classes;
2. \(\sim_{\rm uniform}\): global Q complement — 8 classes;
3. \(\sim_{\rm full}\): arbitrary sector-dependent Q-coordinate redefinition — 1 class.

Common grounding is compatible with all three. The quotient is additional data, not a consequence of the constant grounding map.

## Why the listed analogies do not supply the missing axiom

- Ground timelessness and intrinsic mode-flow are a level relation, not a coordinate transformation acting on the Q valuation fibre. \(\operatorname{Time}(m)=\operatorname{Flow}(m)\) does not identify all mode contents because \(G\) is timeless.
- Physical coordinate/frame equivalence requires an independently given transformation group and invariant observables. It cannot supply \(H_{\rm full}\) without first deciding whether \(I_{s,t}=d(s)\oplus d(t)\) is observable content.
- An asserted collapse such as will/necessity at ground can be an interpretive analogue, but has no AP3 valuation map or preservation theorem and therefore cannot derive valuation gauge.

## Conditional bridge that *would* yield full gauge

One may explicitly add a ground-appearance fibre-quotient postulate:
\[
\mathsf{GAQ}_{\rm trace}:\quad
\operatorname{Trace}(\ell)=\operatorname{Trace}(\ell')
\Longrightarrow \ell\sim_{\rm desc}\ell'.
\]
Since every AP3P2 lift has the same trace, this gives one full-gauge class. But \(\mathsf{GAQ}_{\rm trace}\) says precisely that Q-bit and all cross-sector Q relations add no aspect-content beyond trace. It is a new reductive bridge / minimal-grammar convention, not a theorem from ground unity.

## Registered tests

1. A constant map from 16 lifts to one ground coexists with equivalence relations having 16, 8, or 1 class.
2. Therefore common grounding does not determine the valuation quotient.
3. Full gauge follows conditionally from \(\mathsf{GAQ}_{\rm trace}\), not from common grounding alone.
4. The convention may be used in a labelled minimal fibre-local grammar branch, but may not be misreported as a derived fact about all genuine aspects.

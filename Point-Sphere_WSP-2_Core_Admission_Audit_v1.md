**Object under review:** `Point-Sphere_Weak_Simplicity_Point-Sphere_WSP-2_Entry_Packet_v1.md`  
**Candidate:** \(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}2}\)

## 0. Admission question and protected asymmetry rule

The requested question is narrow:

\[
\mathsf Q_{\rm WSP2}:
\quad
\text{Can the packet's real formal roles be intelligibly nonpartitive and nonindependent,}
\text{ rather than merely so labelled?}
\tag{Admission-Question}
\]

The audit must not use either of the following invalid shortcuts:

\[
\mathsf{DistinctFormalProfile}(d,e)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NumericallyDistinctEntity}(d,e),
\tag{No-Entity-Inflation}
\]

\[
\neg\mathsf{Established}
\bigl(
\mathsf{RealPosImDiff}\Rightarrow\neg\mathsf{PartComposition}

\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Established}
\bigl(
\mathsf{RealPosImDiff}\Rightarrow\mathsf{PartComposition}
\bigr).
\tag{No-Reversal-of-Burden}
\]

Thus a missing defense for nonpartitivity is an incompleteness result; it is not a proof that positive immanent differentiation is impossible or composite.

## 1. Audit basis

The packet fixes a one-bearer structure:

\[
\mathcal I_{\rm WSP\text{-}2}
=
\langle G,D_G,\tau_G,R_G\rangle,
\qquad
D_G^\circ=\{u,s\},
\qquad
\tau_G(u)\neq\tau_G(s).
\tag{Packet-Core}
\]

It declares real formal roles to be excluded from entityhood, parthood, independence, spatiality, temporal staging, and production:

\[
\mathsf{RealFormalRole}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Entity}(d)
\land
\neg\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(d,G)
\land
\neg\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(d,G).
\tag{Packet-Exclusion-Rule}
\]

It also explicitly discloses that weak simplicity does not select this exact two-role field:

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(G)
\not\models
\mathsf{RealPosImDiff}(G,D_G^\circ,R_G^\circ).
\tag{Packet-Residual-Disclosure}
\]

## 2. Check A — typed core consistency

### A.1 One-bearer and nonspatial grammar

Within the packet's declared typed language, the bearer is the only entity, roles are assigned a distinct formal-role type, and the relation has only intrabearer arguments:

\[
R_G\subseteq
D_G\times D_G\times\{\mathsf{ImmanentIncidence}\}.
\tag{Typed-Intrabearer-Relation}
\]

No formula in the packet requires either role to be an external container, a geometric point or sphere, a temporal event, or a separately existing object. The nonliteral Point–Sphere constraint is therefore respected at the level of the declared grammar.

\[
\mathsf{TypedCoreConsistency}
=
\mathsf{Pass}_{\rm declared\ formal\ language}.
\tag{A1-Pass}
\]

### A.2 Strong/weak discriminator

The packet gives a noncircular syntactic contrast between its two simplicity conditions: weak simplicity excludes parts and independent constituents; strong simplicity additionally excludes real positive immanent difference.

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{StrongSimple}^{\sharp}(G)
\quad
\text{within the candidate's stipulated vocabulary.}
\tag{A2-Pass}
\]

This is a defined candidate distinction. It does not establish that the weak condition is metaphysically preferable or globally adequate.

\[
\mathsf{SimplicityDiscriminator}
=
\mathsf{Pass}_{\rm definitional\ only}.
\tag{A2-Status}
\]

### A.3 Formal discernibility

The packet's discernibility condition relies on a role-type inequality or a typed incidence-profile inequality, rather than on actual finite outcomes:

\[
\mathsf{FormallyDiscernible}_{\langle\tau_G,R_G\rangle}(d,e)
\iff
\tau_G(d)\neq\tau_G(e)
\lor
\mathsf{IncidenceProfile}_{R_G}(d)\neq
\mathsf{IncidenceProfile}_{R_G}(e).
\tag{A3-Outcome-Independent-Grammar}
\]

Thus it does not define difference by a desired world, public observation, phenomenology, value, or physical effect.

\[
\mathsf{OutcomeIndependence}
=
\mathsf{Pass}_{\rm grammar}.
\tag{A3-Pass}
\]

## 3. Check B — does type exclusion defend nonpartitivity?

The decisive issue is not whether the packet contains the sentence \(\neg\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(d,G)\). It does. The issue is whether it supplies a reason why a **real** internal contrast may have that status rather than merely assigning it that status.

The packet's support has the following form:

\[
\mathsf{RealFormalRole}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Entity}(d)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(d,G).
\tag{Typed-Exclusion-Path}
\]

That supplies a consistent type rule if the ontology of real formal roles is granted. It does **not** in the packet establish either of these required bridges:

\[
\mathsf{RealFormalRole}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{PositiveRealityWithoutEntityhood}(d,G),
\tag{Missing-Reality-Type-Bridge}
\]

\[
\mathsf{PositiveRealityWithoutEntityhood}(d,G)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PartComposition}(G).
\tag{Missing-AntiComposition-Bridge}
\]

The conclusion is precisely bounded:

\[
\mathsf{NonpartitivityDefense}_{\rm WSP2}
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}.
\tag{B-Result}
\]

This does not produce the contrary result:

\[
\mathsf{NonpartitivityDefense}_{\rm WSP2}
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G).
\tag{B-No-Contrary-Result}
\]

Nor does it establish independent being:

\[
\mathsf{NonpartitivityDefense}_{\rm WSP2}
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}
\not\Rightarrow
\exists d\in D_G\,\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(d,G).
\tag{B-No-Independence-Result}
\]

## 4. Check C — positive content rather than mere type labels

The roles receive names, distinct type values, and a reciprocal incidence:

\[
\mathsf{UnityRole}(u),
\qquad
\mathsf{ArticulationRole}(s),
\qquad
\langle u,s,\mathsf{ImmanentIncidence}\rangle,
\qquad
\langle s,u,\mathsf{ImmanentIncidence}\rangle.
\tag{Role-Profile-As-Given}
\]

However, the packet does not give an intrinsic criterion that determines what makes either role positively the role it is apart from its type label, nor does it explain why the reciprocal incidence is a real formal relation rather than a stipulated graph edge.

The issue is not that an intrinsic criterion must be an actual effect. It must instead be a non-outcome-dependent formal identity condition adequate to distinguish:

\[
\mathsf{PositiveFormalProfile}(u,G)
\neq
\mathsf{PositiveFormalProfile}(s,G)
\tag{Needed-Profile-Difference}
\]

without reducing the distinction to:

\[
\mathsf{Label}(u)\neq\mathsf{Label}(s).
\tag{Insufficient-Label-Difference}
\]

The packet currently defines discernibility from the two type values, but it does not independently ground the two type values. Its positivity and nonbruteness burden is therefore open:

\[
\mathsf{PositiveProfileWarrant}_{\rm WSP2}
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}.
\tag{C-Result}
\]

Again, this is not a refutation of positive formal differentiation. It records that the packet's present grammar provides a typed placeholder, not an independently articulated positive profile.

## 5. Check D — independence

The one-bearer grammar and role-exclusion rule do block an inference from formal discernibility directly to independent entities:

\[
\mathsf{FormallyDiscernible}(u,s)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentEntity}(u)
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentEntity}(s).
\tag{D-No-Entity-Inflation}
\]

But the packet's nonindependence is currently enforced by the same ontological stipulation whose adequate sense remains unargued in Check B. Consequently, it is correct to mark nonindependence as declared but not yet defended:

\[
\mathsf{NonindependenceStatus}_{\rm WSP2}
=
\mathsf{Declared}_{\rm typed\ rule}
\land
\mathsf{NotYetDefended}_{\rm real\ formal\ difference}.
\tag{D-Result}
\]

No conclusion of independent being follows.

## 8. Admission disposition

The typed weak-simplicity grammar, nonliteral Point–Sphere constraint, outcome-independent discernibility grammar, and selector-residual disclosure are adequate as **restricted formal declarations**.

The required substantive bridges for a real, positive, nonpartitive and nonbrute distinction have not been provided. The packet therefore receives this bounded result:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{CoreAdmissionStatus}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm real\ formal\ role\ ontology\ not\ yet\ defended;\\
\rm positive\ role\ profiles\ not\ yet\ independently\ articulated
}}.
}
\tag{WSP2-Entry-Result}
\]

The result does **not** mean \(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}2}\) is disproved, composite, inferior, or globally inadmissible:

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP2}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}2})
\lor
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{ComparativeDefeat}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}2}).
\tag{No-Overread}
\]

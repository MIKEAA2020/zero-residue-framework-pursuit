# Post-PPA Necessary-Emanation: Plotinian Intellect-Domain Bridge Audit v1
## A second, non-Proclean source test for an independently typed emanative output domain

**Lineage:** necessary emanation / plenitude, after closure of the Proclean productive-completion bridge form.  
**Boundary:** this is a source-bounded domain-bridge audit. It is not a new whole-reality formulation, a PPA successor, or a claim that Plotinian Intellect is identical to the current nondual Appearance candidate.

---

## 0. Preregistered question and expiry

The Proclean audit found a conditional productive principle but no independent criterion for the products to which it applies. The present audit tests a nonidentical proposed resource: whether Plotinian Intellect supplies a typed intelligible output domain sufficiently independent of realization to enter a necessary-emanation rival packet.

The charitable target is:

\[
\mathsf{Intellect}_{\rm Pl}(N)
\land
\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}(a,N)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{IntelligibleBeing}_{\rm Pl}(a).
\tag{PID-Target}
\]

The target is not a derivation from the One alone, nor an equivalence between an intelligible Form and every proposed finite mode:

\[
\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}(a,N)
\not\equiv
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(a).
\tag{No-Form-Mode-Identity}
\]

An entry-supporting domain bridge would have to satisfy:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
D_1 &: \text{primary-source support for a distinct Intellect/Form field and its procession relation};\\
D_2 &: \text{a distinction between the historical source architecture and a universal theorem of logic};\\
D_3 &: \text{a noncircular criterion by which an independently proposed }a\text{ is or is not in the Form field};\\
D_4 &: \text{positive, negative, symmetric, and extra-candidate cases};\\
D_5 &: \text{a finite-content, compossibility, and non-composition account adequate to the claimed scope.}
\end{array}
\tag{Domain-Conditions}
\]

**Expiry.** This audit is closed after its source and case assessment. If \(\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}\) fails \(D_3\)–\(D_5\), it cannot be restated as “all coherent contents are already in Intellect,” “the domain is the actual hierarchy,” or “Intellect simply contains the candidate” to claim a domain pass. A later route must submit a genuinely different preregistered input-to-domain bridge.

## 1. Source and interpretation controls

### Primary and interpretive materials

- Plotinus, *Enneads*, especially V.1, V.2, V.4–V.5, and V.9, in the MacKenna–Page translation, accessible through the CCEL edition: https://www.ccel.org/ccel/p/plotinus/enneads/cache/enneads.pdf
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Plotinus,” especially its account of the One, Intellect, the Forms, and the procreative-power and plenitude principles: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/plotinus/

The source control records the following Plotinian architecture without attributing it to Proclus:

\[
\text{One/Good}
\quad\succ\quad
\text{Intellect}\;(N)
\quad\succ\quad
\text{Soul}
\quad\succ\quad
\text{sensible order}.
\tag{Plotinian-Order}
\]

In particular, the One is not treated as a self-thinking intellect. The Forms are associated with the derivative Intellect, which is an articulated intelligible order. The distinction is mandatory:

\[
\text{Plotinian One}
\ne
\text{Plotinian Intellect}
\ne
\text{Proclean unparticipated One}
\ne
\text{nondual self-knowledge content by stipulation}.
\tag{No-Homogenization}
\]

## 2. Charitable local reconstruction

The source supplies a more articulated candidate-domain resource than bare “all possibilities.” In the relevant Plotinian picture, Intellect is a derivative hypostasis containing intelligible Forms in a unified, interrelated order. A conservative reconstruction is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{PID}_{\rm Pl}(N,\mathcal F)
\iff
&\mathsf{DerivativeOfOne}_{\rm Pl}(N)
\land\mathsf{Intellect}_{\rm Pl}(N)\\
&\land\forall a\,[
\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}(a,N)
\rightarrow
\mathsf{IntelligibleBeing}_{\rm Pl}(a)]\\
&\land\mathsf{UnifiedIntelligibleOrder}_{\rm Pl}(N,\mathcal F).
\end{aligned}
\tag{PID-Pl}
\]

This gives three real conditional resources:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
R_1 &: \text{a determinate ontological level for intelligible content, rather than a bare formal}\
&\text{description space};\\
R_2 &: \text{a source-internal unity/multiplicity architecture in which Forms are not external}\
&\text{objects merely juxtaposed to Intellect};\\
R_3 &: \text{a non-temporal procession relation linking an articulated order to a prior source.}
\end{array}
\tag{PID-Resources}
\]

These resources should not be discounted merely because they are nonempirical or historically specific. The question is whether they meet the stronger public entry demand.

## 3. Candidate-domain audit

### 3.1 Independence from realized hierarchy

For entry readiness, the following would be needed:

\[
\mathsf{ProposedCandidate}(x)
\Longrightarrow
\bigl[
\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}(x,N)
\ \lor\ 
\neg\mathsf{FormIn}_{\rm Pl}(x,N)
\bigr]
\tag{Independent-Form-Verdict}
\]

on independently stated truth conditions.

The available source architecture does not supply such a rule for arbitrary \(x\). It identifies Forms within Intellect, but no public candidate-level procedure decides whether a proposed finite item is a Form, an image/instantiation, an individual, a property, a mathematical representation, an incompatible candidate, or a merely named object.

\[
\mathsf{PID}_{\rm Pl}(N,\mathcal F)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentFormVerdict}(x,N).
\tag{No-Independent-Form-Verdict}
\]

To define the domain as “all Forms in the intelligible order” is source-internally meaningful but does not answer the entry question unless membership in that order can classify candidates without assuming the intended Plotinian ontology.

### 3.2 Fixed cases

\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
\textbf{case} & \textbf{resource of the source architecture} & \textbf{remaining burden}\\
\hline
\text{a stipulated intelligible Form} & \text{it is internal to }N\text{ by stipulation} & \text{independent membership test}\\
\text{a concrete finite individual} & \text{may be related to form, soul, or sensible order} & \text{no direct finite-mode verdict}\\
\text{a formal mathematical structure} & \text{formal coherence is distinguishable from an intelligible Form} & \text{ontology/representation bridge}\\
\text{two symmetric candidates} & \text{unified intelligibility does not itself individuate them} & \text{non-gauge particularity rule}\\
\text{a property candidate} & \text{the source has a layered ontology} & \text{Form/property/mode boundary in the entry scope}\\
\text{an extra candidate} & \text{membership could not be decided merely by a new label} & \text{positive inclusion/exclusion condition}\\
\text{incompatible candidates} & \text{Intellect is unified rather than a random set} & \text{explicit compossibility/context semantics}
\end{array}
\tag{PID-Cases}
\]

### 3.3 Exactness and adequacy

Even a successful source-internal Form domain would not by itself derive an exact finite totality, phenomenal modes, norms, or physical structure:

\[
\mathsf{PID}_{\rm Pl}(N,\mathcal F)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactFiniteTotality}
\land
\mathsf{PhenomenalBridge}
\land
\mathsf{NormativeBridge}
\land
\mathsf{PhysicalBridge}.
\tag{No-Whole-Reality-Pass}
\]

The audit does not treat that as an objection peculiar to Plotinus. The same noncompensatory conditions govern every rival entry.

## 5. Disposition

\[
\mathsf{Disposition}(\mathsf{PID}_{\rm Pl})
=
\mathsf{Closed}_{\rm source\text{-}internal\ domain\ resource,\ no\ independent\ membership}.
\tag{PID-Disposition}
\]

The selected necessary-emanation research slot has now produced two non-equivalent, local source resources and no submitted rival entry packet:

\[
\neg\exists F_R\,
\mathsf{SubmittedRivalEntryPacket}_{\rm NEP}(F_R).
\tag{No-NEP-Entry}
\]

A further necessary-emanation attempt must satisfy the post-PPA charter through a new independently specified interface, rather than recombining the closed \(\mathsf{PPC}_{\rm Pr}\) and \(\mathsf{PID}_{\rm Pl}\) forms or asserting that their conjunction fills the missing domain bridge.

\[
\exists![F]_{\simeq}\,\mathsf{ZeroResidue}(F)
\quad\text{remains unproved.}
\tag{Open-Objective}
\]

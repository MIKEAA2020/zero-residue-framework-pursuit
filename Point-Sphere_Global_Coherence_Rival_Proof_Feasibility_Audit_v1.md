# Global-Coherence Rival Proof Feasibility Audit v1
## GC0 — completed typing and dependency audit; no core revision

## Purpose

This audit records the exact status of the proposed claim that **simple ground plus appearance/plenitude is uniquely globally coherent**, and that the same anti-brute move rules out a classical creator–creation framework.

It is a pre-core-revision audit. It does **not** replace the protected creation hard core, declare the conditional appearance branch true, infer a physical theory, or revise the Problem-of-Time ledger. It instead makes the proposed proof testable in topological order.

The supplied `A-posteriori-identity-challenge` transcripts are treated as argument prompts, not authorities. Repeated assistant agreement, rhetorical certainty, or an assertion that a conclusion is “the end” has no evidential force.

## 1. Claim levels that must not be conflated

| Label | Claim | What would establish it |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | A framework is syntactically/formally consistent in a declared model | Model construction or consistency proof |
| L1 | A framework has fewer unexplained differentiators than a rival under a declared standard | Common criterion and matched rival audit |
| L2 | A framework is uniquely globally coherent | A definition of global coherence, exhaustive rival classes, and proof that every rival fails a stated condition or is equivalent |
| L3 | The uniquely globally coherent framework is true | A bridge from L2 to truth that includes adequacy to phenomenal and physical data, not only internal coherence |
| L4 | The framework yields physics | Explicit state space, equations/constraint algebra, recovery regime, and discriminator |

The active proposal aims at L2/L3. Existing AP0–AP4 and R1 artifacts provide partial L0/L1 resources only. No completed artifact has reached L2, L3, or L4.

## 2. Typed vocabulary

Let:

- \(G\): the proposed simple, non-structural ground;
- \(\Omega\): a formally declared domain of candidate finite totalities or aspect descriptions;
- \(\mathcal A\subseteq\Omega\): a domain independently typed as genuine finite self-knowledge contents, not merely coherent descriptions;
- \(R(a)\): \(a\) is realized;
- \(\mathsf{Act}(w)\): created totality \(w\) is actual in a creator–creation rival;
- \(\Delta(x,y)\): a declared difference-maker sufficient to explain why \(x\), rather than \(y\), bears the relevant actuality status;
- \(\mathsf{CI}\): a **to-be-defined** global-coherence condition, not a synonym for consistency, explanatory preference, or truth.

The protected nondual identity, if adopted, is:

\[
\operatorname{GenuineMode}(m)
\equiv
\operatorname{SelfAspect}_G(m)
\equiv
\operatorname{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(m).
\]

It does not authorize the illicit identification \(G\equiv\mathcal M\) with any mathematical description \(\mathcal M\), nor does it make each describable element of \(\Omega\) a genuine aspect.

## 3. Existing results that control the audit

The following completed work must be used rather than repeated or ignored.

1. **AP1/AP2A.** Gauge quotient, physical symmetry, admissibility, and no-preference alone do not derive nonempty realization. On a transitive admissible orbit, nonempty invariant realization entails full realization; neither nonemptiness nor the domain follows from symmetry.

\[
R\neq\varnothing\ \land\ R\subseteq\mathcal A\ \land\ R\text{ invariant}
\Longrightarrow R=\mathcal A,
\]

but

\[
\mathcal A\text{ nonempty and invariant}\not\Longrightarrow R\neq\varnothing.
\]

2. **R1b/R1c.** A determinate necessary totality needs an exact-totality bridge; an orbit proposal needs substantive non-gauge distinctness and, if probabilities are claimed, an appropriate measure; a contingent selection account needs a non-arbitrary, non-collapsing selector.

3. **AP2B/B4.** The aware-ground (AG) package has a conditional comparative advantage under explanatory-unification standards, but the prior rival audit did not establish an aware ground, a maximal realization domain, or public victory over rivals.

4. **AP3P3C.** A formal grammar is an appearance-level description. Coherence, formal usefulness, or a groupoid theorem does not automatically establish mode/aspect fidelity.

## 4. The creation-selection argument: exact conditional form

For a classical creation rival, consider distinct candidate totalities \(w_1,w_2\). The intended no-brute-differentiation condition is:

\[
\mathsf{NBD}:
\quad
\mathsf{Act}(w_1)\land\neg\mathsf{Act}(w_2)
\Longrightarrow
\exists\delta\,\Delta(w_1,w_2;\delta).
\]

If a rival affirms contingent exclusive actualization but supplies no such \(\delta\), then it fails **\(\mathsf{NBD}\)** by definition. If \(\delta\) is outside the ultimate ground, it conflicts with ultimacy; if it is a real differentiating component within a strictly simple ground, it creates a simplicity burden; if it is fully entailed by ground nature, the relevant result is necessary rather than libertarianly contingent.

This yields a genuine conditional dilemma:

\[
\mathsf{StrictSimplicity}(G)
\land
\mathsf{NBD}
\land
\mathsf{ContingentExclusiveCreation}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SelectorDebt}.
\]

It does **not yet** yield a theorem that every classical creator–creation theory is inconsistent. Such a theorem would have to establish that no account of a single undivided, non-arbitrary act can satisfy \(\mathsf{NBD}\) without violating strict simplicity. That is GC2’s exact target.

A rival may deny \(\mathsf{NBD}\), posit primitive agent causation, deny that alternatives are symmetry-equivalent, or adopt necessary determination. Those moves incur costs, but they are not formal contradictions merely by being stated.

## 5. The plenitude hinge: exact conditional form

The proposed maximal-expression closure is:

\[
\mathsf{MEC}:
\qquad
\forall a\in\mathcal A\,
\bigl(\mathsf{GenuineContent}_G(a)\Rightarrow R(a)\bigr).
\]

The desired argument is that a complete ground cannot omit a genuine content of its own non-representational self-knowledge without either a differentiator or a brute restriction.

This is promising only if the following are independently established:

1. \(\mathcal A\) is typed independently of \(R\). Otherwise \(\mathsf{MEC}\) is circular.
2. A non-realized \(a\in\mathcal A\) genuinely constitutes a restriction of complete self-knowledge, not merely a nonactual external possibility.
3. The grounds for inclusion/exclusion cannot be supplied without a selector incompatible with the proposed ground/aspect identity.
4. All incompatible finite contents are handled by declared compossibility/context conditions rather than jointly asserted in one inconsistent finite perspective.

Thus the exact current result is:

\[
\mathsf{AwareGroundIdentity}
+\mathsf{MEC}
\Longrightarrow
\text{conditional immanent plenitude},
\]

not:

\[
\mathsf{Simplicity}
+\mathsf{NoPreference}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{MEC}.
\]

The latter inference is blocked by AP2A unless a further aware-ground identity theorem proves the missing bridge.

## 6. Why the transcript’s “three are only specification problems” claim fails

| Claimed discharge | What actually remains |
|---|---|
| “Admissibility is coherence” | Coherent formal description does not entail genuine aspecthood or realization. A criterion for \(\mathcal A\) is needed. |
| “Maximal proper contexts solve compossibility” | They block one contradiction error but do not prove which contexts are genuine aspects or realized. |
| “Aspect-content avoids composition” | It is the correct nondual typing, but must be shown to preserve genuine finite reality without reifying ground-level parts or reducing modes to labels. |
| “The hinge is forced” | This is the central theorem still to be proved: omission must be shown to be an unacceptable restriction of self-knowledge rather than a permitted absence of external creation. |

These are not equal to the creation-selection debt. The latter is a sharp rival problem under \(\mathsf{NBD}\); the former are exact constructive obligations for Appearance. They must all be discharged before a unique-global-coherence verdict.

## 7. The CI-to-truth bridge is a separate theorem

The inference

\[
\text{complete intelligibility is a norm of inquiry}
\Longrightarrow
\text{reality is completely intelligible}
\]

is not automatic. It moves from an epistemic norm to an ontological conclusion.

A complete proof requires a transcendental theorem showing that denial of \(\mathsf{CI}\) is not only explanatorily unattractive but performatively inconsistent: that any act of assertion/denial presupposes the absence of an ultimate unintelligible remainder. The simple observation that someone consciously argues for a brute fact does not yet prove this; their reason for accepting a brute fact is not thereby an ontological ground of that fact.

Likewise, uniqueness requires more than the fact that two incompatible theories cannot both be true. It requires:

\[
\text{exhaustive rival taxonomy}
+
\text{rival failure/equivalence proofs}
+
\text{phenomenal and empirical adequacy}.
\]

## 8. Feasibility outcome and gate decision

### Completed GC0 result

The proof target is now decomposed without conflating consistency, comparative parsimony, global coherence, truth, and physics. Existing AP/R diagnostics establish that neither bare symmetry nor an unnamed selector settles realization.

### Next topological node

**GC1 — No-Brute-Differentiation theorem audit** is the highest-feasibility decisive next task. It must define \(\mathsf{CI}\) and \(\mathsf{NBD}\) with truth conditions, give rival-acceptable formulations, and attempt both:

- a proof that a non-differentiated exclusive actuality violates \(\mathsf{CI}\); and
- countermodels where a theory retains rational local inquiry while treating an ultimate actuality as primitive.

### Explicit stop rule

If GC1 cannot prove the required transcendental bridge after a predeclared adversarial set of semantics, the programme must retain the weaker claim:

\[
\text{Appearance has a conditional comparative global-coherence advantage},
\]

not upgrade it to an established unique truth.

## Nonclaims

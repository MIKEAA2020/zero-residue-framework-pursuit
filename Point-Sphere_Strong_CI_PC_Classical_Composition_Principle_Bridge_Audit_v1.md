# Strong CI Adjudication — PC Classical Composition-Principle Bridge Audit v1
## Does every real articulation in an ultimate closure incur explanatory residue?

## Status

**A narrow composition principle is conditionally valid for independently real constituents whose unity is unexplained. The universal classical principle needed to infer absolute simplicity from non-dependence has not been established.** B2 remains held.

## 1. The precise B2 target

The classical bridge requires more than the valid result against primitive aggregates:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate}.
\tag{Earned}
\]

It requires a principle sufficient for:

\[
\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(U)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity}(U).
\tag{B2 target}
\]

The proposed engine is a composition principle:

\[
\mathsf{CP}^{*}:
\quad
\mathsf{RealArticulation}(U)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ResidualUnity}(U).
\]

If \(\mathsf{CP}^{*}\) were established, strongest CI would exclude every articulated ultimate. But that is exactly the disputed bridge.

## 2. Four non-equivalent notions

| Notion | Minimal content | Does it itself incur a unity debt? |
|---|---|---:|
| Conceptual/rational distinction | Different descriptions or concepts of one item | no |
| Formal distinction | Non-identical intelligible respects without separable constituents | not yet |
| Constituent composition | Distinct ontological principles/components jointly constituting an item | often, if their unity is unexplained |
| Whole-first articulation | Internally differentiated profile of one prior whole, with elements not independently basic | not merely by being articulated |

A valid B2 argument must specify why a particular distinction in \(U\) belongs to the third category rather than merely calling every difference “composition.”

## 3. Narrow composition principle

The following conditional principle is earned as a diagnostic, not a universal theorem:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{IndependentlyReal}(c_1)
\land
\mathsf{IndependentlyReal}(c_2)
\land
\mathsf{Constitute}(c_1,c_2,U)\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{InternalUnityAccount}(U,c_1,c_2)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ResidualUnity}(U).
\end{aligned}
\tag{CP-N}
\]

The debt arises from **independently real constituent plurality plus absent unification account**, not from plurality of descriptions, finite articulation, or a distinction simpliciter.

This supports the anti-aggregate result. It does not apply to a candidate merely because the candidate has internally distinguishable content.

## 4. The classical strengthening and its required premises

To infer absolute simplicity, a defender needs at least:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{RealArticulation}(U)
\Rightarrow
\exists c_1,c_2\,[
\mathsf{IndependentlyReal}(c_1)
\land
\mathsf{IndependentlyReal}(c_2)\\
&\hspace{7em}\land
\mathsf{Constitute}(c_1,c_2,U)
],
\tag{S1}\\
&\mathsf{InternalUnityAccount}(U,c_1,c_2)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{CircularOrRegressive}(U),
\tag{S2}\\
&\mathsf{ResidualUnity}(U)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(U).
\tag{S3}
\end{aligned}
\]

S1 is denied by whole-first and nondual-aspect profiles. S2 is denied by an intrinsic-unity account. S3 is close to the accepted strongest-CI anti-residue commitment, but only after the prior two claims establish a genuine residual unity.

Therefore:

\[
\mathsf{CP\text{-}N}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{CP}^{*}.
\]

## 5. Rival stress tests

### A. Primitive aggregate

Distinct fundamental members plus an unexplained fact of their unity satisfy CP-N. The candidate carries a residual-unity debt.

### B. Whole-first essential articulation

The candidate says that \(U\), not \(c_1+c_2\), is ontologically primary, and that the elements are dependent intelligible articulations of the whole. CP-N applies only if the whole-first claim is shown to conceal independently real constituents or a primitive internal unity.

Calling the elements “parts” does not show this.

### C. Nondual mode/aspect identity

The protected package says mode, aspect, and content of self-knowledge are identical in type. It rejects an aggregate of independently real parts. It still owes public finite-domain semantics, but that distinct debt does not establish composition residue.

### D. Necessary structural whole

The candidate claims its relations and relata are internally fixed by one global profile. This may fail as a primitive totality; it does not fail merely because relations or structure are mentioned. It needs a separate exact-profile/self-intelligibility argument.

## 6. What a successful B2 argument would require

The classical defender must do one of the following without question-begging:

1. defend S1: prove that every real articulation—even alleged whole-first or aspect articulation—requires independently real constituents;
2. defend S2: show that every internal-unity account either re-describes the required unity or generates a real regress; or
3. give a positive classical metaphysics of act/potency, essence/existence, and unity that derives S1–S3 and distinguishes genuine unity from primitive co-instantiation.

No appeal solely to “no parts,” “no composition,” “absolute simplicity,” or “the One” meets this condition, because each repeats the disputed classification.

## 7. Exact result

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate},
\qquad
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity}.
}
\]

And specifically:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(U)
\land
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity}(U).
}
\]

The classical B2 bridge requires \(\mathsf{CP}^{*}\) or an equivalent substantive composition theory. It has not been supplied.

## 8. Source note

Classical divine simplicity is a substantive constituent-ontology doctrine covering metaphysical as well as spatial parts; it treats essence–existence, act–potency, and subject–attribute distinctions as relevant forms of composition. The doctrine’s coherence and its account of property possession remain contested. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/.

## 9. Expiry condition

B2 advances only with an argument establishing S1 or S2, or with a positive constituent ontology that yields them while meeting the rival stress tests. Otherwise the only retained result is CP-N and the prior anti-primitive-aggregate theorem.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not prove that whole-first articulation, nondual identity, or a necessary structural whole succeeds. It does not reject classical simplicity. It blocks only the unargued universalization from unexplained aggregate composition to all real articulation.

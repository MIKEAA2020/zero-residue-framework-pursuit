# Corrigendum to AP3H: Identity Is Core; Grammar Is the Live Formal Question

## Accepted correction

AP3H established a distinction that must not be described as a new foundational-ontology debt.

The framework’s intended circular identity is:

\[
\text{mode}=	ext{aspect}=	ext{content of self-knowledge}.
\]

On the strong nondual reading, this is not a productive relation between independent relata and not an extra realisation axiom. It is the core identity claim, analogous in role to the point/circle-at-zero image: the distinction is perspectival/formal rather than a division in the ground.

What remains open is not whether a mode needs an independent ontological source. It is the **grammar of self-presentation**:

\[
\smile_{\rm SP}\;:\quad
\text{which determinations are jointly self-presentable as one finite mode?}
\]

## Qualified exclusion of universal compatibility

The core includes real finite appearance. In the AP3G implementation, a mode is a bounded **proper maximal context**. Under that implementation, universal compatibility yields one whole context and therefore zero proper maximal contexts. It is excluded by the core’s appearance commitment:

\[
\text{real finite modes}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\neg\smile_U
\quad\text{within the maximal-proper-context grammar.}
\]

This is not a general proof that universal co-presence is metaphysically impossible under every conceivable mode formalisation. It says that universal compatibility cannot play the required grammar role in the specific bounded-context formal scheme.

## Corrected task

The research question is therefore narrower than “warrant or adopt a membership primitive”:

> **What principled structure of self-presentation makes a determination a finite mode of a self-knowing ground?**

The three routes are:

1. **Phenomenological:** identify the minimal pre-reflective self-presence, boundedness, and self/world distinction a finite mode must instantiate. Explicit propositional recognition that it is the ground cannot be required of every finite mode; the relevant target is structural self-presence, not doctrinal self-recognition.
2. **Formal:** characterize relations satisfying reflexivity, real bounded modes, totality over presentation contents, non-gauge identity, integration, and nonexclusive coexistence.
3. **Physical:** seek a structure that recovers actual integrated records/locality without building a preferred partition into the premise.

If none selects or sharply characterizes a principled grammar, IAR may be adopted as a labelled formalisation of the core with its cost stated. Until then, it is revisable formal work, not a new foundational primitive.

# AP2B/B4 — Adversarial Intrinsic-Nature Adjudication v1
## Applying the frozen ledger without numerical metaphysical scoring

## Decision question

Does the complete, simple, self-knowing **aware-ground hypothesis** (AG) presently have a comparative advantage over neutral monism (NM), Russellian monism (RM), structural realism (SR), and brute necessary totality (BN) as an account of ultimate intrinsic nature?

This adjudication applies the frozen criteria and discriminators in `Point-Sphere_Appearance_Plenitude_Intrinsic_Nature_Comparative_Ledger_v1.md`. It does not ask which position is emotionally attractive, which sounds simplest in isolation, or which has accumulated the most assistant agreement in the transcript.

## 1. AG versus neutral monism

### Strongest AG case

AG begins with phenomenal presence as a datum and supplies a positive intrinsic nature: awareness. It avoids postulating a neutral base whose nature is only named negatively. If fundamental reality is one complete awareness, subjectivity is not a late-arriving anomaly. It also has a principled route to immanent self-aspects and P-internal plenitude.

### Strongest NM reply

The inference from experience to cosmic awareness is underdetermined. A neutral base can underlie both mental and physical organisation without possessing creature-like self-knowledge or a cosmic subject. Calling the basic “awareness” may merely project a feature of organised local consciousness onto the ultimate. AG faces a decombination problem; NM avoids assuming a single cosmic subject in the first place.

### Adjudication

AG has the better **positive intrinsic-nature proposal** under K1/K2: it says what the basic is rather than merely that it is not mental or physical. NM remains live because this positive content is purchased by a larger extrapolation. Neither has passed K3. The outcome is a **conditional AG advantage only for a methodology that prefers positive explanatory unity over ontological reticence**. It is not a refutation of neutral monism.

## 2. AG versus Russellian monism/panprotopsychism

### Strongest AG case

AG accepts the intrinsic-nature pressure but does not attempt to construct one subject from a plurality of micro-subjects. A simple unitary awareness makes macro-experiential unity basic rather than emergent. Finite subjects can be treated as restricted modes, avoiding an unexplained jump from many tiny viewpoints to one large viewpoint.

### Strongest RM reply

RM is more conservative with respect to physics: it retains the entities and causal roles of physics while locating consciousness-relevant categorical properties beneath the structural description. AG’s alleged advantage just reverses the difficult direction. A cosmic/whole awareness must still yield many bounded subjects, and this decombination relation is not presently more intelligible than subject-summing. RM need not make consciousness a universal self-knowing ground.

### Adjudication

RM is the strongest live rival. AG has a **unity resource**; RM has a **physics-continuity resource**. Neither has supplied the required construction under K3/K4. There is no current overall preference between AG and the best cosmopsychist/Russellian alternatives. A claim that AG has already solved the combination problem would be false: it changes the problem to decombination.

## 3. AG versus structural realism

### Strongest AG case

A purely structural account appears unable, on its face, to tell us why there is phenomenal presence or what it is like to be a bounded subject. AG supplies a non-structural intrinsic basis and can regard physics as the relational/structural articulation of modes without treating mathematical form as self-interpreting consciousness.

### Strongest SR reply

Epistemic structural realism does not claim that structure alone is the ultimate intrinsic nature. It says that science warrants structural commitment while cautioning against precisely the speculative metaphysical extrapolation AG makes. Ontic structural realism, meanwhile, may offer a parsimonious ontology of relations/gauge-invariant structure that AG must recover rather than merely reinterpret.

### Adjudication

AG has greater explanatory **ambition** for consciousness. SR has stronger immediate **discipline from successful science**. ESR is not defeated because it can suspend ultimate claims; OSR is not defeated because AG has not shown that structure cannot be ontologically fundamental. AG may only claim that SR alone does not complete a consciousness ontology. K4 remains a major AG debt.

## 4. AG versus brute necessary totality

### Strongest AG case

AG does not merely state that there is a necessary totality. It proposes why necessary reality has an intrinsic character, why consciousness is not accidental, why self-presentation is actual, and—conditionally—why genuine intrinsic aspects are not arbitrarily excluded. It is explanatory unification rather than bare termination.

### Strongest BN reply

Every explanatory programme must stop somewhere. The demand for a further reason can produce pseudo-explanations whose special vocabulary (“complete,” “self-presentation,” “ground”) only redescribes the terminus. A brute necessary totality is sparse and honest, whereas AG adds awareness, simplicity, self-knowledge, aspect identity, and a mode map without independent confirmation.

### Adjudication

AG is superior only if explanatory depth, a positive intrinsic nature, and a non-brute account of consciousness are legitimate theoretical virtues at the fundamental level. BN is undefeated for anyone who rejects a strong principle of sufficient reason or views consciousness as an allowed brute fact. This is a methodological fault line, not a simple factual disagreement.

## 5. Classical simple creator control

CT shows why “simple, complete, and self-knowing” is not by itself enough for plenitude. Classical accounts can identify divine attributes and existence while retaining a distinction between divine self-knowledge, possible contents, and actual dependent creation. They face their own modal-collapse tensions, but remain a coherent countermodel to a bare knowledge-to-actuality inference.

AG responds by denying that its relevant aspects are external contents or creations. They are supposed to be immanent nondual self-presentations. That reply preserves P-internal as a conditional conclusion, but it bears an exact burden:

\[
\text{genuine intrinsic self-aspect}
\quad\neq\quad
\text{merely known possibility or external created object}.
\]

Until the framework supplies a usable aspect/mode criterion, CT prevents promotion from P-internal to P-maximal.

## Verdict

The strict result is **limited conditional preference with unresolved tie-breakers**, not confirmation.

AG currently has the most integrated *positive* story connecting phenomenal reality, intrinsic nature, unity, and an immanent P-internal realisation route. That is a real comparative virtue. It gives a conditional basis for treating the framework as a serious leading hypothesis rather than a mere image.

But the two unresolved debts are decisive:

1. **K3:** bounded-perspective individuation/decombination; and
2. **K4:** recovery of physics without external structure or verbal re-description.

Because RM/cosmopsychist alternatives share the consciousness/intrinsic-nature motivation and because SR retains a strong scientific-discipline advantage, AG is not presently established as the most likely ultimate framework in a public, rival-neutral sense.

The calibrated conclusion is:

> **AG is presently the leading internal candidate under an explanatory-unification methodology, and P-internal plenitude conditionally follows if its nondual aspect identity is valid. AG is not yet the externally established winner; its decisive test is whether it can construct bounded modes and recover public physics.**

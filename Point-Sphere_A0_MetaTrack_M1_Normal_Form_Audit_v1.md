# A0 Meta-Track M1 — NF1–NF7 Normal-Form Audit v1
## Audit of the frozen ten-package ledger under the v2 protocol

## Status

This document performs **M1 only** of `Point-Sphere_A0_MetaTrack_Primary_Taxonomy_Protocol_v2.md`, using the frozen M0 ledger.

It applies the v2 normal-form checks:

\[
\mathsf{FitsNF}(C)
\iff
\bigwedge_{j=1}^{7}\mathsf{NF}j(C)=\mathsf{Yes}.
\]

It does not introduce a new tradition, reconstruct a source anew, certify an unarticulated novelty as a residual, attempt a family lemma, or infer an A0 no-go.

## 1. Audit conventions

### 1.1 Meaning of `Yes` and `No`

`Yes` means only that the package **states the relevant role** at the v2 normal-form level. It does not mean that the role is true, adequate, successful under A0, or source-uncontroversial.

`No` means that the role is absent, is supplied only by an already-admitted inventory, is limited to a different target, or is not publicly stated at the candidate level required by the v2 schema. It does **not** mean that the ontology has no resources or that no strengthened version is possible.

### 1.2 Fixed checks

| Check | M1 question |
|---|---|
| NF1 | Is an independent candidate field and stated scope supplied? |
| NF2 | Is there a stated A0-relevant admission rule with inputs and admit/reject/withhold implications? |
| NF3 | Is there an actuality rule, rather than only relation, identity, status, or description? |
| NF4 | Is there a mode/property/independent-substance/nonentity treatment in the stated scope? |
| NF5 | Is there a declared profile extractor, equivalence class, or public re-encoding condition? |
| NF6 | Is there a public error, mistyping, or inapplicability condition? |
| NF7 | Is there a non-roster check preventing the criterion from taking its desired inventory as input? |

### 1.3 Classification rule

A package receives exactly one M1 label:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{FitsNF} &: \text{all NF1--NF7 are Yes};\\
\mathsf{CertifiedResidual} &: \text{it has a complete v2 residual certificate and does not faithfully fit NF};\\
\mathsf{Unclassified} &: \text{otherwise.}
\end{array}
\]

A certified residual requires the complete certificate stated in v2 §3.3. A label of `Unclassified` blocks coverage; it is not a covert rejection.

## 3. Package-level grounds

### P1 — Classical essence–existence identity

The dossier gives a terminal-question challenge concerning a candidate ground, not an independently scoped field of finite-mode candidates. Essence–existence identity is neither a public finite-mode admission predicate nor an extracted mode/property/substance/nonentity discriminator. The available contrast with contingent beings is philosophically substantive but does not constitute NF1–NF7 for the A0 target.

### P2 — Nondual self-knowledge content

The formal candidate field for \(a\) is available, so NF1 is `Yes`. The protected theorem begins with \(\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)\); it does not provide the missing public premise that determines genuine aspecthood from an independent candidate profile. Hence NF2 and NF3 are not supplied at the required interface. Coherence, gauge, and context do not yet supply an all-and-only type, invariance, error, or anti-roster rule.

### P3 — Proclean participation

The source reconstruction has a conditional proper-effect formula and a proper/parasitic contrast, yielding NF1, NF2, and a limited error resource. But the formula conditions a proposed effect on an already specified cause, natural procession, end, and triad; it does not supply an actuality predicate or framework-neutral A0 types. No profile rule or non-roster candidate-admission rule is recovered.

### P4 — Advaitic *adhyāsa*

The dossier states a field of contents/loci/correcting cognitions and a genuine local sublation/error condition. Its \(\mathsf{Mithyā}\) rule is a status classification of appearance, not an admission rule for genuine finite modes; it does not deliver the needed actuality, type, invariance, or extra-candidate conditions. This labels the package unclassified for A0 without denying its local error semantics.

### P5 — Hegelian determinate negation

A logical-determination field is stated, but no public realization rule takes a derived determination to a genuine finite mode. The dossier records exactly that missing transition. It also has no neutral profile extractor, finite-mode type rule, public error criterion, or anti-roster completeness test. The unclassified label does not deny an account of conceptual determinacy.

### P6 — Leibnizian complete concept / PII

A candidate field and a strong conditional identity resource are stated. PII says when already admitted candidates with complete profiles are identical; it neither generates actual candidates nor determines which full profiles obtain. Consequently, it does not supply the A0 admission, actuality, type, invariance, public-error, or anti-roster roles.

### P7 — Spinozist immanent productivity

The dossier supplies the richest M1 role set in the frozen record: an explicit candidate field, an `ActualFiniteMode` formula, and a substance/mode architecture. Its stated formula still presupposes finite candidate, mode, causal, and attribute determinations whose public candidate-level application is not independently recovered. The type rule does not yet decide all A0 alternatives, no profile-invariance account is supplied, and no public error/non-roster rule decides an arbitrary proposed candidate. Accordingly, it is not a v2 normal-form fit.

### P8 — Rāmānuja body relation

The body relation has an explicit candidate condition and makes a real-dependent actuality claim, so NF1–NF3 are recorded `Yes` at the stated source-internal level. But it starts with \(\mathsf{Dravya}_{R}(x)\) and lacks a public rule deciding that eligibility for a proposed candidate. The partial property/body distinction does not yet provide all A0 types; invariance, error, and anti-roster checks are unrecovered.

### P9 — Aristotelian *energeia*

The actuality tuple gives an explicit local actuality resource (NF3 `Yes`) for a bearer, capacity, form/end, and exercise/completion. It is not a rule admitting an arbitrary finite candidate as a non-property mode, and the record supplies no independent candidate-field profile, re-encoding rule, public error rule, or anti-roster application procedure. This is a scope result, not a charge of circularity against *energeia*.

### P10 — Ṣadrian intensive existence

The reconstructed field of contingent candidates, mental/extra-mental contrast, degrees, and dependence is enough for NF1. But the public-effects audit specifically failed to recover a public admission rule from effects to intensive rank and finite modehood. The source’s mental/extra-mental and individuation resources do not by themselves supply the required type, invariance, public error, or non-roster roles. The broader intensive interface is not thereby excluded: it is not a **certified** residual because no complete v2 §3.3 certificate has yet been supplied.

## 4. M1 adjudication

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\#\mathsf{FitsNF} &=& 0,\\
\#\mathsf{CertifiedResidual} &=& 0,\\
\#\mathsf{Unclassified} &=& 10.
\end{array}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\neg\mathsf{CoverageEstablished}_{\rm Phase\ I}.
}
\]

This result has a narrow reading. It says that none of the ten already frozen packages has been stated with all of the v2 public-admission roles, and none yet has a complete residual certificate. It does **not** say:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{that the ten ontologies are false};\\
&\text{that no package can be developed into an eligible A0 criterion};\\
&\text{that normal-form fit is impossible};\\
&\text{that a residual has been eliminated};\\
&\text{or that A0 is unsatisfiable.}
\end{aligned}
\]

In particular, the audit confirms that the programme cannot use the ten dossiers as a family-level no-go proof. They presently function as a disciplined record of distinct, incomplete bridges.

## 5. M1 consequence and next permitted step

M1 does not terminate Phase I. The next permitted work product is **M2 only**: the predeclared coverage challenge, which must present at least one serious attempted escape for each displayed interface family and record whether it fits NF, obtains a complete residual certificate, or remains unclassified.

M2 may not infer that the ten `Unclassified` labels close any interface family. It may not introduce an eleventh historical tradition or a new whole-reality framework. If M2 leaves any interface or candidate unclassified, coverage remains unproved.

## Nonclaim

This audit does not downgrade the ten source reconstructions into mere failures, conflate their criterion types, turn lack of an extracted profile rule into a proof that no profile rule is possible, certify the Ṣadrian route as closed, or establish a no-go conclusion.

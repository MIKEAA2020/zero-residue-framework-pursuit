# GA2a — Self-Knowledge Requirement / Hinge-Identity Audit v1
## Bounded specification with expiry condition

## Decision question

Can a realization-independent criterion for finite determination of the ground be obtained from what complete self-knowledge *requires*, or does the proposed criterion merely restate the aspect-domain/maximal-expression hinge?

This is a bounded GA2 sub-audit. It does not reopen realization selection, directedness, persistence, public records, physics, or unrestricted all-world plenitude.

## 1. Four candidate routes

For a finite candidate \(a\):

| Route | Proposed criterion | GA2 status |
|---|---|---|
| Realization-defined | \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\leftrightarrow R(a)\) | Circular: the omission theorem takes genuine aspecthood/content as antecedent and derives realization. |
| Structural-defined | \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\leftrightarrow P(a)\), for structural/formal \(P\) | Excluded as a derivation by GA1’s matched structural expansions. |
| Self-presentability | \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\leftrightarrow\mathsf{SP}(a)\) | A possible primitive semantic bridge; if \(\mathsf{SP}\) is reduced to complete self-knowledge, it becomes the next route. |
| Self-knowledge requirement | \(\mathsf{Det}_G(a)\leftrightarrow\mathsf{Req}_K(a)\) | The route under test. |

The third route is not refuted merely because it is primitive. It must, however, either supply an independently typed meaning and discriminator or be recorded honestly as a version of the hinge.

## 2. Requirement semantics

Let \(K_{\rm SK}\) be a fully declared semantic theory of complete self-knowledge. Its object language must exclude \(\mathsf{Det}_G\), \(\mathsf{GA}\), \(R\), \(\mathsf{Mode}\), and any synonym whose satisfaction condition already says that a candidate is actual or a genuine aspect.

Let \(\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)\) mean that \(a\) is a content of self-knowledge. Define requirement in the metalanguage:

\[
\mathsf{Req}_{K_{\rm SK}}(a)
\quad:\\Longleftrightarrow\quad
K_{\rm SK}\models \mathsf{SKCont}_G(a).
\tag{Req}
\]

The model-theoretic quantification is a metatheoretic test of entailment, not a commitment that its models are separately real worlds or aspects.

The proposed criterion bridge is:

\[
\mathsf{H}_{\rm D}:
\qquad
\mathsf{Det}_{G}(a)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{Req}_{K_{\rm SK}}(a).
\tag{HD}
\]

It is realization-independent in syntax only if \(K_{\rm SK}\) satisfies the vocabulary restriction above. Existing protected non-representational identity then gives, for a genuine self-knowledge content,

\[
\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a)\Rightarrow R(a).
\tag{I}
\]

Thus \(\mathsf{H}_{\rm D}\) plus (I) can yield actualization *after* a determination/domain bridge has been supplied. GA2 must not put (I), \(R\), or realized-mode language into the definition of \(\mathsf{Req}\).

## 3. The non-vacuity/richness condition

A mere criterion bridge can be vacuous. To obtain even one finite genuine determination, the theory must also entail a finite required content:

\[
\mathsf{H}_{\rm F}:
\qquad
\exists a\,[\mathsf{Finite}(a)\land
\mathsf{Coh}(a)\land\mathsf{Req}_{K_{\rm SK}}(a)].
\tag{HF}
\]

The existing bare-self-presence witness shows that completeness, self-awareness, and simplicity alone need not entail \(\mathsf{H}_{\rm F}\). A simple complete self-knower can have no finite differentiated content in the model class unless a further semantic articulation principle is included.

Therefore the proposed “one hinge” must be stated at the strength actually needed:

\[
\mathsf{H}_{+}:=
\mathsf{H}_{\rm D}\land\mathsf{H}_{\rm F},
\tag{H+}
\]

with any subsequent GA3/GA4 gauge and proper-context conditions left explicit. Calling \(\mathsf H_+\) one primitive is legitimate as a packaging decision; it must not conceal that it performs two logical roles: criterion and non-vacuous finite articulation.

## 4. Hinge diagnosis

The requirement route is **not automatically** a verbal restatement. It is independent enough to count as a genuine semantic proposal only if:

1. \(K_{\rm SK}\) has an independently stated satisfaction semantics in the restricted vocabulary;
2. \(K_{\rm SK}\models\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a_+)\) for at least one finite positive case and \(K_{\rm SK}\not\models\mathsf{SKCont}_G(a_-)\) for at least one coherent negative case;
3. those results are derived from the stated self-knowledge semantics rather than added as \(\mathsf{Det}_G\), \(\mathsf{GA}\), \(R\), mode, or self-presentation assumptions under another name; and
4. no formal grammar is treated as intrinsic ground structure.

If these conditions are met, GA2 yields an explicitly conditional semantic hinge: \(\mathsf H_+\) is a substantive but typed package, and the protected identity bridge derives P-internal realization for its admitted contents.

The route **collapses into the hinge** if every positive finite \(\mathsf{Req}\) result is supplied only by an axiom that is definitionally or extensionally equivalent to “these are determinations/genuine self-presentations/realized modes.” In that case it is not a new derivation. It is the exact name of the remaining primitive.

## 5. Finite audit target

The executable audit uses a deliberately thin self-knowledge base \(K_0\) with complete self-knowledge, simplicity, and non-representational identity but no finite-articulation axiom. It has:

- a bare-self-presence model with no finite self-knowledge content; and
- a rich model with a finite content.

Because both models satisfy \(K_0\),

\[
K_0\not\models
\exists a\,[\mathsf{Finite}(a)\land\mathsf{Req}_{K_0}(a)].
\]

An enriched theory \(K_1=K_0+F_a\), where \(F_a\) requires one declared finite self-knowledge content, does entail that content. This shows the exact point at which a positive finite output enters. It does not say that \(F_a\) is arbitrary or false; it records it as an additional semantic articulation unless independently derived.

## 6. Outcome and expiry rules

This sub-audit ends the route-4 search after one of the following outcomes is recorded.

| Outcome | Condition | Programme consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent semantic hinge** | All four independence conditions hold and \(\mathsf H_F\) is a theorem of a restricted-vocabulary \(K_{\rm SK}\) | GA2 completes conditionally: the framework is explicitly conditional on \(\mathsf H_+\) plus the protected identity package. |
| **Hinge identification** | Positive finite requirement appears only by a principle coextensive with determination/aspect/actual self-presentation | Stop searching for a further derivation under a different label; record \(\mathsf H_+\) as the remaining aspect-domain primitive and state the framework’s conditional status. |
| **Negative/description result** | No noncircular restricted-vocabulary semantics supplies a non-vacuous finite required domain | Do not infer a ground-to-grammar derivation. Retain grammar as a target-relative description only. |

No fourth, merely verbal reformulation of “requirement” may be opened without a predeclared vocabulary, satisfaction semantics, positive/negative cases, and a reason it is not extensionally the identified hinge.

## 7. Nonclaims

This audit does not prove that the protected aware-ground/non-representational identity package is true, that \(\mathsf H_+\) is compelled by neutral logic, that all coherent objects are aspects, or that any formal grammar, phenomenology, physical law, or Problem-of-Time proposal follows.

# Strong CI Adjudication — CI4 Finite-Reality Relation Audit v1
## Equal-burden comparison of creation, participation, emanation, real aspects, totality, and structure

## Status

**CI4 complete as a relation-architecture audit.** It does not select a framework. It specifies what a whole-reality framework must explain if finite differentiation is real, and applies the same SCI burden to the live architectures.

## 1. The question

Given an ultimate explanatory closure \(U\), how can finite differentiation \(D\) be real and exact without:

\[
\text{(i) a brute second ultimate,}\qquad
\text{(ii) composition of \(U\),}\qquad
\text{(iii) an unexplained selector/domain/law,}\qquad
\text{or (iv) a merely verbal relation?}
\]

A candidate relation \(\mathsf{Rel}_F(U,D)\) must pass all six tests below.

## 2. Common CI4 tests

| Test | Requirement | Failure witness |
|---|---|---|
| R1 — Reality status | State whether finite contents/entities are real, represented, apparent, derivative, or descriptive only | Equivocation between appearance, representation, and ontology |
| R2 — Relation type | State whether the relation is efficient creation, participation, emanation, constitutive grounding, identity/aspect, structural realization, or another type | A relation-name with no source/target/dependence semantics |
| R3 — Non-composition / non-separation | Explain why finite difference neither composes the ultimate nor floats as an independent ultimate | Parts of \(U\), an unexplained second reality, or a relation that does neither explanatory job |
| R4 — Exactness and selector | Explain why this exact finite domain, hierarchy, law, or realization obtains | A primitive selection, primitive restriction, or unexplained exact totality |
| R5 — Genuine-content / individuation | State what makes a finite item genuine rather than a mere formal description, duplicate, or gauge copy | Domain defined by realization, grammar membership, or post hoc naming |
| R6 — Adequacy interface | Identify how the architecture can later face phenomenal and physical targets | Treat metaphysical vocabulary as automatically accounting for experience or physics |

A candidate can be internally conditional and still be SCI-held. A pass requires an explicit non-circular account at every row.

## 4. Established conditional asymmetries

### 4.1 Contingent exclusive creation

GC2 provides a genuine conditional result:

\[
\mathsf{SSS}(G)
\land
\mathsf{CEA}(G;w_0,w_1)
\land
\mathsf{StrongNBD}
\Rightarrow
\bot.
\]

This is the sharpest present relation result. It does not refute all creation, but it rules out a strictly simple, strongly non-brute contingent exclusive-selection account under the frozen definitions.

### 4.2 Nondual identity route

The protected non-representational package gives, conditionally:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_{G}(a)
\Rightarrow
R(a).
\]

Thus, once a candidate supplies a genuine-aspect domain, it avoids a subsequent external selector. But the antecedent is not supplied by coherence, grammar membership, gauge quotient, or realization itself:

\[
\mathsf{Coh}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NG}(a)
\land
\mathsf{Ctx}(a)
\not\models
\mathsf{Det}_{G}(a).
\]

A rich nondual core may stipulate real finite modes as part of its identity package. That is an honestly conditional core position, not a derivation from a thinner one-ground theory that omits finite modes. In a public SCI comparison, it must still articulate why the core’s finite articulation is intrinsically intelligible rather than simply protected from question.

### 4.3 Necessary relation routes

Necessary determinate creation, necessary emanation, holistic totality, and structural realization all avoid an act of contingent exclusive selection only by incurring the same generic burden:

\[
\mathsf{ExactOutput}_F(D)
\quad\text{must be explained by}\quad
\mathsf{Ess}_F(U),
\]

rather than named “necessary” or “expressive.” This is the **exact-totality bridge**. It is the decisive common burden for the updated transcript’s necessary Neoplatonic/plenitude proposal.

\]

\[

\]

\[
\mathsf{NonComposition}(U)
\not\models
\neg\mathsf{RealAspect}(U,D),
\]

\[
\mathsf{Necessary}(D)
\not\models
\mathsf{ExactOutputExplained}(U,D),
\]

\[
\mathsf{RealMode}(a)
\not\models
\mathsf{FormalGrammarFaithful}(a).
\]

The sun/rays and cube/side analogies can illustrate source/effect or partial-description intuitions. They cannot prove any of these arrows. Their spatiality, extension, and physical composition make them especially unsuitable as decision procedures for a non-spatial ultimate relation.

## 6. The Neoplatonic/plenitude candidate at CI4

The updated transcript’s leading candidate is:

\[
\mathsf{SimpleGood}(U)
\land
\mathsf{NecessarilyDiffusive}(U)
\land
\mathsf{Plenitude}(D)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NecessaryEmanation}(U,D).
\]

At CI4, this is a **candidate conditional theorem**, not a pass. It must still provide:

1. a non-circular account of why goodness is necessarily diffusive;
2. an admissibility and compossibility criterion for the relevant possibilities;
3. an explanation of why all and only those possibilities are real;
4. a non-composition account of multiplicity/hierarchy relative to the simple source;
5. a phenomenal and physical adequacy interface.

The principle of plenitude cannot be treated as self-evident merely because unrealized possibilities are described as a “limitation.” That argument needs a prior account of possibility, perfection, realization, and genuine finite determination.

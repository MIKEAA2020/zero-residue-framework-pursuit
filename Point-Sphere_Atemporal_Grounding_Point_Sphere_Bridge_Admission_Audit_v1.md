# Atemporal Point–Sphere Grounding Bridge Admission Audit v1
## Thin derivation versus rich candidate-core grounding within strict nondual Appearance

**User-selected relation type:** atemporal asymmetric grounding.  
**Registration:** a proposed bridge within the current strict pure-negation Appearance candidate, not an independent rival, a PPA successor, or a claim of framework selection.

---

## 0. Corrected bridge shape

The point–sphere contrast can make the intended “appearance obtains” claim only if it is supplemented by a relation of atemporal grounding:

\[
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma),
\tag{AG}
\]

where \(G\) is the simple, spaceless, timeless Absolute and \(\sigma\) is a code or profile for the whole appearance-field.

The relation is constrained as follows:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma)
\Longrightarrow{}&
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Timeless}(G)\\
&\land
\mathsf{WholeAppearanceField}(\sigma)
\land
\neg\mathsf{IndependentEntity}(\sigma)\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(\sigma,G).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Asymmetric-Atemporal-Form}
\]

The last condition makes grounding asymmetric. The relation is neither a temporal event nor a reciprocal interaction:

\[
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists t_0<t_1\,
\mathsf{ProducesAt}(G,\sigma,t_1),
\tag{No-Temporal-Generation}
\]

\[
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PositiveInternalForm}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{Part}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{SecondBeing}(\sigma).
\tag{Strong-Simplicity-Firewall}
\]

“Point” and “sphere” remain diagrams. They do not define the grounding relation by geometry:

\[
\mathsf{PointSphereDiagram}(G,\sigma)
\not\equiv
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma).
\tag{Diagram-Not-Grounding}
\]

## 1. The necessary bifurcation

An atemporal-grounding proposal has two logically distinct readings. They must not be conflated.

### 1.1 Thin derivation reading

The strong intended claim would be:

\[
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Timeless}(G)
\land
\Pi_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma).
\tag{Thin-AG}
\]

This says that the thin Absolute core plus point–sphere polarity itself derives the grounding relation and an appearance field.

### 1.2 Rich candidate-core reading

A different claim is:

\[
\mathsf{Core}_{\rm PAG}
:=
\mathsf{Core}_{\rm NP}
\land
\exists\sigma\,
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma).
\tag{Rich-AG}
\]

On this reading, atemporal grounding of appearance is protected candidate-core content. It is not recovered from the thinner predicates for free.

A rich core can be coherently stated as a candidate position, but it must be classified correctly:

\[
\mathsf{Rich\text{-}AG}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Thin\text{-}AG}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PubliclyDerivedExactAppearance}.
\tag{No-Free-Derivation}
\]

It cannot claim an explanatory derivation merely by treating its desired appearance relation as part of an expanded essence description.

## 2. Thin-reading countermodel

The thin reading is not entailed by the present core or by the contrast grammar. Consider a structure with:

\[
\mathbf E=\{G\},
\qquad
\mathsf{Simple}^{\sharp}(G),
\qquad
\mathsf{Spaceless}(G),
\qquad
\mathsf{Timeless}(G),
\tag{Bare-Absolute}
\]

and an empty extension for actual appearance grounding:

\[
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}=\varnothing.
\tag{Empty-Grounding}
\]

The point–sphere table can remain a diagrammatic contrast schema without an actual sphere-profile being grounded. This structure preserves all thin Absolute-side commitments while denying the conclusion:

\[
\neg\exists\sigma\,
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma).
\tag{No-Appearance-Countermodel}
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Core}_{\rm NP}
\land
\mathsf{PSContrast}
\not\models
\exists\sigma\,
\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}(G,\sigma).
}
\tag{Thin-Nonentailment}
\]

The countermodel does not establish that appearance is impossible or that the rich candidate-core is false. It shows only that polarity cannot serve as the missing derivation unless it supplies an additional noncircular grounding rule.

## 3. What a new grounding rule would have to provide

To be entry-ready as a semantic bridge rather than a rich-core restatement, a grounding proposal must provide a finite public interface:

\[
\mathsf{PSGroundingPacket}
=
\langle
\mathcal U_{\rm app},
\mathsf{Field},
\mathsf{GroundRule},
\mathsf{Outside},
\mathsf{Type},
\mathsf{Equiv},
\mathsf{Error},
\mathcal B,
\mathsf{Expiry}
\rangle.
\tag{PS-Grounding-Packet}
\]

Its roles are fixed as follows.

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\textbf{role} & \textbf{required content}\\
\hline
\mathcal U_{\rm app} & \text{a candidate field independent of already actual appearance}\\
\mathsf{Field} & \text{a noncircular whole-field membership condition}\\
\mathsf{GroundRule} & \text{a typed, total output rule for grounded/not-grounded/outside}\\
\mathsf{Outside} & \text{an exact boundary if the rule is not total}\\
\mathsf{Type} & \text{appearance profile, finite locus, property, substance, and nonentity treatment}\\
\mathsf{Equiv} & \text{diagram/profile-conservative re-encoding criterion}\\
\mathsf{Error} & \text{conditions under which a grounding application or representation is wrong}\\
\mathcal B & \text{positive, negative, symmetric, extra, and incompatible candidate cases}\\
\mathsf{Expiry} & \text{a frozen failure/pass condition without relabelling repair}
\end{array}
\tag{Packet-Roles}
\]

The key rule cannot have the following circular form:

\[
\mathsf{GroundRule}(G,\sigma)=\mathsf{grounded}
\iff
\mathsf{ActualAppearance}(\sigma).
\tag{Forbidden-Circular-Rule}
\]

Nor can it define its field as whatever the theory already calls manifestation:

\[
\mathcal U_{\rm app}
\ne
\{\sigma:\mathsf{ActualAppearance}(\sigma)
\text{ according to the candidate}\}.
\tag{No-Actuality-Loaded-Domain}
\]

## 5. Exact disposition and expiry

The proposed relation has a legitimate **candidate-core articulation** form, but its thin derivation form is blocked:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Status}(\mathsf{Thin\text{-}AG})
=
\mathsf{NotDerived}_{\rm bare\ Absolute+polarity},
}
\tag{Thin-Disposition}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{Status}(\mathsf{Rich\text{-}AG})
=
\mathsf{ConditionalCandidateCore}_{\rm not\ a\ public\ grounding\ pass}.
}
\tag{Rich-Disposition}
\]

Thus this relation is not presently eligible as a new semantic bridge packet:

\[
\boxed{
\neg\mathsf{BridgeEligible}_{\rm current}(\mathsf{AG}_{\rm PS}).
}
\tag{AG-Not-Eligible}
\]

This audit expires the attempt to treat atemporal grounding as already supplied by the point–sphere contrast. It does **not** expire the idea of a rich point–sphere candidate core, nor does it forbid a later separately specified grounding packet meeting \((\mathrm{PS\text{-}Grounding\text{-}Packet})\).

No framework-selection result follows:

\[
\exists![F]_{\simeq}\,\mathsf{ZeroResidue}(F)
\quad\text{remains unproved.}
\tag{Open-Objective}
\]

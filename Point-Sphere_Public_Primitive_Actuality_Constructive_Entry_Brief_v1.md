# Public-Primitive-Actuality Constructive Entry Brief v1
## Candidate-design brief for an independent actuality interface

**Status:** design brief only; it is not an EntryPacket, no candidate has been admitted, and no test is authorized.  
**Role:** prepare a route independent of the productive-code, canonical-representative, quotient, and rooted-graph architecture of WPI-1 through WPI-5.

---

## 1. Aim

The proposed interface begins with a primitive actuality relation but requires public application, withholding, error, and invariance conditions:

\[
\mathsf{Act}_0(x).
\tag{Primitive-Act}
\]

The relation may be a candidate-core commitment. It may not be treated as explanatory merely because it is named, nor may it be implemented by a hidden actual roster, selected external model, or a list of favoured cases.

---

## 2. Minimum packet required before admission

A later candidate \(F_{\rm PPA\text{-}1}\), if proposed, must specify:

\[
\mathsf{PPAPacket}(F)=
\langle
\mathcal U,
\mathsf{Sorts},
\mathsf{Act}_0,
\mathsf{Applicability},
\mathsf{Withhold},
\mathsf{SelectorAudit},
\mathsf{Realization},
\mathsf{TruthError},
\mathsf{Equivalence},
\mathsf{ModelRegime},
\mathsf{FixturePreflight},
\mathsf{FiniteBattery},
\mathsf{Expiry}
\rangle.
\tag{PPA-Packet}
\]

### 2.1 Actuality interface

It must state separately:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{the typed domain of }\mathsf{Act}_0;\\
&\text{a public applicability condition }\mathsf{Applies}(x);\\
&\text{a public withholding condition }\mathsf{Withhold}(x);\\
&\text{one fixed actual control and one fixed nonactual control;}\\
&\text{why no unannounced inventory, world, model, or subset selects the result.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Actuality-Burden}
\]

The candidate must not infer:

\[
\mathsf{Consistent}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Act}_0(x),
\qquad
\mathsf{Named}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Act}_0(x),
\qquad
\mathsf{Possible}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Act}_0(x).
\tag{No-Shortcut}
\]

### 2.2 Realization and truth/error

If it claims real finite beings, it must use a typed bridge:

\[
\mathsf{Realizes}_F(x,e)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{FiniteEnt}(e)
\land\mathsf{DependsOn}(e,G)
\land\neg\mathsf{BPart}(e,G).
\tag{Realization-Burden}
\]

It must separately define:

\[
\mathsf{Claims}(s,c),
\qquad
\mathsf{Anchor}(s,x),
\qquad
\mathsf{Match}(c,x),
\qquad
\mathsf{Correct}(s,c),
\qquad
\mathsf{Error}(s,c),
\tag{Truth-Burden}
\]

with an actual correct control, an actual error control, and an equivalence/invariance control.

### 2.3 Model and adequacy conditions

The candidate must choose exactly one frozen regime:

\[
\mathsf{FiniteFull},
\qquad
\mathsf{FiniteLocalGlobal},
\qquad
\mathsf{ExplicitInfinite}.
\tag{Model-Regime}
\]

It must also supply fixture preflight witnesses for every later phenomenal, normative, and physical case, plus named finite operational targets and expiry conditions.

---

## 3. Anti-confirmation constraint

A public-primitive-actuality packet cannot borrow as undeclared background any of:

\[
\text{productive closure},
\quad
\text{canonical raw syntax},
\quad
\text{quotient classes},
\quad
\text{a selected model},
\quad
\text{a selected actual inventory}.
\tag{Independence}
\]

It may use different resources, but every such resource must be declared before entry and tested under the same actuality, truth/error, type, invariance, preflight, and expiry demands.

# Strong CI Adjudication — TB Inverse-Transcendental Bridge Pre-Registration v1
## Can successful cognition impose a discriminating constraint on ultimate ontology?

## Status

Read with `Point-Sphere_Strong_CI_TB1_Successful_Cognition_to_Object_Side_Constraint_Audit_v1.md`. That first audit splits the original theory-laden \(\mathsf{OCS}\) datum into epistemic and relational readings, and finds TB1 held rather than passed.

**Pre-registered only. No inverse-transcendental selection argument is claimed.** The route is admitted for a narrowly defined test because its datum, bridge type, rival cases, and expiry condition are declared in advance.

## 1. Targeted datum

The route does not begin from “thought is possible” in general. It begins from the weaker, contestable datum:

\[
\mathsf{OCS}:
\quad
\exists s,p,o\,[
\mathsf{CognitivelyEntertains}(s,p)
\land
\mathsf{CorrectOf}(p,o)
\land
\mathsf{NonAccidental}(s,p,o)
].
\]

\(\mathsf{OCS}\) means that at least one actual cognitive episode is non-accidentally correct of an object or state of affairs. It intentionally excludes as starting data:

- merely formal derivation;
- fictional or empty reference;
- accidental true belief;
- false thought; and
- a claim that all thought transparently represents reality.

The datum is defeasible and theory-laden enough to require explicit treatment by every candidate.

## 2. Proposed profile-neutral constraint

The strongest initially permitted conclusion is:

\[
\mathsf{TSC}_{\min}:
\quad
\text{Reality is not wholly unconstrained relative to successful cognition.}
\]

This is deliberately weaker than:

\[
\mathsf{RealityIsFullyIntelligible},
\qquad
\mathsf{OneAwareGround},
qquad
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity},
\qquad
\mathsf{EEIdentity},
qquad
\mathsf{WholeFirstStructure}.
\]

No selected candidate profile occurs in \(\mathsf{TSC}_{\min}\).

## 3. Typed bridge ledger

| Step | Proposed bridge | Status |
|---|---|---|
| TB1 | \(\mathsf{OCS}\Rightarrow\mathsf{TSC}_{\min}\) | Held: requires a theory of truth/reference or object-directed correctness |
| TB2 | \(\mathsf{TSC}_{\min}\Rightarrow\mathsf{GlobalIntelligibility}(R)\) | Not licensed |
| TB3 | \(\mathsf{GlobalIntelligibility}(R)\Rightarrow\mathsf{UniqueProfile}(P)\) | Not licensed |
| TB4 | \(\mathsf{OCS}\Rightarrow\mathsf{MindIndependentExternalReality}\) | Not licensed; nondual and idealist readings require separate treatment |

The route must not collapse TB1–TB4 into a single transcendental inference.

## 4. What TB1 would require

To earn even the minimal constraint, an argument must state:

1. a theory of \(\mathsf{CorrectOf}\), truth, or successful reference;
2. why non-accidental correctness has an object-side condition rather than only an internal coherence/pragmatic condition;
3. how genuine error is possible on the same account;
4. whether \(o\) is an object, state of affairs, aspect, or merely a content; and
5. why the result is ontological evidence rather than an epistemic norm.

This is the typed bridge demanded by CI0B. No chosen framework can supply its own preferred truth theory as a hidden premise and then call TB1 profile-neutral.

## 5. Adversarial cases fixed in advance

| Case | Treatment the route must permit | What would count as failure |
|---|---|---|
| Classical simple-ground profile | Cognition may be grounded in an intelligible created order | Treating transcendental convertibility as already established by OCS |
| Necessary emanation/plenitude | Cognition may participate in a necessary intelligible hierarchy | Inferring diffusion or exact hierarchy from OCS |
| Nondual aspect identity | Cognition may be a finite presentation within self-knowledge | Inferring genuine-aspect domain membership from OCS |
| Whole-first/structural totality | Cognition may track intrinsic global structure | Inferring that the structure is self-intelligible or uniquely fixed |
| Deflationary/brute-remainder rival | May admit local cognitive success while deny global complete intelligibility | Mistaking local OCS for a proof of SCI |

A route that treats only its preferred profile favourably fails the anti-confirmation-bias control.

## 6. Predeclared non-entailments

\[
\mathsf{OCS}
\not\models
\mathsf{SCI},
\qquad
\mathsf{OCS}
\not\models
\mathsf{GlobalIntelligibility}(R),
\qquad
\mathsf{OCS}
\not\models
\mathsf{UniqueProfile}(P).
\]

Also:

\[
\mathsf{Thinkable}(R)
\not\models
\mathsf{FullyIntelligible}(R),
\qquad
\mathsf{SuccessfulReference}
\not\models
\mathsf{MindIndependentExternalReality}.
\]

## 7. Decisive test and expiry condition

The route receives a limited TB1 pass only if it supplies a truth/reference account meeting §4 and yielding a positive object-side constraint without importing a candidate profile.

It receives a discriminating pass only if, after a TB1 pass, the same constraint excludes at least one fully articulated candidate profile while retaining another for independently stated reasons.

The route expires if it yields only \(\mathsf{TSC}_{\min}\), if TB1 silently imports correspondence realism/convertibility/nondual identity, or if every live candidate can satisfy the constraint equally.

## 8. Current result

The only currently justified position is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{The inverse-transcendental proposal is a controlled research route, not a current argument.}
}
\]

Its likely first result, if any, is a weak shared constraint rather than a framework selector.

## Nonclaims

This pre-registration does not derive SCI from cognition, establish a mind-independent external world, select classical, emanation, nondual, or structural ontology, reopen generic CI rhetoric, or weaken strongest CI.

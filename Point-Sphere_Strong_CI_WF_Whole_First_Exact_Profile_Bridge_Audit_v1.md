# Strong CI Adjudication — WF Whole-First Exact-Profile Bridge Audit v1
## Can intrinsic whole-first articulation explain its own exact profile?

## Status

**Whole-first articulation remains a live anti-aggregate architecture, but it has no public exact-profile or self-intelligibility pass.** Ontological priority of the whole can block a primitive-parts question; it does not by itself derive why this exact whole-profile obtains.

## 1. Candidate profile

Let \(U\) be an ultimate whole and \(A\) its internally articulated profile:

\[
\mathsf{WF}(U,A):
\quad
\mathsf{Prior}(U,A)
\land
\mathsf{DependentArticulation}(A,U)
\land
\neg\mathsf{IndependentlyBasicConstituents}(A).
\]

A strong version may add necessity:

\[
\mathsf{NWF}(U,A):=
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\land
\mathsf{Necessary}(U,A).
\]

The profile is neither an aggregate thesis nor automatically a structural-realist thesis. It is a metaphysical proposal that ultimate differentiation is internally dependent on one ontologically prior whole.

## 2. What WF conditionally achieves

Given \(\mathsf{WF}(U,A)\), the question

\[
Q_{\rm combine}:
\quad
\text{“What externally combines independently basic elements into \(U\)?”}
\]

is locally ill-typed, since it imports the independently basic elements denied by the profile.

And conditionally:

\[
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate}(U).
\]

This is a legitimate anti-aggregate resource. It does not establish \(\mathsf{WF}(U,A)\), absolute simplicity, nondual identity, awareness, or an exact finite domain.

## 3. The unclosed question

The indispensable question is:

\[
Q_{\rm WF\text{-}profile}:
\quad
\text{“Why does \(U\) have this exact dependent articulation \(A\), rather than another coherent}\
\text{whole-profile?”}
\]

The target is:

\[
\mathsf{ExactProfileExplained}(U,A).
\]

Neither ontological priority nor the absence of externally independent parts gives that result:

\[
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactProfileExplained}(U,A),
\]

\[
\mathsf{NWF}(U,A)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactProfileExplained}(U,A).
\]

## 4. Four proposed closure moves

| Move | Conditional contribution | Why it does not yet close the profile |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-first priority | Avoids an external-combination question | Does not derive the exact articulation of the prior whole |
| Necessary whole | Eliminates a later contingent selector | Necessity does not itself explain the exact necessary profile |
| Mutual structural determination | May show interdependence of articulations | Mutual fit can be explanatory, circular, or a global stipulation; the relation type must be stated |
| Formal/mathematical structure | Gives a precise representation of \(A\) | A grammar, model, or quotient does not establish that it is ultimate ontology |

The final row is governed by the existing grammar/ontology controls: faithful formal representation is not automatically a ground determination.

## 5. Public whole-first pass condition

A whole-first candidate obtains a public pass only if it supplies:

\[
\mathsf{WFP}(U,A)
\Rightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{a non-aggregative semantics for dependent articulation;}\\
\text{an account of why \(A\), not merely some articulation, follows from \(U\);}\\
\text{typed inclusion/exclusion and compossibility conditions for finite articulation;}\\
\text{a non-circular distinction between intrinsic self-determination and a primitive profile;}\\
\text{positive and negative rival whole-profiles.}
\end{cases}
\tag{WF-P}
\]

This condition does not require an external selector. It requires a way of distinguishing genuine intrinsic determination from “the whole just has this profile.”

## 6. Rival stress tests

| Rival | What WF must not assume away |
|---|---|
| Classical simplicity | A whole may be rejected as composite only if the composition principle is independently defended |
| Necessary emanation | A source–derivative relation is not identical with whole-first dependence merely by metaphor |
| Nondual aspect identity | Identity of modes/aspects/content is not equivalent to a structural whole absent a stated relation type |
| Neutral/structural base | Bare formal structure needs an ontology and exact-profile semantics |
| Brute-remainder rival | Local intelligibility or structural fit does not establish global full intelligibility |

## 7. Physics caution

Ontic structural realism and physical holism are not direct evidence for a metaphysical ultimate whole. They concern disputed interpretations of scientific theory and physics-level part–whole/relational issues. No physical law, recovery result, or Problem-of-Time solution follows from \(\mathsf{WF}\).

## 8. Exact result

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate}(U)
\quad\text{conditionally,}
}
\]

but:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactProfileExplained}(U,A),
\qquad
\mathsf{NWF}(U,A)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SIpass}(U,A).
}
\]

Whole-first articulation is therefore held at its exact-profile bridge, not refuted and not selected.

## 9. Source note

Structural realism includes both epistemic and ontic proposals, and the move from structural description to a distinctive ontology remains contested; this supports treating physical structuralism as neither a direct metaphysical proof nor a substitute for a whole-first exact-profile account. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structural-realism/.

Metaphysical holism itself comes in several forms and states that some wholes are not determined by their parts; it does not by itself supply a self-explanation of an ultimate whole. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/physics-holism/.

## 10. Expiry condition

The WF route advances only with a concrete \(\mathsf{WF\text{-}P}\) derivation/classification, positive and negative whole-profile cases, and a non-circular explanation of intrinsic determination. Reasserting whole priority, necessary structure, or a formal model does not reopen the bridge.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not reject ontological holism, structural realism, whole-first accounts, or nondual identity. It does not claim that every articulated whole is an aggregate. It records only that whole priority is not yet an exact-profile explanation.

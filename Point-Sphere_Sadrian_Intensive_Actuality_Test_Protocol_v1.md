# Sadrian Intensive Actuality Test Protocol v1
## Predeclared A0 test of primacy, gradation, individuation, and finite modes of existence

## Status

This is a preregistered source protocol, **not** a reconstruction or verdict. It implements the proposed Mullā Ṣadrā test while preserving a mandatory possibility that the alleged intensive interface is genuinely new.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can Ṣadrian gradation of existence provide a public, noncircular,}\
\text{profile-invariant criterion for genuine finite modehood?}
}
\]

No conclusion about Ṣadrā, Islamic philosophical traditions, A0 satisfiability, or the correct whole-reality framework is licensed by this protocol.

## 1. Target correction: scripture, source, and articulated candidate

A revealed text and an articulated metaphysical system are not the same unit of A0 testing:

\[
\mathsf{ScripturalSource}(T)
\neq
\mathsf{FormalOntology}(C).
\]

A scripture can itself advance an adjudicable ontological criterion and, if it does, may be tested as a candidate. But a bare label such as “the Qur’an” does not yet identify the kind of candidate, admission rule, error condition, or profile-invariance claim required by A0.

The target here is therefore not “the Qur’an directly,” nor Islam in general. It is the articulated Ṣadrian system of *aṣālat al-wujūd* (primacy of existence), *tashkīk al-wujūd* (gradation/modulation of existence), causal poverty/dependence, substantial motion, and the theory of mental and extra-mental existence. Qur’anic material is to be treated as a source and hermeneutic context where Ṣadrā uses it, not silently converted into an A0 rule.

This does **not** pre-adjudicate either:

\[
\mathsf{Ash'ariteOccasionalism}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\mathsf{IbnArabianTajalli}.
\]

Each remains a possible later source reconstruction. No claim that it fails in exactly the Spinozist or Appearance manner, respectively, is permitted without its own preregistered, source-grounded test.

## 2. Interface status: residual-class protection

The intensive proposal is not to be silently reduced to a relation, manifestation, or production criterion:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm Sadra}
\subseteq
\mathcal K_{\rm new}
\quad\text{provisionally}.
\]

This is an investigative designation, not a conclusion that intensity is irreducible. The source reconstruction must test whether the alleged interface has independent admission work:

\[
\mathsf{IntensiveInterface}(x)
\not\equiv
\mathsf{DependsOn}(x,G),
\quad
\mathsf{ProducedBy}(x,G),
\quad\text{or}\quad
\mathsf{ManifestationStatus}(x,G).
\]

If it does not, the result is a limited classificatory finding, not evidence that all routes fall into \(\mathcal R\cup\mathcal A\). The residual class remains open in every outcome.

## 4. Strongest charitable candidate

The test grants the following package as a live possibility:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SadrianFiniteMode}(x)
\iff {}&
\mathsf{Contingent}(x)\land
\mathsf{ExtraMentalWujud}(x)\\
&\land\mathsf{Degree}_{\tau}(x)\land
\mathsf{DependentOrRelationalTo}_{\tau}(x,G),
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\tau\) is the Ṣadrian theory, and \(\mathsf{Degree}_{\tau}\) is not stipulated as a numerical magnitude. It may involve priority/posteriority, intensity/weakness, perfection/deficiency, and independence/dependence. The formula is a test aid, not a quotation and not an attribution of a finished modern formalism to Ṣadrā.

The charitable proposal is more than either bare dependence or bare divine production:

\[
\mathsf{Wujud}_{\tau}
\text{ is a real, singular, dynamically graded reality,}
\]

such that finite multiplicity is grounded in different intensities/modes of that same reality. The source claims under examination include the further possibility that existence itself, rather than quiddity, individuates.

## 5. Fairness constraints

1. **No metric demand by fiat.** A0 asks for public adjudicability, not a modern cardinal or interval scale. A non-numerical but publicly determinate comparative/ordering relation may pass.
2. **No automatic circularity charge.** The claimed self-evidence or indefinability of existence is not, by itself, a failure. The question is whether application to a finite candidate has independent error conditions.
3. **No reduction by vocabulary.** “Degree,” “manifestation,” “relation,” “causality,” and “intensity” cannot be placed into a prior family merely by verbal resemblance.
4. **No category import.** Aristotelian substance/accident categories, a separately existing subject, a temporal persistence criterion, or a quantitative physics scale may not be imposed unless Ṣadrā’s own argument supplies their role.
5. **No intuition veto by label.** Knowledge-by-presence or illuminative awareness may be assessed for publicity and corrigibility; it is not dismissed merely because it is non-discursive.
6. **No success by intuition alone.** Private access, spiritual authority, or textual endorsement does not by itself meet A0 public adjudicability.

## 6. Predeclared A0 battery

| Test | Required Sadrian showing |
|---|---|
| \(I_0\) — independent interface | Does intensity/gradation do admission work not already done by dependence, production, or manifestation? |
| \(I_1\) — candidate actuality | For a proposed \(x\), what establishes that \(x\) is an extra-mental mode/degree of existence rather than merely a possible quiddity, a fiction, or a verbal posit? |
| \(I_2\) — intensive comparison | What publicly decides \(\mathsf{Degree}_{\tau}(x)<\mathsf{Degree}_{\tau}(y)\), equality, or incomparability without a preaccepted hierarchy of entities? |
| \(I_3\) — type boundary | Does the theory distinguish a genuine finite mode from an accident, a property/predicate, a relation, an essence/quiddity, and the one absolute reality? |
| \(I_4\) — particularity | If two finite candidates are alike in all stated intensive respects, what makes them two? If exact equality of degree is impossible, what grounds that claim noncircularly? |
| \(I_5\) — mental and extra-mental | Does the theory give public error conditions separating a merely mental mode, fictional posit, or privation from the finite extra-mental candidate at issue? |
| \(I_6\) — profile invariance | Is the membership/ranking verdict retained under a profile-conservative re-encoding that preserves the asserted relevant relations, intensities, and distinctions? |
| \(I_7\) — extra candidate | Can a new proposed finite candidate be accepted or rejected without enlarging the hierarchy, quiddity inventory, or causal chain by stipulation? |
| \(I_8\) — causal dependence | Is causal poverty/dependence explanatory of an independently identified degree, or does it covertly supply the admission criterion? |
| \(I_9\) — substantial motion | If dynamic intensification is invoked, what fixes the identity and degree of the changing candidate without importing external time or unargued persistence? |
| \(I_{10}\) — epistemic bridge | If knowledge-by-presence is invoked, what makes competing intensity judgments publicly assessable and corrigible rather than merely private? |

## 7. Fixed rivals and distinctions

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{QuiddityOnly}(x) &: x\text{ is a mental essence or classificatory abstraction without the relevant reality};\\
\mathsf{MentalMode}(x) &: x\text{ has mental existence but not the extra-mental status presently required};\\
\mathsf{PrivationOrFiction}(x) &: x\text{ is not a genuine finite candidate, even if describable};\\
\mathsf{PropertyOfOne}(x) &: x\text{ is only a property/predicate of the absolute, not a finite mode};\\
\mathsf{IndependentSubstance}(x) &: x\text{ is an independent finite substance};\\
\mathsf{DependentDegree}(x) &: x\text{ is a genuine finite grade/mode of existence};\\
\mathsf{RosterDegree}(x) &: x\text{ is admitted only because an ontology lists its degree.}
\end{array}
\]

The reconstruction must not assume that `MentalMode` means unreal, that every dependence relation produces a degree, or that every real degree is a non-property finite mode.

## 8. Outcome rules

### Scoped intensive A0 pass

A pass requires a source-grounded rule that publicly and noncircularly determines the relevant finite candidate’s status and type, with a profile-invariant way to preserve the decision or an equivalent theorem. The rule must meet \(I_0\text{--}I_{10}\) within stated scope and include a genuine error condition.

A successful result would preserve \(\mathcal K_{\rm Sadra}\) as a live independent route and block an attempt to infer A0 failure from the tested comparative pattern.

### Structural intensive failure

A structural failure requires evidence, not a stipulation, that the application of intensification/gradation:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_1 &: \text{presupposes the actual population whose grades it is supposed to admit};\\
S_2 &: \text{uses a prior quiddity, causal, or hierarchical inventory to fix every relevant intensity};\\
S_3 &: \text{cannot distinguish finite modehood from propertyhood, mental being, or absolute being};\\
S_4 &: \text{leaves particularity unresolved for equal/symmetric intensive profiles};\\
S_5 &: \text{lacks a profile-conservative verdict and a public error/correction condition};\\
S_6 &: \text{relies solely on private illumination where a public candidate-admission verdict is required.}
\end{array}
\]

A conclusion may be structural only if it survives the source’s own resources concerning the reality of existence, individuation, mental being, causal relation, and demonstration.

### Inconclusive result

The test is inconclusive if the accessible source base cannot settle whether the claimed degrees are ontological, semantic, epistemic, or soteriological; if key texts support materially distinct readings; or if the A0 target cannot be faithfully mapped onto Ṣadrian mode-language without distortion.

## 9. Limits and downstream bridges

The test concerns an A0 articulation bridge. It does not certify phenomenal presentation as metaphysical modehood, alter the frozen CFD corpus, or provide a physical theory. Substantial motion must not be relabelled a solution to the Problem of Time without a QM/GR bridge, formal recovery result, or testable discriminator.

## Nonclaim

This protocol does not identify Ṣadrā with Ibn ʿArabī, Spinoza, Advaita, or any already tested source; does not establish a numeric ontology of intensity; does not say the Qur’an is philosophically untestable; and does not establish a winner or a no-go result.

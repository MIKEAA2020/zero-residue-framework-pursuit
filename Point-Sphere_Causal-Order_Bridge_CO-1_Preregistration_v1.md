# Point–Sphere Causal-Order Bridge CO-1
## Parallel weak- and strong-simplicity preregistration

**Status:** Preregistered fresh candidate only. No physical spacetime, causal reality, dynamics, geometry, or truth verdict is claimed here.

**User-selected target:** A causal-order bridge is to be tested before tensor-network or group-field routes. The aim is to derive a formal, nonspatiotemporal, causal-set-like order rather than borrow an already physical spacetime.

**Candidate families:**

- **CO-1W:** weak simplicity plus a positive immanent binary self-limitation grammar.
- **CO-1S:** strong simplicity plus an iterated privative-limit grammar.

CO-1W and CO-1S are distinct fresh candidates. They are not amendments to PS-LP-1, PS-LP-2W, PS-LP-2S, WSP-1 through WSP-11, or prior Point–Sphere frameworks.

---

## 1. Scope and core control

Point and Sphere remain nonliteral and nonspatial:

\[
\mathsf{Point}\neq\text{geometrical point},
\qquad
\mathsf{Sphere}\neq\text{geometrical sphere}.
\]

The shared Point constraint is:

\[
\mathsf{BasePoint}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoIndependentConstituents}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoSpatialLocation}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoTemporalIndex}(P).
\]

“Causal-set-like” below means only the mathematical condition of a locally finite strict partial order. It does not entail that the order is physical causation, temporal succession, or spacetime.

\[
\mathsf{FormalCausetLike}(C,\prec)
:iff
\mathsf{Irreflexive}(\prec)
\land
\mathsf{Transitive}(\prec)
\land
\mathsf{LocallyFinite}(C,\prec).
\]

The physical target remains strictly downstream:

\[
\mathsf{FormalCausetLike}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PhysicalCausation}
\lor
\mathsf{SpaceTime}
\lor
\mathsf{Physics}.
\]

## 2. Expiry and result vocabulary

The only authorized test is a four-gate audit:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{F0} & \text{does the registered grammar define a formal locally finite strict partial order?}\\
\mathsf{F1} & \text{does the construction avoid space, time, physical observers, and physical laws as inputs?}\\
\mathsf{F2} & \text{does it explain nonbrute actualization and avoid a hidden selector or independent domain?}\\
\mathsf{F3} & \text{does it provide a bridge from formal order to real causal/spatiotemporal structure?}
\end{array}
\]

Permitted results distinguish a syntactic success from a physical one:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{FormalOrderPass} & \text{F0 alone passes}\\
\mathsf{PrePhysicalPass} & \text{F0 and F1 pass}\\
\mathsf{OntologicalEntryIncomplete} & \text{F2 or F3 is incomplete}\\
\mathsf{PhysicalBridgePass} & \text{requires all F0--F3 plus later recovery tests}
\end{array}
\]

Both candidates expire after this one F0–F3 audit:

\[
\mathsf{Expiry}(C)
:=
\mathsf{Complete}(\mathsf{F0}(C)\land\mathsf{F1}(C)\land\mathsf{F2}(C)\land\mathsf{F3}(C)).
\]

No later algebra, semantics, actualization principle, manifold approximation, action, measure, or physical recovery map may be added under the CO-1 label.

## 3. CO-1W: weak-simplicity binary self-limitation

### 3.1 Core definition

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoIndependentConstituents}(P).
\]

CO-1W registers a positive, prephysical binary formal determination alphabet:

\[
\mathbb B:=\{0,1\}.
\]

The alphabet is a candidate immanent formal resource, not two substances, two parts of \(P\), a spatial direction, a time step, or a pair of physical alternatives. Its standing burden is to explain that status rather than merely stipulate it.

The set of finite formal self-limitation words is:

\[
W:=\mathbb B^{<\omega}.
\]

For each \(w\in W\), the corresponding Sphere-role is:

\[
\Sigma_w:=\mathsf{Profile}(P,w).
\]

No actual profile is inferred merely from its membership in \(W\).

### 3.2 Noncircular limit relation

The registered limit relation is a formal refinement relation, not a physical process:

\[
u\prec_W v
\quad\text{iff}\quad
u\text{ is a proper prefix of }v.
\]

The candidate causal-order object is:

\[
\mathcal C_W:=\langle W,\prec_W\rangle.
\]

No coordinate, duration, causal process, physical observer, metric, field, particle, or existing spacetime is used in this definition.

### 3.3 Registered burdens

CO-1W may not infer:

\[
\mathsf{FormalWord}(w)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualProfile}(\Sigma_w)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PhysicalEvent}(w).
\]

It must account for the positive alphabet, the reality-status of the profiles, whether all or only some profiles are actual, and any selection principle without introducing a hidden external domain or selector.

## 4. CO-1S: strong-simplicity iterated privation

### 4.1 Core definition

\[
\mathsf{StrongSimple}(P)
:iff
\mathsf{NoProperParts}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoComposition}(P)
\land
\mathsf{NoRealInternalFormalDifferentiation}(P).
\]

CO-1S registers a bare privative token \(\ominus\) and a neutral root token \(\varnothing\). The token is not a positive property, physical negation process, spatial direction, duration, or independent being.

Let:

\[
S:=\{s_n\mid s_n=\ominus^n\varnothing,\ n\in\mathbb N\}.
\]

The Sphere-role is only a formal privative profile:

\[
\Sigma_{s_n}:=\mathsf{PrivativeProfile}(P,s_n).
\]

### 4.2 Noncircular limit relation

The registered order is formal iteration/refinement, not temporal succession:

\[
s_m\prec_S s_n
\quad\text{iff}\quad
m<n.
\]

Equivalently, \(s_m\) is a proper unary prefix of \(s_n\). The candidate causal-order object is:

\[
\mathcal C_S:=\langle S,\prec_S\rangle.
\]

This definition uses formal string length only. It does not use physical time or a pre-existing numerical spatial/temporal scale.

### 4.3 Registered burdens

CO-1S must not treat a sequence of symbols as a sequence of actual events:

\[
\mathsf{FormalIteration}(\ominus)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualLimitation}
\lor
\mathsf{PhysicalTime}.
\]

It must account for what makes its privative grammar determinate, why formal iterations are actual rather than merely possible strings, and how no hidden positive reference range, independent possibility-domain, or selector has entered.

## 5. Shared F0 proof obligations

For either candidate, the audit must verify:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Irreflexive}:&\quad \neg(x\prec x),\\
\mathsf{Transitive}:&\quad x\prec y\land y\prec z\Rightarrow x\prec z,\\
\mathsf{LocallyFinite}:&\quad \{z\mid x\prec z\land z\prec y\}\text{ is finite for all }x,y.
\end{aligned}
\]

A formal order pass is not a causal reality pass:

\[
\mathsf{FormalOrderPass}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{CausalRealityPass}.
\]

## 6. Shared F1–F3 controls

### F1: prephysical-input control

The construction is blocked if it uses a physical observer, spacetime manifold, temporal process, metric, known physical law, measurement relation, or physical causal relation as an input.

### F2: actualization and selection control

For W, the positive binary alphabet and its profiles must not become a brute independent possibility-space. For S, the formal count and privative strings must not gain determinate content by concealing a positive reference range.

### F3: formal-to-physical bridge control

Both routes need an independent account of:

\[
\text{formal order}
\longrightarrow
\text{real prephysical relation}
\longrightarrow
\text{causal/spacetime recovery}.
\]

This bridge cannot be read off merely from the words “order,” “prefix,” “profile,” or “privation.”

## 7. Explicit exclusions

CO-1 does not claim to derive:

- actual finite modes;
- a physical event ontology;
- a field, a metric, dimensions, Lorentzian geometry, or a continuum limit;
- public truth/error, phenomenal content, normativity, or physics;
- a selector solution;
- compatibility of either construction with simplicity;
- comparative superiority or the truth of weak or strong simplicity.

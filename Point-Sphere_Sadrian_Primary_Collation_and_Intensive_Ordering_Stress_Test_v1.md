# Sadrian Primary Collation and Intensive-Ordering Stress Test v1
## Addendum to the Sadrian A0 reconstruction

## Status

This addendum advances the Sadrian inquiry by directly collating an accessible Arabic scan of *Kitāb al-Mashāʿir* against the prior reconstruction and by testing the strongest available non-numerical intensive-admission rule.

It does **not** change the result class to an A0 pass or structural failure:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SadrianIntensiveA0Result}
=	ext{still inconclusive.}
}
\]

The direct source substantially strengthens the claim that Ṣadrā has genuine resources of actuality, individuation, intensive difference, and extra-mental error. It also makes clearer why the transition from an ontology of actual degrees to a public admission rule is nontrivial.

## 2. Corrected source findings

### 2.1 Direct actuality resource

In the opening *Mashāʿir* passages, Ṣadrā writes that the reality of existence is self-individuated and that through it every individuated thing is individuated. He identifies the reality of existence, rather than a merely universal notion, as the ground of actual things.

The following short Arabic anchors are transcribed from the collated scan; the accompanying glosses are deliberately minimal:

| ¶ | Arabic anchor | Minimal control gloss |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | `به يتشخص كل متشخص` | through it every individuated thing is individuated |
| 22 | `للوجودات حقائق خارجية` | existences have extra-mental realities |
| 23 | `له وجود يترتب عليه آثاره وأحكامه` | it has an existence on which its effects and consequences depend |
| 81 | `تخصيص كل فرد من الوجود ... بمرتبة من التقدم والتأخر والكمال والنقص ... أو بأمور لاحقة` | specification by ranks of priority/posteriority and perfection/deficiency, or by subsequent features |
| 86 | `الغنى والحاجة والشدة والضعف` | independence/need and strength/weakness are intrinsic differentiators of ranks |
| 91 | `تقوم النقص بالتمام والضعف بالقوة والإمكان بالوجوب` | an effect is constituted by cause as deficiency by completion, weakness by strength, possibility by necessity |
| 92 | `لا يمكن حصول العلم بخصوصية نحو من الوجود إلا بمشاهدة عينية` | knowledge of the particularity of a mode of existence requires direct witnessing, on the text’s terms |

The last anchor is important: it is not a licence to discard the candidate as private. It is a source-grounded pressure on any claim that Ṣadrā has already supplied a wholly public, third-person application procedure.

This supports:

\[
\mathsf{ExistenceReality}_{S}
\neq
\mathsf{RosterPredicate}.
\]

The source does not say merely “whatever is on the list exists.” It presents existence as the actuality through which an essence is actual.

### 2.2 Direct extra-mental / mental discriminator

The scan states that to be extra-mental is to have an existence from which effects and consequences follow, whereas being in the mind is otherwise. It also insists that a merely universal essence does not become a genuine individual through an accumulation of universal determinations.

The narrow source-supported marker is:

\[
\mathsf{ExtraMental}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EffectsAndConsequences}_{S}(x).
\]

This is necessary source evidence for extra-mental reality, not yet an unrestricted sufficient condition. The source’s own distinction matters because it allows a thinker to possess a mental content of a non-extra-mental object.

### 2.3 Direct individuation resource

The scan gives more detail than the earlier dossier. It distinguishes three ways of specifying an existent:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Necessary existence} &: & \text{by its own reality};\\
\text{the \textit{mubdaʿāt} (directly originated existents)} &: & \text{by ranks of priority/posteriority and perfection/deficiency};\\
\text{generated existents} &: & \text{by subsequent features.}
\end{array}
\]

This avoids attributing to Ṣadrā the crude thesis that every finite individual is individuated only by a bare, unstructured token. It also shows the limitation of `existence individuates` when used as a one-line A0 answer: different classes receive different internal treatments.

### 2.4 Direct intensive and causal resource

The same text makes priority/posteriority, completeness/deficiency, independence/need, and strength/weakness integral to the grades of existence. It says that a caused existence is constituted by its cause as deficiency by completion and weakness by strength.

Hence the most charitable reading is not:

\[
\mathsf{Intensity}_{S}(x)=\text{an ungrounded scalar attached to }x,
\]

but a structured source claim:

\[
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\text{ is reflected in the actual order of causal dependence, perfection, and actuality.}
\]

This is a resource. It also raises the crucial question whether such an order can admit a proposed candidate without being supplied a prior actual hierarchy.

## 3. Strongest non-numerical candidate rule

The rule below is a reconstruction for stress testing, not a quotation from Ṣadrā.

Let \(E(x)\) mean that \(x\) is an extra-mental existent in the relevant sense, \(Q(x)\) that \(x\) is a quiddity-only or merely conceptual item, \(\iota(x)\) that \(x\) is individuated according to the source-appropriate form of individuation, and \(D(x)\) that \(x\) occupies a source-grounded degree of existence.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SIA}^{\dagger}_{S}(x)
\iff {}&\mathsf{Contingent}_{S}(x)
\land E(x)
\land \neg Q(x)
\land\iota(x)
\land D(x),\\
D(x)	ext{ is witnessed by }{}&
\mathsf{Priority/Posteriority}_{S}
\lor\mathsf{Completion/Deficiency}_{S}\\
&\lor\mathsf{Independence/Need}_{S}
\lor\mathsf{Strength/Weakness}_{S}.
\end{aligned}
\]

A candidate may have a non-numerical rank. No cardinal measure, fixed count of degrees, or modern physical intensity is imposed.

Crucially, this is not yet a public decision procedure disguised as a definition. As reconstructed, \(E\), \(\iota\), and \(D\) are still source-loaded predicates. The stress test asks whether the text supplies independently applicable conditions for them; it does not treat their occurrence in the right-hand side as an A0 success.

A candidate comparison relation can therefore be attempted as:

\[
\begin{aligned}
x\prec_{S}y
\Rightarrow{}&
\mathsf{Priority}_{S}(y,x)
\lor\mathsf{CausalDependence}_{S}(x,y)\\
&\lor\mathsf{Completion}_{S}(y,x)
\lor\mathsf{Strength}_{S}(y,x).
\end{aligned}
\]

The disjunction is deliberately not made a numerical score. The source treats these as interlocking features of a metaphysical hierarchy, not independently weighted variables.

## 4. Adversarial stress cases

### A. The merely verbal candidate

A proposed entity is given a name and a quiddity-like description but no extra-mental effects or consequence.

\[
\mathsf{Nameable}(x)\land\mathsf{Describable}(x)
\notightarrow E(x).
\]

**Result:** The source has a genuine resource for rejection. Mere conceptual availability does not establish extra-mental reality.

### B. The mental-content / mental-act distinction

A person entertains the content “unicorn.” The mental act has a real mental/psychic existence and may produce bodily effects, while the intentional content need not pick out an extra-mental unicorn.

\[
\mathsf{Effects}(	ext{mental act of entertaining }u)
\notightarrow
E(u).
\]

**Result:** `effects` alone cannot be a flat admission criterion. An application rule needs a typed distinction among the mental act, its intentional content, and any alleged external correlate. Ṣadrian resources make this distinction possible, but the public candidate-level procedure remains to be recovered.

### C. Horizontally related finite candidates

Two finite candidates may proceed from a common higher cause, neither directly causes the other, and their stated coarse degree markers are equal.

\[
\mathsf{CommonCause}(x,y,z)
\land\neg(x\prec_S y)
\land\neg(y\prec_S x).
\]

**Result:** Vertical causal priority does not by itself individuate or order the pair. The source directs generated existents to subsequent features, but an A0-grade public account of which features are metaphysically identity-grounding has not yet been extracted.

### D. Exact intensive symmetry

Suppose two putative candidates agree under every publicly stated degree witness:

\[
\forall R\in\mathcal D_S\,[R(x)\leftrightarrow R(y)],
\qquad
\mathcal D_S=
\{\text{priority, completion, dependence, strength}\}.
\]

**Result:** Ṣadrā can deny that purely universal differentiators constitute individuality, and can hold each existent is self-individuated in existence. This is a substantive metaphysical response. But it is not yet a **public two-candidate decision rule** unless the theory shows how the two distinct self-individuated existences are independently identified without making their assumed numerical difference the answer.

This is an unresolved A0 pressure, not a disproof of the Ṣadrian claim.

### E. The property/accident candidate

A colour, shape, or relation is proposed as a finite reality. The source permits the existence of an accident as accident and treats the categories as conceptual/quiddity-level distinctions, while actual existence is more fundamental.

\[
\mathsf{ActualAccident}_{S}(a)
\notightarrow
\mathsf{NonPropertyFiniteMode}(a).
\]

**Result:** The ontology has resources to say that an accident is real in its appropriate dependent way. The A0 translation from “actual accident” to the required finite-mode/non-property type is unresolved. It may be a scope mismatch, a possible type rule, or a genuine failure; no result is preselected.

### F. The extra candidate

Add proposed \(z\) to an apparently complete hierarchy, stipulating that it has a new degree between two known degrees.

\[
\mathsf{StipulatedIntermediateDegree}(z)
\notightarrow
\mathsf{SIA}^{\dagger}_{S}(z).
\]

**Result:** The source blocks admission by bare declaration. But a non-stipulative positive test of the claimed degree would have to establish actual effects, individuation, and its causal/perfection structure. The present reconstruction has not shown that those inputs are publicly decidable without access to the actual hierarchy.

### G. Conservative re-encoding

Let \(\rho\) preserve all currently stated causal, perfection, dependency, and intensity claims, while changing names, descriptions, and the segmentation of quiddities.

\[
\rho\models
\mathcal D_S(x,y)
\quad\text{but changes their conceptual presentation.}
\]

**Result:** Ṣadrā’s distinction between reality of existence and conceptual quiddity gives a promising invariance intuition. But no recovered theorem tells us when a re-encoding preserves the relevant existential degree rather than silently changes it. The profile-invariance burden remains open.

## 6. Revised intermediate verdict

The primary text defeats two premature negative claims:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{“Ṣadrā supplies only dependence, not actuality”};\\
&\text{“Ṣadrā has no individuation or mental/extra-mental distinction.”}
\end{aligned}
\]

Both are false on the collation.

But it does not yet establish:

\[
\mathsf{SIA}^{\dagger}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicatedActualFiniteMode}(x).
\]

The disciplined result remains:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Ṣadrian intensive actuality is a live, substantial, unrefuted A0 escape candidate;}
\text{ its public admission bridge remains unproven.}
}
\]

## 7. Consequence for the conditional no-go schema

No reduction of the Sadrian route to \(\mathcal R\) or \(\mathcal A\) has been earned. The appropriate status is:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm Sadra}\subseteq\mathcal K_{\rm new}
\quad\text{still provisionally.}
\]

Its failure to pass A0 on the present record cannot be counted as evidence for the universal actuality-interface premise. The no-go schema remains blocked by the residual class and by the source’s unresolved but substantive intensive interface.

## Nonclaim

This stress test does not claim that Ṣadrā’s ontology is verified by effects, that all mental contents are unreal, that every accident is a genuine A0 finite mode, that primary-text access has solved all translation and interpretation issues, or that the absence of a recovered invariance theorem proves the impossibility of one.

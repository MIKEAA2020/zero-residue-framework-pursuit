# Proclean Participation Articulation Reconstruction v1
## Source-grounded candidate extraction, not vocabulary import

## Status

This document reconstructs a **Proclean participation/procession candidate** from a bounded source base. It is not a reconstruction of the prior nondual/Appearance candidate, does not assume that Proclus is correct, and does not award an articulation pass.

This separation matters. In Proclus, the One is unparticipated, while the articulated hierarchy includes participated henads, intellects, souls, and other causes. That is not simply “finite modes as contents of the simple ground’s self-knowledge.” It is a distinct participation candidate that must bear its own simplicity, plurality, exact-profile, phenomenal, and physical burdens.

## 1. Source base

### Primary source

- Proclus, *Elements of Theology*, especially propositions 1–5, 23–24, 28–35, in the Dodds translation/revised text: https://www.viachrista.org/Library/Proclus_Elements_Theology.html

### Interpretive controls

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Proclus,” §§3.1–3.2: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/proclus/
- SEP, “Plotinus,” used only to prevent conflation between Plotinian Intellect and the One: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/plotinus/

No claim below rests on an unquoted generic notion of “emanation.”

## 2. Candidate architecture extracted from the sources

Let \(U\) denote the absolutely unparticipated One, \(Y\) a participated middle term, and \(x\) a participant/effect. The reconstruction uses the following source-attributed commitments:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
P1 &:& \text{a manifold participates unity in some respect};\\
P2 &:& \text{a participant in unity is one-and-not-one, not unity unqualified};\\
P23 &:& \text{the unparticipated gives subsistence to participated terms};\\
P28 &:& \text{a producing cause produces like terms before unlike terms};\\
P30 &:& \text{what is immediately produced remains in its cause and proceeds from it};\\
P31/35 &:& \text{an effect remains in, proceeds from, and reverts upon its cause.}
\end{array}
\]

The mediated pattern is therefore:

\[
U\ \longrightarrow\ Y\ \longrightarrow\ x,
\]

where \(Y\) is not merely a word for \(U\) or \(x\), but is intended to preserve the unparticipated source’s transcendence while accounting for participation in a determinate form.

The primary argument being reconstructed is not “the One has modes.” It is:

\[
\text{if a participant is genuinely dependent upon a cause, its relation has the}\
\text{triadic structure of remaining, procession, and reversion rather than bare separation or identity.}
\]

## 3. The strongest available Proclean condition

Proclus’s discussion of *parhypostasis* supplies the closest source-grounded discriminator between a proper effect and a merely parasitic/accidental outcome. In the interpretive source, a proper effect proceeds from a cause according to its nature toward an intended end; an evil or parasitic existence is characterized as falling outside such goal-directed causation.

A charitable reconstruction is therefore:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ProperEff}_{\rm Pr}(x)\iff\exists c\,[
&\mathsf{Cause}_{\rm Pr}(c,x)
\land\mathsf{PerNaturam}_{\rm Pr}(c,x)\\
&\land\mathsf{End}_{\rm Pr}(c,x)
\land\mathsf{Remain}(x,c)\\
&\land\mathsf{Proceed}(x,c)
\land\mathsf{Revert}(x,c)
].
\end{aligned}
\]

This is an extracted candidate condition, not a verified translation-equivalent formula. Its virtue is that it is an argument-shaped proposal: it makes a claim about causal origin, directedness, and the differentiated-but-connected condition of effects.

## 4. What this achieves

The reconstruction improves on a bare slogan such as “finite things participate in the One.” It supplies a conditional distinction:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{mere identity with cause} &\Rightarrow& \text{no distinct effect};\\
\text{mere separation from cause} &\Rightarrow& \text{no remaining/reversion or causal communion};\\
\text{proper caused effect} &\Rightarrow& \text{remaining + procession + reversion.}
\end{array}
\]

It therefore offers a substantive non-temporal dependence schema that is more than a change of label.

## 5. Test against the A0 structural battery

| Case | Reconstruction result | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| T0: no finite candidate | The source supplies no theorem that a simple source must have this particular finite profile. | No exact-domain result. |
| T1: one determinate candidate | It can be called a proper effect only conditional on a specified cause, natural procession, end, and triad. | Conditional dependence test, not public membership determination. |
| T2: symmetric pair | The source’s hierarchy may order kinds/levels, but no source-derived rule individuates arbitrary members of a symmetric pair. | Particularity remains open. |
| T3: property candidate | The participated/unparticipated triad distinguishes types of participation, but does not by itself give a framework-neutral mode/property test. | Does not satisfy the non-propertyhood requirement. |
| T4: independent candidate | A genuinely independent entity lacks the proposed dependence relation; yet Proclus recognizes self-subsistent hypostases/causes within the hierarchy. | Not a nondual no-substance ontology. |
| T5: re-encoding | No common profile extractor is supplied by the source. | Reformulation invariance has not been established. |
| T6: added token | Adding a new level/item needs a causal and teleological placement, but the source does not derive the complete roster merely from the triad. | Exact-profile/selector burden remains. |

## 6. Main limitation: target mismatch

\(\mathsf{ProperEff}_{\rm Pr}\) is not a condition for:

\[
\mathsf{GenuineMode}_{\rm NDP}(m).
\]

It targets a Proclean proper effect/hypostasis in a stratified causal order. The source itself treats the hierarchy as including distinct hypostases and a plurality of participated henads. Thus it cannot be used to rescue the prior NDP thesis by silently identifying:

\[
\text{participated effect}
=
\text{non-independent mode}
=
\text{self-knowledge content}.
\]

Those are three different ontological constructions.

## 7. Articulation verdict

The source reconstruction earns a limited result:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Proclean participation supplies a nontrivial conditional dependence schema,}\
\text{but not a public, complete criterion for genuine finite modehood.}
}
\]

It does not pass the A0 standard because it lacks, at minimum:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
L_1 &: \text{independent truth conditions for }\mathsf{Cause}_{\rm Pr},\mathsf{PerNaturam}_{\rm Pr},\mathsf{End}_{\rm Pr};\\
L_2 &: \text{a particularity rule for symmetric/distinct finite items};\\
L_3 &: \text{a non-property criterion};\\
L_4 &: \text{a common profile extractor and invariance proof};\\
L_5 &: \text{an exact hierarchy/domain derivation rather than a stipulated cosmological order};\\
L_6 &: \text{phenomenal and physical adequacy bridges.}
\end{array}
\]

## 8. Consequence for the programme

This is a real source test, not a new internally generated metaphor. It establishes that a sophisticated Neoplatonic source has an explicit argument for a structured dependence relation, but that argument does not meet the stronger public-determination gate.

The appropriate next work is either:

1. reconstruct one of the source’s premises—especially \(\mathsf{PerNaturam}_{\rm Pr}\) or the law of mean terms—as a formally scoped conditional claim and test whether it adds a noncircular determination condition; or
2. stop the Proclean dossier at this localized partial result and begin a source-grounded dossier for a genuinely different family.

## Nonclaim

The reconstruction does not prove Proclean metaphysics false, prove that the hierarchy is merely stipulated, show no framework satisfies strongest CI, or identify the correct framework of reality.

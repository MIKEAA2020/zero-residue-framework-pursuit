# Realization-Rule Belt Comparison v1
## R1: formal comparison under the adopted hard core

## Scope box

**Hard core assumed, not argued here:** one simple, complete, fully aware, good, unchanging ground; real dependent creation; temporal order internal to creation.

**Belt question:** what rule fixes the created totality? 

**Live belt candidates:** C-Nec-D, C-Nec-O, and C-Cont. Full C-Nec-P plenitude is deferred, not presumed.

**Does not address:** a public proof of the hard core, a quantum interpretation decision, Born weights, a physics derivation, or the psychophysical bridge.

---

## 1. Common comparison language

Let \(\Omega\) be a specified space of admissible complete created totalities. Let \(E\) denote the hard-core ground’s intrinsic nature and \(\mathcal R(E)\subseteq\Omega\) the totalities realized under the belt rule. “Specified” matters: without a domain, an admissibility criterion, an identity condition for a totality, and a dependence relation, no realization claim is yet formal.

The hard core constrains all candidates by requiring:

- no independent ultimate source outside \(E\);
- real derivative creation, not mere vocabulary for appearance;
- no arbitrary physical simulator or pre-existing material substrate; and
- explicit accommodation of differentiated temporal order, finite agency, and harm.

These are constraints on candidate design, not proofs that any candidate works.

---

## 2. Candidate R-D: C-Nec-D, determinate necessary expression

\[
\mathcal R(E)=\{T\}
\]
for exactly one complete totality \(T\), with its status fixed by the ground’s nature rather than an additional selector.

### Attraction under the hard core

It fits one-act/non-deliberative expression directly: no survey over a landscape and no additional selection distribution. It is compatible with one created order containing many finite grades, histories, perspectives, and lawful regularities.

### Essential belt debt

It needs an **exact-totality bridge**: a formal reason why \(E\) fixes this \(T\), rather than merely the assertion that it does. Laws, constants, boundary conditions, and the form of finite differentiation cannot be silently treated as given. The debt is not “there must be a detailed derivation tomorrow”; it is that the architecture must identify what makes the determination non-arbitrary and non-circular.

### Discriminator / expiry condition

R-D gains support only from a concrete derivation or constraint that narrows \(\Omega\) to one non-gauge totality without importing an unconstrained selector. It loses this advantage if every proposed determination requires a free parameter, unexplained boundary condition, or external rule that does the selecting.

---

## 3. Candidate R-O: C-Nec-O, orbit-restricted maximal expression

Let a group \(G\) act on \(\Omega\). For a seed totality \(T\),
\[
\mathcal R(E)=G\!\cdot\!T.
\]
The realized plurality is neither arbitrary all-world plenitude nor a single selected member, but a specified symmetry orbit.

### Attraction under the hard core

R-O preserves a non-deliberative realization structure while allowing a principled multiplicity of created orders. If a physically substantive symmetry supplies a uniquely appropriate invariant probability structure, it could connect realization to probability without an unconstrained post-hoc factor.

### Essential belt debts

1. **Substantiveness:** show that orbit members are physically/metaphysically distinct, not gauge descriptions of one totality.
2. **Action and orbit:** state the group, its action, and why the ground-level realization relation is equivariant.
3. **Measure:** if probabilities are claimed, establish existence and normalisation of a relevant invariant measure. A Haar measure on a group is not automatically a probability measure on a non-compact orbit.
4. **Physical bridge:** show why this orbit concerns actual quantum/cosmological alternatives and, if relevant, recovers Born weights without assuming them.

### Discriminator / expiry condition

R-O gains support only from a concrete non-gauge symmetry and an invariant measure that produces a new non-post-hoc constraint. If the action is gauge, it reduces to R-D. If its measure is non-normalisable, physically irrelevant, or unable to bear the claimed probabilities, its purported advantage disappears.

## 4. Candidate R-C: C-Cont, non-necessitated selector

\[
\mathcal R(E)=\{T\},
\]
but a selector \(q\)—for example an act, reason, relation, law, or fitness condition—belongs to the full explanation without being entailed by \(E\) alone.

### Attraction under the hard core

R-C gives the most direct formal home to a genuinely contrastive “could have been otherwise” and to traditional personal-creation language. It may model a creator–creation relation without identifying the realized order with an automatic necessary consequence of nature.

### Essential belt debt

The selector must be positively characterized. If \(q\) is fully entailed by \(E\), R-C becomes R-D. If \(q\) is unconnected to a reason, it appears as a brute selection remainder. If \(q\) is external and independent, it conflicts with the hard core’s one-ultimate-ground constraint. The candidate therefore needs a non-arbitrary, non-collapsing account of asymmetric selection.

### Discriminator / expiry condition

R-C gains support only if it specifies such a selector and shows that it is neither reducible to essence-entailment nor a primitive random/free choice insulated from explanation. Persistent failure to state this middle structure is evidence against R-C as an active belt candidate.

There is no ranking yet. The hard core directs the comparison; it does not choose the result by terminology.

## 6. Next formal work

The next R1 subtask is to construct minimal finite model families realizing each candidate with the same \(\Omega\), then test which primitive parameters are indispensable. That will not prove a metaphysical rule, but it can expose hidden selectors, gauge-only multiplicity, and false claims of derivation. A measure proposal is deferred until R-O supplies an actual group/action/orbit rather than a generic appeal to symmetry.

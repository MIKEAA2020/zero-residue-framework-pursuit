# Post-PPA Necessary-Emanation: Proclean Productive-Completion Bridge Audit v1
## A source-bounded test of whether productive completeness supplies an entry-ready actuality bridge

**Selected research slot:** necessary emanation / plenitude.  
**Boundary:** this is a local source-bridge audit, not a new whole-reality formulation, an independent rival entry packet, or a PPA successor.

---

## 0. Preregistered target, criteria, and expiry

The selected necessary-emanation route cannot be restarted by restating “perfect goodness diffuses itself.” The prior exact-totality audit permits new work only if it tests one concrete bridge. This audit therefore tests the following narrow candidate resource in Proclus:

\[
\mathsf{Complete}(c)
\land
\mathsf{Cap}^{\rm Pr}(c,E)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Proc}^{\rm Pr}(c,E),
\tag{PPC-Target}
\]

where \(c\) is a productive cause, \(E\) is the class of its relevant products, \(\mathsf{Cap}^{\rm Pr}\) means capacity/power in the Proclean productive setting, and \(\mathsf{Proc}^{\rm Pr}\) means non-temporal procession or production.

The target is **not** the stronger, unearned claim:

\[
\mathsf{PerfectGood}(U)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{FullRealization}(\mathcal A)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ExactHierarchy}(U,\mathcal A,\mathcal C).
\tag{Not-Target}
\]

The audit is fixed in advance to distinguish the following outcomes:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
O_1 &: \text{a source-grounded conditional productivity resource only};\\
O_2 &: \text{a domain-independent source bridge capable of supporting a rival-entry packet};\\
O_3 &: \text{an interpretively unsupported or circular source reconstruction};\\
O_4 &: \text{an inconclusive result.}
\end{array}
\tag{Outcome-Types}
\]

\(O_2\) requires all of these criteria:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
B_1 &: \text{primary-text support for the claimed productive relation};\\
B_2 &: \text{a distinction between source-internal production and a universal logical theorem};\\
B_3 &: \text{a noncircular criterion for which proposed finite candidates belong to }E;\\
B_4 &: \text{positive, negative, symmetric, and extra-candidate cases};\\
B_5 &: \text{a rule for compossibility, hierarchy, and non-composition sufficient for the stated scope.}
\end{array}
\tag{Bridge-Conditions}
\]

**Expiry.** At the end of this audit, the expression \(\mathsf{Cap}^{\rm Pr}\) may not be redefined ad hoc to include whichever outputs a desired hierarchy contains. If \(B_3\)–\(B_5\) are not met, this productive-completion bridge is closed **in this form**. Any later emanation route must submit a different preregistered bridge rather than relabelling the same capacity/domain gap.

## 1. Source boundary and interpretation controls

### Primary source

The audit uses Proclus, *Elements of Theology*, propositions 23–35, in the Dodds translation available at:

- https://www.viachrista.org/Library/Proclus_Elements_Theology.html

The relevant propositions include:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\mathsf{P23} &: & \text{the unparticipated produces the participated};\\
\mathsf{P25} &: & \text{the complete generates what it is capable of producing, by its completeness};\\
\mathsf{P26} &: & \text{a productive cause produces its consequents while remaining steadfast};\\
\mathsf{P27} &: & \text{productivity of secondary existents is from completeness and superfluity of power};\\
\mathsf{P28\text{-}P29} &: & \text{production/procession is ordered by likeness};\\
\mathsf{P30\text{-}P35} &: & \text{an effect remains, proceeds, and reverts with respect to its cause.}
\end{array}
\tag{Source-Claims}
\]

The source itself calls the first principle the Good/One and treats it as constitutive of all that is in the argument of proposition 25. Those commitments are recorded as Proclean premises; they are not derived here from strongest CI, semantic necessity, or a neutral account of goodness.

### Interpretation controls

The audit does not identify:

\[
\text{Proclean participated effect}
=
\text{Plotinian Intellect}
=
\text{a nondual self-knowledge content}
=
\text{a public finite mode}.
\tag{No-Conflation}
\]

Nor does the source's non-temporal causality supply an external time, a physical dynamics, or a recovery theorem for physics.

## 2. Charitable reconstruction of the local productive rule

Propositions 25–27 provide a substantial alternative to a bare assertion of output. Their joint source-internal form can be reconstructed as:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{PPC}_{\rm Pr}(c,E)
\iff
&\mathsf{Complete}_{\rm Pr}(c)
\land\mathsf{ProductivePower}_{\rm Pr}(c,E)\\
&\land\mathsf{Produces}_{\rm Pr}(c,E)
\land\mathsf{RemainsSteadfast}_{\rm Pr}(c).
\end{aligned}
\tag{PPC-Pr}
\]

The reconstruction has three genuine virtues.

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
V_1 &: \text{productivity is linked to completeness/superfluity rather than to a contingent later choice};\\
V_2 &: \text{the source remains undiminished and unaltered by the procession it grounds};\\
V_3 &: \text{the resulting relation is neither sheer identity nor total separation, because it is}\
&\text{qualified by likeness, remaining, procession, and reversion.}
\end{array}
\tag{Local-Resources}
\]

Thus the primary text supports a **conditional internal productive principle**. It does not support the following unrestricted replacement:

\[
\forall U\,\forall\mathcal A\,
[\mathsf{PerfectGood}(U)
\Rightarrow
\forall a\in\mathcal A\,R(a)].
\tag{Overextension-Blocked}
\]

The reason is structural: the source rule is indexed to what the productive cause is capable of producing and to the articulated order of source, participated term, and participant.

## 3. Domain and exact-totality audit

The decisive entry question is not whether Proclus contains a productivity relation. It is whether that relation classifies an independently proposed candidate without importing the desired hierarchy.

### 3.1 Candidate-domain condition

For a public rival entry, the relevant field would need a rule of this form:

\[
\mathsf{EligibleProduct}_{\rm Pr}(x,c)
\iff
\mathsf{Cap}^{\rm Pr}(c,x),
\tag{Product-Domain}
\]

whose truth conditions do not depend on already knowing that \(x\) occupies a place in the actual Proclean order.

No such rule is supplied by propositions 23–35. They state that the complete generates what it is capable of producing; they do not determine, for an arbitrary candidate \(x\), whether it falls within the power/capacity extension rather than being a formal description, a duplicate, an incompatible candidate, a property, or an unplaced entity.

\[
\mathsf{PPC}_{\rm Pr}(c,E)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EligibleProduct}_{\rm Pr}(x,c).
\tag{No-Independent-Domain}
\]

This is not a criticism that the source must use a modern public-admission vocabulary. It is the result of applying the declared entry task: a relation that operates only after its effect-field is already fixed cannot by itself populate that field.

### 3.2 Fixed structural cases

\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
\textbf{case} & \textbf{what the productive rule can say} & \textbf{unclosed requirement}\\
\hline
\text{no proposed finite product} & \text{no independent output set is generated} & \text{why any finite effect-field obtains}\\
\text{a source-listed participant} & \text{conditionally a product in the articulated hierarchy} & \text{the listing/hierarchy is already supplied}\\
\text{two symmetric candidates} & \text{likeness and procession do not individuate them} & \text{particularity or non-gauge identity rule}\\
\text{formal or property candidate} & \text{the relation does not by itself classify its type} & \text{effect/property/mode boundary}\\
\text{extra candidate} & \text{requires a causal and hierarchical placement} & \text{independent inclusion/exclusion test}\\
\text{incompatible candidates} & \text{not jointly classified by the local rule} & \text{compossibility or context semantics}
\end{array}
\tag{Case-Audit}
\]

### 3.3 Exact hierarchy

Even an antecedently fixed class of products would not yet produce the exact hierarchy:

\[
\mathsf{Complete}_{\rm Pr}(c)
\land
\mathsf{ProductivePower}_{\rm Pr}(c,E)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExactHierarchy}(c,E).
\tag{No-Exact-Hierarchy}
\]

The source's ordered structure is philosophically substantive. Its exact members, levels, relations, and exclusions cannot be read back as deductions merely from the word “productive.”

## 5. Disposition

The selected route has yielded a protected local resource and a closed local bridge form:

\[
\mathsf{Disposition}(\mathsf{PPC}_{\rm Pr})
=
\mathsf{Closed}_{\rm source\text{-}conditional\ resource,\ no\ independent\ domain}.
\tag{PPC-Disposition}
\]

This does not close the necessary-emanation/plenitude family. It leaves two non-identical future paths, neither presently submitted:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
N_1 &: \text{a different source-grounded candidate that independently specifies a product domain,}\
&\text{compossibility, and exact-output relation};\\
N_2 &: \text{a nonhistorical but independently preregistered necessary-emanation interface that}\
&\text{passes the post-PPA entry charter without importing the intended output.}
\end{array}
\tag{Future-Paths}
\]

No PPA-9 drafting, necessary-emanation battery execution, or framework-selection result follows.

\[
\exists![F]_{\simeq}\,\mathsf{ZeroResidue}(F)
\quad\text{remains unproved.}
\tag{Open-Objective}

# AP3P2F Mode Self-Presentation / Trace-Exhaustiveness Audit v1 — Report
## The proposed route is the right final test, but it does not derive full gauge from the independently available self-presentation constraints

## Evaluation

The proposed mode-unity route is better targeted than the common-ground route. It asks about the internal unity of a finite self-presentation rather than inferring horizontal equivalence from vertical grounding.

It is therefore worth testing. However, it is not wholly a new resource: AP3J already established the restricted phenomenological constraints available to this route — immanent reflexivity, non-objectification, bounded horizon, articulated finite content, and weak retentional coherence. What had not yet been tested was whether those constraints make the AP3P2 ordered trace **exhaustive** of a mode's self-presentation content.

They do not.

## The key type distinction remains, in a more precise form

Mode unity is internal to one proposed history:
\[
\operatorname{SP}(\ell,\ell).
\]

Gauge identification compares proposed histories:
\[
\ell\sim\ell'.
\]

Thus:
\[
\operatorname{SP}(\ell,\ell)
\land
\operatorname{SP}(\ell',\ell')
\not\Rightarrow
\ell=\ell'
\quad\text{or}\quad
\ell\sim\ell'.
\]

This is not the earlier vertical-to-horizontal fallacy. It is a different underdetermination: a reflexive unity relation does not, without an identity criterion, compare two possible bearers of that relation.

## Exact countermodels

All of the following satisfy the independently granted self-presentation constraints: immanence, no second observer, non-objectification, finite unity of each already specified lift-history, and bounded horizon.

| Self-presentation content map | Classes | Full gauge? |
|---|---:|---|
| \(C_{\rm SP}(\ell)=\tau(\ell)\) | 1 | Yes |
| \(C_{\rm SP}(\ell)=(\tau(\ell),\ell)\) | 16 | No |
| \(C_{\rm SP}(\ell)=(\tau(\ell),\rho(\ell))\), with \(\rho\) the relative Q-bit signature | 8 | No |

All executable checks pass.

The second and third maps do **not** posit a second subject, public witness, external carrier, or external time. They merely allow the finite immanent content to be richer than its ordered two-content trace.

## Why “the trace is what the mode is self-present as” is the disputed bridge

In AP3P1, the ordered trace is a stipulated retentional overlap. It is not already defined as the complete self-presentation content. The phase grammar itself carries additional determinations: P and Q phases have three contents, while their common continuation trace has two.

Moreover, AP3J’s bounded-horizon result specifically distinguishes prereflective self-presence from exhaustive reflective transparency. It therefore cannot warrant:
\[
C_{\rm SP}(\ell)=\tau(\ell).
\]

If that equation is inserted as a premise, then the desired conclusion follows. Name it plainly:
\[
\mathsf{TE}_{\rm SP}:\qquad
\text{the ordered trace exhausts finite self-presentation content.}
\]

Together with the protected mode/content identity, \(\mathsf{TE}_{\rm SP}\) produces `GAQ_trace`, hence full \((\mathbb Z_2)^4\) gauge. But it does so because it declares every Q-bit and cross-sector Q relation to be non-content beyond trace. It is the sought quotient criterion, not an independently established consequence of bare self-presentation unity.

## What the route can support

At its current warranted strength, self-presentation supports an internal cohesion thought:

> Within a candidate finite history, an ordered retentional trace can be part of its immanent unity rather than an externally read record.

Even that does not identify distinct trace-preserving candidate histories. AP3P1's successor/ordered-trace rule remains an explicit flow-grammar stipulation, not a theorem of reflexivity.

## Conclusion

The proposed derivation was the right last nonredundant test, and it has a clean expiry result:

- **It does not yield full gauge from independently characterized self-presentation.**
- **It would yield full gauge from the additional trace-exhaustiveness bridge \(\mathsf{TE}_{\rm SP}\).**
- \(\mathsf{TE}_{\rm SP}\) is a revisable reductive grammar postulate, equivalent in effect to `GAQ_trace`, not a theorem of the current core or phenomenological constraints.

So fibre-local full gauge remains available as an explicitly stated minimal grammar branch. It cannot honestly be reported as derived merely from reflexive mode unity.

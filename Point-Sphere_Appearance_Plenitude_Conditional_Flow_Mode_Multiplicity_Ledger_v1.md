## 1. Lift multiplicity is descriptive in the minimal branch

There are 16 trace-preserving P-to-Q lift encodings:
\[
\beta_f(a,b,c)=(a,b,c\oplus f(a,b)),
\qquad f:\{0,1\}^2\to\mathbb Z_2.

\[
\#\text{lift description classes}=1.
\]

The 16 encodings therefore do not yield 16 grammar-level mode multiplicities, and conditional plenitude does not multiply them as distinct aspects in this branch.

## 2. Phase positions are not separate modes

For a fixed component label \(\theta\):
\[
P_{\Phi,\theta}=\{p_{n,\theta}:n\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]

The phase-origin gauge and the postulated connected-component rule identify all its positions as phases of one grammar-level mode-flow:
\[
\#\text{grammar-level flows per }\theta=1,
\qquad
\#\text{phases per flow}=\aleph_0.
\]

Thus the bi-infinite index does not yield countably many modes.

## 3. Valuation-labelled component multiplicity

\[

\[

grammar-level successor components in the P1/AP3P2 branch. The postulate treats each connected component as one grammar-level finite mode-flow. Thus:
\[

They are disjoint as phase/valuation graph components. Their P/Q **context forms** overlap structurally in the ordered trace, but this is not graph-component overlap or numerical identity of the flows. The branch contains no nesting relation among the eight components.

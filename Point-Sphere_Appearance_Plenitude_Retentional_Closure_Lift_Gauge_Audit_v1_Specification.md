# AP3P2G Retentional-Closure Lift-Gauge Audit v1
## Can finite immanent retention derive full lift gauge from the exact AP3P1 overlap?

## Motivation

The remaining potentially derivational route is neither common grounding nor bare mode unity. It is the specifically retentional claim that the ordered trace is the entire **immanently reidentifiable cross-phase content** of an adjacent finite self-presentation.

For AP3P1:
\[
C_P=\{r_0,r_1,p_s,p_v\},\qquad
C_Q=\{r_0,r_1,q_s,q_v\},
\]
so the literal content overlap is exactly:
\[
C_P\cap C_Q=\{r_0,r_1\}.
\]
The free valuation coordinates \(c=\nu(p_v)\) and \(d=\nu(q_v)\) belong to distinct, nonoverlapping content positions.

## Candidate derivation bridge

Define **retentional closure**:
\[
\mathsf{RC}:\quad
\text{an intrinsic cross-phase relation in a finite mode must be carried by the declared ordered overlap trace.}
\]

Together with mode/component closure (no undeclared relation between distinct flow components), RC would exclude both:

- an intra-component free-bit comparator
  \[
  J_s=c\oplus d,
  \]
  because \(p_v\) and \(q_v\) are not jointly in the trace; and
- an inter-component Q-bit comparator
  \[
  I_{s,t}=d_s\oplus d_t,
  \]
  because distinct trace sectors are distinct flow components in the candidate grammar.

If all intrinsic observables are thus trace/component internal, every trace-preserving lift is isomorphic under:
\[
\eta_h(a,b,d)=(a,b,d\oplus h(a,b)),
\quad h\in(\mathbb Z_2)^4,
\]
and full gauge follows.

## Critical test

RC must be independently supported, not silently read into “shares a trace.” AP3P1 only says every successor pair **has** a common ordered trace; it does not say that trace exhausts retentional content or all legitimate relations. AP3J supports temporal articulation and bounded horizons, not an exhaustiveness theorem.

The audit therefore asks separately:

1. Is the full redefinition a grammar isomorphism when only declared overlap and component data are retained? (formal question)
2. Does current core/AP3P1/AP3J establish RC? (derivational question)
3. Does immanent self-presentation instead warrant an intrinsic P/Q comparator \(J_s\)? (rival bridge)

## Registered outcomes

- **Conditional theorem:** RC plus component closure and structural identity imply full lift gauge.
- **Current status:** if RC is not independently derived, this theorem cannot be promoted to a core result.
- **Rival:** a cross-phase comparator principle \(\mathsf{CPC}\) makes \(J_s\) intrinsic and blocks full gauge. It is likewise not currently derived.

This test distinguishes a genuine derivation target from a relabelled convention.

# AP3P2M Horizon-Gradient Temporalization and Boundary Audit v1
## Does a structural horizon/clarity gradient derive temporal flow or individuate mode components?

## Candidate route

The proposed finite-temporalization route is:
\[
\text{finite perspective}
\Rightarrow
\text{horizon}
\Rightarrow
\text{gradient of clarity}
\Rightarrow
\text{act proceeds through gradient}
\Rightarrow
\text{temporal flow}.
\]

It must distinguish what is structural and atemporal from what already encodes temporal succession.

## Coherent structural core

For fixed vocabulary \(S\), a context \(C\subsetneq S\) can have:
\[
H(C)=S\setminus C,
\]
and a non-temporal clarity rank:
\[
\kappa_C:S\to\{0,1,2\},
\]
with focal, marginal, and horizonal levels. This is mathematically coherent without any time coordinate or successor relation.

AP3M already proves that finite horizons and proper-overlap neighbours are correctly typed constraints but leave all five structural grammar families viable.

## Static-gradient countermodel

Let:
\[
S=\{a,b,c\},\quad C_0=\{a,b\},\quad C_1=\{a,c\},
\]
\[
\kappa_{C_0}(a,b,c)=(2,1,0),
\quad
\kappa_{C_1}(a,c,b)=(2,1,0).
\]
Both contexts are finite, proper, self-presenting-typed, overlap at \(a\), and have nontrivial clarity gradients. The structure admits no successor relation at all. Therefore horizon/gradient does not entail flow.

The same horizon/gradient data permits several incompatible successor choices: no edge, \(C_0\to C_1\), \(C_1\to C_0\), or a cycle. Thus it determines neither direction, nonterminality, acyclicity, nor bi-infinite order.

## Boundary analysis

For fixed \(S\), \(H(C)=S\setminus C\) extensionally determines a context:
\[
H(C)=H(D)\Rightarrow C=D.
\]
But this does not make horizons generatively individuating: defining \(H(C)\) already presupposes a context \(C\), and different successor choices group the same horizon-bearing contexts into different flow components.

Hence horizon difference can describe already-given context differences. It cannot by itself derive which context sequence is one mode-flow, where a component boundary lies, or that components are modes.

## Circularity marker

The phrase “the act proceeds through the gradient” supplies a directed relation. If it is not independently defined, it is exactly the desired successor relation in phenomenological wording:
\[
\operatorname{ProceedsThrough}(C,D)\equiv C\rightsquigarrow D.
\]
Then Step 4 assumes, rather than derives, temporal flow.

## Registered conclusions

1. Horizon and clarity gradient are coherent non-temporal structural notions.
2. They do not entail temporalization or AP3P1 successor flow.
3. Horizon is extensionally individuating only after contexts are supplied; it does not derive flow-component/mode identity.
4. A successful horizon route would need a new independent theorem from gradient/integration to directed succession, robust against alternatives and the anti-confirmation test.

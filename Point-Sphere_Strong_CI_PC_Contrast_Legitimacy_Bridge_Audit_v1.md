# Strong CI Adjudication — PC Contrast-Legitimacy Bridge Audit v1
## When is “why this rather than another?” a real ultimate question?

## Corrigendum status

Read this dossier with `Point-Sphere_Strong_CI_PC_Contrast_Legitimacy_Corrigendum_v1.md`. The present result concerns global selection, not the legitimacy of conditional local results.

**No candidate has earned a general asymmetry between the essence–existence and intrinsic-articulation cases that selects its exact profile.** Each can conditionally block a narrow question that imports a rejected ontology; neither thereby answers the broader exact-profile question.

## 1. The problem

The current dispute cannot be settled by saying either:

- every contrastive question about an ultimate is legitimate; or
- every contrastive question is ill-posed because the ultimate has no external alternative.

The first ignores category errors. The second lets any candidate immunize its core by definition.

Let:

\[
Q_F(p;q):
\quad
\text{“Why does profile \(p\) obtain in framework \(F\), rather than contrast \(q\)?”}
\]

A contrast need not be an actual second world or an external item. It can be an intelligible rival profile. But it must be typed independently enough that the candidate cannot dismiss it merely by stipulating \(p\).

## 2. Three statuses of a contrastive question

| Status | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Ill-typed** | The question presupposes a relation/category \(d\) that the candidate independently derives to be inapplicable | The narrow formulation fails |
| **Conditionally answered** | Candidate premises independently derive \(p\) and exclude \(q\) | The question is answered relative to those premises |
| **Residual exact-profile question** | \(p\) is asserted as core, while a nontrivial rival profile remains intelligible and no derivation excludes it | The question remains a strongest-CI burden |

“Independently” means: not simply a restatement that \(p\) is the candidate’s ultimate core. It may include a defended ontology, but that ontology itself remains subject to the exact-profile test.

## 3. Fair contrast-legitimacy condition

A candidate can dismiss \(Q_F(p;q)\) as ill-typed only if:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{CL}(F,Q) :=
&\exists d\,[
\mathsf{Presupposes}(Q,d)
\land
\mathsf{Derives}_{F}(\neg d)\\
&\land
\mathsf{Independence}(\neg d,p)
\land
\mathsf{NoProfileReappearance}(F,p)
].
\end{aligned}
\tag{CL}
\]

The final condition prevents a local category-error diagnosis from being mistaken for an answer to the remaining profile question.

## 4. Application: essence–existence identity

Assume the classical profile:

\[
\mathsf{EE}(G):
\quad
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(G).
\]

### Narrow question

\[
Q_{\rm add}:
\quad
\text{“What adds existence to a prior essence of \(G\)?”}
\]

Conditional on a coherent EE ontology, \(Q_{\rm add}\) presupposes an essence/existence composition denied by that ontology. It can therefore be locally ill-typed.

### Residual question

\[
Q_{\rm EE\text{-}profile}:
\quad
\text{“Why is there an actual ultimate with this EE identity profile rather than a different alleged ultimate profile?”}
\]

\(Q_{\rm add}\)'s failure does not answer \(Q_{\rm EE\text{-}profile}\). The latter is the reappearance burden identified in the classical dossiers.

## 5. Application: whole-first intrinsic articulation

Assume the whole-first profile:

\[
\mathsf{WF}(U,A):
\quad
U\text{ is ontologically prior to articulation }A,
\quad
A\text{ has no independently basic constituents.}
\]

### Narrow question

\[
Q_{\rm combine}:
\quad
\text{“What externally combines independently basic elements into \(U\)?”}
\]

Conditional on \(\mathsf{WF}(U,A)\), this question imports independently basic elements and an external combination relation. It can be locally ill-typed.

### Residual question

\[
Q_{\rm WF\text{-}profile}:
\quad
\text{“Why is \(U\) intrinsically articulated by this exact \(A\), rather than another coherent whole-profile?”}
\]

“No \(U\) exists apart from \(A\)” does not alone answer this. It says only that the profile is intrinsic. It does not derive why it is this profile, show alternative profiles incoherent, or give a non-circular self-determination account.

## 6. Application: nondual aspect identity

The same distinction applies to protected nondual identity.

\[
Q_{\rm selector}:
\quad
\text{“What external selector makes a genuine aspect actual?”}
\]

Given the nonrepresentational identity theorem, this may be ill-typed: genuine aspecthood already implies self-knowledge content and realization.

But:

\[
Q_{\rm domain}:
\quad
\text{“Why are these all-and-only finite contents genuine aspects?”}
\]

remains. The aspect-domain dossier has already shown that it needs \(\mathsf{PubDet}_G\) or an equivalent non-circular domain account.

## 7. Symmetry result

The desired classical asymmetry is not presently available:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{EE}(G)
\Rightarrow
\neg Q_{\rm add}
\quad\text{does not show}\quad
Q_{\rm EE\text{-}profile}\text{ is answered.}
}
\]

And:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{WF}(U,A)
\Rightarrow
\neg Q_{\rm combine}
\quad\text{does not show}\quad
Q_{\rm WF\text{-}profile}\text{ is answered.}
}
\]

Therefore neither side may treat its narrow presupposition challenge as a global terminal-closure pass.

## 8. Consequence for B2

The classical route cannot establish B2 by asserting that articulation is a genuine alternative-space whereas existence is not. The whole-first route cannot establish itself by asserting that intrinsic articulation makes all profile questions incoherent.

Both need an actual derivation/classification of their exact profile, or a candidate-specific presupposition challenge satisfying CL without profile reappearance.

## 9. Expiry condition

This audit advances only if a candidate provides:

1. an independently argued ontology locating the alleged false presupposition;
2. a non-circular derivation that excludes the relevant rival profile rather than only an imported relation; and
3. a positive explanation/classification of its own exact profile.

Until then, narrow category-error points remain local and do not select an ultimate ontology.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not prove that all ultimate contrastive questions are legitimate, reject essence–existence identity, refute whole-first articulation, or solve the exact-profile problem. It prevents either candidate from using a local malformed-question diagnosis to evade it.

# Intrinsic-Nature Bridge v1
## A model-theoretic result, a precise psychophysical postulate, and an abductive awareness-ground comparison

## Result in one sentence

A purely structural or causal theory cannot, by itself, entail phenomenal facts unless it contains a bridge axiom identifying or assigning phenomenality. The framework can therefore make the psychophysical bridge explicit, constrain it through the candidate-perspective criterion, and compare an awareness-ground hypothesis with rivals—but it cannot honestly claim to derive awareness from structure alone.

---

## 1. The target distinction

Three questions must be separated.

1. **Carrier question:** Which physical or causal organisation, if any, is the bearer of a finite perspective?
2. **Psychophysical question:** Why or under what law does that organisation have phenomenal presence?
3. **Ultimate-nature question:** What is the intrinsic nature of the reality that realises physical structure and phenomenal presence?

The D/I/S/L/P and \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) programmes address the first question and formulate a hypothesis for the second. They do not answer the third. The awareness-ground thesis is an answer to the third, not a theorem of the first two.

---

## 2. Structural underdetermination theorem

Let \(L_{\rm str}\) be a language whose non-logical vocabulary contains only structural and causal resources: relations, functions, quantities, state spaces, transition kernels, symmetries, and intervention rules. Let \(T_{\rm str}\) be a consistent theory in this language.

Extend the language by a phenomenal predicate or assignment \(\Phi\). For example, \(\Phi(p,q)\) may say that candidate perspective \(p\) instantiates phenomenal organisation \(q\).

### Theorem 1 — No phenomenal entailment from an unextended structural theory

If \(T_{\rm str}\) contains no axiom constraining \(\Phi\), then \(T_{\rm str}\) entails no non-trivial phenomenal claim.

#### Proof

Let \(M\models T_{\rm str}\). Form two expansions with the same \(L_{\rm str}\)-reduct:
\[
M_0=(M,\Phi_0),\qquad \Phi_0=\varnothing,
\]
\[
M_1=(M,\Phi_1),
\]
where \(\Phi_1\) assigns at least one phenomenal organisation to an admissible carrier. Since \(\Phi\) occurs in no axiom of \(T_{\rm str}\), both expansions satisfy \(T_{\rm str}\) and agree on every structural sentence, while disagreeing on phenomenal claims. Therefore no such claim follows from \(T_{\rm str}\) alone. \(\square\)

This is a modest logical result, not an anti-physicalist theorem. A physicalist may add a bridge axiom. The point is that the bridge must be acknowledged.

### Corollary — Structural identity is a substantive bridge

Suppose a theory adds
\[
\forall p\,[\operatorname{Phen}(p)\leftrightarrow C(p)],
\]
where \(C\) is a structural or functional condition. Then phenomenal facts follow in the extended theory. They follow because of the added identity or bridge axiom, not because the original structural vocabulary entailed them unaided.

The same applies to:

- reductive functional identity;
- a psychophysical law;
- neutral-monist realisation;
- Russellian intrinsic properties;
- cosmopsychist participation;
- illusionist replacement of robust phenomenal predicates.

They are rival ways of extending the structural theory. The theorem does not decide among them.

## 3. Isomorphism and the individuation cost

Let \(p,q\) be candidate systems structurally isomorphic under an isomorphism \(f:p\cong q\) preserving the relevant causal, intervention, boundary, and record structure.

### Theorem 2 — Structural twins require a choice

A structural theory alone cannot assign different phenomenal organisations to \(p\) and \(q\). A theory has only three options:

1. **Structural invariance:** impose \(\Phi(p)\cong\Phi(q)\); phenomenality supervenes on the relevant structure.
2. **Extra individuator:** add a non-structural fact, label, haecceity, or hidden variable that differentiates their phenomenal assignments.
3. **Indeterminacy:** deny that the theory determines a phenomenal answer.

#### Reason

An assignment that differs across structural twins is not fixed by the structural language. It therefore requires additional vocabulary or leaves the assignment underdetermined. \(\square\)

This matters for the point–sphere programme. If its psychophysical bridge is invariant under the declared candidate-perspective structure, then it cannot appeal to “the same structure but a different private subject” without adding an individuation principle. If it adds one, that principle must be counted as a further posit.

## 4. A precise maximal-carrier bridge

Let \(\operatorname{MaxPC}_\Theta\) be the set of maximal, non-vacuous D/I/S/L/P candidates at a stated scale and threshold vector \(\Theta\).

The bridge is best stated in layers.

### P1 — Carrier necessity
\[
\operatorname{PhenCentre}(p)
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
p\in\operatorname{MaxPC}_\Theta.
\]

If a finite phenomenal centre occurs in the target domain, its physical carrier must be a maximal candidate perspective.

### P2 — Carrier sufficiency (stronger)
\[
p\in\operatorname{MaxPC}_\Theta
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\operatorname{PhenCentre}(p).
\]

P1 plus P2 is the strong \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) hypothesis. P1 alone is less vulnerable but has less explanatory force.

### P3 — Organisational invariance

For every relevant causal isomorphism \(f:p\cong q\), there is a corresponding phenomenal-organisation isomorphism:
\[
\Phi(q)\cong\Phi(p).
\]

P3 does not require that two numerically distinct but isomorphic centres are literally one centre. It requires matching phenomenal organisation. Numerical identity requires a separate individuation theory.

### P4 — Exclusion and overlap discipline

Two disjoint maximal candidates may carry two distinct centres. Overlapping candidates must be handled by a predeclared rule: same centre at different scale, a nested centre relation, or unresolved ambiguity. A theory must not count every overlapping subnet as a new subject.

### P5 — No physical surplus

The phenomenal assignment does not add an unmodelled force to the physical transition equations. Any claim of a new physical influence must instead be written as an explicit coupling law and tested separately.

P1–P5 are a genuine bridge specification. They answer how physical structure could *carry* a finite perspective under a dual-aspect or cosmopsychist interpretation. They do not answer why the bridge is true.

## 5. Combination, decombination, and what the bridge resolves

The bridge avoids one familiar error. It does not assign a separate subject to every physical microcomponent. A proper subcomponent of \(p\) that fails maximal D/I/S/L/P status is not assigned an independent phenomenal centre merely because it is a physical subsystem.

### Proposition — No micro-subject combination is licensed by the maximal-carrier bridge

Under P1 and P4, non-maximal proper components are not required to be subjects. Therefore the theory does not need to combine a population of stipulated micro-subjects into the subject carried by \(p\).

This is not a proof that phenomenal unity has been explained. It is a rejection of the micropsychist starting point. The remaining problem is **decombination**: why a global intrinsic basis, if one exists, is associated with these bounded maximal carriers rather than with every subsystem or with only one universal centre.

The candidate-perspective programme gives a precise answer at the structural level: bounded, screened, integrated, model-using, persistent organisations are the proposed carriers. The awareness-ground thesis must still explain why such a structural organisation corresponds to localised phenomenal access.

## 6. The abductive awareness-ground comparison

The structural-underdetermination theorem does not select an awareness-ground. It identifies a comparison space.

| Option | Psychophysical move | Principal gain | Principal cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional identity | Phenomenality is identical with a specified structural/functional invariant | Minimal ontological expansion | Must state the invariant and explain why it is phenomenal rather than merely functional |
| Brute psychophysical law | A law maps certain structures to experience | Direct fit to correlations | Adds an unexplained bridge primitive |
| Neutral/Russellian monism | Structure has an intrinsic realiser not exhausted by physics | Addresses intrinsic-nature gap | Realiser may remain opaque; combination/decombination persists |
| Cosmopsychist awareness-ground | One fundamental awareness is the intrinsic basis; maximal candidates localise finite access | Avoids emergence from an allegedly wholly non-experiential base and micro-subject proliferation | Must solve decombination, creation/participation, and explain why one awareness yields many centres |
| Illusionism | Robust phenomenal predicates are replaced or deflated | Avoids adding a psychophysical primitive | Must explain why the datum being denied appears undeniable and preserve the target of explanation |

The awareness-ground earns at most an abductive role. It becomes stronger only if it can show that it:

1. preserves phenomenal data without denial;
2. requires fewer independent bridge primitives than rivals;
3. gives a workable maximal-carrier/decombination model;
4. remains compatible with causal closure and empirical science;
5. avoids importing equal or greater unexplained modal, creation, or individuation costs.

No row wins merely by being called “intrinsic.”

## 7. Evidence and failure conditions

The bridge has different evidential routes at different levels.

### Carrier-level evidence

The EΘ protocol can test whether maximal D/I/S/L/P architecture tracks independently motivated markers of unified or divided access. Its split-architecture prediction is a test of P1/P2 in a restricted domain.

### Structural-supervenience evidence

If systems matched on the entire declared causal/interventional structure systematically differ in independently supported phenomenal organisation, P3 fails or an extra individuator is needed. Conversely, structural matching does not prove P3; it is compatible with multiple bridge theories.

### Awareness-ground comparison

An awareness-ground with P5 is not expected to generate a new physical effect. Its evidence is comparative and abductive, not a direct laboratory signature. It loses force if a functional or neutral theory supplies an equally coherent, independently motivated bridge with fewer primitives and equal coverage of phenomenality and individuation.

### Explicit failure conditions

The programme must weaken or reject the strong bridge in the tested domain if:

- independently well-supported conscious cases fail to contain any plausible maximal candidate under all preregistered scales;
- robust maximal candidates recur in systems that lack every convergent marker of consciousness, despite adequate controls;
- the criterion requires target-label or phenomenology-driven tuning to work;
- structural twins require phenomenally different verdicts with no independently motivated individuator;
- causal-use, overlap, or persistence rules collapse under intervention testing.

These failures do not refute phenomenal consciousness. They refute a particular carrier/bridge model.

---

## 8. What has advanced

This bridge supplies three non-decorative results.

1. **A no-free-entailment theorem:** structural/causal formalisms require a phenomenal bridge axiom to entail phenomenal claims.
2. **An individuation theorem:** structural twins force a choice between organisational invariance, an additional individuator, or underdetermination.
3. **A precisely layered EΘ bridge:** carrier necessity, sufficiency, invariance, exclusion, and no-surplus are separately stated and separately testable.

It does not prove pure awareness. It replaces an equivocation—“experience must somehow be intrinsic, therefore the ground is awareness”—with a disciplined abductive programme that states exactly what an awareness-ground must outperform.

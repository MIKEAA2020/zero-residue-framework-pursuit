The candidates are source-principle attempts. Their intended sequence remains:

\[
\mathsf{SourcePrinciple}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{PrephysicalStructure}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{RealOrder}

\mathsf{SpacetimeWitness}(M^4,g).

No stage may be skipped:

\[
\mathsf{SourcePostulate}

# Part I — SR-2 strong-route audit

## 2. SR-2 S0 — typing of DNE, loci, and dependence

SR-2 introduces:

\[
\mathsf{DNE}(G,\Sigma,L,\triangleleft).
\]

It gives an intended interpretation of \(\Sigma\), \(L\), and \(\triangleleft\), but no typed domain for loci, no formal source or codomain for \(\triangleleft\), no conditions under which a locus is individuated, and no formal criterion for a Sphere/presentation.

In particular, the candidate has not fixed whether \(L\) is:

\[
\text{a set of formal tokens},
\qquad
\text{a set of real dependent loci},
\qquad
\text{a presentation-relative domain},
\qquad
\text{or a logical grammar}.
\]

These readings have materially different implications for strong simplicity and strict nonduality.

### SR-2 S0 result

\[
\mathsf{S0Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm DNE\ locus\ and\ dependence\ types\ not\ fixed}.
\]

---

## 3. SR-2 S1 — independent warrant for DNE

The stated warrant is conditional:

\[
\mathsf{RealFiniteDependentPresentation}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{LimitationBelongsToDependentPresentation}.
\]

This conditional may clarify where a limitation would belong if such a presentation exists. It does not establish that there is a real finite dependent presentation, that DNE is actual, that a plurality of loci exists, or that directed dependence has the stated structure.

\[
\mathsf{ConditionalLocationOfLimitation}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Established}(\mathsf{DNE}).
\]

The rationale therefore does not independently support the source postulate beyond its desired dependent-order output.

### SR-2 S1 result

\[
\mathsf{S1Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm DNE\ is\ explicit\ but\ independently\ unwarranted}.
\]

---

## 4. SR-2 S2 — strong-simplicity and strict-nonduality compatibility

SR-2 improves on SR-1 by refusing to negate the Point's own simplicity:

\[
\mathsf{NonExhaustion}(\ell,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{StrongSimple}(G).
\]

That removes SR-1's direct contradiction. But the candidate now needs to explain how an actual Sphere, an actual plurality of loci, and an actual directed dependence relation are not:

\[
\text{parts of }G,
\qquad\text{independent beings},
\qquad\text{a second realm},
\qquad\text{or real internal formal differentiation of }G.
\]

The statements that loci are nonparts and nonindependent are source conditions, not defended bridges:

\[
\mathsf{DependsOn}(\ell,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PartOf}(\ell,G)
\land
\neg\mathsf{IndependentBeing}(\ell).
\]

### SR-2 S2 result

\[
\mathsf{S2Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm no\ nonpartitivity/nonduality\ compatibility\ bridge}.
\]

No claim of composition or independent beings follows from this incompleteness.

---

## 5. SR-2 S3 — determinate privative grammar

SR-2 defines:

\[
\lambda_\ell
:=
\mathsf{NonExhaustion}(\ell,G).
\]

But “non-exhaustion” has no registered criterion of identity or comparison. To determine whether two loci have the same or different privations, the candidate needs an account of what each locus fails to exhaust. That requires either:

\[
\text{a positive range of features},
\qquad
text{a comparison basis},
\qquad
text{or a formal rule of non-exhaustion}.
\]

None is provided. Thus the move avoids negating \(G\)'s simplicity, but it does not yet yield a determinate privative grammar.

\[
\mathsf{NonExhaustionLabel}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{DeterminatePrivativeGrammar}.
\]

### SR-2 S3 result

\[
\mathsf{S3Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm no\ determinate\ criterion\ without\ a\ hidden\ comparison\ range}.
\]

---

## 6. SR-2 S4 — global actualization

SR-2 explicitly distinguishes branch selection from global-source residue:

\[
\mathsf{NoBranchSelector}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NoGlobalSourceResidue}.
\]

Its actualization is nevertheless a postulate:

\[
\mathsf{GlobalDNEActualization}(G,[\Sigma,L,\triangleleft]).
\]

No rationale accounts for why this order, this set of loci, or DNE itself is actual rather than merely formal or alternative.

### SR-2 S4 result

\[
\mathsf{S4Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm global\ actualization\ remains\ a\ source\ residue}.
\]

## 7. SR-2 S5–S6 — causal bridge and spacetime witness

A directed nontemporal dependence relation is not automatically causal:

\triangleleft

\mathsf{CausalPrecedence}.
\]

SR-2 supplies no interventions, counterfactual asymmetry, dynamics, causal semantics, finite output, recovery map, or \((M^4,g)\) witness.

\mathsf{S5Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=
\mathsf{NotExecutable}_{\rm no\ causal\ semantics},

\[
\mathsf{S6Status}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
=

# Part II — WR-1 weak-route audit

## 8. WR-1 W0 — typing of ISD, field, and relation

WR-1 introduces:

\[
\mathsf{ISD}(G,\mathcal F,\preceq).
\]

It declares \(\mathcal F\) to be a positive formal field and \(\preceq\) an immanent formal relation. But it does not state the type of the field members, whether \(\preceq\) is an order, relation, operation, or dependency map, or what formal operations generate or constrain the field.

\[
\mathsf{PositiveFormalField}(\mathcal F,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Typed}(\mathcal F,\preceq).
\]

The candidate successfully excludes physical inputs at the level of intention, but the prephysical positive structure is not yet mathematically determinate.

### WR-1 W0 result

\[
\mathsf{W0Status}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm field\ members\ and\ relation\ type\ unspecified}.
\]

---

## 9. WR-1 W1 — independent warrant for ISD

The stated warrant says:

\[
\mathsf{ActualityThatGroundsDeterminateReality}
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{NotMerelyIndeterminateBlank}.
\]

This can motivate resistance to an empty or wholly undifferentiated explanation of determinate reality. It does not establish the specific postulate \(\mathsf{ISD}(G,\mathcal F,\preceq)\), an actual formal field, a relation, or a global order.

It also cannot use determinate spacetime as evidence without presupposing the very output the candidate is meant to derive.

\[
\mathsf{RationaleAgainstBlankness}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Established}(\mathsf{ISD}).
\]

### WR-1 W1 result

\[
\mathsf{W1Status}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm no\ independent\ warrant\ for\ ISD\ or\ its\ field/relation}.
\]

---

## 10. WR-1 W2 — nonpartitivity and weak-simplicity compatibility

WR-1 correctly refuses to infer the required properties from the word “formal.” But it supplies no bridge establishing them:

\[
\mathsf{PositiveFormalField}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Nonpartitive}
\land
\mathsf{Nonindependent}
\land
\mathsf{Nontemporal}
\land
\mathsf{Nonspatial}
\land
\mathsf{Nonbrute}.
\]

The field and relation are therefore a positive source postulate with unestablished ontological status.

### WR-1 W2 result

\[
\mathsf{W2Status}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm required\ nonpartitivity/nonindependence\ bridge\ absent}.
\]

This does not prove that positive immanent differentiation is compositional.

\[
\mathsf{NoBranchSelection}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NoGlobalSourceResidue}.

### WR-1 W3 result

\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm global\ actualization\ remains\ a\ source\ residue}.

## 13. Comparative result

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{test} & \mathsf{SR\text{-}2} & \mathsf{WR\text{-}1}\\
\hline
\text{literal contradiction with strong core} & \text{avoided} & \text{not applicable}\\
\text{source fully typed} & \text{no} & \text{no}\\
\text{independent source warrant} & \text{no} & \text{no}\\
\text{core-compatibility bridge} & \text{no} & \text{no}\\
\text{global actualization} & \text{residual} & \text{residual}\\
\text{causal semantics} & \text{absent} & \text{absent}\\
\text{spacetime witness} & \text{absent} & \text{absent}
\end{array}
\]

SR-2's limited improvement is genuine: it removes SR-1's direct self-contradiction by placing privation in a dependent locus rather than negating \(G\)'s simplicity. WR-1's limited improvement is also genuine: it makes positive immanent field, relation, and actualization commitments explicit instead of treating them as derived from weak simplicity.

Neither limited improvement is a physics-generating bridge:

\[
\mathsf{AvoidsOneEarlierDefect}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SpacetimeGeneration}.
\]

## 14. Expiry and bounded conclusion

Both candidates now expire under their preregistered rules.

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}(\mathsf{SR\text{-}2})
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Refuted}(\mathsf{StrongSimplicity})
\lor
\mathsf{Refuted}(\mathsf{PrivativeRoute}),
\]

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}(\mathsf{WR\text{-}1})
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Refuted}(\mathsf{WeakSimplicity})
\lor
\mathsf{Refuted}(\mathsf{PositiveImmanentRoute}).
\]

The comparison establishes no winner. It locates the next real demands:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\mathsf{Strong} & \text{type and warrant a dependent-locus source without a hidden positive comparison range}\\
\mathsf{Weak} & \text{type and warrant a positive formal field while defending nonpartitivity and global actualization}
\end{array}
\]

Neither side has yet produced the prephysical structure needed to attempt an \((M^4,g)\) recovery witness.

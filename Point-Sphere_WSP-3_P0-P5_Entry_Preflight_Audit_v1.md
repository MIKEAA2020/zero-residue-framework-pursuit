## 0. Burden discipline

The candidate bears affirmative burdens: an adverbial determination must be positive, nonpartitive, nonindependent, nontemporal, nonspatial, and nonbrute in the applicable restricted sense.

The audit must not reverse the burden. In particular:

\[
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(p,G)
\neq
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(q,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NumericallyDistinctEntity}(p,q),
\tag{No-Profile-to-Entity-Inflation}
\]

\[
\neg\mathsf{Established}
\bigl(
\mathsf{ADet}(p,G)\Rightarrow\neg\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\bigr)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Established}
\bigl(
\mathsf{ADet}(p,G)\Rightarrow\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\bigr).
\tag{No-Absence-to-Composition-Reversal}
\]

A failed or incomplete gate therefore records an unmet affirmative warrant, not a refutation of immanent formal differentiation.

## 2. P0 — noncircularity and no external domain

WSP-3 defines its candidate category by:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ADet}(p,G)
\iff{}&
\mathsf{IntrinsicAscription}(p,G)
\land
\mathsf{BearerFixed}(p,G)\\
&\land
\mathsf{IdentityCondition}(p,G)
\land
\neg\mathsf{Entity}(p)\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{SpatialLocation}(p)
\land
\neg\mathsf{TemporalStage}(p).
\end{aligned}
\tag{P0-Definition-Under-Test}
\]

The right side does not invoke \(\mathsf{ADet}\), real formal difference, parthood, Sphere, actual effects, public truth/error, or a separately populated possibility space. It is therefore not syntactically circular in the prohibited way:

\[
\mathsf{ADet}\notin
\mathsf{Definiens}(\mathsf{ADet}).
\tag{P0-No-Direct-Circularity}
\]

The stated readings also limit bearer-fixity to the one bearer \(G\), and the definition does not quantify an independent range of possible entities:

\[
\mathsf{P0}_{\rm no\ second\ bearer/modal\ import}
=
\mathsf{Pass}_{\rm declared\ grammar}.
\tag{P0-No-External-Import}
\]

However, \(\mathsf{IdentityCondition}(p,G)\) remains a primitive candidate notion. Calling a condition “identity-relevant” does not itself explain why it is an ontologically real rather than merely descriptive determination:

\[
\mathsf{IdentityCondition}\notin
\mathsf{Definiens}(\mathsf{ADet})
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\mathsf{IndependentRealityWarrant}(\mathsf{IdentityCondition}).
\tag{P0-Primitive-Remainder}
\]

Thus the pass is restricted to noncircular grammar, not an all-purpose metaphysical validation:

\[
\mathsf{P0Status}=
\mathsf{RestrictedPass}_{\rm primitive\ identity\ condition\ retained}.
\tag{P0-Result}
\]

## 3. P1 — positive, asymmetrical profiles

The candidate does more than attach the labels “Unity” and “Articulation” to an otherwise symmetric pair. It makes the labels derivative from the positions of an asymmetric formal-dependence relation:

\[
\mathsf U\prec_f\mathsf A,
\qquad
\neg\exists r\,[r\prec_f\mathsf U],
\qquad
\neg\exists r\,[\mathsf A\prec_f r].
\tag{P1-Directed-Profile}
\]

Conditional on \(\prec_f\) being an intelligible intrabearer identity-condition relation, the two roles differ structurally by their incoming/outgoing formal-dependence profiles:

\[
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(\mathsf U,G)
\neq
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(\mathsf A,G).
\tag{P1-Positive-Profile-Difference}
\]

This is a genuine advance over a mere inequality of primitive type labels **at the level of formal structure**. Neither profile is defined through actual finite effects, phenomenal output, a norm, physical behavior, or an external object.

The audit also finds no temporal or efficient-causal predicate in the stated definition of \(\prec_f\). Its direction is a relation of inclusion among identity conditions, not an asserted earlier/later sequence:

\[
\mathsf U\prec_f\mathsf A
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EarlierThan}(\mathsf U,\mathsf A)
\lor
\mathsf{EfficientCauseOf}(\mathsf U,\mathsf A).
\tag{P1-No-Temporal-or-Causal-Import}
\]

Still, this result is conditional. The formal-dependence relation and its directed instance are declared core/residual; no derivation shows why that relation obtains. The audit therefore records:

\[
\mathsf{P1Status}
=
\mathsf{RestrictedPass}_{\rm structural\ positive\ profile,
\ formal\ dependence\ retained\ as\ primitive}.
\tag{P1-Result}
\]

## 4. P2 — parthood applicability and the anti-composition bridge

WSP-3 proposes this applicability condition:

\[
\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(x,G)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Entity}(x)
\land
\mathsf{BearerCapable}(x).
\tag{P2-Parthood-Criterion}
\]

Together with its adverbial ontology, this yields an internally valid conditional derivation:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ADet}(p,G)
&\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{Entity}(p)
\land
\neg\mathsf{BearerCapable}(p),\\
&\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(p,G),
\quad\text{given \eqref{P2-Parthood-Criterion}.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{P2-Conditional-Derivation}
\]

But the preregistration itself provides no independent warrant for why all proper parts must be entities capable of bearing determinations, instead of merely declaring that WSP-3 reserves parthood for such items. The anti-composition claim therefore depends on the very criterion that needs support:

\[
\mathsf{IndependentWarrant}
(\mathsf{P2\text{-}Parthood\text{-}Criterion})
=
\mathsf{NotYetSupplied}.
\tag{P2-Missing-Warrant}
\]

This produces no proof of composition:

\[
\mathsf{P2Status}=
\mathsf{Incomplete}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G).
\tag{P2-No-Refutation}
\]

It instead blocks entry until the restricted parthood criterion is either defended or replaced only by a **fresh** candidate with another preregistered bridge.

## 5. P3 — bearer-fixity and nonindependence

Bearer-fixity says that an adverbial determination has \(G\) as sole bearer and has no bearer-independent instance:

\[
\mathsf{BearerFixed}(p,G)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{BearerIndependentInstance}(p).
\tag{P3-Declared-Nonindependence}
\]

As a candidate condition, this blocks a simple inference from formal profile difference to independently existing constituents:

\[
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(p,G)
\neq
\mathsf{FormalProfile}(q,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(p,G)
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(q,G).
\tag{P3-No-Simple-Independence-Inference}
\]

However, the sole-bearer reading is still placed in the definition of the candidate category. The packet does not yet show why a real determination with its own identity condition cannot be bearer-independent, nor why its identity condition is not an independently available nature.

\[
\mathsf{RealityOf}(p,G)
\land
\mathsf{BearerFixed}(p,G)
\not\Leftarrow
\mathsf{StipulationAlone}.
\tag{P3-Needed-Defense}
\]

The result is bounded incompleteness:

\[
\mathsf{P3Status}
=
\mathsf{Incomplete}_{\rm nonindependence\ declared\ but\ not\ yet\ defended}.
\tag{P3-Result}
\]

This is not a finding of independent being.

## 7. P5 — residual and local nonbruteness audit

The ordered profile gives a local explanatory constraint: if \(\mathsf A\)'s identity condition includes \(\mathsf U\), then \(\mathsf A\) cannot be simply exchanged with \(\mathsf U\) while retaining the same directed formal profile.

\[
\mathsf U\prec_f\mathsf A
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ExchangeInvariant}
(\mathsf U,\mathsf A;\Phi_G^\triangleright).
\tag{P5-Local-Constraint}
\]

This reduces the local arbitrariness of the two labels: their roles are structurally constrained by \(\prec_f\). Yet it leaves both the relation's actual obtaining and the exact two-position field unresolved:

\[
\mathsf{WeakSimple}_{\rm WSP3}(G)
\not\models
\mathsf U\prec_f\mathsf A
\not\models
\Phi_G^\triangleright.
\tag{P5-Residual-Remains}
\]

The preregistration properly declares this residual rather than concealing it. That earns disclosure credit, but not a selector pass or a global nonbruteness result:

\[
\mathsf{ResidualDisclosure}
=
\mathsf{Pass},
\qquad
\mathsf{SelectorResolution}
=
\mathsf{NotClaimed},
\qquad
\mathsf{GlobalNonbruteness}
=
\mathsf{NotEstablished}.
\tag{P5-Disclosure-Only}
\]

\[
\mathsf{P5Status}=
\mathsf{PartialPass}_{\rm local\ profile\ constraint;\ exact\ field\ residual}.
\tag{P5-Result}
\]

\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{

\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP3}

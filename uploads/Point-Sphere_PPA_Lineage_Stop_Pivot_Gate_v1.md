## 3. Selector-residue preregistration gate

A proposed nonbrute actuality bridge \(\mathcal G\) must be stated on quotient classes before a successor packet is written:

\[
\mathcal G:\overline{\mathbf C}\to\{\mathsf{actual},\mathsf{withheld}\}
\quad\text{or an exactly typed equivalent.}
\]

It must satisfy all of the following noncompensatory conditions.

### G1. Quotient invariance

\[
p\cong_{\rm raw}q
\Longrightarrow
\mathcal G([p])=\mathcal G([q]).
\tag{G1}
\]

### G2. Total, typed actual/withheld division on the declared applicable field

\[
\mathsf{Applicable}(a_{[p]})
\Longrightarrow
\bigl(\mathcal G([p])=\mathsf{actual}\bigr)
\oplus
\bigl(\mathcal G([p])=\mathsf{withheld}\bigr).
\tag{G2}
\]

The applicable field, all parameters, and all dependency operations must be declared before testing.

### G3. Noncircular grounding

The bridge may not use, presuppose, or covertly encode any of:

\[
\mathsf{Act}_0,
\quad
\mathsf{Withheld},
\quad
\text{an actual-code list},
\quad
\text{an attestation label},
\quad
\text{a selected model},
\quad
\text{a realization pointer whose extension already selects actuality}.
\tag{G3}
\]

A claimed explanation that is extensionally just the actual roster under a renamed predicate fails this gate.

### G4. No imported possibility selector

If the proposal uses necessity, possibility, eligibility, maximality, grounding, or explanation, it must define that operation on the same declared quotient field. It may not appeal to an undeclared independent modal or possibility domain:

\[
\neg\mathsf{UndeclaredPossibilityDomain}(\mathcal G).
\tag{G4}
\]

### G5. Public finite specification and adversarial discriminability

The proposal must give a finite, executable account of its inputs and state a finite adversarial battery containing at least:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
\textbf{test} & \textbf{required failure condition}\\
\hline
\text{renaming} & \text{a raw representative changes the verdict}\\
\text{roster encoding} & \text{a parameter/code is extensionally an actual-class list}\\
\text{selector substitution} & \text{a selected model, key, or pointer decides the verdict}\\
\text{modal import} & \text{an undeclared possibility domain is needed}\\
\text{tie/automorphism} & \text{structurally indistinguishable applicable classes receive different verdicts}\\
\text{vacuity} & \text{the bridge has no applicable or no withheld cases}\\
\text{circularity} & \text{the proof assumes the actual/withheld split it purports to derive}
\end{array}
\tag{G5}
\]

A bridge may avoid the automorphism failure only by giving a publicly specified, quotient-invariant differentiator that itself passes G3–G4.

### G6. Expiry

Any omitted input, failed adversarial case, or post hoc differentiator closes that proposed bridge route. It may not be inserted into an already tested packet:

\[
\neg\mathsf{Pass}(	ext{any G1--G5 conjunct})
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{Close}_{\rm frozen}(\mathcal G).
\tag{G6}
\]

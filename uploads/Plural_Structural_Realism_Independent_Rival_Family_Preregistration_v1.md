## 2. Structure ontology and equivalence

A raw structure record is a typed relational tuple:

\[
S_{\rm raw}
=
\langle U,\Sigma,\{R_a\}_{a\in\Sigma_R},\{f_b\}_{b\in\Sigma_F},\{P_c\}_{c\in\Sigma_P}\rangle,
\tag{RAW-STRUCTURE}
\]

where \(U\) is a finite or recursively presented carrier, \(\Sigma\) is a finite typed signature, \(R_a\) are typed relations, \(f_b\) typed operations, and \(P_c\) typed predicates. A candidate structure must have a public finite signature and a decidable finite local presentation for every fixture it uses.

Structural equivalence is signature-preserving isomorphism:

\[
S\cong_{\rm str}T
\iff
\exists h:U_S\overset{\sim}{\to}U_T
\ \text{preserving every declared type, relation, operation, and predicate}.
\tag{STRUCT-ISO}
\]

The primary ontological carrier is the isomorphism class:

\[
\overline{\mathcal S}
=
\mathcal S_{\rm raw}/\cong_{\rm str}.
\tag{STRUCT-QUOTIENT}
\]

All primitive instantiation and actuality predicates apply to \([S]\in\overline{\mathcal S}\), never to an arbitrary raw presentation:

\[
S\cong_{\rm str}T
\Longrightarrow
\bigl(
\mathsf{ActualStruct}([S])
\leftrightarrow
\mathsf{ActualStruct}([T])
\bigr).
\tag{ACTUALITY-INVARIANCE}
\]

## 3. Type and individuality rule

The family rejects unregistered bare haecceities. A local token is a typed role occurrence \((S,u)\), but its fundamental individuality is its orbit under the automorphism group of the represented structure:

\[
\mathsf{Aut}(S)
=
\{h:U_S\to U_S:h\text{ is a signature-preserving automorphism}\}.
\tag{AUT}
\]

\[
[u]_S
=
\{h(u):h\in\mathsf{Aut}(S)\}.
\tag{ROLE-ORBIT}
\]

An actual structural individual is the typed orbit token:

\[
\iota_{[S],[u]_S}.
\tag{INDIVIDUAL}
\]

Two role tokens are fundamentally identical exactly when they have the same type and orbit:

\[
\iota_{[S],[u]_S}=
\iota_{[S],[v]_S}
\iff
[u]_S=[v]_S
\ \text{and}\ 
\mathsf{Type}_S(u)=\mathsf{Type}_S(v).
\tag{INDIVIDUALITY}
\]

This rule permits relationally discernible finite loci without positing primitive identity beyond declared structure. It also means that symmetric positions cannot be counted as fundamentally distinct merely by attaching hidden labels.

# AP3P2H Retentional-Content Adjudication Audit v1
## Does finite prereflective retention warrant overlap closure (RC) or P/Q free-value comparison (CPC)?

## Exact issue

AP3P2G reduces the full-gauge derivation to two rival claims:

\[
\mathsf{RC}:\ \text{cross-phase content is exhausted by declared ordered overlap},
\]
\[
\mathsf{CPC}:\ \text{nonoverlapping P/Q free values possess an intrinsic equality/inequality comparator }J_s=c\oplus d.
\]

Both claims must be tested against the already accepted restricted account of prereflective temporal self-presentation, not assumed from the notation \(\nu:C\to\mathbb Z_2\).

## Retentional resources actually available

AP3J and the recorded phenomenology resources support only:

1. retention makes the just-past phase available as past within an immanent temporal field;
2. this is not a second observer, external memory register, or public record;
3. finite experience is bounded/horizonal rather than exhaustively transparent;
4. no formal history payload, comparator, or identity map for nonoverlapping content positions has been derived.

In particular, retention of a phase can be richer than literal graph-node overlap. Therefore literal overlap alone cannot be inferred to exhaust retention.

## Three compatible retentional payload models

For a P-to-Q adjacent pair with free values \(c,d\):

- **R0 (overlap only):** \(R_0=\{r_0,r_1\}\). This satisfies RC.
- **R1 (past-rich, noncomparative):** \(R_1=\{r_0,r_1,\operatorname{Past}(p_v,c)\}\). It violates RC but does not supply \(J_s\).
- **R2 (comparative):** \(R_2=R_1\cup\{J_s=c\oplus d\}\). It supplies CPC.

All can be interpreted immanently, non-objectively, and without external time. Thus the general retentional premise must not be reported as selecting R0 or R2.

## Consequences

- R0 + component closure + structural identity yields full gauge.
- R2 makes full gauge substantive-invalid.
- R1 is an important middle case: literal-overlap closure fails, but no Boolean P/Q comparator is thereby obtained.

## Success condition

The audit succeeds if it prevents two illicit moves:

1. equating “has a shared trace” with “has no other retentional content”; and
2. equating “the just-past phase is retained” with “two distinct free-value fibres have a canonical XOR comparator.”

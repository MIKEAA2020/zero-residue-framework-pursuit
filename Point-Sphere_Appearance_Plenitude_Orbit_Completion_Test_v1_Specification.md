# Appearance/Plenitude Orbit-Completion Test v1
## AP1 — frozen finite diagnostic

## Purpose

Test the exact mathematical content of the proposed move from non-preference to necessary plenitude. The diagnostic is deliberately finite and does not represent the ground, all reality, or a physical cosmology. Its role is to distinguish:

- gauge redundancy from physical plurality;
- a physical symmetry action from a verbal assertion of symmetry;
- conditional orbit completion from automatic plenitude; and
- ontology (which aspects are realised) from probability (how outcomes are weighted).

## Frozen model

### Description level

Use
\[
\Omega=\mathbb Z_4\times\mathbb Z_3.
\]
The second coordinate is a gauge label. The gauge group \(K=\mathbb Z_3\) acts by
\[
k\cdot(a,u)=(a,u+k\!\!\pmod 3).
\]
The physical aspect space is the quotient
\[
\mathcal A=\Omega/K\cong\mathbb Z_4.
\]

### Physical symmetry level

Let \(H=\mathbb Z_4\) act on \(\mathcal A\) by
\[
h\cdot a=a+h\!\!\pmod 4.
\]
This action is transitive. It is declared physical for this finite diagnostic only; no conclusion about a fundamental physical symmetry follows.

### Candidate supports

Enumerate every \(R\subseteq\mathcal A\). A support is symmetry-respecting exactly when \(hR=R\) for every \(h\in H\).

Register these cases:

- \(R_\varnothing=\varnothing\);
- \(R_{\rm all}=\mathcal A\);
- singleton \(R_0=\{0\}\);
- alternating pair \(R_{\rm alt}=\{0,2\}\).

## Registered claims

1. **Gauge quotient:** the 12 labelled descriptions form exactly four three-element gauge fibres. Gauge labels may not be counted as twelve physical aspects.
2. **Conditional orbit completion:** the only \(H\)-invariant supports are \(\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal A\). Therefore nonempty invariant realisation entails all four physical aspects.
3. **No automatic plenitude:** the empty support is also invariant. Symmetry alone does not establish that any aspect exists or that a particular orbit is the physical domain.
4. **No invariant unique selector:** no \(H\)-invariant map from a symmetry-neutral singleton input to one member of \(\mathcal A\) exists.
5. **Probability distinction:** on the finite transitive set, the only normalized \(H\)-invariant probability distribution is uniform. This result is neither a Born-rule derivation nor a claim about an infinite/nontransitive aspect space.

## Interpretation rule

A passed diagnostic supplies the following restricted result:

> If a specified physical, non-gauge symmetry acts transitively on a specified nonempty aspect orbit, and realisation is required to be symmetry invariant, then the full orbit is realised.

It does not establish any antecedent in a real theory, nor does it establish all-world plenitude, non-dual appearance, or a probability rule.

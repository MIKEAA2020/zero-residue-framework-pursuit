# Appearance/Plenitude: Expiry Condition and Foundational-Status Clarification v1

## 1. Aware-ground expiry condition — completed and binding

The defined AP2B effort was completed in this order:

1. **B1:** make the conditional identity route explicit;
2. **B2:** reconstruct the strongest aware-ground and rival positions;
3. **B3:** freeze criteria, burdens, discriminators, and nonclaims;
4. **B4:** apply the ledger adversarially.

The outcome neither established nor defeated the aware-ground premise in a public, rival-neutral sense. It produced only a limited comparative preference under an explanatory-unification methodology. The expiry condition therefore applies:

> **Necessary plenitude is labelled conditional on the abductive aware-ground identity package.**

The work did then move to direct rival assessment—neutral monism, Russellian monism/panprotopsychism, structural realism, brute necessary totality, plus the classical-simple-creator control—in AP2B/B2–B4.

Nothing in AP3’s grammar work updates the abductive standing of aware ground. AP3 is conditional elaboration:

\[
\operatorname{AwareGround}(G)\land\operatorname{IdentityPackage}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{test a grammar of finite self-presentation}.
\]

It is not new confirmation of \(\operatorname{AwareGround}(G)\).

## 2. Identity, not grammar

AP3H’s corrected result is:

\[
\text{mode}=	ext{aspect}=	ext{content of self-knowledge}.
\]

This circularity is an identity claim internal to the conditional core. It does not posit a productive relation between independently existing ground, aspect, and mode.

It does **not** determine:

\[
\smile_{\rm SP}:
\quad
\text{which determinations are jointly self-presentable as one finite mode?}
\]

The latter is a grammar/formalisation question. It has been narrowed by the core’s finite-appearance and non-gauge-boundary commitments, but is not yet uniquely specified.

## 3. Foundational debt discharged; formalisation debt remains

The correct status is not:

> There is an additional ontological membership primitive that selects which modes exist.

Conditional on the core identity, there is no such primitive:

\[
\operatorname{GenuineMode}(m)
\equiv
\operatorname{SelfAspect}_G(m)
\equiv
\operatorname{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(m).
\]

Conditional plenitude then provides nonexclusive realisation:

\[
\forall m\,[\operatorname{GenuineMode}(m)\Rightarrow R(m)].
\]

What remains is not an ontological source, a selector, or a rival realisation law. It is the question of how a mathematical model represents the phrase “determination/self-aspect” without arbitrary labels or an unprincipled grammar.

Thus IAR is properly classified as a **candidate formal grammar of the core identity**, not a new foundational ground or a competing plenitude postulate. If adopted, its cost is formal/model-theoretic specificity, not addition of another ultimate entity or productive principle.

## 4. Document corrections

Earlier AP3F wording that spoke of “no membership law” is to be read narrowly:

> no existing **formal executable grammar** had yet been specified.

It must not be read as reviving a foundational membership-law debt under the conditional aware-ground identity package.

The active AP3N work is therefore correctly framed as grammar refinement. It must preserve:

- the aware-ground/plenitude conditional label;
- mode-aspect-content identity;
- nonexclusive plenitude of qualified aspects;
- distinction between a formal grammar and a new ontological primitive; and
- the IAR-blindness safeguard for any new formal condition.

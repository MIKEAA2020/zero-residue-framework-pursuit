## 3. Required typing normalization before candidate entry

The preregistration is sound in direction but contains five normalization points that must be frozen before an EMC candidate packet is eligible for entry review.

### 3.1 Raw versus quotient world sort

The source table calls \(\mathbf W\) “quotient classes” while the next display constructs a quotient \(\overline{\mathbf W}=\mathbf W_{\rm raw}/\cong_{\rm W}\). The nonambiguous normalized signature is:

\[
\mathbf W_{\rm raw}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\overline{\mathbf W}
:=
\mathbf W_{\rm raw}/\cong_{\rm W}.
\tag{World-Sort-Normalization}
\]

All modal, choice, creation, and actual-world predicates must take \([w]\in\overline{\mathbf W}\) as an argument. A full candidate must state the complete raw-world signature and prove that its selected predicates descend under \(\cong_{\rm W}\).

### 3.2 Derived actuality predicate

The source lists \(\mathsf{ActualWorld}\) among its “central primitive relations” and then defines it biconditionally. The review adopts:

\[
\mathsf{ActualWorld}([w])
\ \text{is derived, not primitive:}\quad
\mathsf{Poss}([w])
\land
\mathsf{Chooses}(D,[w])
\land
\mathsf{Creates}(D,[w]).
\tag{ActualWorld-Derived}
\]

A later packet may instead take \(\mathsf{ActualWorld}\) primitive, but then it must not retain the biconditional as a definition without demonstrating their equivalence. It must select exactly one treatment.

### 3.3 Actuality of the ground

The singleton sort \(\mathbf G=\{D\}\) and \(\mathsf{Necessary}(D)\) do not by themselves state the family’s actuality convention for \(D\). A full packet must explicitly elect one non-equivocal status:

\[
\mathsf{ActualGround}(D)
\quad\text{as protected core content,}
\tag{Ground-Actuality}
\]

or a fully stated rule for it. This is not a request to derive the ground's actuality from the created world; it prevents that actuality from being tacitly assumed while the rule is applied to creaturely actuality.

### 3.4 World-internal time typing

A global sort \(\mathbf T\) is insufficient by itself to establish that time is world-internal. A full candidate must either use fibered time sorts \(\mathbf T_{[w]}\) or state:

\[
\mathsf{TemporalIndexIn}(t,[w])
\quad\text{and require}\quad
\mathsf{InternalAt}(e,t,[w])
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{TemporalIndexIn}(t,[w]).
\tag{World-Internal-Time}
\]

This avoids accidental import of a time common to the creator and every possible world.

### 3.5 Choice, creation, and created dependence

The present family declaration may treat \(\mathsf{Chooses}\) and \(\mathsf{Creates}\) as irreducible residuals. But a candidate must state their minimum typed link to its claimed world and creature results, including whether:

\[
\mathsf{Creates}(D,[w])
\Longrightarrow
\forall c\,[
\mathsf{ExistsIn}(c,[w])
\to
\mathsf{DependentOn}(c,D)],
\tag{Creation-Dependence-Link}
\]

or whether a different declared dependence principle does the work. No such link is attributed to the preregistration merely because it contains a real-distinction implication for already actual creatures.

# Strong CI Adjudication — CI5 Exact-Totality, Selector, and Plenitude Audit v1
## Why an intelligible ultimate relation must still explain its exact output

## Status

**CI5 complete as a selector/exact-totality audit.** It does not select a unique framework. It applies strongest CI to the question left open by CI4:

\[
\text{Why does this exact finite totality, rather than a non-equivalent alternative, obtain?}
\]

## 1. The common requirement

Let \(U\) be a candidate ultimate nature and \(D\) the exact derivative totality it claims is real. An SCI pass requires more than a claim that \(D\) is actual or necessary:

\[
\mathsf{ExactTotalityPass}_F(U,D)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Expl}_F(\mathsf{Ess}_F(U),\mathsf{Act}_F(D))
\land
\mathsf{NonCircular}_F
\land
\mathsf{DomainIndependent}_F(D).
\tag{ET}
\]

The domain-independence condition prevents a candidate from defining its admissible totality as “whatever is actual” and then calling its actualization explained.

## 2. Five main response families

| Response | Proposed solution | Exact SCI burden | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contingent free selection | An ultimate freely selects one among genuine alternatives | Identify a non-primitive difference-maker for this act/output | Fails under strong NBD where alternatives are genuinely symmetric; otherwise held pending a positive account |
| Reason-guided but non-determining choice | Reasons make selection intelligible without necessitating it | Explain the exact residual difference if several eligible outputs remain | Held; “reasons” cannot merely name an unexplained chooser preference |
| Necessary determinate output | The ultimate nature entails exactly \(D\) | Give a non-circular essence-to-output derivation | Held at the exact-totality bridge |
| Unique-best selection | Exactly one candidate is objectively best | Define candidate space and ordering; prove unique maximality and its essence-grounded realization | Held |
| Plenitude | Every genuine/compossible aspect is realized | Define genuine domain independently of realization; solve compossibility and non-composition | Conditional internal route only; public SCI pass held |

## 3. Contingency and selector debt

Under the frozen GC2 conditions:

\[
\mathsf{SSS}(U)
\land
\mathsf{CEA}(U;D_0,D_1)
\land
\mathsf{StrongNBD}
\Rightarrow
\bot.
\]

This result is narrow but real. It does not say all freedom is incoherent. It says that if there are genuinely open non-equivalent outputs relative to the same complete intrinsic nature, then an exact exclusive actualization needs a non-circular difference-maker. Primitive choice is coherent only by declining the strongest standard at that point.

## 4. Necessity is not explanation

The following inferences are invalid without an essence-to-output bridge:

\[
\Box_R\mathsf{Act}(D)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Expl}(\mathsf{Ess}(U),\mathsf{Act}(D)),
\]

\[
\text{“the hierarchy necessarily emanates”}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{“the exact hierarchy is intelligibly derived”},
\]

\[
\text{“this totality is the nature of the whole”}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{“its internal differences are explained.”}
\]

Every necessary-output architecture must therefore expose a map from its ultimate profile to each non-equivalent exactness fact: laws, domains, degrees, modes, relations, and exclusions.

## 5. Unique-best route

A unique-best solution has the form:

\[
\exists!D\in\mathcal D\;
\mathsf{Best}_U(D)
\quad\land\quad
\mathsf{Ess}_F(U)\prec_F\mathsf{Act}(D).
\]

It needs all of the following:

1. a non-question-begging candidate space \(\mathcal D\);
2. a metric/order \(\mathsf{Best}_U\) that does not merely encode the actual \(D\);
3. proof that a unique maximum exists rather than ties, incomparability, or unbounded improvement;
4. explanation of why the goodness/value order is intrinsic to \(U\); and
5. a relation from bestness to realization.

Without those bridges, “the actual world is uniquely best” moves the selector debt into an unproved optimization claim.

## 6. Plenitude route

The strongest nondual/plenitude route is not:

\[
\forall x\,[\mathsf{Coherent}(x)\Rightarrow R(x)].
\]

It is:

\[
\forall a\,[
\mathsf{GenuineAspect}(a)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ContentOfSelfKnowledge}_G(a)
\Rightarrow
R(a)
].
\tag{P-int}
\]

The protected non-representational identity bridge validates the second implication internally. The exact burdens are upstream:

| Burden | Required condition |
|---|---|
| Domain | \(\mathcal A_{\rm genuine}\) cannot be defined by realization, coherence alone, grammar membership, or a gauge quotient |
| Exactness | Explain why the core’s actual finite articulation contains all and only the genuine contents |
| Compossibility | Incompatible finite contents require maximal proper contexts or another stated architecture, not joint possession in one finite mode |
| Non-composition | Aspect-content must not be treated as literal parts or changing states of the ultimate qua ultimate |
| Fidelity | A formal grammar needs a target-relative mapping, not ontological promotion by coherence |

A rich protected nondual core can assert real finite modes as its own content. That makes its non-vacuity conditional on the core, not a newly discovered selector. In a public SCI competition, it still must show why that core content and its exact genuine-aspect domain are self-intelligible rather than merely stipulated.

does not pass CI5 merely by eliminating contingent choice. It needs at least:

\]

\[

\Rightarrow

\]

\Rightarrow
R(a).
\]

These are substantive bridges. The proposal remains an important live candidate because it addresses selector debt directly. It is not yet the uniquely established survivor.

# Intrinsic-Nature Rival Comparison
## A non-numerical assessment matrix for consciousness and ultimate-nature hypotheses

## Scope and methodological constraint

The hard problem and the structural/phenomenal non-entailment result establish a limited point: a theory expressed only in structural/causal vocabulary does not by itself entail a phenomenal assignment without additional semantics or a bridge. They do **not** establish that awareness is fundamental, that physicalism is false, or that a necessary ground is aware.

This comparison asks which additional ontology and bridge each rival introduces, what it explains, and where its remaining debt lies. It deliberately gives no percentage credences. The source material’s numerical rankings lacked a shared reference class, likelihood model, calibration history, or stable evidence base.

---

## 1. Shared assessment criteria

Every proposal is evaluated against the same questions.

1. **Phenomenal adequacy:** Does it treat first-person presence as a datum to be explained rather than erase it by stipulation?
2. **Structural fit:** Can it retain the predictive and causal successes of physical science without adding unmotivated deviations?
3. **Bridge cost:** What connects physical/functional organisation to phenomenal presence, and is the bridge explicit?
4. **Individuation:** What makes this subject or perspective one rather than many, and separates it from others?
5. **Combination/decombination:** How do local and global experiential aspects relate, if either is posited?
6. **Causal compatibility:** Does experience add independent causal powers, identify with physical roles, or remain epiphenomenal—and what follows?
7. **Evidence and discrimination:** What observations, conceptual constraints, or formal consequences could favour the view over its rivals?
8. **Ultimate-nature relevance:** If a conditional ground exists, does the view give a non-circular reason to identify its intrinsic nature with awareness?

No proposal is required to solve every question immediately; but an unresolved question cannot be called solved by renaming it.

---

## 2. Rival families

### R1. Reductive physicalism / a posteriori identity

**Core claim.** Experiences are identical with physical or neural states, even though phenomenal and physical concepts have different cognitive roles.

**Genuine strength.** It preserves causal continuity and the success of physical explanation. A posteriori identity strategies show that a conceptual or epistemic gap does not automatically entail two substances or properties.

**Bridge/debt.** The view must say whether identity itself explains why the physical state is felt or only states a metaphysical identity. A phenomenal-concept strategy can illuminate why experience is cognitively distinctive while leaving the “why anything is like anything” question live. It also needs a criterion for which physical realisations are conscious that is not simply indexed to ordinary human cases.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** None follows directly. Even a complete physical identity theory does not decide whether a non-derivative ground exists or what its intrinsic nature is.

### R2. Functional identity / representationalism

**Core claim.** A sufficiently rich functional, causal, or representational organisation is constitutively identical with experience.

**Genuine strength.** It offers functional explanations of access, report, integration, memory, control, and behaviour, and it lends itself to computational and empirical modelling.

**Bridge/debt.** Functional organisation alone is structural unless an identity/constitutive bridge is made explicit. The central debt is not a lack of correlates, but why the organisation has phenomenal presence rather than only functional profile. The view must also handle absent/inverted-qualia style challenges or explain why they are incoherent under its theory of realisation.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** It supplies no direct route to awareness as the intrinsic nature of an ultimate ground. It may instead support a physical or structural base with a constitutive phenomenal law.

### R3. Illusionism

**Core claim.** Phenomenal properties as ordinarily conceived do not exist; introspective appearances, dispositions to judge, and reports generate the impression that they do.

**Genuine strength.** It sharply exposes the possibility that intuitions about qualia overreach what cognitive science needs to explain. It can retain ordinary functional science with minimal new ontology.

**Bridge/debt.** It owes an account of the datum it redescribes: why there seems to be an occurrence of pain, colour, or presence, and for whom it seems that way, without covertly reintroducing phenomenal character. Calling it a cognitive illusion does not by itself explain the appearance as opposed to a bare information-processing fact.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** It undercuts one motivation for awareness-first ontology but does not settle grounding or intrinsic nature.

### R4. Neutral or Russellian monism

**Core claim.** Physics captures dispositional/structural relations, while its categorical or intrinsic realisers are neither straightforwardly mental nor merely structural; they may have experiential potential or be neutral.

**Genuine strength.** It respects physical structure while taking the intrinsic-nature question seriously. It can make phenomenality less miraculous than brute emergence if the neutral base is independently characterised.

**Bridge/debt.** “Neutral” can become a placeholder unless the nature of the neutral base, its relation to experience, and the psychophysical bridge are specified. If local physical entities realise experience, a combination or subject-formation problem remains.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** It provides a competitor to pure-awareness grounding: the ultimate could have a non-phenomenal or non-identified intrinsic nature that supports both structure and experience. It blocks any inference from structural incompleteness directly to awareness.

### R5. Micropsychism / constitutive panpsychism

**Core claim.** At least some fundamental physical entities or processes have experiential aspects; complex consciousness is constituted from them.

**Genuine strength.** It resists brute emergence by making experientiality basic at a local level and may preserve an intimate structure–experience relation.

**Bridge/debt.** The combination problem is decisive: many micro-experiential units do not obviously compose into one unified subject. A theory must specify combination, subject-summing, boundary, and causal/structural relations rather than rely on numerical aggregation.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** It makes awareness widespread rather than uniquely ultimate. It therefore does not by itself favour a simple awareness-ground, and it may multiply primitive experiential bearers.

### R6. Cosmopsychism / global experiential base

**Core claim.** A cosmic or global experiential subject/aspect is fundamental; local subjects arise by restriction, partition, or derivative organisation.

**Genuine strength.** It reverses the combination direction and can make unity of a global experiential basis more natural than summing micro-subjects.

**Bridge/debt.** It inherits **decombination**: why bounded, ignorant, locally unified subjects arise from a global basis, what individuates them, and why public causal structure accompanies their boundaries. A restriction of accessible information is not yet a subject.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** It offers a nearer route to awareness-first ground theories, but still does not establish simplicity, necessary existence, goodness, or literal creation. It must earn its global subject rather than merely infer it from the hard problem.

### R7. Simple awareness-ground

**Core claim.** If a non-derivative simple ground exists, phenomenal awareness is its intrinsic nature; finite perspectives are dependent creation or derivative articulation.

**Genuine strength.** It promises one deep source for actuality, intrinsic nature, and phenomenal presence, and avoids beginning with many independent micro-subjects. It treats the hard problem as evidence against a purely structural ultimate base rather than as a physical anomaly.

**Bridge/debt.** It is a compound hypothesis: global ground stack, simplicity, intrinsic-nature bridge, awareness attribution, and finite-perspective/decombination theory. It must not inherit the status of the ground argument without independently defending these extra links. Its decombination, creation/non-duality, causal-compatibility, and suffering debts are especially demanding.

**Ultimate-nature relevance.** This is the only family that directly identifies the ground with awareness, but it receives no deduction from structural non-entailment alone. Its success is comparative and abductive.

## 3. Comparison ledger

| Family | What it handles well | Principal open bridge | Main pressure point | Consequence for awareness-ground |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 physical identity | Causal/empirical continuity; concept-gap restraint | Identity-to-felt-presence | Why identity is explanatory rather than stipulative | Does not entail or refute it |
| R2 functional identity | Access, report, control, implementation | Function-to-phenomenality | Structural description may be underdetermining | Does not entail it |
| R3 illusionism | Ontological economy; cognitive modelling | Appearance without covert phenomenality | “For whom/what is it like?” regress | Removes one motivation if successful |
| R4 neutral/Russellian | Structural realism plus intrinsic nature | Neutral-base-to-experience | Neutrality can be a label | Direct competitor |
| R5 micropsychism | Avoids wholly non-experiential emergence | Combination | Subject-summing/boundaries | Alternative to one awareness source |
| R6 cosmopsychism | Global unity without summing | Decombination | Finite subject formation | Close competitor |
| R7 simple awareness-ground | Unifies ground and phenomenal-base hypotheses | All of ground→awareness→finite-subject chain | Independent support and decombination | Proposal under assessment |

The right conclusion is a research discipline, not a score: a simple awareness-ground gains only if its extra links jointly constrain phenomena better and more economically than R1–R6, without exporting its difficult work into undefined “perspective,” “expression,” or “mystery.”

---

## 4. Discriminating work rather than rhetoric

The rivals require different kinds of progress.

- **R1/R2:** state an identity or constitutive criterion, test its generalisation and invariance, and explain why rival structural twins receive the same or different phenomenal verdict.
- **R3:** provide a non-question-begging theory of introspective appearance that does not merely substitute one phenomenal term for another.
- **R4:** formalise the neutral realiser and its bridge conditions; compare its primitive inventory with alternatives.
- **R5:** provide a mathematically and metaphysically explicit subject-combination rule.
- **R6/R7:** provide a decombination/individuation map covering access, memory, unity through time, overlap, public coordination, and causal compatibility.
- **R7 specifically:** show why the ground’s intrinsic nature is awareness rather than a neutral realiser or a fundamental psychophysical law, and keep that case separate from the conditional argument for a ground.

The existing D/I/S/L/P and \(\mathbf E_\Theta\) programme can contribute only to the carrier/individuation side of R6/R7. Even if successful, it would not independently establish phenomenal sufficiency or an awareness-ground; those are additional bridge claims.

---

## Consolidated verdict

The a-posteriori identity challenge supplies a durable negative constraint: conceptual explanation of the appearance of a gap is not obviously a complete explanation of phenomenal presence. But it does not select a winning metaphysics. The strongest present result is a transparent comparison map: every candidate must price its phenomenality bridge, its subject-formation account, and its causal compatibility. The simple awareness-ground remains a serious but highly composite abductive hypothesis, not the output of the ground stack or a completed theory of consciousness.

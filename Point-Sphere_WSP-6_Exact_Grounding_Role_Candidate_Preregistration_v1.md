## 1. Nonliteral Point–Sphere and weak simplicity

The Point is the nonspatial designation of the single ultimate bearer \(G\). The Sphere is the nonspatial designation of a closed field of intrinsic determinations of that bearer. Neither term introduces geometry, an external container, a second realm, temporal succession, or world-production.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{PointSphereDesignation}(G,\Sigma_G)
\not\Rightarrow{}&
\mathsf{SpatialGeometry}
\lor
\mathsf{ExternalContainer}
\lor
\mathsf{SecondRealm}\\
&\lor
\mathsf{TemporalProduction}
\lor
\mathsf{ActualFiniteWorld}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSP6-Nonliteral-Scope}
\]

Weak simplicity is retained only as a no-parts/no-independent-constituents condition; it does not entail internal formal differentiation:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{WeakSimple}_6(G)
\iff{}&
\mathsf{OneUltimateBearer}(G)
\land
\neg\exists x\,\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(x,G)\\
&\land

\land
\neg\mathsf{SpatiallyExtended}(G)\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{TemporallyProduced}(G),

\tag{WSP6-Weak-Simplicity}

## 2. Exact intrinsic grounding frames

The boundary test begins from an explanatory frame rather than an entity/mode type. An intrinsic grounding frame has the form:

\[
\mathcal X
=
\langle
G,\varphi,\Gamma
\rangle,
\tag{Grounding-Frame}
\]

where \(G\) is the bearer under discussion, \(\varphi\) is an intrinsic formal attribution of \(G\), and \(\Gamma\) is a proposed exact ground-description of that attribution. The frame is metalogical: it is not a public truth/error interface, an actual finite effect, or an additional object in the ontology.

\[
\mathsf{GroundFrame}(\mathcal X)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PublicTruthError}
\lor
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}
\lor
\mathsf{PhenomenalContent}
\lor
\mathsf{PhysicalRecovery}.
\tag{GroundFrame-Scope-Limit}
\]

The primitive \(\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\mathcal X)\) means only that the frame aims to state what is explanatorily sufficient for the intrinsic attribution **without adding or omitting an explanatory relatum**. It has no implication of a selected field or a public verification procedure:

\[
\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\mathcal X)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{FieldSelection}
\lor
\mathsf{PublicVerification}.
\tag{Exactness-Scope-Limit}
\]

## 3. Role-first classification grammar

### 3.1 Subject-role and qualifier-role

A candidate \(x\) plays a **subject-role** in a grounding frame only if it can occur as the bearer of a separately formulable intrinsic attribution within an exact grounding description:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(x,\mathcal X)
\iff{}&
\exists\psi,\Gamma_x\,[
\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\langle x,\psi,\Gamma_x\rangle)\\
&\hspace{37mm}\land
\mathsf{OccursAsBearer}(x,\langle x,\psi,\Gamma_x\rangle)].
\end{aligned}
\tag{Subject-Role}
\]

A candidate \(d\) plays a **qualifier-role** in a frame for \(G\) only if it determines *how \(G\) is intrinsically attributed* while not occupying the bearer position in that frame:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G,\mathcal X)
\iff{}&
\mathcal X=\langle G,\varphi,\Gamma\rangle
\land
\mathsf{DeterminationParameter}(d,\Gamma)\\
&\land
\mathsf{NoneliminableFor}(d,\varphi,G,\Gamma)
\land
\neg\mathsf{OccursAsBearer}(d,\mathcal X).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Qualifier-Role}
\]

\(\mathsf{NoneliminableFor}\) has a strictly explanatory reading: deleting \(d\) from the proposed ground-description no longer states the same intrinsic attribution of \(G\). It does not quantify over a distinct possible-world domain or an actual finite effect:

\[
\mathsf{NoneliminableFor}(d,\varphi,G,\Gamma)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentPossibilityDomain}(d)
\lor
\mathsf{DifferentFiniteEffect}(d).
\tag{Noneliminability-Scope-Limit}
\]

### 3.2 Exact Role Discipline

The candidate's new independent principle is a general discipline for ontology from explanatory form:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\langle G,\varphi,\Gamma\rangle)
\land
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G,\langle G,\varphi,\Gamma\rangle)\\
&\land
\neg\exists\mathcal Y\,\mathsf{SubjectRole}(d,\mathcal Y)\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{LicenseEntityAddition}(d).
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{Exact-Role-Discipline}
\]

The rationale is domain-general. A distinct item is not added to ontology merely because a predicate or explanatory parameter appears in a sentence. Entity-addition needs an explanatory subject-role of its own; a noneliminable qualifier can be real as a way the bearer is without automatically becoming a separate countable relatum.

\[
\mathsf{PredicateOrParameterOccurrence}(d)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EntityAddition}(d).
\tag{No-Grammatical-Reification}
\]

The discipline is not a claim that all qualifiers are real or that all entities are independent. It regulates only when a distinct entity-like node is licensed from an explanatory role:

\[
\mathsf{ExactRoleDiscipline}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AllQualifiersAreReal}
\lor
\mathsf{AllDependentItemsAreNonentities}.
\tag{ERD-Restricted-Scope}
\]

### 3.3 Node and immanent-mode criteria derived from roles

A **constitutive node** is now defined from role capacity, rather than by membership in a preassigned entity-like sort:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(x)
\iff{}&
\exists\mathcal X\,[
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(x,\mathcal X)\\
&\land
\mathsf{ConstitutionallyCountable}(x,\mathcal X)
\land
\mathsf{EligibleConstituentIn}(x,\mathcal X)].
\end{aligned}
\tag{Role-Derived-Node}
\]

A real immanent mode is not defined as a member of a contrast class. It is defined affirmatively as an intrinsic, noneliminable qualifier grounded through the bearer:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6(d,G)
\iff{}&
\exists\mathcal X\,[
\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\mathcal X)
\land
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G,\mathcal X)\\
&\land
\mathsf{IntrinsicDetermination}(d,G,\mathcal X)
\land
\mathsf{GroundedThroughBearer}(G,d,\mathcal X)].
\end{aligned}
\tag{Role-Derived-Mode}
\]

Neither definition invokes the other's type label, parthood, composition, numerical entity plurality, or the Point–Sphere designation:

\[
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6
\notin
\mathsf{Definiens}(\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6),
\qquad
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6
\notin
\mathsf{Definiens}(\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6).
\tag{No-Type-First-Circularity}
\]

## 4. Proposed independent E/M boundary bridge

The candidate does not infer nonnode status from bearer dependence. Instead, it argues conditionally from a role result: when an intrinsic determination has a noneliminable qualifier-role in the exact ground of a bearer attribution and lacks every separately formulable subject-role, Exact Role Discipline blocks entity addition.

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6(d,G)
\land
\neg\exists\mathcal Y\,\mathsf{SubjectRole}(d,\mathcal Y)\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{LicenseEntityAddition}(d)
\land
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(d).
\end{aligned}
\tag{Role-Boundary-Bridge}
\]

The proposed Q2 implication is then restricted but direct:

\[
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6(d,G)
\land
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(d)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ProperPartOf}(d,G).
\tag{WSP6-Nonpartitivity-Bridge}
\]

A dependent trope or nonseparable constituent is not ruled out merely because it depends on \(G\). It remains a candidate node if it has a separately formulable subject-role and is constitutionally countable:

\[
\mathsf{DependentTropeOrConstituent}(t,G)
\land
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(t,\mathcal Y)
\land
\mathsf{ConstitutionallyCountable}(t,\mathcal Y)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(t).
\tag{Dependent-Node-Rival-Admitted}
\]

Thus the proposal distinguishes the category by an explanatory-role test, not by its dependence relation, its name, or an initial sort allocation:

\[
\mathsf{EssentialBearerDependence}(d,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(d).
\tag{No-Dependence-Shortcut}
\]

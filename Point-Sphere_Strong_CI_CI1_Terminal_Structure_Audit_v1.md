# Strong CI Adjudication — CI1 Terminal-Structure Audit v1
## What strongest CI excludes, and what it does not select

## Status

**CI1 complete as a terminal-architecture typing audit.** It does not identify the correct framework. It establishes the exact structural consequence of applying SCI uniformly to ultimate explanatory architectures.

## 1. Question

Does strongest complete intelligibility force one absolutely simple self-explanatory ground, or does it only require that every ultimate explanatory structure supply a non-brute closure account?

The audit uses CI0B’s distinction between formal logic, explanation, grounding, cross-model necessity, and core-relative intrinsic necessity.

## 2. Failure witness

For a candidate framework \(F\), an SCI failure must identify:

\[
\mathsf{Residue}_F(p)
:=
\mathsf{Ultimate}_F(p)
\land
\neg\exists e\,[
\mathsf{Expl}_F(e,p)
\land
\mathsf{NonCircular}_F(e)
\land
\mathsf{Admissible}_F(e)
].
\]

A framework fails U1/U2 only when a particular ultimate fact, structure, relation, or difference is shown to meet this condition. It does not fail merely because it is labelled “infinite,” “plural,” “circular,” “necessary,” or “nondual.”

## 3. Terminal architecture matrix

| Architecture | What SCI excludes | What still needs independent demonstration | CI1 status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bare terminal fact | A fundamental fact asserted without account | Nothing further: this is brute by the adopted SCI standard | Eliminated **conditional on SCI** |
| Unexplained explanatory regress | A chain whose whole has no non-circular explanation | Whether the particular infinite chain has an intrinsic/global principle explaining it | Eliminated only when an unexplained-whole witness is shown |
| Unexplained explanatory circle | A circular pattern whose obtaining is merely asserted | Whether an overarching nature/law explains the pattern without reproducing it | Eliminated only when the circle itself is residue |
| Self-intelligible one-ground architecture | Nothing by label | What makes the claimed intrinsic nature explanatory rather than a renamed terminal fact | Held: a live closure type |
| Necessary holistic totality | Nothing by label | Why the totality/nature is self-intelligible rather than primitive; how internal differences are grounded | Held: a live closure type |
| Plural ultimate roots | Nothing by plurality alone | Why there are several roots, their distinction, and their relation; whether one common nature reduces them to another type | Held: high burden, not a formal contradiction |
| Principled infinite structure | Nothing by infinity alone | Why the global rule/pattern obtains and explains the series non-circularly | Held: a live closure type if a non-brute global account is supplied |
| Nondual immanent-aspect structure | Nothing by denying a second ultimate | How real finite differentiation and genuine aspecthood are non-composite and non-arbitrary | Held: a live closure type |

## 4. Exact result

The only direct structural theorem licensed by SCI is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}(F)
\Rightarrow
\neg\exists p\;\mathsf{Residue}_F(p).
}
\tag{CI1-R}
\]

This is a framework-relative adequacy condition. It does **not** entail:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\exists! G\,[
\mathsf{One}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Simple}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Aware}(G)
].
\tag{CI1-NE1}
\]

Nor does it entail:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Creation},
\qquad
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Emanation},
\qquad
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AspectIdentity}.
\tag{CI1-NE2}
\]

The leading positive structural requirement is deliberately more abstract:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Any SCI-passing architecture must make its ultimate explanatory closure intelligible without a primitive residue.}
}
\tag{CI1-C}
\]

A one-ground account is one possible way to meet this requirement; it is not selected merely by writing down CI.

## 5. Why the standard terminal taxonomy is not exhaustive

The transcript’s taxonomy—brute terminal, infinite regress, circle, self-grounding terminal—conflates architecture type with explanatory success. Necessary totality, nondual aspect identity, principled infinitism, and plural-root accounts can each be brute or non-brute depending on their offered explanation map.

For example:

- an infinite series plus the bare claim “the series necessarily exists” fails if necessity is merely a terminal label;
- an infinite series with a genuinely articulated self-intelligible structural principle is not rejected because of infinity, though the principle faces CI2/CI3 scrutiny;
- a single simple ground fails if its alleged simplicity, existence, or productive relation is simply declared;
- a nondual appearance account fails if it merely names finite appearances without a genuine-aspect and non-composition account, but not because real aspects are formally parts.

## 6. Relation to anti-regress and self-intelligibility

The strongest defensible anti-regress result is:

\[
\text{unexplained regress}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{SCI}.
\]

It is not:

\[
\text{infinite structure}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{SCI}.
\]

Similarly, “self-intelligible” is not literal reflexive grounding. It is a candidate claim that a nature or structure makes its own ultimate profile intelligible without an external ultimate source. CI1 retains it as a required **burden-bearing closure role**, not as a passed explanation.

## 7. Implications for the requested correct framework

At CI1, the correct framework cannot yet be named. The following claims are justified only conditionally:

1. If SCI is accepted, bare ultimate facts and unexplained whole-regresses are excluded.
2. A candidate must articulate an ultimate closure profile rather than merely stop at an unexplained fact.
3. Whether that profile is a simple aware ground, necessary totality, nondual absolute, structural principle, or another architecture is deferred to CI2–CI5.

This is a real narrowing, not a triviality: any framework that declines the need to explain its ultimate differentiators, laws, realization rules, or totality cannot pass the stipulated strongest standard.

## 8. Next node

**CI2 — intrinsic-nature audit** will test the transcript’s central bridge:

\[
\text{self-intelligibility / intelligibility}
\Rightarrow
\text{absolute simplicity, awareness, intellect, goodness, and volition}.
\]

It must test these components separately against neutral, structural, non-attributive, and personal rivals. No conclusion about creation, emanation, appearance, or plenitude is licensed before that audit.

## Nonclaims

CI1 does not defend brute facts, validate an infinite regress, show that a plural or structural architecture succeeds, reject an ultimate ground, prove CI, select a framework, or change any protected core. It types burdens and prevents terminal labels from doing unearned explanatory work.

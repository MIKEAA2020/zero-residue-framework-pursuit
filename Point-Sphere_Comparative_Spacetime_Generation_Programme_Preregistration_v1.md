**Status:** Programme preregistration only. This is not a new Point–Sphere metaphysical candidate and does not amend, reopen, or transfer results from any prior candidate. It fixes a common test architecture under which future fresh candidates may be compared.

**User-selected terminology:** Point–Sphere, not Point–Circle.

**User-selected first recovery target:** spacetime first. The immediate objective is a noncircular recovery of a manifold-like causal/metric structure, not yet gravity, quantum matter, or full empirical physics.

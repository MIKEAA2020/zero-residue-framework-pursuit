# Ṣadrian Public-Effects Bridge Test v1
## Application of the preregistered PE0–PE7 docket

## Preregistration and scope

This document applies, without changing, the fixed docket and outcome rules in:

`Point-Sphere_Sadrian_Public_Effects_Bridge_Protocol_v1.md`

The protocol was frozen before this application. This result concerns only the proposed publicization route:

\[
\mathsf{PEI}_{S}(x;\mathcal P).
\]

It is **not** a verdict that Ṣadrā’s ontology is false, that intensive existence is incoherent, that direct witnessing is irrational, that every Ṣadrian claim is private, or that A0 is unsatisfiable.

## 1. Source facts held fixed

The direct *Mashāʿir* collation supplies the following relevant resources:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
M_1 &: \text{extra-mental existence is associated with effects and consequences (¶23);}\
M_2 &: \text{existence individuates (¶5; ¶27);}\
M_3 &: \text{finite specification can involve rank or subsequent features (¶81);}\
M_4 &: \text{intensive difference is expressed through priority/posteriority, completion/deficiency,}\
&\quad\text{independence/need, and strength/weakness (¶¶81, 86, 91);}\
M_5 &: \text{knowledge of the particularity of a mode is tied to direct witnessing of its cause (¶92).}
\end{array}
\]

The test grants all five as real source resources. It asks the narrower question whether a publicly stated pattern of effects can do the work that \(M_5\) otherwise places under direct witnessing.

## 2. The crucial distinction

The source allows a necessary marker:

\[
\mathsf{ExtraMental}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EffectsAndConsequences}_{S}(x).
\]

The proposed bridge needs much more:

\[
\mathsf{PublicTrace}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ExtraMentalFiniteDegree}_{S}(x)
\land\mathsf{PubliclyDeterminedRank}_{S}(x).
\]

No such converse, rank rule, or application procedure was recovered in the collated text. The audit therefore tests whether the strengthened rule in the protocol can supply it. A necessary marker is not silently upgraded to a sufficient public admission test.

## 4. Why PE2 is decisive for this bridge

The focused hypothesis required public effects to do **two** jobs:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
J_1 &: \text{distinguish a candidate from a merely conceptual/fictional item};\\
J_2 &: \text{locate that candidate in Ṣadrian intensive order.}
\end{array}
\]

The source gives serious help with \(J_1\): effects and consequences are associated with external existence. Even here, PE1 shows that the effect must be assigned to the right ontological item rather than to an associated mental act.

The bridge fails more clearly at \(J_2\): a public effect profile does not itself say whether its candidate is prior or posterior, more complete or deficient, independent or needy, strong or weak **in the Ṣadrian existential sense**. Inferring that rank requires the relevant causal/perfection order; using that order to admit the candidate is the circle the bridge was meant to break.

Formally, the available source support is:

\[
\mathsf{ExtraMental}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EffectsAndConsequences}_{S}(x),
\]

not:

\[
\mathsf{PublicEffectsProfile}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Degree}_{S}(x)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SadrianFiniteMode}(x).
\]

The missing implication is not supplied by multiplying effects, making them publicly observable, or replacing a qualitative relation with a numerical proxy. Those moves would violate the fixed protocol.

## 5. Direct witnessing: exact bearing

The result does not infer blanket privacy from paragraph 92. Rather, after PE2–PE7 fail to publicize rank and individual particularity, paragraph 92 becomes a targeted source-grounded pressure:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{if a candidate’s particular mode/rank is not fixed by the public dossier, and}\\
&\text{the text directs knowledge of that particularity to direct witnessing of its cause,}\\
&\text{then the proposed effects route has not replaced the first-personal/presential burden.}
\end{aligned}
\]

This supports \(F_7\) **as a residual pressure**, not as the principal inference. The principal failures are \(F_3\), \(F_4\), \(F_5\), and \(F_6\), each visible even before any general thesis about private knowledge is asserted.

## 6. Fixed-outcome adjudication

The protocol required success on every PE0–PE7. That condition is not met.

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{PublicEffectsBridgeResult}_{S}
&=\text{targeted bridge failure};\\
\mathsf{FailureBasis}
&=\{F_2,F_3,F_4,F_5,F_6\},\\
\mathsf{F_7}&=\text{a source-grounded residual pressure, not a blanket premise.}
\end{aligned}
}
\]

The exact result is:

\[
\mathsf{PEI}_{S}(x;\mathcal P)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicatedActualFiniteMode}(x)
\quad\text{on the collated source and fixed dossier conditions.}
\]

It is not:

\[
\neg\mathsf{SadrianOntology}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\neg\exists C\,\mathsf{A0Pass}(C).
\]

## 7. What this changes

### 7.1 The Ṣadrian result

The targeted public-effects escape has now been tested and exhausted under its stated expiry rule. The broader Ṣadrian intensive route remains a protected residual because:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm Sadra}\subseteq\mathcal K_{\rm new}
\]

has not been reduced to prior criterion families, and the source continues to supply substantive actuality, individuation, gradation, causal-dependence, and mental/extra-mental resources.

The revised status is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Sadrian intensive actuality: substantively articulated; public-effects externalization fails;}
\text{ any other public bridge remains unrecovered, not disproved.}
}
\]

### 7.2 Programme priority

The predeclared expiry has a methodological consequence. Since the one focused public-effects route did not cross the gate, the A0 meta-track should now become the **primary inquiry priority**, while the protected residual class remains open.

\[
\mathsf{Priority}_{\rm programme}:
\quad
\text{constructive bridge search}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{primary meta-inquiry},
\]

subject to this restraint:

\[
\mathsf{MetaPriority}
\not\Rightarrow
H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}.
\]

The result provides a limited additional datum for \(H_{\rm conv}\): even the strongest newly tested attempt to publicize intensive actuality through effects encountered a recurrent admission/invariance problem. It does not establish exhaustiveness, collapse Ṣadrā into another tradition, or transform the conditional no-go schema into a theorem.

## 8. Expiry recorded

The protocol’s one allowed PE0–PE7 application is complete. No modified public-effects formula, further effects example, or relaxed version of \(\mathsf{PEI}_{S}\) is authorized by this result.

A later reopening requires the protocol’s stated threshold: a newly recovered, passage-level primary-text rule giving an independently applicable public condition for rank, individuation, or error.

## Nonclaim

This test does not say that effects never provide evidence, that first-person knowledge has no epistemic standing, that Ṣadrā lacks a criterion of intensity, that the public A0 gate has been refuted, that the ten historical dossiers are exhaustive, or that the correct framework has been identified.

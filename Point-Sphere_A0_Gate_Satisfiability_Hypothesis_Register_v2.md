# A0 Gate Satisfiability — Hypothesis Register v2
## Predeclared test conditions for the Rāmānuja / Viśiṣṭādvaita reconstruction

## Status and continuity

This supersedes v1 as the active A0 meta-inquiry register. The A0 gate is under examination, not under revision. This is a preregistration of how the next source result will be classified; it is **not** a finding about Rāmānuja.

The gate remains:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{A0Pass}(C)\iff
&\mathsf{Substantive}(C)
\land\mathsf{NonCircular}(C)
\land\mathsf{NoSelectorResidue}(C)\\
&\land\mathsf{ProfileInvariant}(C)
\land\mathsf{TypeDiscriminating}(C)
\land\mathsf{PubliclyAdjudicable}(C).
\end{aligned}
\]

“Public” does not mean vocabulary-free or acceptable to every tradition. A candidate may use internal vocabulary, but its truth conditions may not be fixed merely by acceptance of the candidate’s desired ontology:

\[
\mathsf{Genuine}(m)\not\iff
\text{“}m\text{ belongs to the inventory affirmed by the framework.”}
\]

Framework-internal assent, even unanimous assent, is not an A0 substitute unless paired with a non-stipulative, cross-candidate adjudication burden.

## 1. The exact live hypotheses

The strong formal-satisfiability hypothesis remains:

\[
H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}:\quad
\neg\exists C\;\mathsf{A0Pass}(C)
\]

for public determination of genuine finite metaphysical modehood and actuality.

A weaker, observational research hypothesis is distinct:

\[
H_{\rm conv}:\quad
\text{across diverse attempted criteria, the decisive A0 failure recurs structurally.}
\]

Evidence can incrementally support \(H_{\rm conv}\). It cannot establish
\(H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}\) without an exhaustive class and theorem. Neither hypothesis is a premise of the Rāmānuja reading.

## 2. The comparative diagnostic, carefully stated

The completed reconstructions currently identify non-identical criterion types:

\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\text{Proclus} &:& \text{dependence / proper-effect schema};\\
\text{Advaita} &:& \text{local status / sublation criterion};\\
\text{Hegel} &:& \text{logical-conceptual derivation criterion};\\
\text{Leibniz} &:& \text{conditional identity / individuation criterion}.
\end{array}
\]

The live diagnostic question is whether a synthesis can supply a further criterion of **actual membership**:

\[
\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(m),
\]

rather than silently treating dependence, status, derivability, or individuation as sufficient for actuality. It remains an open question whether all failures eventually prove to occur at one structural location.

## 3. Why Rāmānuja is the next discriminating test

The Rāmānuja case is selected because its *śarīra–śarīrī* (body–indwelling-self) proposal is expressly offered as a synthesis of reality, plurality, unity, and dependence. The primary materials formulate bodyhood through support, control, and subordination-for-purpose; individual selves and the non-sentient world are described as Brahman’s real body/modes.

The strongest charitable target to reconstruct is therefore:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{Body}_{R}(x,G)\iff
&\mathsf{Substance}_{R}(x)\land\mathsf{Sentient}_{R}(G)\\
&\land\mathsf{CompletelySupported}_{R}(G,x)
\land\mathsf{CompletelyControlled}_{R}(G,x)\\
&\land\mathsf{WhollySubordinateFor}_{R}(x,G).
\end{aligned}
\]

The formula is a reconstruction aid, not a substitute for the Sanskrit source, and it must not be read as a claim that Rāmānuja gives a merely formal or framework-neutral relation.

The question is not whether the tradition asserts that every real finite thing is Brahman’s body. It is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can this relation determine genuine finite membership and actuality}\
\text{without first presupposing the Viśiṣṭādvaita roster of substances, selves, matter,}\
\text{Brahman, and divine purposes?}
}
\]

## 5. Predeclared adversarial battery

The source dossier must give a criterion-relative answer before any overall verdict.

| Test | Required question for \(\mathsf{Body}_{R}\) |
|---|---|
| T0: no finite candidate | Does the system permit a bodyless or finite-empty state, and is its answer derived rather than stipulated? |
| T1: one candidate | Can it classify that token as actual body/mode rather than mere property, concept, or alleged substance without importing it into the world inventory? |
| T2: symmetric pair | Does the body relation decide whether there are two real finite items, one item, or an indeterminate orbit without a name, karmic history, or other undeclared selector? |
| T3: property candidate | Does it distinguish an attribute/property of Brahman from a real finite body/mode without simply saying that actual finite things are bodies? |
| T4: independent candidate | Does it have public truth conditions for non-independence, and does refusal of bodyhood avoid a merely verbal charge of dualism? |
| T5: conservative re-encoding | Is the body verdict preserved under a common declared extractor, not merely under translations already judged Vedic or orthodox? |
| T6: extra token | Can it decide whether a proposed additional finite item is actual and belongs without expanding the roster, adding a divine decree, or presupposing an unlisted relation? |

Two further tests are fixed:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
R_1 &: \text{Profile test: declare a shared extractor or explain an equivalent public structure.}\\
R_2 &: \text{Matched-rival test: state what would differ if the same profile were classified}\
&\text{by a rival dependence-only, property-only, or independent-substance account.}
\end{array}
\]

\(R_2\) does not assume the prior CFD rival packet is relevant; it requires a new semantic rival specification. Absence of an operationalized rival means no rival-defeat conclusion, not automatic failure of the source.

## 6. Outcome taxonomy, fixed in advance

### Satisfiability evidence — a limited A0 semantic pass

The Rāmānuja criterion counts as positive evidence that A0 is satisfiable **in principle** only if it supplies, for a clearly delimited scope:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{explicit truth conditions for bodyhood, support, control, purpose/subordination,}\
&\text{actuality, and the relevant types;}\\
&\text{noncircular determinate verdicts for T0--T6;}\\
&\text{a representation-conservative profile account;}\\
&\text{a way for the criterion to be wrong that does not presuppose the intended inventory;}\\
&\text{and a matched-rival contrast once a comparable rival is formulated.}
\end{aligned}
\]

A pass would show:

\[
\exists C\;\mathsf{A0Pass}_{\rm scoped}(C),
\]

and thus defeat \(H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}\) as stated. It would **not** show that Viśiṣṭādvaita is true, that all finite things are actually divine modes, or that phenomenal and physical adequacy has been established.

### Structural A0 failure — evidence for convergence, not a no-go

The Rāmānuja criterion counts as a *structural* A0 failure only if the failure belongs to the criterion as reconstructed, for example if it must:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
S_1 &: \text{assume }\mathsf{Substance}_{R}(x)\text{ or the actual roster before bodyhood can begin};\\
S_2 &: \text{presuppose divine support/control/purpose while using them to establish membership};\\
S_3 &: \text{use a deity, scripture, karmic history, or global inventory as an unannounced selector};\\
S_4 &: \text{alter the verdict under a declared conservative profile re-encoding};\\
S_5 &: \text{collapse real finite body/mode into a property, or into a separately independent substance};\\
S_6 &: \text{lack a public actuality condition distinguishable from the relation of dependence.}
\end{array}
\]

A structural failure increments support for \(H_{\rm conv}\), especially because this source attempts the proposed synthesis. It does not establish \(H_{\rm A0}^{\rm strong}\).

### Inconclusive result

The result is inconclusive if apparent failure instead follows from translation uncertainty, incomplete source access, disputed attribution, a merely internal inconsistency, or a burden belonging only to an optional version of the tradition. Inconclusive results do not count for or against either hypothesis.

## 7. Primary-track activation rule

If, and only if, the Rāmānuja dossier records a **structural** rather than source-specific A0 failure, the meta-inquiry becomes the primary next work package. “Primary” then means:

\[
\text{construct an explicit scope class, candidate taxonomy, exhaustiveness strategy, and escape audit.}
\]

It does **not** mean announce unsatisfiability, stop all constructive work, or replace A0 with framework-internal checkability.

If the dossier records an A0 pass, constructive work continues with the pass’s unclosed compossibility, actuality, phenomenal, and physical obligations. If it is inconclusive, candidate diversification continues without treating the case as confirmation.

## 8. What a genuine no-go would still require

Even a broad pattern across emanationist, sublationist, dialectical, monadological, and synthetic body-relation candidates is evidential rather than demonstrative. A scoped no-go needs:

\[
\forall C\in\mathcal C\,[
\mathsf{PublicModeCriterion}(C)\to
(S_1\vee S_2\vee S_3\vee S_4\vee S_5\vee S_6)
],
\]

together with an independently defended claim that \(\mathcal C\) exhausts the relevant criterion forms. The required escape audit must consider at least emergent, structural, modal, processual, and non-historical candidate families.

## Nonclaim

This register neither identifies a correct framework nor diagnoses A0 as unsatisfiable. It fixes the next comparison so that a success, a structural failure, and an inconclusive source result have different and non-post-hoc consequences.

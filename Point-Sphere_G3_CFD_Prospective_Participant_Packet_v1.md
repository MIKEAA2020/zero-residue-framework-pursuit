# G3/CFD Prospective Participant Packet v1
## Draft neutral data-collection instrument — no participant data included

## Use condition

**This is a draft collection instrument, not authorization to collect human-subject data. It must be reviewed for consent, privacy, data protection, and any applicable institutional/ethical requirements before deployment.**

This packet implements the frozen G3/CFD preregistration without mentioning CFD, nonduality, participation, metaphysics, graph theory, aspecthood, or predicted results.

## 1. Neutral participant information

### Study purpose

This study asks about brief everyday experiences close to the time they occur. It does not test intelligence, personality, spirituality, mental health, or philosophical beliefs. There are no right or wrong descriptions.

### Participation

At randomly prompted moments, you will be asked to describe what was present in your experience immediately before the prompt. You may skip any prompt or stop at any time. Do not report information you prefer not to share.

### Privacy

Use a participant code rather than a name. The project should collect only the minimum data needed for the study. Any deployment must state where records are stored, who can access them, and how long they are retained.

### Safety

Do not respond while driving, operating machinery, in danger, or unable to respond safely. Skip the prompt and resume only when safe.

## 2. Random-prompt procedure

Each participant receives a neutral random prompt from an independently configured scheduler. The scheduler must be set before enrollment and may not adapt its timing based on report content.

At each safe prompt, the participant should:

1. note the episode identifier supplied by the scheduler;
2. describe the moment immediately before the prompt began;
3. complete the structured clarification form without revising the raw narrative; and
4. submit the two records separately.

The data-collection operator may not select prompts because an episode seems theoretically interesting.

# Zero-Residue Model-Regime and Actuality-Bridge Protocol v1
## Prospective candidate-neutral control after the WPI-3 finite-certificate failure

**Preconditions:** `Point-Sphere_Zero_Residue_Constructive_Admission_Docket_v1.md` and `Point-Sphere_WPI-3_Z0_Typing_and_Nonvacuity_Execution_v1.md`  
**Status:** Fresh methodological preregistration; unexecuted.  
**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Purpose:** Fix model-regime, actuality, realization, and truth/error requirements prospectively for any later constructive candidate. It introduces no new ontology, does not repair WPI-3, and does not rank strict pure-negation, weak positive-immanence, or any other core.

---

## 0. Restricted problem

The zero-residue target is:

\[
\mathsf{Goal}_{\rm ZR!}:
\qquad
\exists![F]_{\simeq}\,\mathsf{ZeroResidue}(F).
\tag{ZR!}
\]

A candidate cannot approach this target while leaving any of the following ambiguous:

\[
\mathsf{ModelRegime},
\qquad
\mathsf{ActualityBridge},
\qquad
\mathsf{RealizationBridge},
\qquad
\mathsf{TruthErrorBridge}.
\tag{Upstream}
\]

This protocol controls those upstream requirements only. It does not adjudicate phenomenality, normativity, evil, freedom, or general empirical physics; later batteries address those only after the present gates close.

\mathsf{Z0Result}(F_{\rm WPI\text{-}3})

\tag{WPI3-Closed}

\[

## 2. Frozen model regimes

Every later candidate must choose exactly one regime before its grammar, closure, or battery fixtures are encoded:

\[
\mathsf{MR}(F)
\in
\{
\mathsf{FiniteFull},
\mathsf{FiniteLocalGlobal},
\mathsf{ExplicitInfinite}
\}.
\tag{Regime-Choice}
\]

### 2.1 \(\mathsf{FiniteFull}\)

A finite-full candidate asserts that all sorts, primitive extensions relevant to the candidate, and the generated closure are finite in its stated witness.

It must provide:

\[
\langle
\text{all carrier sets},
\text{all constants},
\text{all primitive extensions},
\text{all closure stages},
\text{satisfaction checks}
\rangle.
\tag{FiniteFull-Cert}
\]

A diagram alone is insufficient.

### 2.2 \(\mathsf{FiniteLocalGlobal}\)

A finite-local/global candidate may have an explicitly infinite global theory or model, but every battery case must identify a finite local fragment \(L_b\) and a declared embedding:

\[
\iota_b:L_b\hookrightarrow\mathfrak M_F.
\tag{Embedding}
\]

It must prove or state as a frozen bridge condition:

\[
\mathsf{LocalVerdict}(L_b)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{GlobalVerdict}(\mathfrak M_F,b)
\tag{Local-Global}
\]

for each verdict the battery uses. The candidate must also state whether the required property is preserved under extension, restriction, or directed union. A finite fragment may not be treated as the entire actual universe merely because it is testable.

### 2.3 \(\mathsf{ExplicitInfinite}\)

An explicit-infinite candidate must specify:

\[
\langle
\text{base logic/set or type theory},
\text{sort cardinalities},
\text{global closure operator},
\text{existence theorem or model},
\text{cardinality of generated closure},
\text{local test-slice map}
\rangle.
\tag{Infinite-Cert}
\]

It must give an actual model or a proof of model existence in the stated background logic, rather than pointing only to a grammar with infinitely many names.

For every finite battery case \(b\), it must declare a finite slice:

\[
\mathsf{Slice}_F(b)
\subseteq
\mathfrak M_F,
\tag{Slice}
\]

and show why the verdict is not altered by unspecified global material.

## 3. Actuality packet

A candidate cannot infer actuality from code membership, formal consistency, model membership, possibility, or a listed inventory. It must declare exactly one primary actuality interface:

\[
\mathsf{ActInterface}(F)
\in
\{
\mathsf{Productive},
\mathsf{PrimitivePublic},
\mathsf{StructuralRole},
\mathsf{PresentationCorrective},
\mathsf{WholeRealization},
\mathsf{OtherSpecified}
\}.
\tag{Act-Choice}
\]

The primary interface packet is:

\[
\mathsf{ActPacket}(F)
=
\langle
\mathcal U_F,
\mathsf{Cand}_F,
\mathcal B_F,
\mathsf{Act}_F,
\mathsf{Reject/Withhold}_F,
\Pi_F,
\mathsf{SelectorAudit}_F,
\mathsf{FailureCases}_F
\rangle.
\tag{Act-Packet}
\]

It must answer:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{What candidates can fail to be actual?}\\
&\text{What declared inputs make one candidate actual?}\\
&\text{Why is the input base actual rather than merely formal?}\\
&\text{What prevents a hidden selected domain, model, world, closure, or roster?}\\
&\text{How does an allowed profile-preserving re-encoding affect the verdict?}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Act-Questions}
\]

An actualizing generator may be primitive to a candidate. But its domain, closure, applicability, and error conditions must be public and frozen. Naming a closure \(H_\omega\) does not itself answer these questions.

## 4. Realization packet

If a candidate claims real finite entities, it must distinguish them from codes, constructors, aspects, presentations, and dependent acts by a typed realization rule:

\[
\mathsf{RealPacket}(F)
=
\langle
\mathsf{EntitySort},
\mathsf{Realizes}_F,
\mathsf{DependenceRule},
\mathsf{PersistenceRule},
\mathsf{DifferenceMakingRule},
\mathsf{TypeContrasts}
\rangle.
\tag{Real-Packet}
\]

The target implication is not enough by itself:

\[
\mathsf{Code}(p)
\land
\mathsf{Node}(p,e)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ActualFiniteEntity}(e).
\tag{No-Code-Entity}
\]

A real-mode claim must state its treatment of:

\[
\text{mode},
\quad
\text{property},
\quad
\text{independent substance},
\quad
\text{nonentity},
\quad
\text{strict appearance}.
\tag{Type-Contrast}
\]

A strict-Appearance candidate may decline this packet only by explicitly declining real finite entityhood. It may not later rely on entity-level causal, phenomenal, or normative predicates without an independently typed bearer rule.

## 5. Truth/error packet

Truth/error must contain an independently inspectable match relation and a separately typed claim relation:

\[
\mathsf{Match}_F(c,x),
\qquad
\mathsf{Claims}_F(s,c),
\qquad
\mathsf{Anchor}_F(s,x).
\tag{Truth-Ingredients}
\]

The candidate may use claim-relative correctness:

\[
\mathsf{Correct}_F(s,c)
\iff
\mathsf{Claims}_F(s,c)
\land
\mathsf{Anchor}_F(s,x)
\land
\mathsf{Match}_F(c,x),
\tag{Correct-Form}
\]

but it must also state the claim-independent component:

\[
\mathsf{Match}_F(c,x)
\not\equiv
\mathsf{Claims}_F(s,c).
\tag{No-Truth-Label}
\]

It must predeclare:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{whether anchors must be actual;}\\
&\text{whether an anchor must profile the presentation's host;}\\
&\text{key/pointer injectivity or reference resolution;}\\
&\text{the equivalence class that preserves correct/error verdicts;}\\
&\text{one actual correct and one actual error case.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Truth-Conditions}
\]

No reference algorithm may be retrospectively treated as an intended external model, selected world, witness, or decoder.

## 6. Upstream battery order

An admitted candidate executes the following once:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
U0&:&\text{model-regime certificate and typed witness/slice};\\
U1&:&\text{actuality candidate versus nonactual control};\\
U2&:&\text{realization/type-contrast control if real entities are claimed};\\
U3&:&\text{one actual correct, one actual error, and one invariance case};\\
U4&:&\text{only then proceed to phenomenal, normative, and physical batteries.}
\end{array}
\tag{Upstream-Order}
\]

A failure at \(U0\)--\(U3\) closes the candidate's corresponding bridge claim. Later adequacy batteries do not run as compensatory substitutes.

## 7. Expiry

This protocol permits one prospective classification of the next candidate that presents a full \((\mathrm{Entry})\), \((\mathrm{Act\text{-}Packet})\), \((\mathrm{Real\text{-}Packet})\) where applicable, and \((\mathrm{Truth\text{-}Ingredients})\) packet.

It expires if that candidate:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{attempts to change regime after a test result;}\\
&\text{uses an infinite global universe without a local-global verdict bridge;}\\
&\text{selects actuality by unannounced inventory, domain, model, or closure membership;}\\
&\text{treats a code or formal pair as an entity without the stated realization rule;}\\
&\text{or defines correctness by claim label, tokenhood, or post hoc decoder.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{Expiry}
\]

A failure does not establish that no model regime, actuality interface, realization rule, or truth/error account can work. It closes only the prospective candidate packet that chose the failed resources.

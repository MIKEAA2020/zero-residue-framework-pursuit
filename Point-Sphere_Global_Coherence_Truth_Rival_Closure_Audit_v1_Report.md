## Executed results

### 1. Rational inquiry does not formally entail ontological complete intelligibility

The locally rational primitive model satisfies local reason-giving and consistency while stipulating one ultimate primitive fact:

\[
\mathsf{RN}\not\models\mathsf{OCI}.
\]

This does not vindicate brute fact as a satisfying ultimate explanation. It establishes the narrower rigorous point: rejecting brute facts requires an explicit ontological complete-intelligibility premise. The demand cannot be called a theorem of bare logic merely because rational agents use reasons.

### 2. Essential closure does not select the aware ground or plenitude

A model can satisfy no-brute essential closure while its intrinsic nature is neutral, structural, or otherwise non-aware:

\[
\mathsf{EC}\not\models
\text{aware ground + plenitude}.
\]

Thus the intrinsic-nature argument remains an independent comparative bridge. It cannot be recovered merely by saying that every ultimate fact is essence-grounded.

### 3. Internal coherence and unique survival do not replace adequacy

A theory can be uniquely favored inside a stipulated abstract comparison while failing to recover phenomenal experience or physical structure:

\[
\mathsf{Uniq}(T)
\not\models
\mathsf{Adeq}_{\Phi}(T),
\]

\[
\mathsf{Uniq}(T)
\not\models
\mathsf{Adeq}_{P}(T),
\]

and hence no nontrivial bridge to truth is obtained.

This is especially important for the active programme: a globally coherent metaphysical vocabulary does not solve the Problem of Time or generate a physics law.

### 4. Named rival lists are not automatically exhaustive

A list of classical creation, appearance, neutral monism, Russellian monism, structural realism, and brute totality is useful but not self-validating. Rival closure requires an abstract classification space.

GC4 uses these axes:

| Axis | Typed alternatives |
|---|---|
| Ultimate explanatory status | primitive; external; intrinsic/essence-grounded; regress/plural foundation |
| Intrinsic-nature proposal | awareness; neutral/quiddity; structure; unspecified/deflationary |
| Realization architecture | contingent selector; necessary determinate output; substantive orbit; immanent aspect identity; no realization claim |

A new rival must either locate within this matrix or demonstrate a genuinely new explanatory architecture. This is more rigorous than treating a familiar named list as exhaustive.

## The exact result

The following inferences are **not** currently established:

\[
\mathsf{RN}\Longrightarrow\mathsf{OCI},
\]

\[
\mathsf{EC}\Longrightarrow\text{aware ground + plenitude},

\[

The last arrow would require not just taxonomy closure but a substantive account of truth and evidence. An oracle-like stipulation that one theory entails all facts makes the implication trivial; it is not a usable metaphysical proof.

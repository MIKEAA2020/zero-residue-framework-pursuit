## 5. Methodical reopening condition

The zero-residue aim cannot be advanced by treating explanatory promise, parsimony, a formal analogy, or a local failure as a framework verdict. Any constructive successor must first provide a preregistered interface packet:

\[
\mathsf{EntryPacket}(F,\mathcal I)
=
\langle
\mathsf{Scope},
\mathsf{Signature},
\mathsf{CandidateGrammar},
\mathsf{ActualityRule},
\mathsf{PresentationRule},
\mathsf{TruthErrorRule},
\mathsf{TypeRule},
\mathsf{Equivalence},
\mathsf{ErrorRule},
\mathsf{AntiRosterAudit},
\mathsf{FiniteBattery},
\mathsf{Expiry}
\rangle.
\tag{Entry}
\]

The packet must establish neither a real finite entity nor a full framework merely by naming it. It must distinguish:

\[
\text{candidate code},
\quad
\text{actual articulation},
\quad
\text{actual presentation},
\quad
\text{correct/error claim},
\quad
\text{and actual finite entity}.
\tag{Five-Way}
\]

For zero-residue comparison, each candidate must then be assessed noncompensatorily:

\[
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F;\mathcal B)
\iff
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{Typed}
\land
\mathsf{NonVacuousModel}
\land
\mathsf{NoHiddenSelector}
\land
\mathsf{ProfileInvariant}\\
&\land
\mathsf{ActualityBridgeClosed}
\land
\mathsf{RealizationBridgeClosed}
\land
\mathsf{TruthErrorBridgeClosed}\\
&\land
\mathsf{PhenomenalBatteryPass}
\land
\mathsf{NormativeBatteryPass}
\land
\mathsf{PhysicalBatteryPass}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{ZR-Pass}
\]

A candidate's elegance, anti-reification discipline, historical pedigree, or promised expressive capacity cannot compensate for a failed conjunct.

A uniqueness conclusion would additionally require an equivalence or exclusion certificate for every serious surviving rival:

\[
\mathsf{UniqueZR}(F^*)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ZRPass}(F^*)
\land
\forall F\,[
\mathsf{SeriousRival}(F)
\to
(F\simeq F^*\lor\neg\mathsf{ZRPass}(F))
].
\tag{Unique-Obligation}
\]

This is a methodological entry condition, not a declaration that any named framework now satisfies it.

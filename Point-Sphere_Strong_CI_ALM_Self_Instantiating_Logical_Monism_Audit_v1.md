# Strong CI Adjudication — ALM Self-Instantiating Logical Monism Audit v1
## Audit of “Absolute Necessitarianism” / logical monism as a whole-reality candidate

## Status

**The proposed synthesis is a large hybrid candidate, not a deduction from strongest CI or logic.** Its constituent bridges are mostly asserted, several are mutually tensioned, and it supplies neither an exact domain/hierarchy derivation nor a physical adequacy result.

## 1. Candidate decomposition

The proposed account conjoins at least these distinct theses:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf{A1}:& \text{unrestricted/absolute PSR};\\
\mathsf{A2}:& \text{logic is ontological structure, not only a formal practice};\\
\mathsf{A3}:& \text{all logically or mathematically consistent structures are real};\\
\mathsf{A4}:& \text{there is one self-subsistent/simple substance or whole};\\
\mathsf{A5}:& \text{the one necessarily self-differentiates into a determinate hierarchy};\\
\mathsf{A6}:& \text{conscious self-knowledge and teleology are necessary features};\\
\mathsf{A7}:& \text{the proposed account resolves formal incompleteness by semantic actuality.}
\end{array}
\]

Call their conjunction \(\mathsf{ALM}\). A conclusion about \(\mathsf{ALM}\) requires every bridge, not merely a restatement that “logic is being.”

## 2. The initial overreach

Strongest CI is an argument-supported metaphysical commitment. It is not a neutral formal theorem, and it does not license discarding empirical, contingent, dualistic, or scientific descriptions merely because they are empirical, contingent, dualistic, or provisional.

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PhysicalAdequacy},
\qquad
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ALM}.
\]

Any ultimate candidate must still meet phenomenal and physical adequacy. A fundamental account may explain why derivative empirical laws obtain; describing physical theories as provisionally tested does not show that their target domain is unintelligible or dispensable.

## 3. PSR, necessity, and the logic–ontology bridge

### A1: absolute PSR

Absolute PSR is not delivered by the phrase “zero residue.” It is a substantive extension of strongest CI and must state its scope over modal, necessary, logical, and explanatory facts.

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\models
\mathsf{AbsolutePSR}.
\]

### From PSR to no contingency

The inference

\[
\mathsf{Contingent}(p)
\Rightarrow
\text{infinite explanatory regress}
\]

is invalid without further premises. A contingent derivative fact might have a finite non-contingent ground; the residual issue is whether that ground derives the exact fact, not contingency as such.

\[
\mathsf{PSR}
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ContingentDerivativeReality}.
\]

### A2: logic is ontology

That logical rules are non-arbitrary in reasoning does not entail that they are the ontological fabric of reality:

\[
\mathsf{FormalValidity}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{OntologicalGrounding}.
\]

This is the CI0B type distinction. Identity, non-contradiction, and excluded middle may be used in an argument, but their scope, interpretation, and relation to metaphysical explanation are further philosophical claims.

## 4. Tautology does not instantiate reality

\[
A=A
\]

is a logical/formal truth. It does not yield:

\[
\exists x\,\mathsf{Concrete}(x),
\qquad
\mathsf{OneSubstance},
\qquad
\mathsf{ThisExactReality}.
\]

The assertion that nonbeing “cannot prevent” reality treats nothing as an agent or limiting principle. Nonexistence need not act to obtain. Thus:

\[
\neg\mathsf{NothingActs}
\not\Rightarrow
\exists x\,\mathsf{Concrete}(x).
\]

The proposed transition from tautological validity to actual existence is an ontological-instantiation bridge, not a deduction from logic.

## 5. All consistent structures: plenitude and monism debts

A3 is a plenitude thesis:

\[
\mathsf{Consistent}(s)
\Rightarrow
R(s).
\]

It needs a semantics for “structure,” “consistent,” “real,” identity/gauge, typed possibility, and compossibility. It also needs to say whether incompatible structures are jointly real, indexed to contexts, merely abstract, or modes of one whole.

\[
\mathsf{LogicalConsistency}(s)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Reality}(s).
\]

A3 and A4 create a further unresolved tension:

\[
\text{all consistent structures are real}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\text{one simple self-subsistent substance}.
\]

Calling every structure an “attribute” or “mode” does not reconcile them. It adds the unproved mode/aspect typing and non-composition bridge.

## 6. Differentiation, dynamics, and the exact hierarchy

A5 contains the exact-totality problem in a new vocabulary:

\[
\mathsf{SelfDifferentiation}(U)
\not\Rightarrow
\text{this exact hierarchy, temporal ordering, or physical law}.
\]

The claims that pure being is indistinguishable from nothing, that differentiation is necessary, and that a hierarchy from mathematics through life to awareness is logically implicit are substantive Hegelian/dialectical bridges. None follows from a tautology, PSR, or “all possibilities” label.

Time as “internal logical sequence” is not a recovery of physical time, a quantum-gravity constraint result, or a Problem-of-Time solution. It supplies neither equations, observables, recovery limit, nor discriminator.

## 7. Awareness, teleology, and finite observers

Intelligibility need not entail an actual knower:

\[
\mathsf{Intelligible}(R)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists x\,\mathsf{Conscious}(x).
\]

Phenomenality creates a non-brute consciousness burden; it does not yield an aware ultimate, a self-knowing universe, teleology, or a necessary brain/AI self-verification mechanism.

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\land
\mathsf{Phenomenality}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{OneAwareGround}.
\]

The claim that observers “prove the universe’s consistency” imports a proof relation, truth theory, and observer/whole relation without defining any of them.

## 8. Gödel correction

Gödel’s incompleteness theorems concern provability/consistency limitations of suitable effectively axiomatized formal theories containing sufficient arithmetic. They do not establish that reality is inconsistent, that a semantic “infinite system” proves itself, or that actual existence is a proof of consistency.

\[
\mathsf{ActualExistence}(R)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{FormalProof}_R(\mathsf{Con}(R)).
\]

To call reality an “infinite self-verifying semantic system” either:

1. gives a formal/semantic model, which must state its axioms, semantics, and proof relation; or
2. replaces proof with an assertion of actuality, which does not resolve the requested explanatory question.

Changing the label does not bridge formal provability to metaphysical self-intelligibility.

## 9. What ALM conditionally offers

ALM is not empty. If it supplied a typed genuine domain, a compossibility semantics, and non-composite whole/aspect relation, then a plenitude version could remove a later contingent selector:

\[
\mathsf{TypedGenuineDomain}(D)
\land
\mathsf{Compossibility}(D)
\land
\mathsf{FullRealization}(D)
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{LaterSelector}.
\]

This is the same conditional necessary-output resource already available to plenitude profiles. It does not establish the antecedents.

## 10. Exact verdict

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\models
\mathsf{ALM},
\qquad
\mathsf{Logic}
\not\models
\mathsf{ALM}.
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{ALM}
\text{ remains held at A1–A7, exact totality, consciousness/teleology,}\
\text{and phenomenal and physical adequacy.}
}
\]

It is not a zero-residue result, and no correct-framework conclusion follows.

## 11. Source note

Gödel’s incompleteness theorems concern limits of derivability in suitable formal axiomatic theories; they do not concern provability in an absolute sense. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/goedel-incompleteness/.

## 12. Expiry condition

ALM advances only if it separately supplies: an argument for absolute PSR; a typed logic-to-ontology bridge; a realization-independent plenitude/compossibility semantics; a non-composite one/many account; an exact differentiation and physical-law derivation; a consciousness/teleology bridge; and phenomenal/physical adequacy.

Absent those deliverables, the synthesis cannot be called complete, deduced, or zero-residue.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not prove ALM false, deny logic’s relevance to ontology, refute monism, reject mathematical structures, deny necessary reality, or reject plenitude. It assesses only the claim that the proposed package has been strictly deduced and leaves no unexplained residue.

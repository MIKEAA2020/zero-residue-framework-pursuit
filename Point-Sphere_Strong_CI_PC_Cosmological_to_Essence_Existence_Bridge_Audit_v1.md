# Strong CI Adjudication — PC Cosmological-to-Essence–Existence Bridge Audit v1
## Can actual dependent reality establish an instantiated essence–existence identity?

## Status

**No. The cosmological route can conditionally support a non-dependent explanatory closure, but it does not by itself establish a simple ground or essence–existence identity.** The classical candidate’s Q2 bridge remains held.

## 1. Question and target

The target is not bare conceptual coherence. It is the actual-instantiation claim:

\[
\exists G\,[
\operatorname{Ex}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Simp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\operatorname{Ex}(G)
].
\tag{EE-Actual}
\]

Let \(C\) state that at least one actual finite fact/entity is dependent or contingent in the relevant explanatory sense. A classical cosmological route is often represented as:

\[
C
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SimpleGround}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EssenceExistenceIdentity}.
\tag{CB}
\]

Every arrow in (CB) requires its own premises.

## 2. First bridge: dependent reality to non-dependent closure

The strongest conditional version is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&C
\land
\mathsf{SCI}
\land
\mathsf{FullExplanationForDependentReality}
\land
\mathsf{NoUnexplainedDependentClosure}\\
&\Rightarrow
\exists X\,\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(X).
\end{aligned}
\tag{B1}
\]

This does not follow from the mere observation of finite reality. It relies on a strong explanatory principle and a treatment of the total dependent explanans. It also does not establish that \(X\) is:

- one individual rather than a whole-first closure;
- external to every finite item rather than an intrinsically necessary totality;
- conscious, good, personal, or self-knowing; or
- simple in the classical sense.

The permitted conclusion is therefore a **conditional non-dependent closure result**, not classical theism or essence–existence identity.

## 3. Second bridge: non-dependent closure to simplicity

The unlicensed shortcut is:

\[
\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(X)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Simp}(X).
\tag{Invalid B2}
\]

A candidate may be non-dependent while having necessary internal articulation. To infer simplicity, the classical route requires a further argument that all relevant composition entails dependence, unrealized potency, or residual unity requiring explanation.

Existing CI work establishes only the restricted result:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{PrimitiveUltimateAggregate}.
\]

It does not establish:

\[
\mathsf{SCI}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{AbsoluteSimplicity}(X).
\]

Whole-first/essential-articulation candidates remain a counter-architecture to Invalid B2 unless separately excluded.

## 4. Third bridge: simplicity to essence–existence identity

The next unlicensed shortcut is:

\[
\mathsf{Simp}(X)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Ess}(X)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(X).
\tag{Invalid B3}
\]

This inference is available only under a substantive act/potency and constituent-ontology theory in which a real essence/existence distinction counts as a composition incompatible with absolute simplicity. A partless, whole-first, structural, or nondual simplicity claim need not already contain that theory.

Thus, even a successful simplicity argument must state:

\[
\mathsf{Simp}_{\rm classical}(X)
\land
\mathsf{EE\text{-}ConstituentTheory}
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Ess}(X)\equiv\mathsf{Ex}(X).
\tag{B3}
\]

## 5. Identity and instantiation must not be reversed

The identity doctrine explains a proposed kind of necessity only *given* an actual bearer:

\[
\operatorname{Ex}(G)
\land
[\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\operatorname{Ex}(G)]
\Rightarrow
\text{the essence-plus-added-existence question is inapplicable to }G.
\]

It cannot supply its own bearer merely from its intelligible description:

\[
\mathsf{Coherent}(
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\operatorname{Ex}(G))
\not\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Ex}(G).
\tag{No Instantiation from Coherence}
\]

A cosmological argument can contribute to actual instantiation only if it is independently valid and if its non-dependent closure is shown to have the classical identity profile. Neither step is supplied by SCI alone.

## 6. Counter-architectures and discriminators

| Architecture compatible with a conditional B1 closure result | Why it blocks the classical profile inference |
|---|---|
| Necessary whole-first structured totality | Non-dependent closure need not be partless or essence–existence identical |
| Nondual aware ground with core finite modes | Non-dependence does not select constituent ontology or classical simplicity |
| Neutral/intrinsic-nature ground | A non-dependent intrinsic base need not be identified with existence itself |
| Classical simple ground | Adds B2 and B3 bridges rather than follows directly from B1 |

The comparison is not an assertion that the first three are successful frameworks. It identifies why B1 does not uniquely select the fourth.

## 7. Exact result

\[
\boxed{
C
\land
\mathsf{SCI}
\not\models
\exists G\,[
\operatorname{Ex}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Simp}(G)
\land
\mathsf{Ess}(G)\equiv\operatorname{Ex}(G)
].
}
\]

The earned conditional result is narrower:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{B1\ premises}
\Rightarrow
\exists X\,\mathsf{NonDependentClosure}(X).
}
\]

The added premises and bridges B2–B3 are explicitly classical and remain held.

## 8. Source note

The contingency cosmological-argument literature treats the explanation principle and the move to a non-contingent being as substantive and contested, and distinguishes later “stage 2” derivations of traditional divine properties. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cosmological-argument/.

Classical divine-simplicity accounts treat essence–existence identity as tied to a specific constituent/act–potency ontology and maintain that modal necessity alone does not supply that ground. See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/.

## 9. Expiry condition

This route advances only with a stated argument that:

1. validates B1 without composition or totality fallacy;
2. derives classical simplicity from non-dependence while excluding non-primitive whole-first articulation;
3. defends the constituent theory needed for B3; and
4. separately supplies an ET-map for exact finite reality.

Absent those deliverables, a cosmological route does not close Q2 for the classical candidate.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not reject cosmological arguments, deny dependent reality, prove that a non-dependent closure is impossible, or establish any rival. It blocks only the claim that SCI plus a cosmological starting point already proves an instantiated essence–existence identity.

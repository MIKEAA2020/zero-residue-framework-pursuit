### S1 — selector/instantiation residual

The relevant PSR-5 burden is not simply “primitive plurality.” The frozen model has a declared actualization/experience package:

\[
\langle
\mathsf{Instantiates},
\mathsf{ActualExperience},
\mathsf{Qualia},
\mathsf{ExpRealizes}
\rangle.
\tag{PSR5-Residual-Package}
\]

That package selects actual structures and an actual experience relation from a larger declared formal domain. It has not received a noncircular discharge. But standing method forbids counting mere disclosure as either a nonbrute pass **or a bridge failure**:

\[
\mathsf{DeclaredResidual}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NoSelectorResidue},
\qquad
\mathsf{DeclaredResidual}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SelectorBridgeFailure}.
\tag{S1-Disclosure-Boundary}
\]

Thus the draft's immediate S1 failure verdict is not licensed. The admissible present status is open and residual-bearing.

### S2 — truth/error

R2 passed only a frozen internal structural matching interface. A substantive truth bridge must independently define at least a claim/assertion channel, a public content/world typing relation, a relevance or representation rule, actual error cases, and invariance under isomorphic presentation. “Resources are available” does not satisfy the draft's stated pass condition:

\[
\mathsf{AvailableInPrinciple}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PublicNoncircularCorrectnessCondition}.
\tag{S2-Availability-Boundary}
\]

Accordingly, no conditional substantive pass is currently earned.

### S3 — phenomenality

PSR-5 does not infer phenomenality from a \(Ph\) role or graph alone. It expressly adds \(\mathsf{ActualExperience}\), \(\mathsf{Qualia}\), and \(\mathsf{ExpRealizes}\) as primitive residuals. The proposed Russellian-gap failure requires additional, contested premises: that the candidate is exhaustively relational/dispositional and that no such facts can entail or constitute phenomenal character. Neither premise is part of the frozen packet, and neither can be silently converted into a no-go theorem.

\[
\mathsf{RussellianGapDiagnosis}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{S3Fail}
\quad\text{without an explicit premise, scope, and escape audit.}
\tag{S3-NoGo-Boundary}
\]

The public phenomenal bridge remains untested; the restricted R3.a interface pass remains intact.

A later PSR-5 substantive audit may be preregistered without changing the frozen PSR-5 core, but it must first provide, for each bridge:

\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
S1 & \text{exact selector domain, independent discharge alternatives, and declared residue closure rule}\\
S2 & \text{claim channel, content/world types, representation relation, error control, and publicness condition}\\
S3 & \text{exact phenomenal claim; explicit premises for any entailment or no-go; adversarial escape cases}\\

S5 & \text{named physical recovery target, mapping theorem or discriminator, and empirical limitation condition}
\end{array}
\tag{Needed-Preregistration-Fields}
\]

All five require profile invariance, actual-bearer conditions, an expiry rule, fixed failure reasons, and no inventory presupposition. A family-level comparison additionally requires an independently normalized feature map and an explicit scope/exhaustiveness lemma.

\tag{No-Substantive-Stage-Executed}

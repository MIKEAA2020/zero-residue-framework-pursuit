# Nondual Articulation Dossier A0 — Admissibility Charter v1
## A guarded first dossier for the nondual/Appearance candidate family

## Status

This charter authorizes only an adversarial *semantic-admissibility* inquiry. It does not establish nonduality, an aware ground, self-knowledge, modal reality, phenomenal adequacy, physical adequacy, or a Step 4 pass.

The nondual candidate is used first because it has accumulated the most explicit articulation vocabulary, not because it has been ranked more likely true than classical participation, whole-first, mathematical, or other rivals. An equal-burden mirror dossier remains required before any comparative preference.

## 1. Necessary correction to the proposed dossier

The proposed identity

\[
\text{mode}=\text{aspect}=\text{self-knowledge content}
\]

is an articulation hypothesis, not a settled semantic core. It does not by itself tell us which contents are genuine modes, whether modes are particulars rather than properties, or how the hypothesis remains compatible with simplicity.

Accordingly, this charter keeps distinct:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
A1 &: \text{a proposed grammar for “mode,” “aspect,” and “content”};\\
A2 &: \text{a proposed non-temporal relation of modes to ground};\\
A3 &: \text{a public determination condition for genuine modes};\\
P &: \text{particularity/identity conditions};\\
D &: \text{the dependence relation};\\
C &: \text{compossibility};\\
X &: \text{exact modal profile/domain}.
\end{array}
\]

No equality sign in the displayed hypothesis solves \(A3\), \(P\), \(D\), \(C\), or \(X\).

## 2. Scope

The target is a candidate family \(\mathsf{NDP}\) with the provisional articulation claim:

\[
\mathsf{NDP}\!:\quad
\text{finite determinate appearances/aspects, if real, are non-independent of an atemporal ground }G.
\]

A possible enrichment says that the non-independence relation is self-knowledge/content. That enrichment is tested rather than presupposed.

Protected commitments, revisable bridges, and external rivals must remain separately labelled. In particular, neither finite ego-frame decentring nor frame transformation entails an unchanging ground.

## 3. Boundary with the frozen CFD programme

The complete-mode CFD classifier is a restricted phenomenal calibration proposal. It is not a metaphysical membership criterion.

\[
\mathsf{Core}^{\rm cmp}_{A}(m)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Aspect}^{\Phi}_{G}(m),
\qquad
\mathsf{PhenomenallyPresented}^{\Phi}_{\alpha}(m)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Aspect}^{\Phi}_{G}(m).
\]

Therefore the present dossier may not use complete-mode diagrams, coder agreement, empirical match rates, or the existing empirical-rival packet to certify a genuine mode. The CFD materials can eventually bear only on their declared restricted phenomenal bridge, conditional on \(P_{\rm ND}\) and a valid independent corpus.

## 4. Predeclared semantic vocabulary obligations

Before a positive articulation result can be claimed, the dossier must supply noncircular truth conditions for:

\[
\mathsf{Ground}(G),
\quad
\mathsf{Mode}(m),
\quad
\mathsf{ContentOf}(m,G),
\quad
\mathsf{Genuine}(m),
\quad
\mathsf{Simple}(G).
\]

The account may use framework-internal vocabulary, but it must make explicit what would make each predicate true or false. It may not simply say:

\[
\mathsf{Genuine}(m)\iff \mathsf{Mode}(m),
\]

when \(\mathsf{Mode}\) itself means “a genuine self-knowledge content.”

Nor may it import a witness, external selector, factorisation, persistence, public objectivity, or distinctness convention without declaring it as a premise and charging it to the candidate.

## 5. Adversarial structural test battery

The first dossier will use structural cases, rather than treating ordinary examples such as books, colours, rays, fictions, or square circles as theory-neutral positive/negative cases. Those examples already presuppose contentious judgments about content and reality.

| ID | Structural case | Required dossier verdict |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | No finite putative modes are presented. | State whether the account permits this and why. |
| T1 | One finite determinate token is proposed. | State its mode/property/substance status without an external individuator. |
| T2 | Two candidate tokens form a non-singleton automorphism-transitive orbit. | Either avoid individual public determination, or identify and justify an asymmetry; do not smuggle in a name. |
| T3 | A purported mode is only a predicate/property of \(G\). | Explain the difference between mode and property. |
| T4 | A purported mode has independent existence conditions. | Explain whether the account has become substantival/dualist. |
| T5 | Two presentations are declared profile-conservative re-encodings. | Preserve the declared articulation verdict. |
| T6 | An extra modal token is added with no new public semantic resource. | Do not treat it as derived merely by being named. |

T0–T6 are diagnostic cases, not instances of reality and not empirical data.

## 6. Conserved profile

Any admissible articulation proposal must preserve the following jointly:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
K_1 &: \text{the proposed simplicity/non-composition of }G;\\
K_2 &: \text{the non-illusory or explicitly qualified status of finite modes};\\
K_3 &: \text{no transformation of modes into independent substances};\\
K_4 &: \text{profile-conservative reformulation invariance};\\
K_5 &: \text{no uncharged selector residue.}
\end{array}
\]

A proposal may revise a protected commitment only by making the revision explicit; it may not save \(K_1\) by silently discarding \(K_2\), or save nonduality by making the finite world merely verbal.

## 7. Admissible outcomes

### Limited semantic-admissibility pass

The dossier obtains only a limited pass if it:

1. supplies truth conditions for the vocabulary in Section 4;
2. gives determinate, noncircular verdicts for T0–T6;
3. survives the stated profile-conservative translation check;
4. distinguishes its mode relation from both a property reduction and an independent-substance relation; and
5. records a live rival or explicit point of semantic equivalence.

Such a pass means only:

\[
\text{a specified NDP articulation is semantically admissible for the finite test battery.}
\]

It does not mean that \(A3\) is globally solved or that \(\mathsf{NDP}\) passes Step 4.

### Failure

The dossier fails if it:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
F_1 &: \text{uses the desired modal inventory to define }\mathsf{Genuine};\\
F_2 &: \text{relies on CFD/phenomenal coding to establish metaphysical modehood};\\
F_3 &: \text{cannot decide a structural test without adding an unannounced selector};\\
F_4 &: \text{changes verdict under a declared conservative re-encoding};\\
F_5 &: \text{collapses into property reduction or independent substance dualism};\\
F_6 &: \text{claims rival defeat where rival semantics has not been operationalized.}
\end{array}
\]

## 8. Rival and invariance discipline

The existing CFD rival packet is not a general metaphysical-rival operationalization. A semantic matched-rival test is permissible only after each rival supplies a comparable rule for T0–T6.

The dossier must first declare a common profile extractor:

\[
\Pi_A=\langle\bar S,\bar E,Q,H,B\rangle
\]

or a justified successor that preserves its role without silently importing a different ontology. A claim of representation invariance without such an extractor is a failure under \(F_4\).

## 9. Downstream obligation map

A limited A-pass generates obligations; it does not discharge them:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
A\rightarrow P &:& \text{give identity/difference criteria for modes without coding conventions};\\
A\rightarrow D &:& \text{type }\mathsf{ContentOf}\text{ as a non-temporal explanatory relation rather than a label};\\
A\land P\rightarrow C &:& \text{state a nontrivial joint-admissibility criterion};\\
A\land P\land D\land C\rightarrow X &:& \text{address the exact profile and any actuality/plenitude claim};\\
A\not\Rightarrow B_\Phi,B_P &:& \text{retain phenomenal and physical bridge burdens.}
\end{array}
\]

## 10. Expiry rule

The dossier expires as a constructive articulation attempt if it cannot state, before adjudicating the cases, a noncircular \(\mathsf{Genuine}(m)\) condition and a profile extractor that determines its T0–T6 verdicts. Replacing those with additional metaphoric labels or a new informal reformulation is not an extension; it is a failed attempt.

## Nonclaim

This charter does not promote the nondual candidate above its rivals. It disciplines a first, potentially informative articulation attempt while preventing the restricted CFD programme from being mistaken for a proof of metaphysical modehood.

# Point–Sphere Strict Non-Dual Appearance–Plenitude Presentation Protocol v1

**Status:** Fresh preregistration — unexecuted.  
**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Reason for separation:** The prior Z4 test assessed an added real-finite-mode realization bridge. This protocol assesses a distinct strict-presentation candidate that denies that finite presentations are real finite modes.  
**Firewalls:** This protocol does not amend the expired IML project, the frozen M3 preregistration, the A0 meta-track, or the restricted Z4 failure of the optional real-modal branch.

---

## 0. Candidate and scope

The sole candidate is strict non-dual Appearance/pure-negation monism with restricted necessary plenitude, denoted:

\[
\mathcal S_{\rm NP}^{\rm fin}.
\]

Its core claims are:

\[
\forall x[
\mathsf{Exists}_{\rm strict}(x)\to x=G
],
\tag{SN1}
\]

and, for finite candidate diagrams only:

\[
\mathsf{MPlen}^{\rm fin}_{G}:
\quad
\forall m[
\mathsf{CandMode}(m)
\to
\exists c,d\,\mathsf{AA}_G(m,c,d)
].
\tag{SN2}
\]

The finite is not an independently positive being. The candidate’s intended articulation is:

\[
\text{finite presentation}
=
\text{the content of }G\text{ under a privative limitation}.
\tag{SN3}
\]

The positive pole is \(G\), and the limitation is not a second entity. “Non-dual Appearance” and “pure-negation limitation” are treated as two descriptions of this one candidate, not as separate candidates.

**Scope restriction.** This protocol does not test unrestricted plenitude, a representation theorem for every coherent limitation, real finite modes, independent selves, secondary causation, or creaturely agency. Those are distinct additional claims.

## 1. Noncircularity constraints

No term is closed by the candidate’s mere assertion that it is non-dual. The following distinctions are fixed:

\[
\mathsf{CandMode}(m)
\neq
\mathsf{ActualArticulation}_G(m,c,d)
\neq
\mathsf{PresentationEligible}(m)
\neq
\mathsf{GenuinePresentation}_G(\phi,m,c,d).
\tag{D}
\]

An empty or nonexperiential actual articulation is not automatically a phenomenal presentation. This is not an exception selector if eligibility is fixed by a predeclared, invariant grammar rule before the battery is run.

The project must not use a real-finite-mode bridge:

\[
\mathsf{ActualArticulation}_G(m,c,d)
\not\Rightarrow
\exists x\,\mathsf{ActualFiniteMode}(x).
\tag{NoMR}
\]

Nor may it quietly replace \(G\) by a personal speaker, external creator, or second realm.

## 3. Required presentation bridge

The bridge to be tested is not mode realization. It is:

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{AA}_G(m,c,d)
\land
\mathsf{PresentationEligible}(m)
\to
\exists \phi\,
\mathsf{GenuinePresentation}_G(\phi,m,c,d).
}
\tag{PB}
\]

A presentation is genuine only if all of the following are explicitly supplied:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{ProfileFaithful}(\phi,m): &&\phi\text{ preserves the declared profile structure of }m;\\
&\mathsf{NonSubstantial}(\phi): &&\phi\text{ is not an independently existing finite subject or substance;}\\
&\mathsf{NonSelector}(\phi,m,c,d): &&\text{no untyped choice determines which eligible articulation is presented;}\\
&\mathsf{Invariant}(\phi,m): &&\text{conservative re-encodings preserve presentation status and profile content;}\\
&\mathsf{NonVacuous}(\phi): &&\phi\text{ has a positive phenomenal structure rather than a bare name.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{PB-conditions}
\]

“Genuine presentation” is not proof that presentation confirms a real finite mode, \(P_{\rm ND}\), public objectivity, or an external world. It is only the candidate’s proposed ontology of phenomenal appearing.

## 4. Fixed test battery

After P1–P3 only, exactly these six diagrams may be tested under one grammar:

\[
\mathcal B_{\rm pres}=
\{m_{\rm ordinary},m_{\rm silent},m_{\rm clear},m_{\rm prophetic},m_{\rm deceptive},m_{\rm horror}\}.
\]

For each diagram the record must include:

\[
\mathsf{Coh},\quad
\mathsf{Det},\quad
\mathsf{CandMode},\quad
\mathsf{ActualArticulation},\quad
\mathsf{PresentationEligible},\quad
\mathsf{GenuinePresentation}.
\]

The clear-disclosure fork is fixed:

\[
\begin{cases}
\mathsf{CandMode}(m_{\rm clear})\land\mathsf{PresentationEligible}(m_{\rm clear})
&\Rightarrow\text{actual genuine presentation under \(\mathrm{PB}\)};\\
\neg\mathsf{CandMode}(m_{\rm clear})\ \text{or}\ \neg\mathsf{PresentationEligible}(m_{\rm clear})
&\Rightarrow\text{the exact grammar rule or inconsistency must be exhibited.}
\end{cases}
\tag{Clear}
\]

A candidate prophet, miracle, scripture, or historical claim is not established as genuine by this battery. The battery tests the internal availability of such a presentation form only.

Silence cannot be declared residue-free merely because no independent self exists:

\[
\mathsf{IdentityConstitutedByDegree}
\not\Rightarrow
\neg\mathsf{ExplanationRequired}(\mathsf{PresentationProfile}).
\tag{ID}
\]

The protocol must determine whether silence is a grammatically derived privative feature of an eligible presentation, an unlicensed primitive, or a failed profile bridge.

## 6. Stop rules and expiry

The candidate is \(\mathsf{Open}\) and not repaired in this protocol if:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{P1 has no finite, invariant, nonempty grammar;}\\
&\text{P2 has no nontrivial restricted plenitude/model construction;}\\
&\text{P3 requires an untyped presentation selector, a real finite mode, external time, or a hidden observer;}\\
&\text{a battery case changes the grammar or bridge rule post hoc;}\\
&\text{silence, error, horror, or normativity is simply renamed “appearance” without a profile-level account.}
\end{aligned}
\]

**Expiry.** This protocol permits one execution of P1–P3 and the fixed six-case battery for \(\mathcal S_{\rm NP}^{\rm fin}\). It permits no amended eligibility rule, alternative presentation bridge, additional candidate, or successor limitation theory. Any modification requires a distinct preregistration.

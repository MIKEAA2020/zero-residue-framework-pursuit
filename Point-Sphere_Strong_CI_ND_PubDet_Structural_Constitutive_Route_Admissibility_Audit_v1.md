# Strong CI Adjudication — ND Public Determination: Structural/Constitutive Route Admissibility Audit v1
## Can a structural or atemporal generative criterion be specified without covertly importing the missing domain?

## Status

**No structural or constitutive successor is yet admissibly specified.** This is not a finding that such a criterion is impossible. It records that the current proposals remain schemas with no independently typed relation, input, positive case, or non-circular output rule.

## 1. Entry controls

Any successor to essential-content semantics must not import:

1. realization \(R(a)\), actual modehood, or a synonym for already-admitted aspecthood;
2. a formal grammar, graph, quotient, factorization, or code as ontology merely by stipulation;
3. external time, temporal production, a later selector, persistence relation, or public-record decoder;
4. a full candidate domain disguised as an input/seed; or
5. a primitive “self-articulation” relation with no independent semantics.

It must predeclare a relation type, input, output, positive/negative cases, compossibility rule, non-gauge condition, and expiry test.

## 2. Structural schema

A structural proposal has this generic form:

\[
\mathsf{SDet}_G(a):
\quad
\exists\Sigma[
\mathsf{SelfPresentationStructure}(G,\Sigma)
\land
\mathsf{Role}_{\Sigma}(a)
\land
\mathsf{NonGauge}(a)
].
\tag{S}
\]

For (S) to be a public criterion, \(\Sigma\), \(\mathsf{Role}_{\Sigma}\), and their relation to genuine ontology must be given independently of already knowing which \(a\)'s are genuine.

### Structural admissibility trilemma

| Reading of \(\Sigma\) | Result |
|---|---|
| Formal syntax, graph, grammar, or model | Gives a representation; requires the unearned grammar-to-ontology bridge |
| Exact protected domain in structural notation | Restates rich core membership |
| Primitive intrinsic structure of the ground | Leaves \(\mathsf{Role}_{\Sigma}\) semantically unspecified and cannot generate independent cases |

Thus no current structural proposal identifies a public \(\Sigma\).

## 3. Constitutive schema

A constitutive proposal must be atemporal. Let:

\[
\mathsf{CDet}_G(a):
\quad
\exists\sigma[
\mathsf{AtemporalConstitutes}(\sigma,G,a)
\land
\mathsf{TypedInput}(\sigma,G)
\land
\mathsf{NonGauge}(a)
].
\tag{C}
\]

“Constitutes” must not mean temporal production or a later actualization.

### Constitutive admissibility trilemma

| Reading of \(\sigma\) | Result |
|---|---|
| A process, flow, succession, or transition | Imports time and a later-production relation |
| A formal generative rule | Requires a grammar-to-ontology and seed-to-domain bridge |
| The protected identity itself | Restates mode = aspect = self-knowledge content without classifying independent candidates |
| A primitive atemporal constituting relation | Has no independent semantics, cases, or anti-circular test |

Thus no current constitutive proposal identifies a public \(\sigma\).

## 4. Positive/negative-case barrier

A proposal must identify a positive candidate \(a_+\) and a coherent, non-gauge negative candidate \(a_-\) without declaring their aspect status at the outset.

Under the available schemas:

\[
\mathsf{Role}_{\Sigma}(a_+)
\quad\text{or}\quad
\mathsf{AtemporalConstitutes}(\sigma,G,a_+)
\]

cannot be established until \(\Sigma\) or \(\sigma\) is semantically specified. And specifying it by reference to accepted aspects makes the test circular.

\[
\text{No independent }\langle a_+,a_-\rangle\text{ pair is currently available.}
\]

## 5. Compossibility barrier

Neither (S) nor (C) supplies collective compatibility:

\[
\mathsf{SDet}_G(a)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Comp}_G(a,b),
\qquad
\mathsf{CDet}_G(a)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Comp}_G(a,b).
\]

A context relation may be introduced only with its own typed semantics. It must not be inferred from one total formal model or from actual realization.

## 6. Exact result

\[
\boxed{
\text{The structural and constitutive routes are currently schemas, not candidate}\
\mathsf{PubDet}_G\text{ semantics.}
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{SDet}_G
\text{ and }
\mathsf{CDet}_G
\text{ are not admitted for truth-evaluation until \(\Sigma\) or \(\sigma\) is}\
\text{independently defined and its cases are predeclared.}
}
\]

This is an admissibility hold, not a refutation of all structural or constitutive determination.

## 7. Next admissible deliverable

A successor may be registered only by supplying:

\[
\langle
\text{relation type},
\text{atemporal status},
\text{input semantics},
\text{output/domain rule},
\text{positive case},
\text{negative case},
\text{compossibility rule},
\text{non-gauge rule},
\text{expiry test}
\rangle.
\]

Absent this tuple, “structure,” “generation,” “self-articulation,” and “self-presentation form” are prohibited as merely new labels for the unclosed domain debt.

## Nonclaims

This audit does not prove that \(\mathsf{PubDet}_G\) is impossible, deny atemporal constitution, deny protected nondual identity, or reject target-relative formal grammars. It applies the predeclared anti-circularity and no-import controls before a new route may be treated as a candidate solution.

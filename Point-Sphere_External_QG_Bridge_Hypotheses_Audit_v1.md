## 5. Point–Sphere compatibility discipline

None of the physical additions supplies a Point–Sphere derivation.

For strong simplicity:

\[
\mathsf{CSH}\lor\mathsf{EAH}\lor\mathsf{CGH}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{StrongPointSphereCompatible}.
\]

Each package contains positive plurality and relations. A strong Point–Sphere interpretation would require a fresh derivative-level grounding account, not a relabeling of the package as privation.

For weak simplicity:

\[
\mathsf{CSH}\lor\mathsf{EAH}\lor\mathsf{CGH}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{WeakPointSphereCompatible}.
\]

A weak interpretation could propose that the package is immanent formal structure, but would need an independent bridge showing it is nonpartitive, nonindependent, nontemporal, nonspatial, and nonbrute.

Thus:

\[
\mathsf{PhysicalHypothesisCompatibility}
\neq
\mathsf{PointSphereDerivation}.
\]

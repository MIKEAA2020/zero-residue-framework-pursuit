# AP2B/B3 — Intrinsic-Nature Comparative Ledger v1
## Frozen criteria, rival-specific discriminators, and current non-numerical record

## Purpose

This ledger makes the next comparison auditable. It forbids settling the aware-ground question through atmosphere, crude “simplicity” slogans, or invented numerical credences. It follows AP2B/B2 and precedes any decision to carry the aware-ground package into perspective individuation.

The candidates are:

- **AG:** simple, complete, self-knowing aware ground; aspects are nondual modes.
- **NM:** neutral monism.
- **RM:** Russellian monism, including panpsychist/panprotopsychist variants.
- **SR:** epistemic or ontic structural realism; entries must specify which.
- **BN:** brute necessary totality.
- **CT:** classical simple creator, retained as a control for the self-knowledge-to-actuality inference rather than as the main intrinsic-nature rival.

## Rules of use

1. A resource counts only if it is accompanied by a declared bridge, not merely a suggestive label.
2. A debt counts only if it names a missing derivation, a positive incompatibility, or a new primitive.
3. “Unknown” is not a negative result; it is a work item.
4. No candidate wins by having fewer stated claims. A theory that suspends judgement may be more epistemically modest but supplies less positive explanation.
5. No status in this ledger counts as a physics prediction, a proof of the ground, or a numerical posterior probability.

## Frozen criteria

| ID | Criterion | Pass condition | Failure/disconfirmation condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **K1** | Phenomenal adequacy | Gives a non-eliminative account of why phenomenal consciousness is real and not a surplus fact | Treats phenomenality as illusion, unexplained brute addition, or a verbal relabelling |
| **K2** | Positive intrinsic nature | Positively characterises what ultimate reality is, or explicitly and defensibly limits the aim to epistemic suspension | “Intrinsic nature” is a placeholder that does no explanatory work |
| **K3** | Unity and individuation | Supplies a model for one subject or many bounded perspectives, including identity/access conditions | Invokes micro-subject summing, cosmic partition, or perspective labels without an individuating relation |
| **K4** | Structural-physics constraint | Allows the gauge-invariant, relational, mathematical successes of physics without contrary empirical claims | Conflicts with established physical structure or exempts itself from physical recovery indefinitely |
| **K5** | Explanatory termination | States why the final posit terminates explanation and what it renders non-arbitrary | Simply redescribes a brute totality while claiming an explanation |
| **K6** | Modal/realisation clarity | Distinguishes actual aspects, possibilities, descriptions, and gauge copies | Equates conceivability, representation, and actuality without a bridge |
| **K7** | Economy without erasure | Reduces independent primitives without deleting the datum of consciousness or perspective | Achieves parsimony by eliminating what must be explained |

## Rival-specific decisive discriminators

These are not demands for impossible certainty. They state what new result would materially change the comparison.

| Candidate | What would strengthen it | What would count materially against it |
|---|---|---|
| **AG** | A non-arbitrary mode/perspective construction yielding bounded access, record stability, and identity without a second substance; plus a concrete physics recovery bridge | A proof or compelling countermodel that simple self-knowing awareness cannot yield differentiated bounded modes, or that its required aspect map necessarily reintroduces independent primitives |
| **NM** | A positive neutral primitive and a derivation of phenomenal/physical roles and bounded subjects from it | Neutrality remains only a negative label, or phenomenality must be added as an unexplained special feature |
| **RM** | A non-ad hoc account of micro-to-macro subject constitution or a cosmopsychist decombination account compatible with physical causation | Combination/decombination demonstrably fails for its proposed constitution relation, or quiddities make no discriminating explanatory contribution |
| **SR** | A structural account that either derives phenomenal facts or principled reasons why a positive intrinsic ontology is unnecessary | Its structure-only ontology requires unacknowledged intrinsic/phenomenal primitives or cannot individuate qualitative perspective |
| **BN** | A defensible reason why brute necessity is the proper terminus and why consciousness/modal profile require no further account | Its claimed necessity is merely stipulated, or it reintroduces multiple brute psychophysical/modal facts while retaining no explanatory gain |
| **CT** | A coherent account retaining simplicity plus contingent creation/self-knowledge without equivocation | A valid modal-collapse result that shows simple self-knowledge requires actualisation of all relevant contents, or a successful nondual alternative with lower cost |

## Immediate comparative consequences

1. **AG has not passed K3 or K4.** Its advantages in K1/K2/K5 are not enough for a final framework claim.
2. **RM and cosmopsychist versions are not dismissed.** They share the consciousness/intrinsic-nature motivation but owe combination or decombination work.
3. **SR constrains every candidate.** It has not thereby supplied a complete intrinsic-nature ontology; ESR and OSR must never be merged.
4. **BN is coherent as a terminus but cannot claim explanatory unification it declines to offer.**
5. **CT is a live control.** It proves that simplicity plus self-knowledge do not, without the strong nondual aspect identity, transparently entail plenitude.
6. **P-internal is the only plenitude claim currently eligible for conditional derivation.** P-maximal remains blocked by K3/K6 domain work.

## B3 acceptance and expiry conditions

B3 is complete when the criteria, burden rules, discriminators, and current record are frozen—as they are here.

B3 does **not** decide which view is true. The next task is B4: an adversarial argument reconstruction that applies this ledger to the strongest available forms of AG and its rivals. B4 expires with one of two honest results:

- a conditional comparative preference for AG, explicitly limited by unresolved K3/K4 debts; or
- an underdetermination finding, with AG retained as a live hypothesis rather than a preferred framework.

## Nonclaims

This ledger supplies no deduction of awareness, no deduction of plenitude, no consciousness theory, no solution to the Problem of Time, and no empirical confirmation. It is a discipline for the comparative task.

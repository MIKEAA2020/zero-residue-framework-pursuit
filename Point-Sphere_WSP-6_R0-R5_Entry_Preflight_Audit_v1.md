## 0. Audit standards

### 0.1 Role-independence standard

Role-first classification can break the prior type-first circle only if each role has an application condition that does not rely on knowing in advance which ontological category its candidate occupies:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{IndependentRoleTest}(\mathsf R)
\Longrightarrow{}&
\mathsf{ApplicationCondition}(\mathsf R)
\text{ is fixed without }\mathsf{EntityModeClassification}\\
&\land
\mathsf{RivalCaseDiscrimination}(\mathsf R)
\text{ is available without }\mathsf{DesiredBoundaryResult}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{Role-Independence-Standard}
\]

A merely renamed category contrast does not meet the test:

\[
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(x)
\iff
\mathsf{EntityLike}(x)
\quad\text{or}\quad
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d)
\iff
\mathsf{ModeLike}(d)
\tag{Forbidden-Role-Redescription}
\]

would not be role-first progress.

### 0.2 Formal-feature route

The candidate's proposed promising route would require features such as independent countability, reidentification conditions, separate bearer-capacity, or a distinct explanatory subject-slot on the one hand, versus subject-relative qualifier position, nontransfer ascription grammar, and adverbial rather than substantival explanatory form on the other. But those features must themselves be fixed without merely importing the entity/mode conclusion:

\[
\mathsf{FormalFeatureList}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{IndependentRoleTest}
\quad\text{unless each feature has a fixed non-question-begging application condition.}
\tag{Feature-List-Not-Yet-Test}
\]

### 0.3 Protected burden rule

Failure of a positive-immanent warrant is not proof of composition, independent being, or comparative defeat:

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP6}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{IndependentConstituentOf}(d,G)
\lor
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6}).
\tag{No-Reversal-of-Burden}
\]

## 4. R2 — independent identifiability of subject-role and qualifier-role

R2 is decisive. The packet defines subject-role by the ability to occur as the bearer of a separately formulable intrinsic attribution:

\[
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(x,\mathcal X)
\iff
\exists\psi,\Gamma_x\,[
\mathsf{ExactIntrinsicGround}(\langle x,\psi,\Gamma_x\rangle)
\land
\mathsf{OccursAsBearer}(x,\langle x,\psi,\Gamma_x\rangle)].
\tag{R2-Subject-Role-Under-Test}
\]

It defines qualifier-role by determination-parameter status, noneliminability, and failure to occur as bearer in the frame for \(G\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G,\mathcal X)
\iff{}&
\mathsf{DeterminationParameter}(d,\Gamma)
\land
\mathsf{NoneliminableFor}(d,\varphi,G,\Gamma)\\
&\land
\neg\mathsf{OccursAsBearer}(d,\mathcal X).
\end{aligned}
\tag{R2-Qualifier-Role-Under-Test}
\]

### 4.1 The unavailable discriminator

The source packet supplies no independently specified formal test for any of the pivotal notions \(\mathsf{OccursAsBearer}\), \(\mathsf{DeterminationParameter}\), or separate formulability. As a result, deciding whether \(d\) is a subject or a qualifier still requires deciding whether it is apt to be treated as an item/bearer or merely as a manner of \(G\):

\[
\mathsf{Classify}
(d,\mathsf{SubjectRole}\;\text{vs.}\;\mathsf{QualifierRole})
\rightsquigarrow
\mathsf{Presupposes}
(d,\mathsf{EntityLike}\;\text{vs.}\;\mathsf{ModeLike}).
\tag{R2-Circle-Diagnosis}
\]

A real mode could be described using a nominal term and then granted a separate attribution; a dependent trope could be described adverbially and denied one. The packet gives no form-sensitive criterion that blocks those competing redescriptions:

\[
\mathsf{NominalForm}(d)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(d),
\qquad
\mathsf{AdverbialForm}(d,G)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G).
\tag{R2-Grammatical-Form-Insufficient}
\]

Likewise, independent countability, reidentifiability across explanatory contexts, and distinct bearer-capacity are identified as possible routes but are not defined or used as actual application tests in WSP-6:

\[
\mathsf{Countability}
\lor
\mathsf{Reidentifiability}
\lor
\mathsf{BearerCapacity}
\notin
\mathsf{FixedApplicationTests}_{\rm WSP6}.
\tag{R2-Formal-Feature-Route-Not-Implemented}
\]

### 4.2 R2 disposition

WSP-6 has genuinely relocated the question from a primitive \(\mathbf E/\mathbf M\) type allocation to a role distinction. That is methodological progress. It has not yet provided formal features sufficient to identify those roles independently, and so it has not broken the circle.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{R2Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm role\!\text{-}first\ methodology\ stated;\\
\rm subject\!\text{-}role/qualifier\!\text{-}role\ discriminator\ not\ independently\ supplied
}}.
}
\tag{R2-Result}
\]

This is not a proof that no such discriminator can exist:

\[
\mathsf{NoImplementedIndependentRoleTest}_{\rm WSP6}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{NoPossibleIndependentRoleTest}.
\tag{R2-No-Impossibility-Claim}
\]

## 5. R3 — Exact Role Discipline

ERD has a genuine general anti-reification rationale:

\[
\mathsf{PredicateOrParameterOccurrence}(d)
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{EntityAddition}(d).
\tag{R3-General-AntiReification-Credit}
\]

That rule is not tailored to Point–Sphere monism. It correctly warns against inferring a new entity simply from a grammatical predicate or explanatory parameter.

However, ERD's operative condition is the absence of every subject-role for \(d\):

\[
\mathsf{QualifierRole}(d,G,\mathcal X)
\land
\neg\exists\mathcal Y\,\mathsf{SubjectRole}(d,\mathcal Y)
\Longrightarrow
\neg\mathsf{LicenseEntityAddition}(d).
\tag{R3-ERD-Dependence-on-R2}
\]

Without a fixed role discriminator, ERD cannot decide whether an alleged qualifier has no subject-role. It thereby supplies a conditional anti-reification rule, not an independently executed nonentity verdict:

\[
\mathsf{ERD}\text{ is general}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ERD}\text{ settles }\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(d)
\quad\text{without R2.}
\tag{R3-Conditionality}
\]

\[
\mathsf{R3Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6})
=
\mathsf{PartialPass}_{\rm general\ anti\!\text{-}reification\ rationale;\ application\ conditional\ on\ R2}.
\tag{R3-Result}
\]

## 6. R4 — dependent trope and nonseparable-constituent rival

WSP-6 handles the earlier rival more carefully than WSP-4 or WSP-5: it explicitly allows a dependent trope or nonseparable constituent to count as a node if it has a subject-role and is constitutionally countable:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf{DependentTropeOrConstituent}(t,G)
\land
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(t,\mathcal Y)\\
&\land
\mathsf{ConstitutionallyCountable}(t,\mathcal Y)
\Longrightarrow
\mathsf{ConstitutiveNode}_6(t).
\end{aligned}
\tag{R4-Rival-Admitted}
\]

This successfully avoids the invalid dependence-to-nonnode shortcut.

But the candidate does not demonstrate that the rival possesses an independently identifiable subject-role while a real immanent mode lacks one. Both allocations still depend on the contested tests from R2:

\[
\mathsf{DependentTrope}(t,G)
\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\mathsf{SubjectRole}(t,\mathcal Y),
\qquad
\mathsf{RealImmanentMode}_6(d,G)
\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\neg\exists\mathcal Y\,\mathsf{SubjectRole}(d,\mathcal Y).
\tag{R4-Unresolved-Allocation}
\]

\[
\mathsf{R4Status}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm dependent\ rival\ admitted;\\
\rm non\!\text{-}question\!\text{-}begging\ allocation\ test\ not\ supplied
}}.
\tag{R4-Result}
\]

No conclusion follows that every immanent difference is a trope or part.

## 9. Preflight disposition and stop

WSP-6 earns a substantive methodological credit: it makes explicit that the question is not merely which primitive type a candidate receives, but whether a role-first criterion can generate the boundary. Exact Role Discipline is a plausible general anti-reification principle at the stated level of generality.

The decisive R2 test, however, does not pass. The formal features that might independently identify subject-role and qualifier-role—countability, reidentification, bearer-capacity, and form-sensitive explanatory position—are named as possible resources but are not actually fixed as independent tests in the frozen candidate.

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf{PreflightStatus}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6})
=
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\substack{
\rm R0:\ role\ grammar\ typing\ incomplete;\\
\rm R1:\ exact\ grounding\ cluster\ semantically\ underdefined;\\
\rm R2:\ role\ distinction\ not\ independently\ identifiable;\\
\rm R4\text{-}R5:\ rival\ allocation\ and\ real\ qualifier\ condition\ unresolved
}}.
}
\tag{WSP6-Preflight-Result}
\]

The result is not a refutation, a composition verdict, or a comparative defeat:

\[
\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP6}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathsf{Refuted}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6})
\lor
\mathsf{PartComposition}(G)
\lor
\mathsf{ComparativeDefeat}(F_{\rm WSP\text{-}6}).
\tag{No-Overread-of-WSP6}
\]

No field selection, actuality bridge, finite-mode bridge, public interface, or whole-framework evaluation is authorized:

\mathsf{EntryIncomplete}_{\rm WSP6}

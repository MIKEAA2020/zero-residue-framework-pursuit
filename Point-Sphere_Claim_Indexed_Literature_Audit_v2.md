# Point–Sphere Claim-Indexed Literature Audit v2
## Exact bearer, composition, concurrence, consciousness science, and quantum/QFT extensions

## Scope and method

This extends—not replaces—`Point-Sphere_Claim_Indexed_Literature_Audit_v1.md`. Each module asks only what the cited literature licenses: whether a distinction, rival, or formal dependency is live. No citation upgrades a framework premise into a theorem. The audit is directed at the next dependencies identified in v1.

---

## A. Exact bearer and truthmaker theory

### Framework dependency

The foundation stack requires a bridge from a foundation fact \(F\) to an actual, exact, externally non-derivative bearer \(G\). In the master this is represented by TB1–TB4 and, for stronger conclusions, composition reflection, modal dependence, and rigid identity conditions.

### Literature result

The truthmaker literature confirms that this bridge cannot be treated as automatic. The very formulation of truthmaking depends on a prior account of truthbearers, and live proposals differ between existence-entailment, necessitation, subject-matter/projection, and grounding accounts. The [SEP entry on truthmakers](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/truthmakers/) explicitly distinguishes truthmaking as entailment, primitive necessitation, and grounding; it also notes that the truthmaker/truthbearer relation itself is disputed. Grounding-based truthmaker formulations—e.g. \(x\)'s existence grounds the truth that \(p\)—are available, but they already presuppose a substantive grounding idiom rather than deriving it.

### Consequence for the programme

- A truthmaker for \(F\) need not be the unique **bearer** of \(F\); it may be partial, non-minimal, plural, or specified only relative to a proposition/truthbearer theory.
- Truthmaking is non-causal and cannot be silently treated as an entity-producing causal relation.
- Exact-bearer uniqueness therefore remains TB2, not an output of generic truthmaker theory.
- The move from “the existence of \(G\) grounds the truth of \(F\)” to “\(G\) is simple, wholly self-explanatory, or the only entity” needs further independent premises.

**Audit status:** TB1–TB4 remain framework-specific bridge principles. The literature strengthens the demand for exact typing of facts, propositions, grounding, and bearers; it does not establish the bearer transfer.

## B. Composition reflection and entity simplicity

### Framework dependency

A proposed composition-reflection premise would infer something about the bearer’s internal composition from the grounding profile of a foundation fact. This is stronger than fact-level monism or uniqueness of a foundation fact.

### Literature result

Mereology does not supply that inference for free. The [SEP discussion of mereology](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mereology/) treats composition principles as substantive and warns that a sufficient composition condition can become an extremely strong biconditional. The [SEP discussion of identity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity/#ComAsIde) distinguishes weak composition-as-identity—an analogy—from the strong thesis that parts are literally collectively identical with their whole. Neither thesis says that a unique ground fact can have only a simple bearer, or that a composite bearer must inherit an external ground from its constituents.

### Consequence for the programme

The following inferences remain invalid without additional stated principles:

\[
\text{unique foundation fact}\ \nRightarrow\ \text{one mereologically simple bearer},
\]
\[
\text{bearer has parts}\ \nRightarrow\ \text{its existence is externally grounded},
\]
\[
\text{part-level dependence}\ \nRightarrow\ \text{external dependence of the composite itself}.
\]

A defensible composition-reflection premise must declare its parthood theory, what counts as metaphysically explanatory composition, whether the parts are distinct entities/facts, and why their dependence transfers to the whole rather than being internally explained by its nature.

**Audit status:** no upgrade. The premise is a live, costly optional bridge and needs targeted countermodels before use in a strong-aseity proof.

## C. Divine simplicity, concurrence, creation, and modal collapse

### Literature result

The modal-collapse issue remains a structured dispute, not a settled inference. The [SEP entry on divine simplicity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-simplicity/) presents the pressure between strong simplicity/necessary divine action and contingency of creatures, while also recording contested responses. The [SEP entry on divine freedom](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/divine-freedom/) distinguishes questions about possible alternatives, unsurpassable worlds, and partial versus total collapse.

Concurrence literature also blocks a crude choice between creaturely autonomy and occasionalism. The [SEP entry on occasionalism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/occasionalism/) characterises concurrentism as a middle position: created causes can contribute genuinely while their activity depends on divine concurrence. The [SEP discussion of Leibnizian causation](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/leibniz-causation/) similarly describes a version in which created substances have genuine powers while divine involvement remains direct and continuous.

### Consequence for the programme

- OA (one simple intrinsic act/nature) still does not entail a necessary totality-realisation rule NR, nor universal actualisation AP.
- A concurrence model can make room for genuine secondary causal roles only after its causal typing and determination relation are specified; it does not solve selector, freedom, evil, or modal-collapse issues by labelling causes “non-competitive.”
- C-Nec, C-Cont, and strict ND remain live branches. Their costs are different; the literature does not select one.

**Audit status:** the master’s conditional OA/NR/AP distinction and creation architecture ledger are retained unchanged.

## D. Empirical consciousness science

### Literature result

Current empirical consciousness science supports adversarial, preregistered comparison—not retrospective identification of a favoured carrier metric with consciousness. The 2025 COGITATE consortium study, “[Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1),” precommitted competing predictions with theory proponents and theory-neutral investigators across fMRI, MEG, and intracranial recordings. Its results challenge important predictions of both IIT and GNWT rather than simply choosing a winner.

This supports the programme’s methodological direction—explicit competing predictions, locked analyses, theory-neutral evaluation, and informative failure—but supplies no validation of D/I/S/L/P or \(\mathbf E_\Theta\). IIT, global-workspace, recurrent-processing, predictive-processing, higher-order, illusionist, and other accounts use different operationalisations and bridge claims. Neural or behavioural discrimination between them does not by itself settle phenomenality or ultimate intrinsic nature.

### Consequence for the programme

A future Tier-2 carrier study must:

1. pre-register differential predictions against existing rival measures and matched controls;
2. distinguish report, task performance, access, memory, arousal, and putative experience measures;
3. keep boundaries/scales/thresholds locked before consciousness-labelled data;
4. report theory failures rather than recode them as support; and
5. avoid treating an NCC-like correlation as evidence for an awareness-ground.

**Audit status:** supports the three-gate methodology only. It does not upgrade carrier necessity/sufficiency, phenomenality, or awareness-ground claims.

## E. AQFT, quantum interpretation, and lattice/continuum theory

### Literature result

The [SEP QFT entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/quantum-field-theory/) explains why AQFT is a distinct axiomatic framework centred on algebras/nets of local observables and why infinite-degree-of-freedom QFT faces inequivalent representations. That mathematical framework does not follow merely from generic structural, grounding, or perspective vocabulary.

Quantum interpretations remain underdetermined by the shared formalism. For example, the [SEP account of Everettian quantum mechanics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-everett/) describes pure wave mechanics plus relative states and a typicality programme; the [SEP account of Bohmian mechanics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qm-bohm/) describes an alternative completion with its own ontology and effective-collapse analysis. Decoherence can explain stable record structures within several approaches; it is not by itself a consciousness-triggered collapse theory.

For lattice field theory, the physical continuum claim is inseparable from regulator removal, parameter tuning, and renormalised observables. Standard lattice sources emphasise that a continuum limit is not a one-spacing match and that bare parameters must be adjusted along a stated limiting trajectory. This supports the newly frozen finite-volume \(\lambda\phi^4_2\) target specification, but does not turn a successful simulation into a derivation of QFT from the framework.

### Consequence for the programme

- AQFT, Lorentzian reconstruction, quantum interpretation, Born weights, and physical record claims remain separate successors, each needing their own axioms and comparators.
- No branch/relative-state language warrants “all possibilities are actual” or “observers create outcomes” without an interpretation-specific argument.
- The finite-volume \(\lambda\phi^4_2\) target is an interacting numerical testbed, not an AQFT construction or a quantum interpretation.

**Audit status:** no physics or quantum bridge is upgraded.
